摘要 · Abstract

本文追溯了用于文本分类的语言模型的演变历程，从早期配合朴素贝叶斯和逻辑回归使用的词袋表示，到 CNN 和 RNN 等深度神经网络架构，最后到基于 Transformer 的模型，以此将近期出现的 Jev AI 分类器置于这一历史脉络中加以理解。文章解释了词袋方法将变长文本转换为固定大小的词频向量，从而实现廉价而有效的分类，但丢弃了词序信息；而词嵌入和 RNN 则以更难的训练为代价保留了序列结构。核心论点是：Jev 本质上是一个文本分类器，但又不仅仅是一个文本分类器：它比狭窄的特定任务模型更通用，又比大型通用 LLM 更快、更便宜，占据了一个实用的中间地带。对读者而言，结论是：理解这段历史有助于揭开围绕 Jev 的炒作迷雾，并厘清此类分类器真正擅长之处。

This article traces the evolution of language models for text classification, from early bag-of-words representations used with naive Bayes and logistic regression to deep neural architectures such as CNNs and RNNs, and finally to transformer-based models, in order to contextualize the recent Jev AI classifier. It explains that bag-of-words converts variable-length text into fixed-size word-frequency vectors, enabling cheap and effective classification but discarding word order, while word embeddings and RNNs preserve sequential structure at the cost of harder training. The core argument is that Jev is essentially a text classifier, yet not just a text classifier: it is more general than narrow task-specific models and much faster and cheaper than large general-purpose LLMs, occupying a practical middle ground. The conclusion for readers is that understanding this history helps demystify the hype around Jev and clarifies where such classifiers genuinely excel.

1.1 词袋模型：朴素贝叶斯、逻辑回归与 XGBoost 1.1 Bag-of-words: naive Bayes, logistic regression, and XGBoost

回想当年，15 年前我还是研究生的时候，尽管循环神经网络已经存在（后面会详细讨论），文本分类通常还是采用词袋表示，因为它简单直接，并且能在中等规模的数据集上取得不错的效果。

Back in the day, when I was a grad student 15 years ago, even though recurrent neural networks already existed (more on that later), text classification was usually done with a bag-of-words representation because it was straightforward and could get good results on moderately sized datasets.

简而言之，我们可以将词袋表示视为一种方法，它使得不同长度的自由形式文本输入能够与经典分类器（朴素贝叶斯、逻辑回归、SVM、随机森林、XGBoost 等）兼容，这些分类器期望固定大小的输入向量。

In short, we can think of the bag-of-words representation as a method that makes free-form text input of different lengths compatible with classic classifiers (naive Bayes, Logistic Regression, SVMs, Random Forest, XGBoost, to name a few), which expect a fixed-size input vector.

流行的实际应用包括从新闻文章分类到电子邮件垃圾邮件过滤等。而且，据说连 Gmail 最初的垃圾邮件过滤器也使用了带有词袋表示的朴素贝叶斯模型。

Popular real-world applications include anything from news article classification to email spam filtering. And yes, allegedly even Gmail's original spam filter used a Naive Bayes model with a bag-of-words representation.

顺便提一下，我正好在 12 年前写过关于这种方法的文章。那是我在 arXiv 上分享的最早内容之一。

As a side note, I wrote about this approach exactly 12 years ago. It was one of the first things I shared on arXiv.

Text classification with Naive Bayes goes back to at least 1961, where it was used to sort computer abstracts; see the Automatic Indexing: And Experimental Inquiry paper.

词袋模型首先构建词汇表，该词汇表由训练集中所有唯一单词组成（可选地，可以去除所谓的停用词，如“a”和“the”，这些词在大多数上下文中几乎不携带语义信息）。

A bag-of-words model starts by building the vocabulary, which consists of all unique words in the training set (optionally, one can get rid of so-called stopwords like "a" and "the", which are words that carry little to no semantic meaning in most contexts).

词袋表示通过为词汇表中的每个单词在向量中分配一个位置，从而产生这些固定大小的输入。然后，我们统计每个单词在文档中出现的频率。例如，如果词汇表包含 50,000 个唯一单词，那么无论输入是仅十个单词还是 300k 个单词，都会生成一个包含 50,000 个条目的固定大小向量。注意，大多数条目为零，因为每个文档只包含词汇表的一小部分。（除了表示原始计数外，还有像 TF-IDF 这样的归一化方案。）

A bag-of-words representation results in these fixed-size inputs by assigning each word in a vocabulary its own position in a vector. We then count how often each word occurs in a document. For example, if we have a vocabulary of 50,000 unique words, it produces a fixed-size vector with 50,000 entries, regardless of whether the input consists of only ten words or 300k words. Note that most entries are zero because each document contains only a small subset of the vocabulary. (Instead of representing the raw counts, there are also normalization schemes like TF-IDF.)

然后，一旦我们有了这些词频向量，就可以在带标签的训练集上训练分类器，例如标记为垃圾邮件或非垃圾邮件的电子邮件。例如，逻辑回归模型会学习特征权重，将某些单词（及其词频）与特定标签关联起来。例如，某些单词可能会增加预测的垃圾邮件概率，而其他单词可能会降低它。

Then, once we have these word frequency vectors, we can train a classifier on a labeled training set, such as emails labeled as spam or non-spam. For example, a logistic regression model would then learn feature weights that correlate certain words (and word counts) with particular labels. For instance, certain words might increase the predicted spam probability, and others may decrease it.

这种方法计算成本低，并且当特定词语为标签提供强烈线索时，效果可能很好。在诸如垃圾邮件分类这样的简单分类任务中，这通常足以快速获得合理准确的结果。

This approach is computationally cheap and can work well when particular words provide strong clues about the label. In a simple classification task such as spam classification, this is often enough to get quick, reasonably accurate results.

但这种方法最大的缺点之一是，由于词袋表示的本质，它丢失了词序。因此，例如，“狗咬人”和“人咬狗”尽管描述不同事件，却产生相同的向量。

But one of the biggest downsides of this approach is that, because of the nature of the bag-of-words representation, it loses word order. So, for example, “the dog bites the man” and “the man bites the dog” produce identical vectors despite describing different events.

（有一些变通方法可以通过添加词对或更长的序列（称为 n-gram）作为特征来保留一些局部顺序，尽管这会增加词汇量。）

(There are some workarounds to preserve some local order by adding word pairs or longer sequences, called n-grams, as features, although this increases the vocabulary size.)

尽管有这些缺点，我仍然认为词袋在某些低风险应用中有一席之地，因为它非常廉价，而且词袋表示 + 逻辑回归仍然是我处理每个文本分类问题的首选基线，因为它非常容易实现。

Despite the shortcomings, I still think that a bag-of-words has its place in certain low-stakes applications because it’s so cheap, and a bag-of-words representation + logistic regression remains my go-to baseline for every text classification problem, since it’s so easy to implement.

1.2 用于文本分类的深度神经网络 1.2 Deep neural networks for text classification

上述词袋模型也可以与（简单的）深度神经网络（如多层感知机）配合使用。但其缺点仍然在于，我们会丢失句子结构和词序。

The aforementioned bag-of-words model would also work with (simple) deep neural networks, like multilayer perceptrons. But the downside still is that we would lose the sentence structure and word order.

然而，更复杂的神经网络架构避免了词袋这一权宜之计：卷积神经网络（CNN）和循环神经网络（RNN），它们可以将词嵌入作为输入。

However, more sophisticated neural network architectures avoid the bag-of-words workaround: convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and recurrent neural networks (RNNs), which can take word embeddings as input.

1.2.1 词嵌入 1.2.1 Word embeddings

首先，在将输入文本馈入模型之前，我们必须将其转换为合适的表示形式。一种表示形式是词袋（bag-of-words）。另一种是词嵌入向量。区别在于，词袋向量通过统计每个词汇出现的频率来表示整个文本，而词嵌入则将单个词表示为学习得到的稠密数值向量。

First, before feeding the input texts into a model, we have to convert them into a suitable representation. One such representation is bag-of-words. Another is word embedding vectors. The difference is that a bag-of-words vector represents the entire text by counting how often each vocabulary word occurs, while a word embedding represents an individual word as a dense vector of learned numbers.

图 5：创建词嵌入的示意图。

Figure 5: Illustration of creating word embeddings.

* 词嵌入的工作原理类似于 LLM 中的嵌入层，即它们将输入 token 转换为稠密向量。嵌入可以在模型外部进行（例如，两种经典且流行的学习方法是 Word2Vec 和 GloVe），或者嵌入层可以是神经网络架构本身的一部分，并在模型训练期间进行学习和调整。

* Word embeddings work similarly to embedding layers in LLMs, i.e., they convert input tokens into dense vectors. Embeddings can happen outside the model (e.g., two classic, popular methods for learning them are Word2Vec and GloVe), or the embedding layer can be part of the neural network architecture itself and be learned and tuned during model training.

这些经典嵌入在查找时是与上下文无关的。例如，单词“bank”在“river bank”和“bank account”中得到相同的向量（如你所知，上下文可以通过注意力机制等概念来处理）。

These classic embeddings are context-independent at lookup time. The word “bank”, for example, gets the same vector in “river bank” and “bank account” (as you may know, context can be handled via concepts like attention).

1.2.2 循环神经网络（RNN） 1.2.2 Recurrent neural networks (RNNs)

鉴于你们中的许多人可能已经熟悉循环神经网络（RNN），我将保持这一节简短。RNN 是自然语言处理中经典的常用神经网络架构，其流行变体可以追溯到 20 世纪 80 年代和 90 年代初。2017 年引入的 Transformer（使用了最初在 RNN 中引入的注意力机制；简要时间线见我的《理解大型语言模型》），随后在许多 NLP 应用中逐渐取代了它们。

Since many of you are probably familiar with recurrent neural networks (RNNs), I will keep this section short. RNNs are a classic go-to neural network architecture for natural language processing, and popular variants go back to the 1980s and early 1990s. Transformers, which were introduced in 2017 (and using the attention mechanisms that were first introduced in RNNs; see my Understanding Large Language Models for a brief timeline), then gradually replaced them in many NLP applications.

RNN 一次读取一个词的序列（如文本）。在每一步，它们将当前词嵌入（上一节讨论过）与前一步的隐藏状态相结合。我们可以将隐藏状态视为一个固定大小的向量，它总结了到目前为止处理的文本，因此这使得词序变得重要，因为重新排列词语会改变状态更新的序列。

RNNs read a sequence (like text) one word at a time. At each step, they combine the current word embedding (discussed in the previous section) with a hidden state from the previous step. We can think of the hidden state as a fixed-size vector that summarizes the text processed so far, so this makes word order matter, because rearranging the words changes the sequence of state updates.

RNN 以难以训练而闻名，并且 RNN 有一些重要的改进，例如 1997 年提出的长短期记忆（LSTM）网络，以及 2014 年提出的门控循环单元（GRU），它们使用学习到的门来控制信息的保留和更新方式。（还有更近期的 xLSTM：扩展长短期记忆，于 2024 年提出）。

RNNs were notoriously hard to train, and there are important improvements to RNNs, like Long short-term memory (LSTM) networks, introduced in 1997, and gated recurrent units (GRUs), introduced in 2014, which use learned gates to control how information is retained and updated. (There is also the more recent xLSTM: Extended long short-term memory, introduced in 2024).

此外，状态空间模型受到这种固定大小隐藏状态顺序更新的思想的启发，这比 Transformer 的注意力机制更便宜。然而，瓶颈仍然是隐藏状态能够保留多少信息，而且它仍然必须顺序处理。（有趣的事实：注意力机制最初是为 RNN 开发的，之后才出现 Transformer 架构，但这是另一个故事了；我在我的《理解大型语言模型》文章中写过这一点。）

Also, state-space models are inspired by this idea of a fixed-size hidden state updated sequentially, which is cheaper than transformer attention. However, the bottleneck is still how much information the hidden state can retain, and it still has to be processed sequentially. (Fun fact: attention was first developed for RNNs before the transformer architecture came along, but it's a story for another time; I've written about it in my Understanding Large Language Models article.)

顺便问一下，为什么选择 BERT 或 GPT 风格的基于 Transformer 的模型，而不是之前提到的更简单的 RNN 和 CNN 架构？从技术角度来看，上述方法对两者都适用。但基于 Transformer 的模型扩展性非常好，这意味着它们可以在更大的数据集上进行预训练，并比其他模型获益更多。此外，它们能很好地利用上下文提供的信息（得益于注意力机制）。因此，如果我们希望模型能很好地泛化到不同的目标任务，而无需对每个任务进行显式微调，那么在高质量数据集上预训练基于 Transformer 的模型，可能比基于 RNN 或 CNN 的模型更接近目标。

By the way, why BERT- or GPT-style transformer-based models over simpler RNN and CNN architectures mentioned earlier? From a technical perspective, the approach outlined above works with either. But transformer-based models scale really well, meaning they can be pre-trained on larger datasets and benefit more than other models. Also, they can make good use of the information provided in the context (thanks to attention). So, if we want our model to generalize well to different target tasks without explicit fine-tuning on each one, pre-training a transformer-based model on a high-quality dataset likely gets us closer than RNN- or CNN-based models.

5.1 关于校准 5.1 On calibration

校准并非一个新话题，对于任何想要使用和评估类别归属概率的生产模型，我都推荐这一步。简而言之，校准会调整模型的概率估计，使其更好地匹配观测到的类别频率。

Calibration is not a new topic and a step I recommend for any production model where you want to use and evaluate the class-membership probabilities. In short, calibration adjusts the model's probability estimates so they better match observed class frequencies.

例如，再次考虑我们的 IMDb 电影分类问题。模型可能将一条评论判定为 74% 正面和 26% 负面。这些是概率估计，但模型可能过度自信或信心不足。如果不评估校准，我们就不能假设这些数值是可靠的。也就是说，在 50% 的阈值下，74% 正面和 54% 正面的预测都会得到类别标签“正面”，尽管模型对 74% 的情况表达了更高的置信度。

For example, consider once more our IMDb movie classification problem. A model might assign a review 74% positive and 26% negative. These are probability estimates, but the model may be overconfident or underconfident. We cannot assume that the numerical values are reliable without evaluating calibration. I.e., predictions of 74% positive and 54% positive both yield the class label "positive" at a 50% threshold, although the model expresses more confidence in the 74% case.

校准技术使用一个单独的带标签数据集（例如，留出的验证集）来调整这些概率估计。成功校准后，在许多被赋予约 74% 正面概率的评论中，大约 74% 应该实际上是正面的。如需更多信息，我推荐经典的 scikit-learn 文档。

Calibration techniques use a separate labeled dataset, e.g., a held-out validation set, to adjust these probability estimates. After successful calibration, among many reviews assigned a positive probability of approximately 74%, roughly 74% should actually be positive. For more information, I recommend the good old scikit-learn documentation.

有很多方法可以实现这一点。例如，我在我的 AI 检测器项目中使用的一种方法是温度缩放。温度缩放会在应用 softmax 之前，将模型的 logits 除以一个温度（\(T\)）值。我们通过在保持模型权重固定的情况下，最小化校准数据集上的交叉熵损失来学习 \(T\)（一个正数）。温度高于 1 会降低对最高概率类别的置信度，而温度在 0 到 1 之间则会增加置信度。然后，我们将这个相同的温度用于新的预测。由于这种缩放保持了 logits 的顺序，当我们选择最高概率类别时，预测的类别保持不变。

There are many approaches for this. For example, one that I used in my AI detector project is temperature scaling. Temperature scaling divides the model's logits by a temperature (\(T\)) value before applying softmax. We learn \(T\) (a positive number) by minimizing the cross-entropy loss on the calibration dataset while keeping the model weights fixed. A temperature above 1 reduces confidence in the highest-probability class, while a temperature between 0 and 1 increases it. We then use this same temperature for new predictions. Because this scaling preserves the ordering of the logits, the predicted class remains unchanged when we select the highest-probability class.

Jev 的 RLCD 训练方法仍是专有的，但我们可以讨论一个相关的想法——**基于校准奖励的强化学习**（RLCR），它是在 2025 年的《Beyond Binary Rewards: Training LMs to Reason About Their Uncertainty》论文中提出的。（注意，这两种方法之间没有官方确立的联系，但我假设它们可能相关。）

Jev's RLCD training method remains proprietary, but a related idea we can discuss is _Reinforcement Learning with Calibration Rewards_ (RLCR), which was introduced in the 2025 Beyond Binary Rewards: Training LMs to Reason About Their Uncertainty paper. (Note that there is no officially established connection between the two methods, but I am assuming that they could be related.)

5.3 附注：校准是否必要？ 5.3 Side note: Is calibration necessary?

交叉熵已经鼓励准确的概率估计。在理想条件下，这足以获得校准的预测，而无需额外的惩罚项。

Cross-entropy already encourages accurate probability estimates. Under ideal conditions, that is enough to obtain calibrated predictions without an additional penalty.

即，使用一个类似于电影评论分类的简单抛硬币例子，两个类别是“正面”和“反面”。如果是一枚有偏的硬币，有 80%的时间（期望上）落在正面，那么真实概率是 80%正面和 20%反面。在一个具有代表性的训练集上，当我们优化交叉熵时，模型在最小化交叉熵（负对数似然）的过程中已经会作为副作用学到这些概率。

I.e., using a simple coin flip example analogous to the movie review classification, the two classes are “heads” and “tails.” If it’s a biased coin that lands heads 80% of the time (in expectation), the true probabilities are 80% heads and 20% tails. On a representative training set, when we optimize cross-entropy, the model will already learn these as a side-effect of minimizing cross-entropy (negative-log-likelihood).

所以，简而言之，如果模型学到了真实的类别概率，其预测就是校准的，额外的惩罚项是不必要的。（并且，Brier 损失与交叉熵具有相同的理论最优值，如 Gneiting 和 Raftery 在 2007 年的《严格适当评分规则、预测与估计》中所述。）

So, in short, if the model learns the true class probabilities, its predictions are calibrated and an additional penalty is unnecessary. (And Brier loss has the same theoretical optimum as cross-entropy, as described in Strictly Proper Scoring Rules, Prediction, and Estimation by Gneiting and Raftery, 2007.)

然而，在实践中，我们从未训练到（期望上的）真正最优值，而是在有限数据集上训练。

In practice, however, we never train to the real optimum (in expectation) but train on a finite dataset.

因此，一旦神经网络正确分类了大多数训练样本，它可以通过变得更加自信来进一步降低训练集上的交叉熵。这些置信度估计可能随后在测试集和新数据上泛化得很差。

So, once a neural network correctly classifies most training examples, it can further reduce cross-entropy on the training set by becoming more confident. These confidence estimates may then generalize poorly to the test set and new data.

例如，在《On Calibration of Modern Neural Networks》中，Guo 等人（2017）观察到“神经网络可以在不对 0/1 损失过拟合的情况下对 NLL 过拟合。”

For example, in On Calibration of Modern Neural Networks, Guo et al. (2017) observed “neural networks can overfit to NLL without overfitting to the 0/1 loss.”

这意味着测试准确率仍可能提高，而测试交叉熵却恶化。因此，即使分类准确率持续提升，过拟合也可能出现在概率估计中。

That means test accuracy can still improve while test cross-entropy worsens. So overfitting can show up in the probability estimates even when classification accuracy continues to improve.

添加 Brier 损失会改变训练过程中预测误差的加权方式。但这是否能改善校准，当然需要在留出数据上进行检验。（我的实验中观察到的适度改进是针对此设置的经验观察，我们不应假设添加 Brier 总会有所帮助。）

Adding Brier loss changes how prediction errors are weighted during training. But whether this improves calibration needs to be checked on held-out data, of course. (The modest improvement in my experiment is an empirical observation for this setup, and we should not assume that adding Brier will always help.)

在我的实验中（见前图），将 Brier 损失添加到交叉熵中仅带来了非常小的额外校准收益。

In my experiments (see previous figure), adding the Brier loss to cross-entropy provided only a very small additional calibration benefit.

但在强化学习（RL）情境中，动机更为直接，例如 RLCR 中所述，我们衡量答案正确性，并且尚未最小化交叉熵。因此，在 RL 情境中，Brier 项在奖励答案正确性的同时，增加了报告准确置信度的激励。

But the motivation is more direct in RL contexts, such as described in RLCR, where we measure answer correctness and don’t already minimize cross-entropy. So, in RL contexts, the Brier term adds an incentive to report accurate confidence alongside the reward for answer correctness.

所以，我的观点仍然成立。有许多可靠的替代方案。但让 Jev 如此受欢迎的不是一个独特的想法（如前所述，分类器早已存在），而是它在如此多不同任务上都表现出色。据称它唯一表现不佳的任务（一位读者告诉我）是非英语的法律评论。（但可以通过将其连接到一个廉价的翻译模型来规避多语言性能弱的问题，例如 GPT-6 Luna，只需很小的延迟开销。）

So, my point still stands. There are many solid alternatives. But what makes Jev so popular is not a unique idea (as mentioned before, classifiers existed for a long time) but the fact that it performs so well at so many diverse tasks. The only task where it allegedly doesn’t do well (as a reader told me) is legal reviews in non-English languages. (But the weak multilingual performance can be circumvented by connecting it to a cheap translation model, e.g., GPT-6 Luna with a small latency overhead.)

结论 Conclusion

总而言之，乍看之下，Jev 似乎并没有提供任何根本性的新东西。毕竟，人们可能会说“它不过是一个分类器”（上面加了个好用的 API）。然而，它在大量任务上都表现得出奇地好。从这个意义上说，它是专用分类器的即插即用版本。

All in all, at first glance, Jev doesn't seem to offer anything fundamentally new. After all, one might say that "it's just a classifier" (with a nice API on top of it). However, it works surprisingly well across a huge range of tasks. In that sense, it's the plug-and-play version of dedicated classifiers.

当然，专家们仍会微调专用模型，但如今要证明微调一个专用模型的合理性，门槛已经高得多，因为直接扔一个 Jev 或类似 Jev 的模型上去，就能得到足够好的结果。

Sure, experts will still fine-tune specialist models, but the bar to justify fine-tuning a specialized model is now much higher, since it's easy to just throw a Jev or Jev-like at it and get good-enough results.

此外，我并不指望 Jev 和类似 Jev 的模型能解锁新能力或解决以前无法解决的任务。然而，如果我们把这类模型纳入我们的智能体框架中，帮助昂贵的 GPT-6 或 Opus 5.5 模型在该框架内进行决策，那么未来使用智能体框架可能会变得更快、更便宜。

Also, I don't expect Jev and Jev-likes to unlock new capabilities or solve previously unsolvable tasks. However, if we make such models part of our agent harnesses to aid the expensive GPT-6 or Opus 5.5 models in decision-making within that harness, using agent harnesses could become much faster and cheaper in the future.
