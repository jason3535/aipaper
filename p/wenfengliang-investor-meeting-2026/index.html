  • 坚持开源战略，通过打破技术黑盒来构建开发者生态。
    Adheres to open-source strategy, building a developer ecosystem by breaking technology black boxes.
  • 在算力受限下通过极致的算法效率实现顶尖模型突破。
    Achieves top-performing model breakthroughs via extreme algorithmic efficiency under computing constraints.
  • 降低AI使用门槛，推动技术普惠，重塑全球AI发展生态。

    Forms a sharp contrast with the closed-source path of giants like OpenAI, challenging the monopoly of computing power.

局限 · Limitations

  • 开源模式面临商业可持续性和长期竞争优势的挑战。
    Open-source model faces challenges in commercial sustainability and long-term competitive advantage.
  • 算法效率在固定算力约束下存在理论和实践极限。
    Algorithmic efficiency has theoretical and practical limits under fixed compute constraints.
  • 与资金充足的OpenAI等巨头竞争需持续资源投入。
    Competing with well-funded giants like OpenAI requires sustained resource investment.
  • 依赖算法创新可能不足以对抗未来硬件规模扩张优势。
    Reliance on algorithmic innovation may be insufficient against future hardware scaling advantages.
  • 极端降低成本的路径可能在某些场景牺牲鲁棒性或通用性。
    The technical path of extreme cost reduction may sacrifice robustness or generality in some scenarios.
