RVT: Robotic View Transformer for 3D Object Manipulation

摘要 · Abstract

对于 3D 物体操作，构建显式 3D 表示的方法比仅依赖相机图像的方法表现更好。但使用体素等显式 3D 表示会带来巨大的计算成本，影响可扩展性。在这项工作中，我们提出了 RVT，一种用于 3D 操作的多视角变换器，兼具可扩展性和准确性。RVT 的关键特性包括一种跨视角聚合信息的注意力机制，以及从机器人工作空间周围的虚拟视角重新渲染相机输入。在仿真中，我们发现单个 RVT 模型在 18 个 RLBench 任务（含 249 个任务变体）上表现良好，相对成功率比现有最先进方法（PerAct）高出 26%。在达到相同性能的情况下，其训练速度比 PerAct 快 36 倍，推理速度是 PerAct 的 2.3 倍。此外，RVT 在现实世界中仅需每个任务少量（约 10 个）演示即可执行多种操作任务。视觉结果、代码和训练模型可在 https://robotic-view-transformer.github.io/获取。

For 3D object manipulation, methods that build an explicit 3D representation perform better than those relying only on camera images. But using explicit 3D representations like voxels comes at large computing cost, adversely affecting scalability. In this work, we propose RVT, a multi-view transformer for 3D manipulation that is both scalable and accurate. Some key features of RVT are an attention mechanism to aggregate information across views and re-rendering of the camera input from virtual views around the robot workspace. In simulations, we find that a single RVT model works well across 18 RLBench tasks with 249 task variations, achieving 26% higher relative success than the existing state-of-the-art method (PerAct). It also trains 36X faster than PerAct for achieving the same performance and achieves 2.3X the inference speed of PerAct. Further, RVT can perform a variety of manipulation tasks in the real world with just a few ($\sim$10) demonstrations per task. Visual results, code, and trained model are provided at https://robotic-view-transformer.github.io/.

1 引言 1 Introduction

机器人学习的一个基本目标是构建能够在无约束 3D 环境中解决各种操作任务的系统。一类流行的学习方法直接处理来自单个或多个相机的图像。这些基于视图的方法在各种抓取放置和物体重排任务上取得了令人瞩目的成功[1, 2, 3, 4]。然而，它们在需要 3D 推理的任务上的成功有限。如 James 等人[5]和 Shridhar 等人[6]所示，基于视图的方法在 RLBench[7]上的 3D 操作任务中表现不佳，成功率低于 2%。

A fundamental goal of robot learning is to build systems that can solve various manipulation tasks in unconstrained 3D settings. A popular class of learning methods directly processes image(s) viewed from single or multiple cameras. These view-based methods have achieved impressive success on a variety of pick-and-place and object rearrangement tasks [1, 2, 3, 4]. However, their success on tasks that require 3D reasoning has been limited. As shown by James et al. [5] and Shridhar et al. [6], view-based methods struggle at 3D manipulation tasks on RLBench [7] with less than 2% success.

为了解决这个问题，研究人员提出了使用显式 3D 场景表示的方法。C2F-ARM[5]使用多分辨率体素表示场景，在困难的 RLBench 任务上取得了强劲性能。PerAct[6]通过使用感知器 Transformer[8]处理体素，在行为克隆方面改进了 C2F-ARM。然而，与基于图像的方法相比，创建和推理体素的计算成本更高，因为体素数量随分辨率呈立方增长，而图像像素呈平方增长。这使得基于体素的方法在可扩展性上不如基于视图的方法。事实上，在 8 个 V100 GPU 上训练 PerAct 完成 18 个 RLBench 任务需要 16 天（3072 GPU 小时）。这阻碍了快速开发和原型设计。此外，当扩展到包含更多任务和多样性的更大数据集时，这种计算需求变得更加难以承受。

To address this, methods have been proposed that reason with explicit 3D representations of the scene. C2F-ARM [5] represents the scene with multi-resolution voxels and achieves strong performance on difficult RLBench tasks. PerAct [6] improves upon C2F-ARM in behavior cloning by using perceiver transformer [8] to process voxels. However, creating and reasoning over voxels comes at a higher computing cost compared to reasoning over images, since the number of voxels scales cubicly with the resolution as opposed to squarely for image pixels. This makes voxel-based methods less scalable compared to their view-based counterparts. In fact, training PerAct on 18 RLBench tasks takes 16 days using 8 V100 GPUs (3072 GPU hours). This hinders fast development and prototyping. Moreover, such computing requirements become even more prohibitive when scaling to larger datasets with more tasks and diversity.

因此，一个关键问题是——我们能否构建一个不仅性能优异，而且继承基于视图方法可扩展性的操作网络？为此，我们提出了 RVT（机器人视图 Transformer），它在成功率和训练时间上均显著优于最先进的基于体素的方法，如图 1 所示。在相同硬件上，RVT 在 36 倍更短的时间内达到 PerAct 的峰值性能，将训练时间从 14 天缩短至仅 10 小时。除了训练速度更快，RVT 在 RLBench 的 18 个任务（249 个任务变体）上的平均成功率也比 PerAct 高出 26%。RVT 在 RLBench 上 88.9%的任务中优于 PerAct，同时推理速度是 PerAct 的 2.3 倍（11.6 vs 4.9 fps）。此外，我们发现 RVT 在现实世界中同样表现出色，仅需 51 个演示，单个 RVT 模型即可学习执行各种操作任务（5 个任务，13 个变体），如打开抽屉、将物体放在架子上、按压洗手液和堆叠物体（见图 4）。

Hence, a key question is – can we build a manipulation network that not only performs well but also inherits the scalability of view-based methods? To this end, we propose RVT (Robotic View Transformer) that significantly outperforms the SOTA voxel-based method both in terms of success rate and training time, as shown in Fig. 1. With the same hardware, RVT achieves the peak performance of PerAct in 36X less time, decreasing the training time from 14 days to just 10 hours. Apart from being much faster to train, RVT also achieves a 26% higher success rate than PerAct, averaged over 18 tasks (249 task variations) on RLBench. RVT outperforms PerAct on 88.9% of tasks on RLBench while achieving 2.3X the inference speed (11.6 vs 4.9 fps). Further, we find that RVT also works well in the real world, where with only 51 demonstrations, a single RVT model can learn to perform a variety of manipulation tasks (5 tasks, 13 variations) like opening a drawer, placing objects on a shelf, pressing hand sanitizer, and stacking objects (see Fig. 4).

RVT 的核心是一种基于视图的方法，利用 Transformer 架构。它联合关注场景的多个视图，并跨视图聚合信息。然后生成视图级热图和特征，用于预测机器人末端执行器姿态。我们广泛探索了多视图架构的设计，并报告了几个有用的发现。例如，我们观察到，在连接所有图像块进行联合注意力之前，强制 Transformer 首先关注同一图像内的图像块，可以获得更好的性能。

At its core, RVT is a view-based method that leverages the transformer architecture. It jointly attends over multiple views of the scene and aggregates information across the views. It then produces view-wise heatmaps and features that are used to predict robot end-effector pose. We extensively explore the design of the multi-view architecture and report several useful findings. For example, we observe a better performance when enforcing the transformer to first attend over patches within the same image before concatenating the patches for joint attention.

另一个关键创新是，与之前的基于视图的方法不同，我们通过从虚拟视图重新渲染图像，将相机图像与输入到 Transformer 的图像解耦。这使我们能够控制渲染过程，并带来若干好处。例如，我们可以从对任务有用的视角（例如，桌子正上方）重新渲染，而不受现实世界物理约束的限制。此外，由于 RVT 的多视图输入是通过重新渲染获得的，即使只有一个传感器相机，我们也可以使用 RVT——正如我们在现实世界实验中所做的那样。

Another key innovation is that, unlike prior view-based methods, we decouple the camera images from the images fed to the transformer, by re-rendering the images from virtual views. This allows us to control the rendering process and leads to several benefits. For example, we can re-render from viewpoints that are useful for the task (e.g., directly above the table) while not being restricted by real-world physical constraints. Also, since the multi-view input to RVT is obtained via re-rendering, we can use RVT even with a single sensor camera – as done in our real-world experiments.

总之，我们的贡献有三点：首先，我们提出了 RVT，一种用于 3D 物体操作的多视图 Transformer，既准确又具有可扩展性；其次，我们研究了多视图 Transformer 的各种设计选择，这些选择带来了更好的物体操作性能；最后，我们在仿真和现实世界中进行了多任务物体操作的实证研究。

To summarize, our contributions are threefold: first, we propose RVT, a multi-view transformer for 3D object manipulation that is accurate and scalable; second, we investigate various design choices for the multi-view transformer that lead to better object manipulation performance; and finally, we present an empirical study for multi-task object manipulation in simulation and the real world.

2 相关工作 2 Related Work

基于视觉的物体操作。机器人控制策略的学习传统上使用低维状态观测进行研究[9, 10, 11, 12, 13]。近年来，基于视觉的策略[14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 19, 20, 21]越来越受到关注，因为高维视觉感官输入提供了跨任务更可泛化的观测表示，并且在现实世界感知系统中更易获取。研究者探索了各种形式的视觉输入。先前的工作直接将 RGB 图像编码为低维潜在空间，并依赖基于模型[22, 23]或无模型[24, 25]的强化学习（RL）来训练策略在该空间中操作。最近，RT-1[26]通过利用 Transformer 架构[27]从图像历史中推断机器人的动作。我们提出的 RVT 也使用 Transformer 来预测动作，但与 RT-1 不同，我们还利用深度信息构建多视图场景表示。深度输入的使用也得到了广泛研究。像 CLIPort[3]和 IFOR[1]这样的方法直接处理 RGB-D 图像进行物体操作，因此仅限于 2D 自上而下设置中的简单抓取放置任务。为了克服这个问题，研究者利用了显式 3D 表示，如点云。C2F-ARM[5]和 PerAct[6]将点云体素化，并使用 3D 卷积网络作为控制推理的主干。然而，高精度任务通常需要高分辨率的体素化，导致高内存消耗和慢训练速度。我们的方法属于这一类，但通过将点云转换为来自多个视图的一组 RGB-D 图像来解决可扩展性问题。我们表明，与直接使用 RGB(-D)或点云输入相比，这显著提高了内存占用和训练效率，并带来了更高的性能（见表 1）。另一个相关工作 MIRA[28]也使用新视图图像来表示 3D 场景以进行动作推理。MIRA 通过从一组 RGB 图像隐式构建场景的神经辐射场表示（NeRF），然后从优化的 NeRF 模型生成新视图图像来实现这一点。然而，优化场景 NeRF 模型的要求减慢了测试时的推理速度，并且依赖于密集视图的 RGB 图像作为输入。相比之下，我们的方法可以实现显著更快的推理速度，并且可以仅使用单视图 RGB 图像工作。

Vision-based Object Manipulation. The learning of robotic control policy has been traditionally studied with low-dimensional state observations [9, 10, 11, 12, 13]. Recently, vision-based policies [14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 19, 20, 21] have gained increasing attention since the high-dimensional visual sensory input provides more generalizable observation representation across tasks and is more accessible in real-world perception systems. Various forms of visual input have been explored. Prior work has directly encoded the RGB images into a low-dimensional latent space and relied on model-based [22, 23] or model-free [24, 25] reinforcement learning (RL) to train policies to operate in this space. More recently, RT-1 [26] infers the robot’s actions from a history of images by leveraging transformer architectures [27]. Our proposed RVT also uses a transformer to predict actions, however, unlike RT-1, we additionally leverage depth to construct a multi-view scene representation. The use of depth input has also been extensively studied. Methods like CLIPort [3] and IFOR [1] directly process the RGB-D images for object manipulation, and hence are limited to simple pick-and-place tasks in 2D top-down settings. To overcome this issue, explicit 3D representations such as point clouds have been utilized. C2F-ARM [5] and PerAct [6] voxelize the point clouds and use a 3D convolutional network as the backbone for control inference. However, high-precision tasks typically require high resolution of voxelization, resulting in high memory consumption and slow training. Our approach falls into this category but addresses the scalability issue by transforming the point cloud into a set of RGB-D images from multiple views. We show that this significantly improves memory footprint and training efficiency, and leads to higher performance when compared to directly working with RGB(-D) or point cloud input (see Table. 1). Another relevant work is MIRA [28], which also uses novel view images to represent the 3D scene for action inference. MIRA achieves this by implicitly constructing a neural radiance field representation (NeRF) of the scene from a set of RGB images and then generating novel view images from the optimized NeRF model. However, the requirement of optimizing a scene NeRF model slows down the inference speed at test time and relies on RGB images from a dense set of views as input. In contrast, our approach can achieve significantly faster inference speed and can work with even a single-view RGB image.

机器人中的多任务学习。学习一个模型完成许多不同任务最近引起了机器人社区的特别兴趣。大量工作通过使用可泛化的任务或动作表示（如物体点云[18, 19]、语义分割和光流[1]以及以物体为中心的表示[29, 30]）来实现多任务泛化。然而，这些表示的有限表达能力限制了它们只能在任务类别内泛化。研究者使用强化学习研究了任务参数化[31, 32]和离散任务相关输出层[33, 34]方法，以学习不同类别任务的策略。随着大型语言模型的最新突破，多任务机器人学习已通过使用自然语言指定广泛任务并从大型预收集数据集学习策略来实现[35, 26, 36, 2, 37, 38, 39, 40, 41]。我们受到这一成功的启发，但提出使用小型演示数据集学习语言条件化的多任务策略。

Multi-Task Learning in Robotics. Learning a single model for many different tasks has been of particular interest to the robotics community recently. A large volume of work achieves the multi-task generalization by using a generalizable task or action representation such as object point cloud [18, 19], semantic segmentation and optical flow [1], and object-centric representation [29, 30]. However, the limited expressiveness of such representations constrains them to only generalize within a task category. Task parameterization [31, 32] and discrete task-dependent output layer [33, 34] approaches are investigated with reinforcement learning to learn policies for tasks in different categories. With the recent breakthrough in large language models, multi-task robot learning has been approached by using natural language to specify a broad range of tasks and learning the policy from large pre-collected datasets [35, 26, 36, 2, 37, 38, 39, 40, 41]. We are inspired by this success but propose to learn language-conditioned multi-task policies with a small demonstration dataset.

用于物体操作的 Transformer。Transformer 在视觉和 NLP 中的成功已将其引入机器人学习[42, 43, 44, 17]。特别是在物体操作中，具有注意力机制的基于 Transformer 的模型可用于从感官输入中提取特征，以改进策略学习[45, 46, 47, 48, 49]。与大多数先前工作不同，我们不使用大型数据集进行训练。RVT 从少量演示中高效学习，处理多个视图作为视觉输入，并融合来自语言目标的信息以处理多个操作任务。

Transformers for Object Manipulation. The success of transformers in vision and NLP has led its way into robot learning [42, 43, 44, 17]. Especially in object manipulation, transformer-based models with an attention mechanism can be utilized to extract features from sensory inputs to improve policy learning [45, 46, 47, 48, 49]. Unlike most prior work, we do not use large datasets for training. RVT efficiently learns from a small set of demonstrations, handle multiple views as visual inputs, and fuses information from language goals to tackle multiple manipulation tasks.

计算机视觉中的多视图网络。多视图表示已在各种视觉问题中得到探索。对于点云识别，SimpleView[50]展示了简单的基于视图的方法如何优于复杂的基于点的方法。后续工作如 MVTN[51]和 Voint cloud[52]进一步改进了 SimpleView 的架构。多视图表示也已用于其他问题，如 3D 视觉定位[53]和视图合成[54]。与它们不同，我们专注于预测机器人动作以进行物体操作的问题。

Multi-View Networks in Computer Vision. Multi-view representations have been explored in various vision problems. For point cloud recognition, SimpleView [50] showed how a simple view-based method outperforms sophisticated point-based methods. Follow-up works like MVTN [51] and Voint cloud [52] have further improved upon SimpleView’s architecture. Multi-view representations have also been used for other problems like 3D visual grounding [53] and view synthesis [54]. Unlike them, we focus on the problem of predicting robot actions for object manipulation.

3 方法 3 Method

我们的目标是学习一个能够完成各种操作任务的单一模型。输入包括：（1）任务的语言描述，（2）当前视觉状态（来自 RGB-D 相机），以及（3）当前夹爪状态（打开或关闭）。模型应预测一个动作，由下一个关键帧的目标末端执行器姿态和夹爪状态指定。关键帧表示任务执行期间夹爪的重要或瓶颈步骤[55]，例如预抓取、抓取或放置姿态。给定目标末端执行器姿态，我们假设存在一个低级运动规划器和控制器，可以将末端执行器移动到目标姿态。为了训练模型，我们假设给定一个数据集𝒟={D1,D2,⋯,Dn}，包含覆盖各种任务的 n 个专家演示。每个演示 Di=({o1…mii},{a1…mii},li)是一个长度为 mi 的成功轨迹，其中 li 是任务的语言描述，{o1i,o2i,…,omii}是来自 RGB-D 相机和夹爪状态的观测序列，{a1i,a2i,…,amii}是相应的机器人动作序列。该演示数据集可用于通过行为克隆训练模型。

Our goal is to learn a single model that can complete a wide range of manipulation tasks. The input consists of (1) a language description of the task, (2) the current visual state (from RGB-D camera(s)), and (3) the current gripper state (open or closed). The model should predict an action, specified by a target end-effector pose and gripper state at the next key-frame. The key-frames represent important or bottleneck steps of the gripper during the task execution [55], such as a pre-pick, grasp, or place pose. Given a target end effector pose, we assume a low-level motion planner and controller that can move the end effector to the target pose. 我们提出的方法（RVT）是一个 Transformer 模型[27]，它处理在机器人工作空间周围重新渲染的图像，为每个视图生成输出，然后反投影到 3D 以预测夹爪姿态动作，如图 2 所示。

Our proposed method (RVT) is a transformer model [27] that processes images re-rendered around the robot workspace, produces an output for each view, and then back-projects into 3D to predict gripper pose actions, as shown in Fig. 2.

渲染。第一步是相机输入的重新渲染。给定由一个或多个传感器相机捕获的 RGB-D 图像，我们首先重建场景的点云。然后从一组锚定在机器人基座中心空间的虚拟视点重新渲染点云（见图 2 和图 3）。具体来说，对于每个视图，我们渲染三个图像映射，共 7 个通道：（1）RGB（3 通道），（2）深度（1 通道），以及（3）世界坐标系中点的(x,y,z)坐标（3 通道）。(x,y,z)坐标有助于建立跨视图像素的对应关系，即如果来自不同视图的像素共享相同的(x,y,z)，则它们对应于 3D 中的同一点。我们使用 PyTorch3D[56]进行渲染。我们实证验证了渲染流程中的各种设计选择（见表 2（左））。

Rendering. The first step is the re-rendering of camera input. Given the RGB-D image(s) captured by one or multiple sensor cameras, we first reconstruct a point cloud of the scene. The point cloud is then re-rendered from a set of virtual viewpoints anchored in the space centered at the robot’s base (see Fig. 2 and Fig. 3). Specifically, for each view, we render three image maps with a total of 7 channels: (1) RGB (3 channels), (2) depth (1 channel), and (3) (x,y,z)𝑥𝑦𝑧(x,y,z) coordinates of the points in the world frame (3 channels). The (x,y,z)𝑥𝑦𝑧(x,y,z) coordinates help establish the correspondence of pixels across views, i.e., if pixels from different views share the same (x,y,z)𝑥𝑦𝑧(x,y,z), they correspond to the same point in 3D. We use PyTorch3D [56] for rendering. We empirically verify various design choices in our rendering pipeline (see Tab. 2 (left)).

重新渲染过程将输入图像与输入到 Transformer 的图像解耦。这提供了若干好处，例如：能够在任意且有用的位置（例如，桌子正上方）重新渲染，而不受现实世界相机放置的限制；即使只有一个传感器相机也能进行多视图推理；允许使用正交图像而不是通常提供的透视图像；便于 3D 点云增强，并启用传感器图像中原本不存在的额外通道，如点对应。我们通过实验发现，这些因素有助于基于视图的网络实现高性能（见第 4.1 节）。

The re-rendering process decouples the input images to the ones fed to the transformer. This offers several benefits such as: the ability to re-render at arbitrary and useful locations (e.g., directly above the table) while not being constrained by real-world camera placements; multi-view reasoning even with a single sensor camera; allowing the use of orthographic images instead of generally provided perspective ones; facilitating 3D point-cloud augmentations and enabling additional channels like point correspondence which are not natively presented in the sensor images. We empirically find that these contribute to achieving high performance with view-based networks (see Sec. 4.1).

联合 Transformer。重新渲染的图像、任务的语言描述和夹爪状态（打开或关闭）由联合 Transformer 模型处理（见附录图 5）。对于语言，我们使用预训练的 CLIP[57]嵌入（ResNet-50 变体），为每个单词提供一个 token。对于虚拟图像，我们将每幅图像分割成 20×20 的图像块，并通过多层感知器（MLP）生成图像 token，类似于 ViT[58]。对于夹爪状态，类似于 PerAct[6]，我们通过 MLP 处理并将其连接到图像 token。我们还为所有图像和语言 token 添加位置嵌入，以保留位置信息。

Joint Transformer. The re-rendered images, the language description of the task, and the gripper state (open or close) are processed by a joint transformer model (see Fig. 5 in the appendix). For language, we use pretrained CLIP [57] embeddings (ResNet-50 variant), which provide one token for each word. For the virtual images, we break each of them into 20×20202020\times 20 patches and pass through a multi-layer perceptron (MLP) to produce image tokens, similar to ViT [58]. For the gripper state, similar to PerAct [6], we pass it through an MLP and concatenate it to the image tokens. We also add positional embeddings to all the image and language tokens to preserve the positional information.

总体而言，RVT 有八个自注意力层。在前四层中，图像 token 只能关注来自同一图像的其他 token。这促使网络在跨图像共享信息之前先处理单个图像。之后，我们将所有图像 token 与语言 token 连接起来。在最后四层中，我们允许注意力层在不同图像和文本之间传播和累积信息。最后，图像 token 被重新排列回原始空间配置，从而得到每个图像的特征通道。

Overall, RVT has eight self-attention layers. In the first four layers, an image token is only allowed to attend to other tokens from the same image. This biases the network to process individual images first before sharing information across images. We concatenate all the image tokens along with the language tokens afterward. In the last four layers, we allow the attention layers to propagate and accumulate information across different images and text. Finally, the image tokens are rearranged back to the original spatial configuration, resulting in the feature channels of each image.

动作预测。模型输出一个 8 维动作，包括 6 自由度目标末端执行器位姿（3 自由度平移和 3 自由度旋转）、1 自由度夹爪状态（打开或关闭），以及一个二进制指示器，用于指示低级运动规划器是否允许碰撞（详见[6]）。对于平移，我们首先根据联合 Transformer 的每图像特征（如附录图 5 所示）为每个视图预测一个热力图。然后，不同视图的热力图被反投影，以预测密集覆盖机器人工作空间的离散化 3D 点的分数。最后，末端执行器平移由分数最高的 3D 点确定。注意，这种用于平移预测的多视图热力图表示扩展了先前在 2D 俯视图设置中的方法[4]。因此，RVT 通过将视觉输入和动作表示在相同的空间结构中，继承了优越的样本效率优势[4]。

Action Prediction. The model outputs an 8-dimensional action, including the 6-DoF target end effector pose (3-DoF for translation and 3-DoF for rotation), 1-DoF gripper state (open or close), and a binary indicator for whether to allow collision for the low-level motion planner (see [6] for details). For translation, we first predict a heatmap for each view from the per-image features from the joint transformer (as shown in Fig. 5 in the appendix). The heatmaps across different views are then back-projected to predict scores for a discretized set of 3D points that densely cover the robot workspace. Finally, the end effector translation is determined by the 3D point with the highest score. Note that this multi-view heatmap representation for translation prediction extends prior approaches in the 2D top-down view setting [4]. Hence, RVT inherits the benefit of superior sample efficiency by representing the visual input and action in the same spatial structure [4].

对于末端执行器旋转，我们遵循 PerAct 使用欧拉角表示，其中每个角度离散化为 5°分辨率的区间。夹爪状态和运动规划器碰撞指示器表示为二进制变量。为了预测旋转、夹爪状态和碰撞指示器，我们使用全局特征（𝒢）。全局特征由以下两部分拼接而成：（1）沿空间维度的图像特征之和，由预测的平移热力图加权；（2）沿空间维度的最大池化图像特征。具体来说，令 f_i 为第 i 张图像的图像特征，h_i 为预测的平移热力图。则全局特征𝒢由𝒢 = [φ(f_1 ⊙ h_1); ...; φ(f_K ⊙ h_K); ψ(f_1); ...; ψ(f_K)]给出，其中 K 是图像数量，⊙表示逐元素乘法，φ和ψ分别表示在高度和宽度维度上的求和与最大池化。加权求和操作为靠近预测末端执行器位置的图像位置提供更高的权重。

For end effector rotation, we follow PerAct to use the Euler angles representation, where each angle is discretized into bins of 5∘superscript55^{\circ} resolution. The gripper state and the motion planner collision indicator are represented as binary variables. The weighted sum operation provides higher weights to image locations near the predicted end effector position.

损失函数。我们使用混合损失训练 RVT。对于热力图，我们对每张图像使用交叉熵损失。真实值由围绕真实 3D 位置 2D 投影的截断高斯分布获得。对于旋转，我们对每个欧拉角使用交叉熵损失。我们对夹爪状态和碰撞指示器使用二分类损失。

Loss Function. We train RVT using a mixture of losses. For heatmaps, we use the cross-entropy loss for each image. The ground truth is obtained by a truncated Gaussian distribution around the 2D projection of the ground-truth 3D location. For rotation, we use the cross-entropy loss for each of the Euler angles. We use binary classification loss for the gripper state and collision indicator.

Note that the view correspondence channel is not present in sensor images but is rendered along with RGB(D) images in RVT. (c) Adding the depth channel along with RGB channels helps, likely because it aids 3D reasoning.

（d）在合并所有图像 token 之前，独立处理单个图像的 token 有帮助。这可能是因为这种设计期望网络在联合推理之前为每个图像提取有意义的特征。

(d) Independently processing the tokens from a single image, before merging all the image tokens, helps. It is likely because this design expects the network to extract meaningful features for each image before reasoning over them jointly.

（e）对于立方体和真实摄像头位置，使用正交投影渲染图像比使用透视投影效果更好。我们假设这是因为正交投影保留了物体的形状和大小，无论其与摄像头的距离如何（见图 3（e-f））。这也突出了重新渲染的优势，因为真实传感器通常使用透视投影渲染。

(e) Rendering images with orthographic projection performs better than rendering with perspective projection, for both the cube and real camera locations. We hypothesize that it is because orthographic projection preserves the shape and size of an object regardless of its distance from the camera (see Fig. 3 (e-f)). It also highlights the advantage of re-rendering, as real sensors generally render with perspective projections.

（f）正如预期，在渲染之前对点云使用 3D 旋转增强有帮助。为了利用 3D 增强，重新渲染过程是必要的。

(f) As expected, using 3D rotation augmentation in the point cloud before rendering helps. To take advantage of 3D augmentations, the re-rendering process is necessary.

（g）具有 5 个视图围绕立方体（图 3（a））的模型表现最佳，其次是具有 3 个视图（前、顶、左）围绕立方体（图 3（b））的模型。单视图模型（如 TransporterNet [4]那样将第三坐标预测为偏移量）表现明显更差，这表明 3D 操作需要多个视图。这也突出了重新渲染的优势，因为通过重新渲染，即使只有一个传感器摄像头，我们也可以利用多个视图。我们还经验性地发现，将摄像头位置相对于桌子（和机器人）旋转 15°（见图 3）会降低性能。这可能是因为与桌子和机器人对齐的视图更容易推理（例如，俯视图、对齐的前视图）。

(g) The model with 555 views around a cube (Fig. 3 (a)) performs the best followed by the one with 333 views (front, top, left) around a cube (Fig. 3 (b)). The single view model, where we predict the third coordinate as an offset like TransporterNet [4], performs substantially worse, calling for the need for multiple views for 3D manipulation. It also highlights the advantage of re-rendering as with re-rendering we can leverage multiple views even with a single sensor camera. We also empirically find that rotating the location of the cameras by 15∘superscript1515^{\circ} (see Fig. 3) with respect to the table (and robot) decreases performance. This could be likely because views aligned with the table and robot might be easier to reason with (e.g., overhead top view, aligned front view).

（h）与使用传感器摄像头图像相比，RVT 使用重新渲染的图像表现更好（表 2（左），倒数第二行）。传感器摄像头图像使用透视投影（物理渲染过程）渲染，并且在不重新渲染的情况下不容易应用 3D 增强（例如旋转）。此外，传感器摄像头的位置可能对于 3D 推理不是最优的，例如，视图不与桌子或机器人轴向对齐（见图 3（d））。所有这些因素都导致 RVT 使用重新渲染的图像比使用传感器摄像头图像表现更好。

(h) RVT performs better with re-rendered images as compared to using sensor camera images (Tab. 2 (left), second last row). The sensor camera images are rendered with perspective projection (physical rendering process) and are not straightforward to apply 3D augmentations (e.g., rotation) without re-rendering. Also, the location of sensor cameras may be sub-optimal for 3D reasoning, e.g., the views are not axially aligned with the table or robot (see Fig. 3 (d)). All these factors contribute to RVT performing better with re-rendered images than with sensor camera images.

值得注意的是，人们可能会考虑重新排列传感器摄像头以匹配重新渲染的视图，从而绕过重新渲染。然而，这将失去使用正交投影、3D 增强和添加对应关系带来的收益。这也严格需要多摄像头设置（图 3（a）），这在现实世界中比使用一个传感器摄像头更昂贵且便携性更差。最后，我们简要探索了视图选择，并找到了一个在任务中表现良好的选项。视图的进一步优化，包括传感器和重新渲染的视图，是一个有趣的未来方向。

Notably, one might consider rearranging the sensor cameras to match the re-rendering views in order to bypass re-rendering. However, this will void the gains from using orthographic projections, 3D augmentation, and adding correspondences. This also strictly requires a multi-camera setup (Fig. 3 (a)), which is more costly and less portable in the real world than using one sensor camera. Finally, we have briefly explored view selection and found an option that works well across tasks. Further optimization of views, including the sensor and re-rendered ones, is an interesting future direction.

4.2 真实世界 4.2 Real-World

我们通过在真实世界设置上训练和测试模型，研究了 RVT 在真实视觉感官数据上的性能。有关设置和模型性能的更多详细信息，请参见随附的视频 111 视频提供于 https://robotic-view-transformer.github.io/。

We study the performance of RVT on real visual sensory data by training and testing the model on a real-world setup. See the attached videos111Videos are provided at https://robotic-view-transformer.github.io/. for more details about the setup and model performance.

真实世界设置。我们在一个桌面设置上使用静态安装的 Franka Panda 机械臂进行实验。场景通过静态安装在第三人称视角的 Azure Kinect（RGB-D）相机感知。在将感知到的点云传入 RVT 之前，我们校准了机器人-相机外参，并将其转换到机器人基坐标系。给定 RVT 的目标夹爪位姿，我们使用 FrankaPy [63]通过轨迹生成和反馈控制将机器人移动到目标位置。

Real World Setup. We experiment on a table-top setup using a statically mounted Franka Panda arm. The scene is perceived via an Azure Kinect (RGB-D) camera statically mounted in a third-person view. We calibrate the robot-camera extrinsics and transform the perceived point clouds to the robot base frame before passing into RVT. Given a target gripper pose from RVT, we use FrankaPy [63] to move the robot to the target with trajectory generation and feedback control.

任务。我们采用了总共 5 个与 PerAct [6]类似的任务（见表 2（右））：堆叠方块、按压洗手液、将马克笔放入杯子/碗中、将物体放入抽屉、将物体放入架子。每个任务可以通过语言描述定义的不同变体来实例化。例如，对于堆叠方块，一些变体可能是“将黄色方块放在蓝色方块上”和“将蓝色方块放在红色方块上”。给定一个任务和变体，我们通过将任务相关物体和一组干扰物体以随机配置放置在桌子上来采样场景。

Tasks. We adopt a total of 5 tasks similar to the ones in PerAct [6] (see Tab. 2 (right)): stack blocks, press sanitizer, put marker in mug/bowl, put object in drawer, put object in shelf. Each task can be instantiated with different variations defined by the language description. For example, for stack blocks, some variations could be “put yellow block on blue block” and “put blue block on red block”. Given a task and variation, we sample a scene by placing the task-related objects and a set of distractor objects on the table in a random configuration.

数据收集。我们首先通过人类演示收集用于训练 RVT 的数据集。给定一个采样的任务和场景配置，我们要求人类演示者通过动觉移动机械臂来指定一系列夹爪目标位姿。一旦我们有了目标位姿序列，我们将机器人重置到起始位姿，然后控制它按照指定顺序依次移动到每个目标位姿。在机器人向目标移动的过程中，我们同时记录来自相机的 RGB-D 流。这为我们提供了与目标位姿注释配对的 RGB-D 帧数据集。总共，我们在所有 5 个任务上收集了 51 个演示序列。

Data Collection. We first collect a dataset for training RVT through human demonstration. Given a sampled task and scene configuration, we ask the human demonstrator to specify a sequence of gripper target poses by kinesthetically moving the robot arm around. Once we have the target pose sequence, we reset the robot to the start pose, and then control it to sequentially move to each target pose following the specified order. We simultaneously record the RGB-D stream from the camera during the robot’s motion to the targets. This provides us with a dataset of RGB-D frames paired with target pose annotations. In total, we collected 51 demonstration sequences over all 5 tasks.

结果。我们在真实世界数据上训练了 10K 步，使用与模拟数据相同的优化器、批量大小和学习率调度。结果报告在表 2（右）中。总体而言，RVT 在堆叠方块任务（100%）和按压洗手液任务（80%）上取得了高成功率。即使在更长视野的任务中，例如将物体放入抽屉和架子（例如，机器人必须首先打开抽屉/架子，然后拿起物体），我们的模型也达到了 50%的成功率（见图 4）。我们发现 RVT 在与马克笔相关的任务上表现不佳，这可能是由于点云稀疏且有噪声。我们进一步将结果分为两组：“+ markers”（完整集）和“- markers”。我们的模型在非马克笔任务上总体达到了 82.5%的成功率。马克笔问题可能可以通过将相机安装在夹爪上以捕获更高质量的点云来解决。另一种可能性是使用类似于 C2F-ARM [5]的放大视图。

Results. We train on real-world data for 10K steps, with the same optimizer, batch size, and learning rate schedule as the simulation data. We report the results in Tab. 2 (right). Overall, RVT achieves high success rates for the stack block task (100%) and the press sanitizer task (80%). Even on longer horizon tasks such as putting objects in drawers and shelves (e.g., the robot has to first open the drawer/shelf and then pick up the object), our model achieves 50% success rates (see Fig. 4). We found RVT struggled with marker-related tasks, which is likely due to sparse and noisily sensed point clouds. We further divide the results into two sets: “+ markers” (full set) and “- markers”. Our model overall achieves an 82.5% success rate on non-marker tasks. The marker issue can potentially be addressed by attaching the camera to the gripper to capture point clouds at higher quality. Another possibility is to use zoom-in views similar to C2F-ARM [5].

5 结论与局限 5 Conclusions and Limitations

我们提出了 RVT，一种用于 3D 物体操作的多视图 Transformer 模型。我们发现 RVT 在各种 3D 操作任务上优于先前的最先进模型，如 PerAct 和 C2F-ARM，同时更具可扩展性和更快。我们还发现 RVT 仅需少量演示即可在真实世界操作任务中工作。

We proposed RVT, a multi-view transformer model for 3D object manipulation. We found that RVT outperforms prior state-of-the-art models like PerAct and C2F-ARM on a variety of 3D manipulation tasks, while being more scalable and faster. We also found that RVT can work on real-world manipulation tasks with only a few demonstrations.

尽管我们发现 RVT 取得了最先进的结果，但我们识别出一些局限性，这些局限性为未来的研究提供了令人兴奋的方向。我们简要探索了各种视图选项，并找到了一个在任务中表现良好的选项，但如果视图规范可以从数据中优化或学习，那将令人兴奋。此外，与先前的基于视图的方法相比，RVT（以及像 PerAct 和 C2F-ARM 这样的显式体素方法）需要校准从相机到机器人基座的外参。探索消除这一约束的扩展将令人兴奋。

Although we found RVT to achieve state-of-the-art results, we identify some limitations that present exciting directions for future research. We briefly explore various view options and found an option that works well across tasks, but it would be exciting if view specification can be optimized or learned from data. Further, when compared to prior view-based methods, RVT (as well as explicit voxel-based methods like PerAct and C2F-ARM), requires the calibration of extrinsics from the camera to the robot base. It would be exciting to explore extensions that remove this constraint.
