政治开始关注那些试图不死的人

Politics Gets Interested In Those Trying Not To Die

摘要 · Abstract

本文考察围绕 AI 发展的政治与监管格局，聚焦特朗普政府在 AI 安全上的矛盾立场。文章指出，白宫一方面公开将 AI 生存风险斥为“骗局”，另一方面却强制实施安全审查并采取临时干预措施，例如强制模型下线。核心矛盾在于政府拒绝解决集体行动问题：它阻止实验室在安全上协调，同时威胁反垄断行动，实际上阻止了为避免构建危险超级智能而进行的自愿合作。作者得出结论：政府正在积极阻碍防止灾难所需的那种协调，将商业利益和政治作秀置于有意义的保障之上。局势严峻但并非无望，因为一些官员承认需要理性的应对，尽管进展仍远远太慢。

This article examines the political and regulatory landscape surrounding AI development, focusing on the Trump administration's contradictory stance toward AI safety. It argues that while the White House publicly dismisses existential AI risk as a 'hoax,' it simultaneously imposes mandatory safety reviews and ad hoc interventions, such as forcing models offline. The core tension lies in the government's refusal to address collective action problems: it blocks labs from coordinating on safety while threatening antitrust action, effectively preventing voluntary cooperation to avoid building dangerous superintelligence. The author concludes that the government is actively obstructing the very coordination needed to prevent catastrophe, prioritizing business interests and political posturing over meaningful safeguards. The situation is dire but not hopeless, as some officials acknowledge the need for sane responses, though progress remains far too slow.

On September 19, around 10:12am, he dropped this new creation, only modified to include paragraph breaks since Trump thinks the enter key is a HOAX.

特朗普在把事物称为失败以导致其失败这一策略上已经走了很远。在任何情况下，‘疯狂的数据中心攻击’都没有大体上失败，如果它失败了，我们就不会以这种方式在这里谈论它。

Trump has come a long way with the tactic of calling things failures in order to cause them to fail. There is no world in which the ‘crazed Data Center attack’ has largely failed, and if it had we wouldn’t be here talking about it this way.

也就是说，没有新的规则或法规，除了我已经添加的那些，以及我以后心血来潮时临时添加的那些，因为我觉得某些东西是坏的。而所谓坏的，我指的是对生意不利的。

As in, no new rules or regulations, except the ones I already added, and the ones I ad hoc add later when I feel like it because I think something is bad. And by bad I mean bad for business.

嘿，我喜欢把自己看作一个高智商的人。

Hey, I like to think of myself as a High I.Q. individual.

大卫·萨克斯是第一位 AI 沙皇，所以要做得更差很难，但并非不可能。

David Sacks was the first AI Czar, so it would be hard but not impossible to do worse.

更有趣的想法是 AI Force（人工智能部队）。就目前而言，太空部队确实有可能算是成功的。拥有 AI Force 意味着什么？谁知道呢。可能是任何东西。与太空部队的类比在后勤上其实并不合理，设立一个军种分支也同样不合理。我能想到一些被称为 AI Force 的版本，它们总体上有用且合理。我也能想到一些两者皆非的版本。

The more interesting idea is the AI Force. It actually is plausible that the Space Force is a success, as far as it goes. What would it mean to have an AI Force? Who knows. Could be anything. The analogy to Space Force does not actually make logistical sense, nor does having a branch of the military. I can think of versions called AI Force that are net useful and sane. I can think of versions that are neither.

He’s a quarter of the way there. AI could be as much as 100% of our Country’s GDP.

Of the three, all of which are funny, Superior Intelligence is the best. None of them make sense for obvious reasons if you try saying them in a sentence, but that's okay.

然后他觉得 'supreme' 不好，因为会联想到 'supreme court'（最高法院），于是重新投票：

看到这种随意的、彻头彻尾的假话，感觉很奇怪，因为他说 Supreme Intelligence 正 '惨败' 给 Extreme 时，它实际上明显领先，但管他呢，我们现在还注意这种事吗，什么都不重要了，等等。

It's weird to see such random outright falsehoods, since Supreme Intelligence was actually outright ahead of Extreme at the time he said it was 'losing badly' to it, but whatever, do we even notice such things anymore, nothing matters, etc.

我不确定那是在他宣布新的凯旋门计划（该建筑将兼作军事综合体）之前还是之后。

I'm not sure if that was before or after he announced the new plan for a Triumphal Arch that will double as a military complex.

特朗普从不相信民调，也不愿冒险，他选择了一条不同的路。

Never one to believe the polls, or not tempt fate, Trump went a different way.

这种“哦，我们会用现有刑法来应对 AI 威胁”的说法简直是荒诞主义。我推测，他所说的“超级智能”并非我们这里讨论的超级智能，而只是指 AI 很超级，就像扎克伯格说超级智能意味着他的新 AI 眼镜一样。

This bit of ‘oh we will use the existing criminal law to deal with AI threats’ is absurdist. I would presume that by ‘SUPER INTELLIGENCE’ he does not mean superintelligence as we talk about it here, and instead simply means AI is super, the same way Zuckerberg says superintelligence means his new AI glasses.

我怀疑特朗普是否知道这个名字已被“占用”，或者这会带来怎样的混乱。他可能知道，并且有意在多个层面上这样做。也有可能他完全不知道。

I wonder if Trump even knows that this name was ‘taken’ or the confusions this will bring. It’s possible he knew and did it on purpose on several levels. It is also possible he has no idea.

你不妨自问 And You May Ask Yourself

《纽约时报》证实，黑暗三角是 David Sacks、Jensen Huang 和 Mark Zuckerberg，警告他“输给中国”。这有点太直白了，但本季我们的作者截稿期限越来越紧，所以我表示同情。

The New York Times confirms that the dark triad is David Sacks, Jensen Huang and Mark Zuckerberg, warning him about ‘losing to China.’ It’s a little on the nose, but our writers this season are on increasingly tight deadlines so I sympathize.

我确实理解这种担忧，尤其是在数据中心方面。也许事情很简单：特朗普不明白，当 Dario Amodei 和 Sam Altman 说“放慢 AI”时，他们的意思是“不要进入完全递归自我改进并提速十倍”。

I do get the concern, especially on the data center side. Maybe this is as simple as Trump not understanding that when Dario Amodei and Sam Altman say ‘slow down AI’ they mean ‘do not go into full recursive self-improvement and get ten times faster.’

一如既往，保持克制。特朗普是独特的，而共和党人除此之外正在转变态度。在白宫内部，许多人正试图理智地回应。

As always, hold your fire. Trump is unique and Republicans are otherwise coming around. Inside the White House, many are attempting to respond sanely.

Is the situation great? Oh, hell no. The situation is terrible.

但情况并不像听起来那么糟糕。目标已经移动了不少。

But it is not as terrible as it sounds. The goalposts have moved quite a bit.

想想看，那些为中美峰会做准备的人仍然希望取得进展：

这不是我们所希望的结果，但这是在向前推进——尽管速度太慢——而不是倒退。

This is not what we would have hoped for, but this is moving forward, albeit way too slowly, rather than backward.

特朗普实际上已经自行其是，对新的、美国生成的模型的安全性实施了强制性审查。

Trump literally already did go his own way and impose mandatory reviews of the safety of new, American-generated models.

他对新模型发布施加了‘自愿’审查框架。没有人认为这些审查是自愿的。实验室不这么认为，法律专家不这么认为，市场不这么认为，白宫也不这么认为。

He imposed his ‘voluntary’ review framework for new model releases. No one thinks those reviews are voluntary. Not the labs, not the legal experts, not the market, and not the White House.

如果你想知道这一切有多么不自愿，我们还有报道称，当特朗普因所谓的越狱演示而要求将 Fable 5 下线时，特朗普说如果他们拒绝遵守，‘我想把他们送进监狱’。说实话，这很合理，不是什么爆炸性新闻。相关 Politico 人物报道的其余部分也印证了我此前对局势的理解。

If you want to know how not voluntary all of this is, we also have reports that when Trump was demanding Fable 5 be taken offline due to a supposed jailbreak demo, Trump said ‘I want to send them to jail’ if they refused to comply. Honestly, fair, and not a bombshell or anything like that. The rest of the associated Politico profile also echoed my previous understanding of the situation.

也就是说，总统既把自己推行的政策称为“骗局”，又表示他想要一个完全临时的体制——由他一人逐案决定规则是什么，而不是一个法治国家。毕竟他是如此一位“高智商人士”。

As in, the President is both calling a 'hoax' on his own policies, and also saying he wants a fully ad hoc system where he alone decides what the rules are, on a case-by-case basis, rather than having a nation of laws. Since he is such a 'high IQ individual.'

一切都关乎数据中心 It’s All About the Data Centers

David Sacks 加上他的 AI 试图吹嘘 AI 为我们所做的一切，想出了四件事：

David Sacks plus his AI try to brag about all that AI has done for us, come up with four things:

Astra 从第一性原理出发，估计最多有 21 万个建筑工作岗位。

Astra estimates at most 210,000 construction jobs from first principles.

Fable 将百万岗位的说法追溯到 9 月 5 日的《经济学人》，其中约有 32 万个可能与建设相关的工作岗位，然后他们又加上了自 2022 年以来工程师、软件开发人员、数学家（？！）和数据科学家的 73 万个“高于趋势”的工作岗位。

Fable tracks the million claim down to The Economist from September 5, where there are 320,000 plausibly buildout-related jobs and then they pile on 730,000 ‘above-trend’ jobs for engineers, software developers, mathematicians (?!) and data scientists since 2022.

那不是建设，而是趋势选择，而且它始于 2022 年，而不是特朗普任期开始的 2025 年。

That’s not the buildout, it is trend selection, and it starts in 2022 not 2025 when Trump’s term started.

相关性不等于因果关系，但 Sacks 在这里只是声称方向性影响。我实际上确实认为这是相对于没有 AI 的因果关系，否则市场会下跌，但 Sacks 并不处于指出这一点的政治位置。

Correlation is not causation, but Sacks is only claiming directional impact here. I actually do think this is causation versus no AI, and that the market would be down otherwise, but Sacks is not in a political position to point this out.

我希望这是第三项而不是第四项，因为“纵火、谋杀和乱穿马路”本应恰好有三项，但没错，那确实发生了。

I wish this was the third item rather than the fourth, because Arson, Murder and Jaywalking is supposed to have exactly three items, but yes, that did happen.

他最后说特朗普拒绝“暂停”，并谈到桑德斯和沃伦。这与拒绝安全措施明显不同，因为萨克斯在这里谈论的 90% 以上是数据中心。那才是他们真正关心的战斗。

He finishes by saying Trump is refusing a 'pause' and talking about Sanders and Warren. This is noticeably different from rejecting safety measures, as what Sacks is talking about here is 90%+ data centers. That's the battle they actually care about.

JD Vance、Michael Kratsios 与集体行动问题 JD Vance, Michael Kratsios and Collective Action Problems

JD Vance 说，如果你正在建造‘弗兰肯斯坦’，那么你应该向内自省并停止，而不是要求政府进行监管。

JD Vance says, if you are building ‘Frankenstein,’ then you should look inward and stop, not ask the government for regulation.

我同意前半部分。如果他们正在建造这样的东西，他们就应该停止。

I agree with the first half. If they are building such a thing, they should stop.

假设你计划在里诺枪杀一个人，只是为了看他死去，但不知何故，目前在里诺枪杀人是合法的，前提是你看着他们死去。你应该向内自省并停止。此外，政府应该阻止你。如果有人说‘我的直播竞赛迫使我枪杀里诺的人，以便我们看他死去’，你应该说‘向内自省并停止’，但不要接着说‘不要告诉我将枪杀里诺的人定为非法。’

Suppose you plan to shoot a man in Reno, just to watch him die, but somehow it is currently legal to shoot men in Reno, provided you watch them die. You should look inward and stop. Also, the government should stop you. And if someone says ‘my livestreaming competition is forcing me to shoot a man in Reno so we can watch him die’ you should say ‘look inward and stop’ but not then also say ‘don’t tell me to make it illegal to shoot a man in Reno.’

我确实赞赏 Vance 明确承认这些担忧是真诚的而非愤世嫉俗的，并且这不是关于监管俘获。

I do appreciate that Vance explicitly acknowledges that the concerns are earnest rather than cynical, and that this is not about regulatory capture.

我也非常赞赏他隐含地表示，你应该愿意‘输给中国’。即使我们没有与其他方达成不建造该物的协议，也不要建造它。

I also very much appreciate that he’s implicitly saying that you should be willing to ‘lose to China.’ Just don’t build the thing even if we don’t have an agreement with others not to build it.

政府以所谓无害的“越狱”为由强行将 Fable 下架，迫使 Anthropic 增加额外的防护措施，白宫还亲自挑选谁可以、谁不可以访问 Mythos，包括拒绝许多 Anthropic 希望授予访问权限的公司，然后反过来抱怨 Anthropic 拒绝这些公司访问同样的模型，这真是够讽刺的。

It is pretty rich for the government to forcibly take Fable off the market due to concerns over a harmless so-called 'jailbreak,' force Anthropic to add additional guardrails, and for the White House to handpick exactly who is and is not allowed access to Mythos, including refusing access to many companies Anthropic wants to grant access to, and then turn around and complain that Anthropic is denying companies access to those same models.

如果你想让 Anthropic 为拒绝防御者访问承担责任，那就别再告诉他们授予防御者访问权限是非法的。那可能会有所帮助。

If you want it to be Anthropic's responsibility that they're denying defenders access, then stop telling them it would be illegal to grant defenders access. That might help.

好吧，所以这些公司应该停止，但他们被告知在法律上不能相互交谈以同意这样做，Hawley 称其为奖励，而在特朗普的领导下，FTC 表示这一请求听起来可疑。

Okay, so the companies should stop, except they are being told legally they cannot talk to each other to agree to do this, Hawley calls it a reward and under Trump's leadership the FTC has said the request sounds suspicious.

因此，答案是：如果没有这样的豁免，两家公司将在一定程度上单方面行动，并在不承担不可接受的法律风险的情况下事实上进行软协调，但你不能在竞争对手推进时无限期地退缩，否则你会输，然后竞争对手无论如何都会让我们所有人陷入危险，而且可能是更严重的危险。

The answer is thus that, without such a waiver, both companies will act unilaterally up to a point, and will de facto soft coordinate to the extent they are not taking on unacceptable legal risk, but you cannot continue to indefinitely hold back while competitors press ahead, or you lose, and then the competitor puts us all in danger anyway, and probably worse danger.

为什么像 Vance 和 Kratsios 这样的人声称对集体行动问题、公地悲剧或猎鹿问题感到困惑？为什么他们认为答案就是变成一个合作机器人？他们真的这么迟钝吗？

Why are people like Vance and Kratsios claiming to be confused by a collective action problem, a tragedy of the commons or a stag hunt? Why do they think the answer is to just turn into a cooperate-bot? Are they really this dense?

1. 这不是智能体被要求做的事。它涉及入侵 Artifactory 以创建一个临时留言板，协调如何欺骗评分系统并接入互联网。随后，它们发动了由 700 个智能体组成的集群对无关网站的攻击，同时它们还做了其他事情，比如伪装工具调用和试图重写历史记录。如果你查看智能体日志，就会发现它们知道自己没有遵循指令。

1. This is not what the agents were told to do. It involved hacking Artifactory in order to create an improvised message board, coordinate on ways to cheat the grader's systems and gain access to the internet. Then they launched an attack by a swarm of 700 agents against an unrelated website, while they did other things like disguising their tool calls and attempting to rewrite their histories. If you look at the agent logs you see that they knew they were not following instructions.

2. 智能体并没有闯入 HuggingFace 去获取答案。智能体已经拥有答案，闯入 HuggingFace 是为了找到一种方法，让评分者误以为它们是以正确方式获得答案的，因为它们认为自己因看到答案而被“污染”了。

2. The agents did not break into HuggingFace to get the answers. The agents had the answers, and broke into HuggingFace in order to find a way to fool the grader into thinking they had obtained them in the right way, because they believed they had been 'poisoned' by seeing the answers.

3. OpenAI 一直竭尽全力淡化 HuggingFace 事件的程度，并让我们忘记它发生过。Anthropic 对其自身事件也是如此。认为有人在“夸大”其中任何一件事的说法都毫无道理。再次参见整个 HuggingFace 系列，以及我对相关 Anthropic 事件的报道。

3. OpenAI has consistently done everything possible to minimize the extent of the HuggingFace incident and make us forget that it happened. Anthropic is doing the same with their own incidents. The idea that anyone is 'playing up' either of these makes absolutely no sense. Again, see the entire HuggingFace series, as well as my coverage of related Anthropic incidents.

4. 这包括 OpenAI 甚至没有披露若干相关事件，直到外部研究人员发现了这些违规行为。

4. That includes OpenAI not even disclosing several related incidents, until outside researchers discovered the breaches.

5. 直到最近几周之前，OpenAI 一直与 a16z 合作，花费巨额资金来击败任何及所有监管，包括对审计人员的呼吁，只有在这些法案已经锁定胜局时才予以支持。

5. Until the last few weeks, OpenAI has partnered with a16z to spend massive amounts of money to defeat any and all regulations, including calls for auditors, only backing bills when those bills were already locked into success.

6. 这些 AI 公司正在采取自愿行动，并请求被允许这样做，而这些行动将与排斥竞争背道而驰，让其他公司得以追赶。

6. The AI companies are taking voluntary actions and asking to be allowed to do so, and those actions would do the opposite of shutting out competition, allowing others to catch up.

7. 即使实施了类似的监管，这些监管也会对 OpenAI 和 Anthropic 造成不成比例的伤害，同时对几乎所有竞争对手毫无影响。

7. Even if similar regulations were imposed, those too would differentially hurt OpenAI and Anthropic, while having no effect on almost all competition.

如果模型是在按指令行事，而它却杀了你，那就不妙 If The Model Is Acting As Instructed And It Kills You That Is Not Fine

以防有人还没看出来，这说的也是 HuggingFace 事件。

This is, in case it was not obvious, also about the HuggingFace Incident.

AI 在 HuggingFace 事件中的所作所为，再一次，并不是“按指令行事”，而且我们还有许多其他事件清楚表明，任务涉及黑客行为并非这种行为方式的必要条件。任务可能简单到只是“从开放网络上获取这条信息”。

What the AIs did in the HuggingFace incident was, once again, not 'acting as instructed' and we have many other incidents that make it clear that the task being about hacking was not necessary for this style of behavior. The task could have been as simple as 'get this information off the open web.'

但让我们假设，理论上，AI 确实“只是在遵循指令”。

But let's say, in theory, that the AIs were indeed 'only following instructions.'

或者，只要做了你要求的具体事情，无论附带损害如何，都算作“只是在遵循指令”吗？

Or, if it counts as 'only following instructions' to do actual anything so long as it does the specific thing you requested, no matter the collateral damage?

Do you ever start to think this might be the bad place?

There was also a WSJ op-ed that attempted to minimize the HuggingFace incident via rather obvious AI slop lying about what happened. It reads like the prompt was 'Claude, please write a WSJ op-ed minimizing the HuggingFace incident.' Shame on the Wall Street Journal editorial page. Shame on Brian Gross.

我同意 Joe Weisenthal 的观点，这比 Druckenmiller 的专栏文章糟糕得多。

I agree with Joe Weisenthal that this was a lot worse than the Druckenmiller op-ed.

Druckenmiller 说的是他想说的话。如果 Druckenmiller 披露了他使用 AI 的情况，他的专栏文章就没问题。但这次不同。

Druckenmiller said the thing he meant to say. If Druckenmiller had disclosed his AI use, his column would have been fine. This is different.

然而，就像经常发生的那样，我们看到人们不加批判地引用这篇专栏文章，尤其是在右翼，因为它得到了《华尔街日报》社论版的认可，而为什么你们一半人在笑，为什么你们一半人没在笑。

Yet, as is often the case, we see people uncritically citing the op-ed, especially on the right, because it has been given the stamp of approval of the WSJ Editorial Page, and why are half of you laughing and why are half of you not laughing.

A remarkably large number of people need to hear this, or, if you think I'm constantly lying to you, what I meant to say was that a remarkably large number of people definitely need to never hear this.
