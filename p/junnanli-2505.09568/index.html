摘要 · Abstract

统一图像理解与生成在近期多模态模型研究中日益受到关注。尽管图像理解的设计选择已被广泛研究，但针对包含图像生成的统一框架，其最优模型架构和训练方案仍待探索。受自回归模型和扩散模型在高质量生成与可扩展性方面强大潜力的启发，我们对其在统一多模态设置中的应用进行了全面研究，重点关注图像表示、建模目标和训练策略。基于这些研究，我们提出了一种新方法，采用扩散 Transformer 生成语义丰富的 CLIP 图像特征，而非传统的基于 VAE 的表示。该设计同时提高了训练效率和生成质量。此外，我们证明，统一模型的顺序预训练策略——先训练图像理解，再训练图像生成——通过保持图像理解能力同时发展强大的图像生成能力，提供了实际优势。最后，我们精心整理了一个高质量指令微调数据集 BLIP3o-60k，用于图像生成，通过使用涵盖各种场景、物体、人类手势等的多样化描述提示 GPT-4o 生成。基于创新的模型设计、训练方案和数据集，我们开发了 BLIP3-o，一套最先进的统一多模态模型。BLIP3-o 在大多数流行的图像理解和生成任务基准上均取得了优越性能。为促进未来研究，我们完全开源了模型，包括代码、模型权重、训练脚本以及预训练和指令微调数据集。

Unifying image understanding and generation has gained growing attention in recent research on multimodal models. Although design choices for image understanding have been extensively studied, the optimal model architecture and training recipe for a unified framework with image generation remain underexplored. Motivated by the strong potential of autoregressive and diffusion models for high-quality generation and scalability, we conduct a comprehensive study of their use in unified multimodal settings, with emphasis on image representations, modeling objectives, and training strategies. Grounded in these investigations, we introduce a novel approach that employs a diffusion transformer to generate semantically rich CLIP image features, in contrast to conventional VAE-based representations. This design yields both higher training efficiency and improved generative quality. Furthermore, we demonstrate that a sequential pretraining strategy for unified models-first training on image understanding and subsequently on image generation-offers practical advantages by preserving image understanding capability while developing strong image generation ability. Finally, we carefully curate a high-quality instruction-tuning dataset BLIP3o-60k for image generation by prompting GPT-4o with a diverse set of captions covering various scenes, objects, human gestures, and more. Building on our innovative model design, training recipe, and datasets, we develop BLIP3-o, a suite of state-of-the-art unified multimodal models. BLIP3-o achieves superior performance across most of the popular benchmarks spanning both image understanding and generation tasks. To facilitate future research, we fully open-source our models, including code, model weights, training scripts, and pretraining and instruction tuning datasets.

2.1 动机 2.1 Motivation

统一多模态架构的发展，即同时支持图像理解和生成，已成为近期研究中的一个有前景的方向。诸如 Janus chen2025janus 、Show-o xie2024show 、MetaMorph tong2024metamorph 、Janus-Pro chen2025janus 和 LMFusion shi2024llamafusion 等模型代表了在单一框架内桥接图像理解和生成的早期努力。最近，OpenAI 的 GPT-4o gpt4o 通过展示在高质量图像生成和强大多模态理解方面的令人印象深刻的能力，进一步激发了人们对这一范式的兴趣。尽管兴趣日益增长，但实现这种统一能力的基本设计原则和训练策略仍探索不足。本工作旨在系统性地研究和推进统一模型的发展，我们首先清晰阐述构建统一多模态模型的关键动机。

同时支持图像理解和生成的统一模型自然促进了上下文学习能力。在此类模型中，先前生成的多模态输出可以作为后续生成的上下文，从而无缝支持迭代图像编辑、视觉对话和逐步视觉推理。这消除了模式切换或依赖外部处理流程的需要，使模型能够保持连贯性和任务连续性。

Unified models that jointly support image understanding and generation naturally facilitate in-context learning capabilities. In such models, previously generated multimodal outputs can serve as context for subsequent generation, enabling seamless support for iterative image editing, visual dialogue, and step-by-step visual reasoning. This eliminates the need for mode switching or reliance on external processing pipelines, allowing the model to maintain coherence and task continuity.

随着人工智能向通用人工智能（AGI）迈进，未来的系统需要超越基于文本的能力，无缝感知、解释和生成多模态内容。实现这一目标需要从纯文本架构转向能够跨多种模态进行推理和生成的统一多模态架构。此类模型对于构建能够以整体、类人方式与世界互动的通用智能至关重要。

As artificial intelligence advances toward artificial general intelligence (AGI), future systems need to go beyond text-based capabilities to seamlessly perceive, interpret, and generate multimodal content. Achieving this requires a shift from text-only architectures to unified multimodal architectures that can reason and generate across diverse modalities. Such models are essential for building general-purpose intelligence capable of engaging with the world in a holistic, human-like manner.

3.1 图像编码与重建 3.1 Image Encoding and Reconstruction

图像生成通常首先使用编码器将图像编码为连续潜在嵌入，然后使用解码器从该潜在嵌入重建图像。这种编码-解码流程可以有效降低图像生成中输入空间的维度，促进高效训练。下面，我们讨论两种广泛使用的编码器-解码器范式。

Image generation typically begins by encoding an image into a continuous latent embedding using an encoder, followed by a decoder that reconstructs the image from this latent embedding. This encoding-decoding pipeline can effectively reduce the dimensionality of the input space in image generation, facilitating efficient training. In the following, we discuss two widely used encoder–decoder paradigms.

变分自编码器（VAE）kingma2013auto ; rezende2014stochastic 是一类生成模型，学习将图像编码到结构化的连续潜在空间。编码器近似给定输入图像下潜在变量的后验分布，而解码器从该潜在分布中抽取的样本重建图像。潜在扩散模型在此基础上构建，通过学习建模压缩潜在表示的分布，而非原始图像像素。通过在 VAE 潜在空间中操作，这些模型显著降低了输出空间的维度，从而降低了计算成本并实现了更高效的训练。在去噪步骤之后，VAE 解码器将生成的潜在嵌入映射为原始图像像素。

Variational Autoencoders (VAEs) kingma2013auto ; rezende2014stochastic are a class of generative models that learn to encode images into a structured, continuous latent space. The encoder approximates the posterior distribution over the latent variables given the input image, while the decoder reconstructs the image from samples drawn from this latent distribution. Latent diffusion models build on this framework by learning to model the distribution of compressed latent representations, rather than raw image pixels. By operating in the VAE latent space, these models significantly reduce the dimensionality of the output space, thereby lowering computational costs and enabling more efficient training. After the denoising steps, the VAE decoder maps the generated latent embeddings into raw image pixels.

CLIP radford2021learning 模型已成为图像理解任务的基础编码器 liu2023visual ，这得益于其通过在大规模图像-文本对上进行对比训练，提取丰富、高级语义特征的强大能力。然而，利用这些特征进行图像生成仍然是一个不小的挑战，因为 CLIP 最初并非为重建任务设计。Emu2 sun2024generative 通过将基于 CLIP 的编码器与基于扩散的解码器配对，提出了一种实用解决方案。具体而言，它使用 EVA-CLIP 将图像编码为连续视觉嵌入，并通过从 SDXL-base podell2023sdxl 初始化的扩散模型重建它们。在训练期间，扩散解码器被微调，以使用来自 EVA-CLIP 的视觉嵌入作为条件，从高斯噪声中恢复原始图像，而 EVA-CLIP 保持冻结。这个过程有效地将 CLIP 和扩散模型组合成一个图像自编码器：CLIP 编码器将图像压缩为语义丰富的潜在嵌入，基于扩散的解码器从这些嵌入重建图像。值得注意的是，尽管解码器基于扩散架构，但它使用重建损失而非概率采样目标进行训练。因此，在推理期间，模型执行确定性重建。

CLIP radford2021learning models have become foundational encoders for image understanding tasks liu2023visual , owing to its strong ability to extract rich, high-level semantic features from images through contrastive training on large-scale image–text pairs. However, leveraging these features for image generation remains a non-trivial challenge, as CLIP was not originally designed for reconstruction tasks. Emu2 sun2024generative presents a practical solution by pairing a CLIP-based encoder with a diffusion-based decoder. Specifically, it uses EVA-CLIP to encode images into continuous visual embeddings and reconstructs them via a diffusion model initialized from SDXL-base podell2023sdxl . During training, the diffusion decoder is fine-tuned to use the visual embeddings from EVA-CLIP as conditions to recover the original image from Gaussian noise, while the EVA-CLIP remains frozen. This process effectively combines the CLIP and diffusion models into an image autoencoder: the CLIP encoder compresses an image into semantically rich latent embeddings, and the diffusion-based decoder reconstructs the image from these embeddings. Notably, although the decoder is based on diffusion architecture, it is trained with a reconstruction loss rather than probabilistic sampling objectives. Consequently, during inference, the model performs deterministic reconstruction.

这两种编码器-解码器架构，即 VAE 和 CLIP-扩散，代表了图像编码和重建的不同范式，各自具有特定的优势和权衡。VAE 将图像编码为低级像素特征，并提供更好的重建质量。此外，VAE 作为现成模型广泛可用，可以直接集成到图像生成训练流程中。相比之下，CLIP-扩散需要额外训练以适应不同的 CLIP 编码器。然而，CLIP-扩散架构在图像压缩比方面提供了显著优势。例如，在 Emu2 sun2024generative 和我们的实验中，无论图像分辨率如何，每张图像都可以编码为固定长度的 64 个连续向量，提供既紧凑又语义丰富的潜在嵌入。相比之下，基于 VAE 的编码器倾向于为更高分辨率的输入产生更长的潜在嵌入序列，这增加了训练过程中的计算负担。

These two encoder–decoder architectures, i.e., VAEs and CLIP-Diffusion, represent distinct paradigms for image encoding and reconstruction, each offering specific advantages and trade-offs. VAEs encode the image into low-level pixel features and offer better reconstruction quality. Furthermore, VAEs are widely available as off-the-shelf models and can be integrated directly into image generation training pipelines. In contrast, CLIP-Diffusion requires additional training to adapt the diffusion models to various CLIP encoders. However, CLIP-Diffusion architectures offer significant benefits in terms of image compression ratio. For example, in both Emu2 sun2024generative and our experiments, each image regardless of its resolution can be encoded into a fixed length of 64 continuous vectors, providing both compact and semantically rich latent embeddings. By contrast, VAE-based encoders tend to produce a longer sequence of latent embeddings for higher-resolution inputs, which increases the computational burden in the training procedure.

在获得连续图像嵌入后，我们继续使用自回归架构对其进行建模。给定用户提示（例如，“一位面带雀斑、戴着草帽的年轻女性。”），我们首先使用自回归模型的输入嵌入层将提示编码为嵌入向量序列 C，并在 C 后附加一个可学习的查询向量 Q，其中 Q 是随机初始化并在训练中优化的。当组合序列[C; Q]通过自回归 Transformer 处理时，Q 学习关注并从提示 C 中提取相关语义信息。得到的 Q 被解释为自回归模型生成的中间视觉特征或潜在表示，并被训练以逼近真实图像特征 X（从 VAE 或 CLIP 获得）。下面，我们介绍两种训练目标：均方误差（MSE）和流匹配，用于学习将 Q 与真实图像嵌入 X 对齐。

Unlike discrete tokens, which inherently support sampling-based strategies for exploring diverse generation paths, continuous representations lack this property. Specifically, under an MSE-based training objective, the predicted visual features 𝐐\mathbf{Q} becomes nearly deterministic for a given prompt. This determinism highlights a key limitation of the MSE objective: it constrains the model to produce a single, fixed output for each prompt, thereby limiting generation diversity.

相比之下，流匹配框架使模型能够继承扩散过程的随机性。这使得模型能够在相同提示条件下生成多样化的图像样本，促进输出空间的更广泛探索。然而，这种灵活性以增加模型复杂度为代价。与 MSE 相比，流匹配引入了额外的可学习参数。在我们的实现中，我们使用扩散 Transformer（DiT），并经验性地发现扩展其容量可带来显著的性能提升。

In contrast, the flow matching framework enables the model to inherit the stochasticity of the diffusion process. This allows the model to generate diverse image samples conditioned on the same prompt, facilitating broader exploration of the output space. However, this flexibility comes at the cost of increased model complexity. Flow matching introduces additional learnable parameters compared to MSE. In our implementation, we use a diffusion transformer (DiT), and empirically find that scaling its capacity yields significant performance improvements.

In the second stage, we freeze the MLLM backbone, and train only the image generation module like LMFusion shi2024llamafusion and MetaQuery pan2025transfer .

在联合训练设置中，尽管如图 Metamorph tong2024metamorph 所示，图像理解和生成任务可能相互促进，但有两个关键因素影响其协同效应：(i) 总数据量，以及(ii) 图像理解与生成数据之间的数据比例。相比之下，顺序训练提供了更大的灵活性：它允许我们冻结自回归主干并保持图像理解能力，将所有训练能力专用于图像生成，避免了联合训练中的任务间干扰。同样受 LMFusion shi2024llamafusion 和 MetaQuery pan2025transfer 启发，我们将选择顺序训练来构建统一的模态模型，并将联合训练留待未来工作。

In joint training setting, although image understanding and generation tasks possibly benefit each other as demonstrated by Metamorph tong2024metamorph , two critical factors influence their synergistic effect: (i) the total data size and (ii) the data ratio between image understanding and generation data. In contrast, sequential training offers greater flexibility: It lets us freeze the autoregressive backbone and maintain the image understanding capability. We can dedicate all training capacity to image generation, avoiding any inter-task effects in joint training. Also motivated by LMFusion shi2024llamafusion and MetaQuery pan2025transfer , we will choose sequential training to construct our unified multimodal model and defer joint training to future work.

5.1 模型架构 5.1 Model Architecture

我们开发了两种不同规模的模型：基于专有数据训练的 8B 参数模型和仅使用开源数据的 4B 参数模型。鉴于存在强大的开源图像理解模型，如 Qwen 2.5 VL bai2025qwen2，我们跳过了图像理解训练阶段，直接在 Qwen 2.5 VL 上构建图像生成模块。在 8B 模型中，我们冻结 Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct 主干，训练扩散 Transformer，总计 1.4B 可训练参数。4B 模型采用相同的图像生成架构，但使用 Qwen2.5-VL-3B-Instruct 作为主干。

We develop two different size models: 8B parameter model trained on proprietary data and 4B parameter model using only open source data. Given the existence of strong open source image understanding models, such as Qwen 2.5 VL bai2025qwen2 , we skip image understanding training stage and build our image generation module directly on Qwen 2.5 VL. In the 8B model, we freeze the Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct backbone and train the diffusion transformers, totaling 1.4 B trainable parameters. The 4B model follows the same image generation architecture but uses Qwen2.5-VL-3B-Instruct as backbone.

我们利用 Lumina-Next 模型 zhuo2024lumina 的架构作为我们的 DiT。Lumina-Next 模型基于改进的 Next-DiT 架构构建，这是一种可扩展且高效的扩散 Transformer，专为文生图和通用多模态生成设计。它引入了 3D 旋转位置编码，以编码时间、高度和宽度上的时空结构，而不依赖可学习的位置标记。每个 Transformer 块采用三明治归一化（注意力/MLP 前后的 RMSNorm）和分组查询注意力，以增强稳定性并减少计算量。基于实证结果，该架构实现了快速、高质量的生成。

We leverage the architecture of the Lumina-Next model zhuo2024lumina for our DiT. The Lumina-Next model is built on the improved Next-DiT architecture, a scalable and efficient diffusion transformer designed for text-to-image and general multimodal generation. It introduces 3D Rotary Position Embedding to encode spatial-temporal structure across time, height, and width without relying on learnable position tokens. Each transformer block employs sandwich normalization (RMSNorm before and after attention/MLP) and Grouped-Query Attention to enhance stability and reduce computation. Based on empirical results, this architecture achieves fast, high-quality generation.

5.2 训练方案 5.2 Training Recipe

对于 8B 模型，我们结合了约 2500 万开源数据（CC12M changpinyo2021conceptual、SA-1B kirillov2023segment 和 JourneyDB JourneyDB）以及额外的 3000 万专有图像。所有图像标题均由 Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct 生成，产生平均长度为 120 个标记的详细描述。为了提高对不同提示长度的泛化能力，我们还加入了约 10%（600 万）来自 CC12M changpinyo2021conceptual 的较短标题（约 20 个标记）。每个图像-标题对使用提示“请根据以下标题生成图像：<标题>”格式化。对于完全开源的 4B 模型，我们使用了 2500 万张公开可用的图像，来自 CC12M changpinyo2021conceptual、SA-1B kirillov2023segment 和 JourneyDB JourneyDB，每张图像配以相同的详细标题。我们还混合了约 10%（300 万）来自 CC12M changpinyo2021conceptual 的短标题。为了支持研究社区，我们发布了 2500 万详细标题和 300 万短标题。

For 8B model, we combine around 25 million open-source data (CC12M changpinyo2021conceptual , SA-1B kirillov2023segment , and JourneyDB JourneyDB ) with an additional 30 million proprietary images. All image captions are generated by Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct, yielding detailed descriptions with an average length of 120 tokens. To improve generalization to varying prompt lengths, we also include around 10% (6 million) shorter captions with around 20 tokens from CC12M changpinyo2021conceptual . Each image–caption pair is formatted with the prompt: “Please generate an image based on the following caption: &lt;caption&gt;“. For the fully open-source 4B model, we use 25 million publicly available images, from CC12M changpinyo2021conceptual , SA-1B kirillov2023segment , and JourneyDB JourneyDB , each paired with the same detailed captions. We also mix in around 10% (3 million) short captions sourced from CC12M changpinyo2021conceptual . To support the research community, we release 25 million detailed captions and 3 million short captions.

7 相关工作 7 Related Work

最近的研究强调了统一多模态（能够同时进行图像理解和生成）是一个有前景的研究方向。例如，SEED-X ge2024seed、Emu-2 sun2024generative 和 MetaMorph tong2024metamorph 通过回归损失训练图像特征，而 Chameleon team2024chameleon、Show-o xie2024show、EMU3 wang2024emu3 和 Janus wu2024janus; chen2025janus 采用自回归离散词元预测范式。同时，DreamLLM dong2023dreamllm 和 Transfusion zhou2024transfusion 利用扩散目标进行视觉生成。据我们所知，我们首次对自回归和扩散框架中的设计选择进行了系统研究。

Recent studies have highlighted unified multimodal, capable of both image understanding and generation, as a promising avenue of research. For example, SEED-X ge2024seed , Emu-2 sun2024generative , and MetaMorph tong2024metamorph train image features via regression losses, while Chameleon team2024chameleon , Show-o xie2024show , EMU3 wang2024emu3 , and Janus wu2024janus ; chen2025janus adopt an autoregressive discrete token prediction paradigm. In parallel, DreamLLM dong2023dreamllm and Transfusion zhou2024transfusion leverage diffusion objectives for visual generation. To our knowledge, we present the first systematic study of design choice in the autoregressive and diffusion framework.

关于统一模型训练策略，LMFusion shi2024llamafusion 基于冻结的 MLLM 骨干网络，同时使用 Transfusion zhou2024transfusion 方法引入 Transformer 模块进行图像生成。我们的方法与 LMFusion 的一个关键相似之处在于，两种方法都冻结了 MLLM 骨干网络，仅训练图像特定组件。然而，LMFusion 引入了用于图像扩散的并行 Transformer 模块，显著扩大了模型规模。相比之下，我们的方法引入了一个相对轻量级的扩散头来实现图像生成，保持了更易于管理的整体模型规模。同时期的工作 MetaQuery pan2025transfer 也使用可学习查询来桥接冻结的预训练 MLLM 与预训练扩散模型，但其扩散模型采用 VAE + Flow Matching 策略，而不是我们 BLIP3-o 中更高效的 CLIP + Flow Matching 策略。

Regrading to the unifed model training strategy, LMFusion shi2024llamafusion builds on the frozen MLLM backbone while incorporating transformer modules for image generation using Transfusion zhou2024transfusion . A key similarity between our approach and LMFusion is that both methods freeze the MLLM backbone and train only the image-specific components. However, LMFusion incorporates parallel transformer modules for image diffusion, significantly expanding the model size. In contrast, our method introduces a relatively lightweight diffusion head to enable image generation, maintaining a more manageable overall model size. Concurrent work MetaQuery pan2025transfer also uses learnable queries to bridge frozen pre-trained MLLMs with pre-trained diffusion models, but the diffusion models are in VAE + Flow Matching strategy instead of the more efficient CLIP + Flow Matching one in our BLIP3-o.

8 结论 8 Conclusion

总之，我们首次系统探索了用于统一多模态建模的混合自回归和扩散架构，评估了三个关键方面：图像表示（CLIP 与 VAE 特征）、训练目标（Flow Matching 与 MSE）和训练策略（联合与顺序）。我们的实验表明，CLIP 嵌入结合 Flow Matching 损失既能实现更快的训练效率，又能产生更高质量的输出。基于这些见解，我们推出了 BLIP3-o，这是一系列最先进的统一模型，并通过 60k 指令微调数据集 BLIP3o-60k 进行了增强，显著改善了提示对齐和视觉美学。我们正在积极研究统一模型的应用，包括迭代图像编辑、视觉对话和逐步视觉推理。

In summary, we have presented the first systematic exploration of hybrid autoregressive and diffusion architectures for unified multimodal modeling, evaluating three critical aspects: image representation (CLIP vs. VAE features), training objective (Flow Matching vs. MSE), and training strategy (joint vs. sequential). Our experiments demonstrate that CLIP embeddings paired with a flow matching loss deliver both faster training efficiency and higher quality outputs. Building on these insights, we introduce BLIP3-o, a family of state-of-the-art unified models enhanced with a 60k instruction tuning dataset BLIP3o-60k that substantially improves prompt alignment and visual aesthetics. We are actively working on applications for the unified model, including iterative image editing, visual dialogue, and step-by-step visual reasoning.
