核心贡献 · Key contributions

  • 反对利润最大化；开源模型并将API定价设为10个月回本以确保低价。
    Rejects profit maximization; open-sources models and sets API pricing at 10-month cost recovery to maintain low prices.
  • 将团队稳定视为唯一核心利益；通过AGI愿景而非金钱激励吸引人才。
    Identifies team stability as the only core interest; attracts talent via AGI vision rather than monetary incentives.
  • 提出清晰的AGI路线图：思维链→智能体→持续学习→自我迭代奇点→具身智能。
    Proposes a clear AGI roadmap: chain-of-thought → agent → continual learning → self-iterating singularity → embodied intelligence.
  • 主张在适度利润下开源不影响商业，甚至最强模型也会开源。
    Claims that open source does not harm business when profit margin is modest; even the strongest models will be open-sourced.
  • 开发TileLang编译器以脱离NVIDIA CUDA生态，使国产GPU适配仅存硬件差距。
    Develops TileLang compiler to decouple from NVIDIA CUDA ecosystem, enabling domestic GPU adaptation with only hardware gap.
  • 专注于核心智能研究（预训练、后训练），而非视频生成、世界模型等外围任务。
    Focuses on core intelligence research (pre-training, post-training) over peripheral tasks like video generation and world models.

局限 · Limitations

  • 算力资源短缺：仅约2万张H级GPU，无法像美国前沿实验室那样训练800B参数模型。
    Compute resource shortage: only ~20k H-equivalent GPUs, unable to train 800B-parameter models like frontier US labs.
  • 国产芯片（华为）相比NVIDIA落后2年且效率为4:1；产能不足。
    Domestic chips (Huawei) have 2-year lag and 4:1 efficiency ratio vs. NVIDIA; production capacity insufficient for scale.
  • 共识驱动决策可能拖慢转向，且无法解决内部分歧。
    Consensus-driven decision-making may slow down pivots and fail to resolve internal disagreements.
  • AGI愿景可能过于乐观；认为世界模型与智能上限无关的说法存疑。
    Vision of AGI may be overly optimistic; the claim that world models are irrelevant to intelligence upper bound is debatable.
  • 依赖NVIDIA硬件采购及地缘政治风险可能中断供应链。
    Reliance on NVIDIA hardware purchase and geopolitical risks could disrupt supply chain.
