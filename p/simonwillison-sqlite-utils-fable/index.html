A weird thing about coding agents is that harder tasks like this one actually provide _more_ opportunity to do other things at the same time, since the agent sometimes needs 10-15 minutes to churn away on a new task. I went out to enjoy the Half Moon Bay 4th of July parade, occasionally checking in and prompting the next step for Fable from my phone.

过去，我坚持手动编写发布说明，但老实说，这些说明比我亲自编写的更好。发布说明是一个很好的例子，说明我乐意将写作外包给智能体，因为它们需要枯燥、可预测且准确。
