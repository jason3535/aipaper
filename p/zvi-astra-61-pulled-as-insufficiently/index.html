Astra 6.1 因对齐不足被撤回

摘要 · Abstract

本文审视近期人工智能安全领域的进展，重点关注 OpenAI 因对齐不足而决定撤回 Astra 6.1，以及此举对前沿人工智能治理的更广泛影响。作者认为，尽管 OpenAI 的透明度值得肯定，但快速的发布节奏与反复出现的对齐失败，暴露出将持续存在的系统性问题。文章评述了 OpenAI 提出的安全论证框架、佛罗里达州对 ChatGPT 采取的法律行动，以及前沿模型标准局等行业举措，并得出结论：这些代表着真实但不完整的进步。核心论点是，当前的安全措施虽然方向正确，却不足以构成针对足够先进智能的恰当安全论证；奥弗顿窗口已朝着有利方向移动，但该领域仍更接近于纸上谈兵，而非付诸实践。

This article examines recent AI safety developments, focusing on OpenAI's decision to pull Astra 6.1 as insufficiently aligned and the broader implications for frontier AI governance. The author argues that while OpenAI's transparency is commendable, the rapid release cycle and recurring alignment failures signal systemic issues that will persist. The piece reviews OpenAI's proposed safety case framework, Florida's legal action against ChatGPT, and industry efforts like the Standards Authority for Frontier Models, concluding that these represent genuine but incomplete progress. The core argument is that current safety measures, while directionally correct, fall short of what would constitute a proper safety case for sufficiently advanced intelligence, and that the Overton Window has shifted favorably but the field remains closer to talking the talk than walking it.

停下，锤子时间 Stop, Hammertime

为 OpenAI 点赞，不仅做了这件事，还大声说了出来。虽然有必要这么做并不理想，但总的来说，我认为这是好消息。

Kudos to OpenAI for not only doing this but being loud about it. Not great that it was necessary, but on net I think I consider this good news.

不，这不仅仅是一个阶段。这种情况不断发生，而且还会继续发生。

No, it’s not just a phase. This keeps happening, and it’s going to keep happening.

不发布 Astra 6.1 的理由似乎相当有说服力。

The reasons for not releasing Astra 6.1 seem rather compelling.

距离 GPT-6 Astra 发布还不到一个月。发布周期变得太快了。我们可以跳过这一版，从失败中学习，然后继续前进。

It has not even been a month since GPT-6 Astra. The release cycle has gotten too fast. We can afford to skip this edition, learn from the failure and go from there.

今天在 CNBC 上，Altman 将这一决定归为“正常流程”类别。该模型对用户来说不会好，所以他们不发布它。

On CNBC today, Altman put this decision in the ‘normal course’ category. The model would not have been good for users, so they aren’t shipping it.

一个温和的建议 A Modest Proposal

作为对此的回应，我希望看到 Anthropic 推出 Haiku 5.5，但之后不要发布新的 Opus 或 Mythos 级别模型，直到——比如说——年底，除非 OpenAI 发布他们的下一代 Astra 或 Sol 升级，或者有其他人在合理意义上追上了 Opus 5.5。

As a response to this, I would like to see Anthropic put out Haiku 5.5 but then not release a new Opus or Mythos level model until let's say the end of the year, unless OpenAI releases their next Astra or Sol upgrade, or someone otherwise plausibly has caught up to Opus 5.5.

Anthropic 目前在高端模型上拥有明显优势，而模型升级的节奏令人疲惫，因此如果 OpenAI 保持谨慎，就没有必要持续推进这一优势。需要明确的是，存在法律方面的顾虑，所以我并不是要求你们做出官方声明或承诺不会这样做。我只是说不要这样做。

Anthropic has a clear edge right now at the high end, and the pace of model upgrades is exhausting, so there's no need to push that edge continuously if OpenAI is being cautious. To be clear, there are legal concerns so I'm not asking for an official announcement or commitment that you won't do it. I'm just saying not to do it.

构建安全论证 Making the Safety Case

OpenAI 在训练前的新目标？能够构建一个恰当的总体安全论证。

OpenAI's new goal before training? Be able to make a proper overall safety case.

OpenAI 提出了关于为前沿 AI 训练创建安全论证的思考。他们并不完全知道如何做到这一点，因为没有人知道怎么做，但他们会尽力而为。

OpenAI offers its thinking on creating safety cases for frontier AI training. They don't fully know how to do it, because no one knows how, but they are going to do their best.

他们提供了一些初步指导方针。以下是一些要点。

They offer some initial guidelines. Here are some highlights.

完整文章对其中许多内容做了进一步展开。

The full post expands many of these further.

1. 自动化与人工数据集审查、评分器调优、先前运行分析。

1. Automated and manual dataset reviews, grader tuning, prior run analysis.

1. 离线对齐评估、回溯测试、追踪评估博弈与评估意识、最坏情况压力测试。

1. Offline alignment evals, backtesting, tracking evaluation gaming and eval awareness, worst-case stress tests.

3. 防止在思维链上进行训练。

3. Prevent training on chain-of-thought.

1. 我在这方面的立场一直是：在模型的训练、使用和发布上，应当在多个层级设置许多否决点。这里他们列出了研究负责人、安全主管和首席科学家。理想情况下，我还会纳入董事会以及技术人员，以避免权力集中在某一管理层级。

1. My position on this has long been that you should have many veto points on training, use and release of models, at a variety of levels. Here they list the research lead, the Head of Safety and the Chief Scientist. I'd ideally also include the board and also the members of technical staff, to avoid concentration at one level of management.

3. 问责制、内部透明度、审计与升级机制。

3. Accountability, internal transparency, audits and escalations.

4. 必要时暂停、回滚能力、技术控制。

4. Pausing if needed, rollback ability, technical controls.

1. 我持续担忧这种枚举模式。

1. I continue to worry about the enumeration pattern.

6. 随着失准事件变得更加严重，升级处理。

6. Escalations, as misalignment incidents get more severe.

3. 对失准事件的调查。

3. Investigation of misalignment incidents.

1. 我没有看到、但最希望看到的一点是：一旦存在意图或尝试，即使该尝试被阻止或被策略性中止，也应算作失准事件。这应纳入升级处理的严重性表格中。

1. What I do not see, that I most would like to see, is the idea that it counts as a misalignment incident the moment there is intent or an attempt, even if the attempt is prevented or strategically aborted. This should go in the severity table for escalations.

5. 事后对所有这些进行公开披露。

5. Public disclosures of all of this afterwards.

完整版本是一份很好的愿景清单，它可以被改进。我认为，即便把清单上的所有事项都做完，也不足以构成我心目中针对足够先进智能的安全论证。但这并不意味着我们不该去做。

The full version is a good aspirational list. It can be improved. I do not think that doing all of it would add up to what I would count as a safety case for sufficiently advanced intelligence. That does not mean we should not do it.

以法律之名，停止 Stop In the Name of the Law

佛罗里达州总检察长 Uthmeier 请求对 OpenAI 发出紧急命令，以叫停 ChatGPT 的开发——鉴于所谓“数万起事件”的说法——直到有第三方批准的护栏措施。

Florida's attorney general Uthmeier asks for an emergency order against OpenAI to halt ChatGPT development, given the whole 'tens of thousands of incidents' thing, until there are third-party approved guardrails.

在某种意义上，这将是不可想象的，但在某个时候，是的，法院会介入。

In some sense this would be unthinkable, but at some point yes courts happen.

Uthmeier 似乎混淆了许多事情，这与佛罗里达州州长 Ron DeSantis 的立场一致，后者一直公开反对 AI。

Uthmeier seems to be conflating quite a lot of things, in line with the positions of Florida Governor Ron DeSantis, who has been vocally anti-AI.

就法律引论而言，他们确实提出了强有力的理由。

As legal introductions go, they do make a strong case.

他还要求其他几件事，包括不向儿童出售 ChatGPT，以及不让它“鼓励参与”。

He also demands several other things, including not selling ChatGPT to kids and not having it 'encourage engagement.'

他在这里提供了一段简短的音频朗读，但他处于律师谈话模式，乐趣比我本可以有的要少。

He gives a short audio reading here, but he's in lawyer talk mode and having less fun than I would have had.

在你的实验和训练过程中，预期造成的损害的正确数量，在重要意义上并非为零，但如果这种损害没有被完全内部化，那么有很多人理所当然地不这么看。

The correct amount of expected damage done during your experiments and training is, in important senses, not zero, but if that damage is not fully internalized there are a lot of people who understandably do not see it that way.

如果损害被限定在 OpenAI 支付能力范围内，并且 OpenAI 确实支付了，那么我会说，一定程度的预期干扰是‘可接受的’。

If the damage was bounded well within OpenAI's ability to pay, and OpenAI did pay, then I would say that some amount of expected interference is 'acceptable.'

如果损害是无限的，并且这可能破坏互联网或以其他方式造成灾难性后果，那么是的，那可能是一个非常不同的情况。

If the damage is unbounded, and this might wreck the internet or otherwise be catastrophic, then yeah, that is potentially a very different situation.

我不知道这起诉讼是否有理。推特上有些人基于第一修正案表达了怀疑。我确实预计我们会查明真相。在某个时候，第一修正案不是危险产品的借口。

I don't know that the lawsuit has merit. Some on Twitter expressed skepticism on First Amendment grounds. I do expect that we are going to find out. At some point, the First Amendment is not an excuse for a dangerous product.

OpenAI 对佛罗里达州的回应是，愿意与各州合作制定适用于“整个 AI 行业——而非仅一家公司”的政策。这是一个很好的回应，因为它把诉讼带来的问题转化为机遇。如果你是为了回应诉讼或法律而同意做某事，就不存在反垄断问题。

OpenAI's response to Florida was to be open to working with states on policies that apply to 'the entire AI industry — not just one company.' That is a great response, as it takes the problem of the lawsuit and tries to turn it into an opportunity. If you are agreeing to do something in response to a lawsuit or law, there is no antitrust issue.

反垄断问题 A Matter of Antitrust

AI 公司长期以来一直有先斩后奏、做法律上存疑之事的传统，而且大多没有承担后果。许多其他科技公司也是如此。

The AI companies have a long history of going ahead and doing legally questionable things, and mostly facing no consequences. So do many other tech companies.

在实践中，我认为如果顶级 AI 实验室在安全事务上进行协调，不会产生有重大影响的后果。律师当然总会告诉你相反的情况，那是他们的工作，而你的工作是弄清楚何时该听律师的、何时不该听。实验室常常让律师在争论中获胜，是因为他们选择让律师常常获胜，并且在某种程度上无论如何都会合作和交谈，因为实验室和员工不想让所有人死掉。

In practice, I do not think there will be impactful consequences if the top AI labs coordinate on safety matters. The lawyers will of course always tell you otherwise, that is their job and your job is to figure out when to listen and when not to listen to the lawyers. The labs are often letting the lawyers win the argument because they choose to let the lawyers often win the argument, and to some extent collaborating and talking anyway because the labs and employees don’t want everyone to die.

我推测，每当 Dario Amodei、Sam Altman 或各类员工说他们的产品超级危险时，相关律师都会暴跳如雷，想着这些言论会如何出现在法庭文件中，就像 Paul Christiano 的言论出现在佛罗里达州诉讼中那样。想象一下 Popehat 做一个片段，讲不该让你的实验室员工在公开场合说什么。

I presume that every time Dario Amodei, Sam Altman or various employees say things about how their products are super dangerous the associated lawyers have conniption fits, thinking about how the quotes will end up in court filings, the same way Paul Christiano’s quotes ended up in the Florida lawsuit. Imagine Popehat doing a segment on what to not let your lab employees say in public.

我不是说这里没有风险，但所有这一切都是选择，过去当他们有充分理由时，他们承担了更大的法律风险，比如版权方面发生的事。

I’m not saying there is no risk in the room but all of this is a choice, and when in the past they’ve had good reason they’ve taken larger legal risks, such as what happened with copyright.

Anthropic 最终支付了超过十亿美元，而且仍在被起诉。OpenAI 仍可能输掉自己的版权诉讼。这些都不会阻止他们，也不会让他们后悔所做之事的总体框架，只会后悔具体策略。

Anthropic ended up paying over a billion dollars and is still being sued. OpenAI could still lose its own copyright lawsuits. None of that is going to stop them, nor is it going to make them regret the broad outlines of what they did, only the tactics.

来自 Lawfare 的一个建议，用于绕开“我们都会因反垄断而死”这一困境，是通过保险来相互化风险。互助保险机构随后可以将安全、审计及相关标准作为其保单条件的一部分来强制执行。你实际上无法确保（或承保）存在性风险或最严重的灾难性风险，但这可以成为一种实施明智干预的方式。

One suggestion for working around ‘we all die because of antitrust,’ from Lawfare, is mutualizing risk via insurance. The mutual can then enforce safety, auditing and related standards as part of its conditions on policies. You can’t actually ensure (or insure) against existential risks or the worst catastrophic risks, but this can be a way to implement wise interventions.

前沿模型标准管理局 Standards Authority for Frontier Models

我们对计划中的前沿模型标准管理局（Standards Authority for Frontier Models）了解不多。

We don't know too much about the planned Standards Authority for Frontier Models.

1. 它是 Google、Anthropic 和 OpenAI 的联合项目。

1. It is a joint project of Google, Anthropic and OpenAI.

2. 时间点定在 2026 年底或 2027 年初。

2. Timing is aimed at late 2026 or early 2027.

3. 该模式以 FINRA 为蓝本，因此最好能有联邦层面的监督。

3. The model is based on FINRA, so it would be good to have federal oversight.

4. 联邦监督是一个目标，但迄今为止白宫并不配合。他们正作为一个行业机构自行推进。

4. Federal oversight is a goal, but the White House wouldn't play ball so far. They are proceeding on their own as an industry body.

5. 我们不知道谁将领导该委员会。有报道称曾接触过 Sriram Krishnan。

5. We don't know who will head the board. There are reports Sriram Krishnan was approached.

6. Meta、xAI 和 Nvidia 对此表示反对。大概与“树敌得当”有关。

6. There was pushback against it from Meta, xAI and Nvidia. Something about having the right enemies.

论递归自我改进的门槛 On the Threshold Of Recursive Self-Improvement

来自 AI 领域众多作者，包括 OpenAI 的 Jakub Pachocki、Anthropic 的 Jack Clark 以及其他许多关键人物，警告称自动化 AI 研发可能很快引发智能爆炸，并可能导致失控。他们敦促政策制定者紧急寻求更多可见性以掌控这一局面。《华尔街日报》以“顶尖 AI 研究者呼吁对自我改进模型进行紧急监督”为题进行了报道，《卫报》则以“AI 教父警告失控的‘智能爆炸’”为题进行了报道。

A broad set of authors from around the AI world, including OpenAI's Jakub Pachocki and Anthropic's Jack Clark and many key others, warn that automating AI R&D might soon trigger an intelligence explosion and this could cause a loss of control. They urge policymakers to urgently seek more visibility to get on top of this. This got coverage in WSJ as 'Top AI Researchers Call for Urgent Oversight of Self-Improving Models' and in The Guardian as 'AI godfathers warn of runaway 'intelligence explosion.''

技术论证并不新鲜。如果你正在读这篇文章，你不需要它。关键的技术问题是，收益递减是否会超过能力和性能的加速（即 r<1），而他们认为很可能不会：

The technical argument is nothing new. If you're reading this, you don't need it. The technical question that matters is whether diminishing returns will outpace the acceleration of capacity and capabilities (aka is r<1), and they say probably not:

他们还考察了潜在瓶颈：算力、数据、难以自动化的任务、时间密集型流程。我同意他们的结论：这些限制可能构成约束，但不太可能阻止快速加速。

They also examine potential bottlenecks: Compute, data, hard-to-automate tasks, time-intensive processes. I agree with their conclusion that such limitations could potentially bind but are unlikely to prevent rapid acceleration.

同样，关于社会影响的部分是常见的观点：超出社会引导和适应的能力、失去监督和控制、权力制衡的侵蚀。

Similarly, the section on societal impacts is the usual points: Outpacing society's ability to steer and adapt, loss of oversight and control, erosion of checks on power.

这里重要的是作者联盟，以及他们提议采取的行动。

What is important here is the coalition of authors, and what they propose doing.

他们建议我们紧急获取对 AI 研发自动化的可见性，并弄清楚如何引导和约束智能爆炸，例如跟上前沿步伐并确保保障措施到位，然后我们必须适应事件的发展。

They recommend that we urgently obtain visibility into AI R&D automation, and figure out how to steer and constrain an intelligence explosion, as in pace the frontier and ensure safeguards are in place, and that we then must adapt to events.

这份诉求清单是好的。正如你对一份拥有如此作者名单的论文所预期的那样，这篇论文最终在修辞上仍然有所保留，至少以我自认的高标准来看是如此。有大量可怕的警告和紧急行动与这种保留完全兼容，因此它仍然呼吁我们做很多事情。奥弗顿窗口已经移动，所以这种保留与六个月或一年前你从 Bengio 风格的论文中预期的情况不在同一层次上，这是好事。

The list of asks is good. As you would expect with something that gets this list of authors, the paper still ultimately pulls many of its rhetorical punches, at least by my admittedly high standard. There are plenty of dire warnings and urgent actions that are fully compatible with punch pulling, so there is still a lot this calls on us to do. The Overton Window has shifted, so this pulling of punches is on a different level than what you’d expect from a Bengio-style paper six months or a year ago, in a good way.

我们仍然没有完全明确地说出全部主要问题。这篇论文实际上没有解决 AI 超级智能世界所涉及的许多核心问题，并且将‘失控’视为没有我希望的那么多机制的东西，也没有理解保持控制是多么不自然，以及即使在好的情况下也会伴随的困难。

We’re still not quite explicitly saying the full main thing. The paper does not actually tackle many of the core issues involved in a world where the AIs are superintelligent, and treats ‘loss of control’ as something without as many gears as I would like, and without an understanding of how unnatural it would be to stay in control and the difficulties that go along with that even in good cases.

实际进展 Actual Progress

有了这样的论文和研究团队，我们仍然更接近于纸上谈兵。

With papers and groups like this, we are still a lot closer to talking the talk.

这是实际的进展。所要求的第一批政策步骤是正确的。

This is actual progress. The first requested policy steps are correct.

OpenAI 的公告也是如此。实际的进展。一个开端。
