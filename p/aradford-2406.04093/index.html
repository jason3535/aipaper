摘要 · Abstract

稀疏自编码器通过从稀疏瓶颈层重建激活，提供了一种有前景的无监督方法，用于从语言模型中提取可解释特征。由于语言模型学习众多概念，自编码器需要非常大才能恢复所有相关特征。然而，由于需要平衡重建和稀疏性目标以及存在死亡潜在单元，研究自编码器缩放特性很困难。我们提出使用 k-稀疏自编码器[Makhzani and Frey, 2013]直接控制稀疏性，简化调优并改善重建-稀疏性前沿。此外，我们发现了即使在尝试的最大规模下也能导致很少死亡潜在单元的修改。利用这些技术，我们发现了关于自编码器大小和稀疏性的清晰缩放规律。我们还引入了几个基于假设特征恢复、激活模式可解释性和下游效应稀疏性的新指标来评估特征质量。这些指标通常随自编码器大小增加而改善。为了展示我们方法的可扩展性，我们在 GPT-4 激活上训练了一个 1600 万潜在单元的自编码器，处理了 400 亿个 token。我们发布了训练代码和开源模型的自编码器，以及一个可视化工具。

Sparse autoencoders provide a promising unsupervised approach for extracting interpretable features from a language model by reconstructing activations from a sparse bottleneck layer. Since language models learn many concepts, autoencoders need to be very large to recover all relevant features. However, studying the properties of autoencoder scaling is difficult due to the need to balance reconstruction and sparsity objectives and the presence of dead latents. We propose using k-sparse autoencoders [Makhzani and Frey, 2013] to directly control sparsity, simplifying tuning and improving the reconstruction-sparsity frontier. Additionally, we find modifications that result in few dead latents, even at the largest scales we tried. Using these techniques, we find clean scaling laws with respect to autoencoder size and sparsity. We also introduce several new metrics for evaluating feature quality based on the recovery of hypothesized features, the explainability of activation patterns, and the sparsity of downstream effects. These metrics all generally improve with autoencoder size. To demonstrate the scalability of our approach, we train a 16 million latent autoencoder on GPT-4 activations for 40 billion tokens. We release training code and autoencoders for open-source models, as well as a visualizer.

局限 · Limitations

  • TopK 强制每个词元恰好 k 个活跃潜变量，可能次优；期望 L0 约束可能更好。
    TopK forces exactly k active latents per token, which may be suboptimal; expected L0 constraint might be better.
  • 探针损失和可解释性等指标依赖于对自然特征的假设，可能无法捕捉所有方面。
    Metrics like probe loss and explainability rely on assumptions about natural features and may not capture all aspects.
  • N2G 解释仅限于简单的 n-gram 模式，无法捕捉复杂的特征交互。
    N2G explanations are limited to simple n-gram patterns and cannot capture complex feature interactions.
  • 训练仅使用 64 词元上下文，可能不足以捕捉 GPT-4 中的长程行为。
    Training uses only 64-token context, potentially insufficient for capturing long-range behaviors in GPT-4.
  • 缩放定律在极端规模下可能因每个潜变量的词元预算次线性而失效。
    Scaling laws may break at extreme scales due to sublinear token budget per latent.

4.1 下游损失 4.1 Downstream loss

具有非零重建误差的自编码器可能无法成功建模与行为最相关的特征（Braun 等人，2024）。为了衡量我们是否建模了与语言建模相关的特征，我们遵循先前的工作（Bills 等人，2023；Cunningham 等人，2023；Bricken 等人，2023；Braun 等人，2024），考虑下游 Kullback-Leibler（KL）散度和交叉熵损失。由于完美重建会导致非零交叉熵损失，我们实际上考虑与完美自编码器交叉熵的差异（“delta 交叉熵”）。在这两种情况下，我们通过在前向传播中用重建值替换残差流来测试自编码器，并观察它如何影响下游预测。我们发现，与先前方法相比，k-稀疏自编码器在下游损失上的改进大于 MSE（图 5a）。我们还发现，当保持稀疏性 L0 固定且仅改变自编码器大小时，MSE 与 KL 散度和交叉熵损失差异之间存在清晰的幂律关系（图 5b）。注意，虽然这种趋势对我们训练的自编码器是清晰的，但我们可以观察到它被打破的情况，例如在测试时调节 k 时（见第 5.3 节）。

An autoencoder with non-zero reconstruction error may not succeed at modeling the features most relevant for behavior (Braun et al., 2024). To measure whether we model features relevant to language modeling, we follow prior work (Bills et al., 2023; Cunningham et al., 2023; Bricken et al., 2023; Braun et al., 2024) and consider downstream Kullback-Leibler (KL) divergence and cross-entropy loss.999Because a perfect reconstruction would lead to a non-zero cross-entropy loss, we actually consider the difference to the perfect-autoencoder cross-entropy (“delta cross-entropy”). In both cases, we test an autoencoder by replacing the residual stream by the reconstructed value during the forward pass, and seeing how it affects downstream predictions. We find that k𝑘k-sparse autoencoders improve more on downstream loss than on MSE over prior methods (Figure 5a). We also find that MSE has a clean power law relationship with both KL divergence, and difference of cross entropy loss (Figure 5b), when keeping sparsity L0 fixed and only varying autoencoder size. Note that while this trend is clean for our trained autoencoders, we can observe instances where it breaks such as when modulating k𝑘k at test time (see Section 5.3).

另一个问题是，仅凭原始损失数字难以解释——我们想知道它在绝对意义上有多好。先前的工作（Bricken 等人，2023；Rajamanoharan 等人，2024）使用将激活消融为零的损失作为基线，并报告从该基线恢复的损失比例。然而，由于将残差流消融为零会导致非常高的下游损失，这意味着即使对行为的解释非常差，也可能获得高分。作为参考，我们 1600 万自编码器的零消融保真度指标为 98.2%。

One additional issue is that raw loss numbers alone are difficult to interpret—we would like to know how good it is in an absolute sense. Prior work (Bricken et al., 2023; Rajamanoharan et al., 2024) use the loss of ablating activations to zero as a baseline and report the fraction of loss recovered from that baseline. However, because ablating the residual stream to zero causes very high downstream loss, this means that even very poorly explaining the behavior can result in high scores.101010For completeness, the zero-ablation fidelity metric of our 16M autoencoder is 98.2%.

相反，我们认为更自然的指标是考虑训练一个具有可比下游损失的语言模型所需的预训练算力的相对量。例如，当我们的 1600 万潜变量自编码器被替换到 GPT-4 中时，我们得到的语言建模损失对应于 GPT-4 预训练算力的 10%。

Instead, we believe a more natural metric is to consider the relative amount of pretraining compute needed to train a language model of comparable downstream loss. For example, when our 16 million latent autoencoder is substituted into GPT-4, we get a language modeling loss corresponding to 10% of the pretraining compute of GPT-4.

4.3 为特征寻找简单解释 4.3 Finding simple explanations for features

根据经验，我们的自编码器发现许多特征具有快速可识别的模式，这些模式在查看随机激活时暗示了解释（第 E.1 节）。然而，这可能会产生一种“可解释性错觉”（Bolukbasi 等人，2021），其中解释过于宽泛，因此召回率高但精确率低。例如，Bills 等人（2023）提出了一种自动可解释性得分，该得分不成比例地依赖于召回率。他们发现一个特征在短语“don’t stop”或“can’t stop”的末尾激活，但一个在所有“stop”实例上激活的解释获得了很高的可解释性得分。随着我们扩展自编码器，特征变得更稀疏和更具体，这种失败变得更加严重。

Anecdotally, our autoencoders find many features that have quickly recognizable patterns that suggest explanations when viewing random activations (Section E.1). However, this can create an “illusion” of interpretability (Bolukbasi et al., 2021), where explanations are overly broad, and thus have good recall but poor precision. For example, Bills et al. (2023) propose an automated interpretability score which disproportionately depends on recall. They find a feature activating at the end of the phrase “don’t stop” or “can’t stop”, but an explanation activating on all instances of “stop” achieves a high interpretability score. As we scale autoencoders and the features get sparser and more specific, this kind of failure becomes more severe.

4.4 解释重构 4.4 Explanation reconstruction

当我们的目标是使模型的激活可解释时，我们可以问的一个问题是：如果我们只使用模型中我们可以解释的部分，我们会牺牲多少性能？

When our goal is for a model’s activations to be interpretable, one question we can ask is: how much performance do we sacrifice if we use only the parts of the model that we can interpret?

我们的下游损失指标衡量我们捕获了多少性能（但我们的特征可能不可解释），而基于解释的指标衡量我们的特征有多单义（但它们可能无法解释模型的大部分）。这表明结合我们的下游损失和解释指标，通过使用我们的解释来模拟自编码器潜在变量，然后在解码后检查下游损失。该指标还有一个优点，即它以原则性的方式同时重视召回率和精确度，并且对于激活更密集的潜在变量更重视召回率。

Our downstream loss metric measures how much of the performance we’re capturing (but our features could be uninterpretable), and our explanation based metric measures how monosemantic our features are (but they might not explain most of the model). This suggests combining our downstream loss and explanation metrics, by using our explanations to simulate autoencoder latents, and then checking downstream loss after decoding. This metric also has the advantage that it values both recall and precision in a way that is principled, and also values recall more for latents that activate more densely.

4.5 消融效应的稀疏性 4.5 Sparsity of ablation effects

如果语言模型学习到的底层计算是稀疏的，一个假设是自然特征不仅在激活方面是稀疏的，在下游效应方面也是稀疏的（Olah 等人，2024）。根据经验，我们观察到消融效应通常是可解释的（参见我们的可视化工具）。因此，我们开发了一个指标来衡量输出 logits 上下游效应的稀疏性。

If the underlying computations learned by a language model are sparse, one hypothesis is that natural features are not only sparse in terms of activations, but also in terms of downstream effects (Olah et al., 2024). Anecdotally, we observed that ablation effects often are interpretable (see our visualizer). Therefore, we developed a metric to measure the sparsity of downstream effects on the output logits.

在特定的词元索引处，我们获取残差流处的潜在变量，然后逐个消融每个自编码器潜在变量，并比较消融前后的 logits。这个过程导致每个消融和受影响的词元有 V 个 logit 差异，其中 V 是词元词汇表的大小。由于每个 logit 上的恒定差异不影响 softmax 后的概率，我们在每个词元处减去 logit 差异的中位数。最后，我们将这些向量在一组 T 个未来词元（在消融索引处或之后）上连接起来，得到一个包含 V·T 个数字的向量。然后我们通过(L1/L2)^2 测量该向量的稀疏性，这对应于“受影响的词汇词元的有效数量”。我们除以 V·T 以得到 0 到 1 之间的分数，较小的值对应更稀疏的效应。

At a particular token index, we obtain the latents at the residual stream, and proceed to ablate each autoencoder latent one by one, and compare the resulting logits before and after ablation. This process leads to V𝑉V logit differences per ablation and affected token, where V𝑉V is the size of the token vocabulary. Because a constant difference at every logit does not affect the post-softmax probabilities, we subtract at each token the median logit difference value. Finally, we concatenate these vectors together across some set of T𝑇T future tokens (at the ablated index or later) to obtain a vector of V⋅T⋅𝑉𝑇V\cdot T total numbers. We then measure the sparsity of this vector via (L1L2)2superscriptsubscriptL1subscriptL22(\frac{\text{L}_{1}}{\text{L}_{2}})^{2}, which corresponds to an “effective number of vocab tokens affected”. We normalize by V⋅T⋅𝑉𝑇V\cdot T to have a fraction between 0 and 1, with smaller values corresponding to sparser effects.

5.1 TopK 防止激活收缩 5.1 TopK prevents activation shrinkage

L1 惩罚的一个主要缺点是它倾向于将所有激活向零收缩（Tibshirani, 1996）。我们提出的 TopK 激活函数防止了激活收缩，因为它完全消除了对 L1 惩罚的需求。为了经验性地测量激活收缩的幅度，我们考虑在固定解码器的情况下，不同（可能更大）的激活是否会带来更好的重建。我们首先运行编码器以获得一组激活的潜在变量，保存稀疏掩码，然后仅优化非零值以最小化 MSE。这种细化方法已被多次提出，例如在 k-SVD（Aharon 等人，2006）、松弛 Lasso（Meinshausen, 2007）或 ITI（Maleki, 2009）中。我们使用投影梯度下降在正性约束下求解最优激活。

A major drawback of the L1 penalty is that it tends to shrink all activations toward zero (Tibshirani, 1996). Our proposed TopK activation function prevents activation shrinkage, as it entirely removes the need for an L1 penalty. To empirically measure the magnitude of activation shrinkage, we consider whether different (and potentially larger) activations would result in better reconstruction given a fixed decoder. We first run the encoder to obtain a set of activated latents, save the sparsity mask, and then optimize only the nonzero values to minimize MSE.141414unlike the “inference-time optimization” procedure in Nanda et al. (2024) This refinement method has been proposed multiple times such as in k𝑘k-SVD (Aharon et al., 2006), the relaxed Lasso (Meinshausen, 2007), or ITI (Maleki, 2009). We solve for the optimal activations with a positivity constraint using projected gradient descent.

这种细化过程倾向于平均增加 ReLU 模型中的激活，但不会增加 TopK 模型中的激活（图 9a），这表明 TopK 不受激活收缩的影响。细化幅度对于 TopK 模型也比 ReLU 模型小。在 ReLU 和 TopK 模型中，细化过程显著改善了重建 MSE（图 9b）和下游下一个词预测交叉熵（图 9c）。然而，这种细化仅缩小了 ReLU 和 TopK 模型之间的一部分差距。

This refinement procedure tends to increase activations in ReLU models on average, but not in TopK models (Figure 9a), which indicates that TopK is not impacted by activation shrinkage. The magnitude of the refinement is also smaller for TopK models than for ReLU models. In both ReLU and TopK models, the refinement procedure noticeably improves the reconstruction MSE (Figure 9b), and the downstream next-token-prediction cross-entropy (Figure 9c). However, this refinement only closes part of the gap between ReLU and TopK models.

5.2 与其他激活函数的比较 5.2 Comparison with other activation functions

其他关于稀疏自编码器的近期工作提出了不同的方法来解决 L1 激活收缩，并帕累托改进 L0-MSE 前沿（Wright 和 Sharkey, 2024; Taggart, 2024; Rajamanoharan 等人，2024）。Wright 和 Sharkey（2024）提出微调每个潜在变量的缩放参数，以纠正 L1 激活收缩。在门控稀疏自编码器中（Rajamanoharan 等人，2024），选择哪些潜在变量是活跃的与估计激活幅度是分开的。这种分离使自编码器能够更好地估计激活幅度，并避免 L1 激活收缩。另一种方法是用 ProLU（Taggart, 2024）（也称为 TRec（Konda 等人，2014）或 JumpReLU（Erichson 等人，2019））替换 ReLU 激活函数，该函数将所有低于正阈值的值设为零：J_θ(x)=x·1_{(x>θ)}。由于参数θ不可微，它需要近似梯度，例如 ReLU 等价物（ProLU-ReLU）或直通估计器（ProLU-STE）（Taggart, 2024）。

Other recent works on sparse autoencoders have proposed different ways to address the L1 activation shrinkage, and Pareto improve the L0-MSE frontier (Wright and Sharkey, 2024; Taggart, 2024; Rajamanoharan et al., 2024). Wright and Sharkey (2024) propose to fine-tune a scaling parameter per latent, to correct for the L1 activation shrinkage. In Gated sparse autoencoders (Rajamanoharan et al., 2024), the selection of which latents are active is separate from the estimation of the activation magnitudes. This separation allows autoencoders to better estimate the activation magnitude, and avoid the L1 activation shrinkage. Another approach is to replace the ReLU activation function with a ProLU (Taggart, 2024) (also known as TRec (Konda et al., 2014), or JumpReLU (Erichson et al., 2019)), which sets all values below a positive threshold to zero Jθ​(x)=x⋅𝟏(x&gt;θ)subscript𝐽𝜃𝑥⋅𝑥subscript1𝑥𝜃J_{\theta}(x)=x\cdot\mathbf{1}_{(x&gt;\theta)}. Because the parameter θ𝜃\theta is non-differentiable, it requires a approximate gradient such as a ReLU equivalent (ProLU-ReLU) or a straight-through estimator (ProLU-STE) (Taggart, 2024).

我们比较了这些不同方法在重建 MSE、活跃潜在变量数 L0 和下游交叉熵损失方面的表现（图 2 和图 5）。我们发现它们显著改善了重建-稀疏性帕累托前沿，其中 TopK 整体性能最佳。

5.3 渐进恢复 5.3 Progressive recovery

在渐进编码中，部分传输仍然允许以合理的保真度重建信号（Skodras 等人，2001）。对于自编码器，学习渐进编码意味着按激活幅度排序潜在变量提供了一种逐步恢复原始向量的方法。为了研究这一特性，我们在训练后用 TopK(k')激活函数替换自编码器激活函数，其中 k'与训练时不同。然后我们通过将每个 k'值放在 L0-MSE 平面中评估（图 10）。

In a progressive code, a partial transmission still allows reconstructing the signal with reasonable fidelity (Skodras et al., 2001). For autoencoders, learning a progressive code means that ordering latents by activation magnitude gives a way to progressively recover the original vector. To study this property, we replace the autoencoder activation function (after training) by a TopK(k′superscript𝑘′k^{\prime}) activation function where k′superscript𝑘′k^{\prime} is different than during training. We then evaluate each value of k′superscript𝑘′k^{\prime} by placing it in the L0-MSE plane (Figure 10).

我们发现，使用 TopK 训练仅在训练时使用的 k 值范围内提供渐进编码。MSE 在略高于 k 的值上持续改善（这一结果也在（Makhzani 和 Frey, 2013）中描述），然后随着 k'的增加而显著变差（注意对下游损失的影响更为缓和）。这可以解释为对 k 值的某种过拟合。

We find that training with TopK only gives a progressive code up to the value of k𝑘k used during training. MSE keeps improving for values slightly over k𝑘k (a result also described in (Makhzani and Frey, 2013)), then gets substantially worse as k′superscript𝑘′k^{\prime} increases (note that the effect on downstream loss is more muted). This can be interpreted as some sort of overfitting to the value k𝑘k.

为了缓解这个问题，我们求和多个具有不同 k 值的 TopK 损失（Multi-TopK）。例如，使用 L(k)+L(4k)/8 足以在所有 k'上获得渐进编码（但注意，使用 Multi-TopK 训练在 k 处比 TopK 稍差）。使用基线 ReLU 训练仅在对应于使用所有正潜在变量的值范围内提供渐进编码。

To mitigate this issue, we sum multiple TopK losses with different values of k𝑘k (Multi-TopK). For example, using ℒ​(k)+ℒ​(4​k)/8ℒ𝑘ℒ4𝑘8\mathcal{L}(k)+\mathcal{L}(4k)/8 is enough to obtain a progressive code over all k′superscript𝑘′k^{\prime} (note however that training with Multi-TopK does slightly worse than TopK at k𝑘k). Training with the baseline ReLU only gives a progressive code up to a value that corresponds to using all positive latents.

对于使用 TopK 训练的自编码器，测试时的 TopK 和 JumpReLU 曲线仅在对应于 L0 低于训练 L0 的值上重叠，否则 JumpReLU 激活比 TopK 激活差。这种差异在使用 Multi-TopK 时消失，两条曲线几乎重叠，这意味着模型可以在不损失重建的情况下使用固定或动态数量的每词元潜在变量。对于使用 ReLU 训练的自编码器，两条曲线也重叠。有趣的是，有时使用低 L1 惩罚训练 ReLU 模型并在测试时使用 TopK 或 JumpReLU，比使用更高 L1 惩罚以获得类似稀疏性水平更有效（这一结果在 Nanda 等人（2024）中独立描述）。

7 相关工作 7 Related work

过完备字典上的稀疏编码由 Mallat 和 Zhang（1993）提出。Olshausen 和 Field（1996）通过提出从数据中无监督学习字典来完善这一思想。这种方法在图像处理中特别有影响力，例如参见（Mairal 等人，2014）。后来，Hinton 和 Salakhutdinov（2006）提出了自编码器架构来进行降维。结合这些概念，稀疏自编码器被开发出来（Lee 等人，2007；Le 等人，2013；Konda 等人，2014），以训练具有稀疏先验（如 L1 惩罚）的自编码器，从而提取稀疏特征。Makhzani 和 Frey（2013）通过引入 k-稀疏自编码器完善了这一概念，该自编码器使用 TopK 激活函数代替 L1 惩罚。Makelov 等人（2024）使用一种度量来评估自编码器，该度量衡量从先前发现的电路中恢复特征的能力。

Sparse coding on an over-complete dictionary was introduced by Mallat and Zhang (1993). Olshausen and Field (1996) refined the idea by proposing to learn the dictionary from the data, without supervision. This approach has been particularly influential in image processing, as seen for example in (Mairal et al., 2014). Later, Hinton and Salakhutdinov (2006) proposed the autoencoder architecture to perform dimensionality reduction. Combining these concepts, sparse autoencoders were developed (Lee et al., 2007; Le et al., 2013; Konda et al., 2014) to train autoencoders with sparsity priors, such as the L1 penalty, to extract sparse features. Makhzani and Frey (2013) refined this concept by introducing k𝑘k-sparse autoencoders, which use a TopK activation function instead of the L1 penalty. Makelov et al. (2024) evaluates autoencoders using a metric that measures recovery of features from previously discovered circuits.

最近，稀疏自编码器被应用于语言模型（Yun 等人，2021；Lee Sharkey，2022；Bricken 等人，2023；Cunningham 等人，2023），并在小型开源语言模型上训练了多个稀疏自编码器（Marks，2023；Bloom，2024；Mossing 等人，2024）。Marks 等人（2024）表明，稀疏自编码器产生的特征可以在语言模型中找到稀疏电路。Wright 和 Sharkey（2024）指出，稀疏自编码器会受到 L1 惩罚导致的激活收缩，这是 Tibshirani（1996）首次描述的 L1 惩罚的一个特性。Taggart（2024）和 Rajamanoharan 等人（2024）提出使用不同的激活函数来解决稀疏自编码器中的激活收缩问题。Braun 等人（2024）提出在下游 KL 散度上训练稀疏自编码器，而不是重建 MSE。

More recently, sparse autoencoders were applied to language models (Yun et al., 2021; Lee Sharkey, 2022; Bricken et al., 2023; Cunningham et al., 2023), and multiple sparse autoencoders were trained on small open-source language models (Marks, 2023; Bloom, 2024; Mossing et al., 2024). Marks et al. (2024) showed that the resulting features from sparse autoencoders can find sparse circuits in language models. Wright and Sharkey (2024) pointed out that sparse autoencoders are subject to activation shrinking from L1 penalties, a property of L1 penalties first described in Tibshirani (1996). Taggart (2024) and Rajamanoharan et al. (2024) proposed to use different activation functions to address activation shrinkage in sparse autoencoders. Braun et al. (2024) proposed to train sparse autoencoders on downstream KL instead of reconstruction MSE.

Kaplan 等人（2020）研究了语言模型的缩放定律，探讨损失如何随各种超参数变化。Clark 等人（2022）使用双线性拟合探索了与稀疏性相关的缩放定律。Lindsey 等人（2024）专门研究了自编码器的缩放定律，将损失定义为重建和稀疏性的特定平衡（而不是简单地重建，同时保持稀疏性固定）。

Kaplan et al. (2020) studied scaling laws for language models which examine how loss varies with various hyperparameters. Clark et al. (2022) explore scaling laws related to sparsity using a bilinear fit. Lindsey et al. (2024) studied scaling laws specifically for autoencoders, defining the loss as a specific balance of reconstruction and sparsity (rather than simply reconstruction, while holding sparsity fixed).
