摘要 · Abstract

神经活动内在思想的逻辑演算* WARREN S. MCCULLOCH 和 WALTER PITTS 伊利诺伊大学医学院，伊利诺伊神经精神病学研究所精神病学系，芝加哥大学，芝加哥，美国 基于神经活动的“全或无”特征，神经事件及其相互关系可以通过命题逻辑来处理。我们发现，每个网络的行为都可以用这些术语来描述，对于包含回路的网络，则需要添加更复杂的逻辑手段；并且对于任何满足特定条件的逻辑表达式，都可以找到一个网络以它所描述的方式行为。结果表明，在可能的神经生理学假设中，许多特定选择是等价的，即对于在每个假设下行为的每个网络，都存在另一个网络在另一个假设下行为并给出相同的结果，尽管时间可能不同。本文讨论了该演算的各种应用。

A LOGICAL CALCULUS OF THE IDEAS IMMANENT IN NERVOUS ACTIVITY* WARREN S. MCCULLOCH AND WALTER PITTS University of Illinois, College of Medicine, Department of Psychiatry at the Illinois Neuropsychiatric Institute, University of Chicago, Chicago, U.S.A. of the “all-or-none” character of nervous activity, neural events and the relations among them can be treated by means of propositional logic. It is found that the behavior of every net can be described in these terms, with the addition of more complicated logical means for nets containing circles; and that for any logical expression satisfying certain conditions, one can find a net behaving in the fashion it describes. It is shown that many particular choices among possible neurophysiological assumptions are equivalent, in the sense that for every net behaving under one assumption, there exists another net which behaves under the other and gives the same results, although perhaps not in the same time. Various applications of the calculus are discussed.

核心贡献 · Key contributions

  • 证明神经活动因其全或无特性，可用命题逻辑处理。
    Shows neural activity can be treated by propositional logic due to all-or-none character.
  • 每个神经网络的行为都可用逻辑术语描述，循环网络需使用递归函数。
    Every net's behavior can be described in logical terms, with circles requiring recursive functions.
  • 满足特定条件的任何逻辑表达式都可由神经网络实现。
    Any logical expression satisfying certain conditions can be realized by a neural net.
  • 证明不同神经生理学假设在扩展意义上具有等价性。
    Demonstrates equivalence of different neurophysiological assumptions under extended sense.
  • 提供了一种为特定时间命题表达式构建神经网络的方法。
    Provides a method to construct nets for specified temporal propositional expressions.

局限 · Limitations

  • 假设全或无定律和固定阈值，忽略了神经的连续变化。
    Assumes all-or-none law and fixed thresholds, ignoring continuous neural variations.
  • 简化了突触延迟和时间总和，未捕捉详细的生物物理学特性。
    Simplifies synaptic delay and temporal summation, not capturing detailed biophysics.
  • 循环网络分析复杂，仅限定于图灵机可计算函数。
    Circular net analysis is complex and limited to computable functions via Turing machine equivalence.
  • 许多理论主张在真实生物网络中缺乏经验验证。
    Lack of empirical validation for many theoretical claims in real biological nets.
  • 假设结构静态，未考虑超出简单突触形成的学习和可塑性。
    Assumes static structure, not accounting for learning and plasticity beyond simple synapse formation.
