摘要 · Abstract

近年来，基于大语言模型的文本转语音技术因其高自然度和零样本能力而成为主流。在该范式中，语音信号被离散化为标记序列，由大语言模型以文本为提示进行建模，并通过基于标记的声码器重建为波形。显然，语音标记在基于大语言模型的文本转语音模型中起着关键作用。当前的语音标记以无监督方式学习，缺乏明确的语义信息和与文本的对齐。本文提出用监督语义标记表示语音，这些标记通过将向量量化插入编码器，从多语言语音识别模型中导出。基于这些标记，我们进一步提出可扩展的零样本文本转语音合成器 CosyVoice，它由用于文本到标记生成的大语言模型和用于标记到语音合成的条件流匹配模型组成。实验结果表明，监督语义标记在零样本语音克隆的内容一致性和说话人相似性方面显著优于现有的无监督标记。此外，我们发现利用大规模数据可进一步提高合成性能，表明 CosyVoice 的可扩展能力。据我们所知，这是首次尝试将监督语音标记引入文本转语音模型。

Recent years have witnessed a trend that large language model (LLM) based text-to-speech (TTS) emerges into the mainstream due to their high naturalness and zero-shot capacity. In this paradigm, speech signals are discretized into token sequences, which are modeled by an LLM with text as prompts and reconstructed by a token-based vocoder to waveforms. Obviously, speech tokens play a critical role in LLM-based TTS models. Current speech tokens are learned in an unsupervised manner, which lacks explicit semantic information and alignment to the text. In this paper, we propose to represent speech with supervised semantic tokens, which are derived from a multilingual speech recognition model by inserting vector quantization into the encoder. Based on the tokens, we further propose a scalable zero-shot TTS synthesizer, CosyVoice, which consists of an LLM for text-to-token generation and a conditional flow matching model for token-to-speech synthesis. Experimental results show that supervised semantic tokens significantly outperform existing unsupervised tokens in terms of content consistency and speaker similarity for zero-shot voice cloning. Moreover, we find that utilizing large-scale data further improves the synthesis performance, indicating the scalable capacity of CosyVoice. To the best of our knowledge, this is the first attempt to involve supervised speech tokens into TTS models.

  • 评估仅限于英语和中文；其他语言的多语言性能尚未充分探索。
    Evaluation limited to English and Chinese languages; multilingual performance on other languages not fully explored.
  • 依赖预训练的 ASR 模型进行监督令牌提取可能引入偏差。
    Dependence on a pre-trained ASR model for supervised token extraction may introduce biases.

    Single codebook with 4096 codes; impact of codebook size and number on performance not investigated.

    Zero-shot voice cloning requires a reference speech sample; performance may degrade with very short or noisy samples.
  • 指令微调数据仅限于特定情感和副语言特征；更广泛的可控性尚未测试。
    Instruct fine-tuning data limited to specific emotions and paralinguistic features; broader controllability not tested.

1 引言 1 Introduction

文本转语音（TTS）技术近年来取得了显著进展，从机器合成语音转变为产生几乎与人类说话者无法区分的语音。这一进步的前沿是大语言模型（LLM），它们越来越多地被用于 TTS 系统，以生成具有更高自然度和零样本合成能力的语音（Betker, 2023; Wang et al., 2023; Lajszczak et al., 2024）。这些基于 LLM 的 TTS 模型通过将语音信号转换为词元序列来工作，LLM 以文本为条件对这些词元序列进行建模。然后使用词元声码器从词元化语音中重建原始波形（Kong et al., 2020; Défossez et al., 2022）。

Text-to-Speech (TTS) technology has made remarkable strides in recent years, transitioning from robotic-sounding speech to producing voices that are nearly indistinguishable from human speakers. At the forefront of this advancement are Large Language Models (LLMs), which have been increasingly utilized in TTS systems to generate speech with a higher degree of naturalness and the ability to synthesize voices in a zero-shot fashion (Betker, 2023; Wang et al., 2023; Lajszczak et al., 2024). These LLM-based TTS models function by converting speech signals into sequences of tokens, with the LLM utilizing text as a condition to model these token sequences. A token vocoder is then employed to reconstruct the raw waveforms from the tokenized speech (Kong et al., 2020; Défossez et al., 2022).

TTS 过程的一个关键方面是语音词元的表示。传统上，词元通过无监督学习获得，这可能无法捕获明确的语义信息或与相应文本良好对齐（Hsu et al., 2021; Défossez et al., 2022）。认识到这一差距，我们的工作引入了从多语言语音识别模型 Whisper（Radford et al., 2023）中提取的监督语义词元，方法是将向量量化集成到编码器中。这一创新使得更准确的语义表示和与文本的对齐成为可能。早期研究表明，在通用语音模型（USM）上，带有辅助自动语音识别（ASR）损失的量化器在语音到文本翻译和 ASR 任务中优于 k-means 聚类，如 Rubenstein 等人（2023）所示。此外，Ye 等人（2024）使用 Gumbel-Softmax 向量量化来提取优先考虑 ASR 相关信息的离散语音表示，用于 ASR 任务。然而，这些方法对文本转语音（TTS）的影响尚不清楚。

A critical aspect of the TTS process is the representation of speech tokens. Traditionally, tokens are acquired through unsupervised learning, which may not capture explicit semantic information or align well with corresponding text (Hsu et al., 2021; Défossez et al., 2022). Recognizing this gap, our work introduces supervised semantic tokens extracted from a multilingual speech recognition model, Whisper (Radford et al., 2023), by integrating vector quantization into the encoder. This innovation allows for more accurate semantic representation and alignment with text. Early studies have shown that quantizers with auxiliary automatic speech recognition (ASR) loss outperform k-means clustering on the universal speech model (USM) for speech-to-text translation and ASR tasks, as demonstrated in Rubenstein et al. (2023). Additionally, Ye et al. (2024) employed Gumbel-Softmax vector quantization to extract discrete speech representations that prioritize ASR-relevant information for ASR tasks. However, the impact of these approaches on text-to-speech (TTS) remains unclear.

此外，利用这些监督词元，我们提出了 CosyVoice，一个可扩展且高效的零样本 TTS 合成器。CosyVoice 由一个用于将文本转换为语义词元序列的 LLM 和一个用于从这些词元合成语音的条件流匹配模型组成。与之前的系统如 TorToise TTS（Betker, 2023）不同，后者使用 LLM 结合去噪扩散概率模型（DDPM）（Ho et al., 2020），CosyVoice 采用条件流匹配方法，因为与传统的扩散模型相比，它已被证明可以加速训练和推理（Le et al., 2024）。虽然现有方法在 TTS 中使用了流匹配（Le et al., 2024; Guo et al., 2023; Mehta et al., 2023; Guan et al., 2023），但它们通常依赖于音素持续时间预测，需要使用额外的音素器和强制对齐器。然而，CosyVoice 绕过了这些依赖，提供了从文本到语音更直接和高效的路径。

Furthermore, leveraging these supervised tokens, we propose CosyVoice, a scalable and efficient zero-shot TTS synthesizer. CosyVoice is comprised of an LLM for converting text into semantic token sequences and a conditional flow matching model for the subsequent synthesis of speech from these tokens. In contrast to prior systems like TorToise TTS (Betker, 2023), which employs an LLM in conjunction with a denoising diffusion probabilistic models (DDPM) (Ho et al., 2020), CosyVoice utilizes a conditional flow matching approach, as it has been demonstrated to accelerate both training and inference compared to traditional diffusion models (Le et al., 2024). While existing methods incorporate flow matching in TTS (Le et al., 2024; Guo et al., 2023; Mehta et al., 2023; Guan et al., 2023), they often rely on phoneme duration prediction, necessitating the use of supplementary phonemizers and forced aligners. CosyVoice, however, bypasses these dependencies, offering a more direct and efficient pathway from text to speech.

我们的研究在语音生成领域做出了几项新颖的贡献：

Our research contributes to the field of speech generation in several novel ways:

我们首次将监督语音词元集成到 TTS 模型中，增强了零样本语音克隆的内容一致性和说话人相似度。

We are the first to integrate supervised speech tokens into TTS models, enhancing content consistency and speaker similarity in zero-shot voice cloning.

我们提出了 CosyVoice，一个可扩展的零样本 TTS 合成系统，它结合了用于文本到词元生成的 LLM 和用于词元到语音合成的条件流匹配模型，无需额外的音素器和强制对齐器。

We propose CosyVoice, a scalable zero-shot TTS synthesis system that combines an LLM for text-to-token generation with a conditional flow matching model for token-to-speech synthesis, forsaking the need for additional phonemizers and forced aligners.

为了进一步提高生成语音的质量，我们将 x-vector（Snyder et al., 2018）纳入 LLM，将语音建模分解为语义、说话人和韵律组件。LLM 对语义内容和韵律进行建模，而条件流匹配模型捕获音色和环境信息。我们通过无分类器指导（Ho and Salimans, 2022a）、余弦调度器和掩码条件等技术优化了流匹配过程。

To further refine the quality of generated speech, we incorporate the x-vector (Snyder et al., 2018) into the LLM to separate the modeling of speech into semantic, speaker, and prosody components. The LLM models the semantic content and prosody, while the conditional flow matching model captures timbre and environmental information. We optimize the flow matching process with techniques such as classifier-free guidance (Ho and Salimans, 2022a), a cosine scheduler, and masked conditions.

我们的实验结果表明了监督语义词元相对于无监督对应物的优越性。此外，CosyVoice 的可扩展性通过利用大规模数据时合成性能的提高得到证明。因此，这项工作代表了在开发自然、多功能 TTS 系统方面迈出的重要一步。

Our experimental results demonstrate the superiority of supervised semantic tokens over unsupervised counterparts. Additionally, the scalability of CosyVoice is evidenced by improved synthesis performance when utilizing large-scale data. This work, therefore, represents a significant step forward in the development of natural-sounding, versatile TTS systems.

6 结论 6 Conclusion

在本文中，我们介绍了 CosyVoice，一个可扩展的多语言语音生成模型，支持零样本上下文学习、跨语言语音克隆、指令生成以及情感和副语言特征的细粒度控制。实验结果表明，CosyVoice 的系统架构对说话人相似度很重要，而文本和语音分词器对内容一致性影响更大。此外，我们发现扩大模型大小和数据量可以显著提升性能。因此，CosyVoice 实现了人类水平的生成质量。

In this paper, we introduce CosyVoice, a scalable multi-lingual speech generation model, which supports zero-shot in-context learning, cross-lingual voice cloning, instructed generation and fine-grained controlling of emotion, paralinguistic features. Experimental results show that the system architecture of CosyVoice is important for speaker similarity, while the text and speech tokenizers affect the content consistency much. Besides, we find that scaling up the model size and data volume can improve the performance significantly. As a result, CosyVoice achieves the human parity generation quality.
