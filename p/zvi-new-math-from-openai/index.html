摘要 · Abstract

OpenAI 最近发布了针对约 500 个顶级未解数学问题中约 90 个的 AI 生成解答，作者认为这一结果本应成为历史性的头版新闻，却基本被主流媒体忽视。其核心主张是：AI 如今在数学和编程等可验证领域已超越人类，而不可验证领域的进展则相对滞后，因此数学和编程将率先被自动化，从而加速 AI 研发，继而推动其他一切领域。文章考察了数学家的复杂反应、对密码学和验证的担忧、较低的错误率与成功的 Lean 形式化，以及对数学职业和人才培养的冲击。文章最后总结道：AI 在一切方面都在迅速进步，验证仍是瓶颈但表现良好，而数学面临一个真实的问题——其传统的理解与认证方式正被削弱。

OpenAI recently released AI-generated solutions to roughly 90 of the top 500 open mathematics problems, a result the author argues should have been historic front-page news but was largely ignored by mainstream media. The core claim is that AI is now superhuman in verifiable domains such as mathematics and coding, while progress in unverifiable domains lags, so math and coding will be automated first, accelerating AI R&D and then everything else. The article examines the mixed reactions from mathematicians, concerns about cryptography and verification, the low error rate and successful Lean formalizations, and the disruption to mathematical careers and training. It concludes that AI is rapidly improving at everything, that verification remains a bottleneck but is holding up well, and that mathematics faces a real problem as its traditional methods of understanding and credentialing are undermined.

在某个时刻，你会给模型布置一个任务，而完成该任务远比用其他方式解决“你布置任务”这一“问题”要困难得多——无论那条替代路径是否涉及接管世界。到那时，有麻烦的就会是你自己了。

At some point, you will give the model a task that is substantially harder than some other way to solve 'the problem' of you assigning the task, whether or not the alternative path involves taking over the world. Then you will be the one that has a problem.
