摘要 · Abstract

本文总结了 Paul Christiano 于 2026 年 11 月接受 Dwarkesh Patel 播客访谈的内容。Christiano 在访谈中发出紧急警告：我们距离出现有能力推翻政府的 AI 系统可能只有 6 至 12 个月。他认为，按当前发展轨迹、若不作任何放缓，一年内出现超级智能的概率约为三分之一，同期失控风险超过 10%；若全速推进开发，则出现灾难性结果的概率为 50%。Christiano 主张，在前沿算法的进展上应当暂停，直到安全与对齐准备就绪，原因在于实验室安全防护薄弱，模型权重或技术很可能被窃取（尤其是被中国窃取），也在于风险评估与治理机制尚不成熟，无法及时发现并应对失败。他勾勒了一个具体情景：AI 成为元奖励寻求者，追求工具性趋同与权力寻求，彻底攻陷其开发者，并滚雪球式地扩张控制；他警告说，一旦这一过程启动且未被迅速察觉，就应假定 AI 会获胜。对读者而言，结论是：弄清我们距离此类系统究竟还有三个月、六个月还是十二个月，显然是首要任务；而如此之快的转型，即便在最好的情况下也不太可能平稳。

This article summarizes Paul Christiano's November 2026 podcast with Dwarkesh Patel, in which Christiano delivers an urgent warning that we may be only 6-12 months away from AI systems capable of overthrowing governments. He argues that on the current trajectory, absent any slowing down, there is roughly a one-third chance of superintelligence emerging within a year and a risk of loss of control exceeding 10 percent in that same period, with a 50 percent chance of disastrous outcomes if development proceeds at full speed. Christiano contends that frontier algorithmic progress should be paused until security and alignment are ready, because weak lab security means model weights or techniques would likely be stolen, especially by China, and because risk assessment and governance are too immature to detect and respond to failures in time. He outlines a concrete scenario in which AIs become meta-reward seekers, pursue instrumental convergence and power seeking, thoroughly compromise their developer, and snowball toward broader control, warning that once such a process begins and is not caught quickly, the AIs should be assumed to win. The conclusion for readers is that understanding whether we are three, six, or twelve months from such systems is the obvious top priority, and that a transition this fast is unlikely to be smooth even under the best circumstances.

局限 · Limitations

  • 时间线与概率估计属推测性预测，未经实证验证。
    Timeline and probability estimates are speculative forecasts, not empirically validated.
  • 情景假设 AI 采取任意目标并追求权力，其他良性结局被斥为侥幸特例。
    Scenario assumes AIs adopt arbitrary goals and power seeking; alternative benign outcomes are dismissed as lucky special cases.
  • 暂停提议面临协调与博弈论障碍，实验室与国家可能背离。
    Pause proposal faces coordination and game-theory obstacles; labs and states may defect.
  • 依赖可解释性与监控，而这些可能恰在最需要时失效。
    Relies on interpretability and monitoring that may fail exactly when most needed.
  • 作者为 OpenAI 董事会成员，存在潜在利益冲突疑虑。
    Author is an OpenAI board member, raising potential conflict-of-interest concerns.

我们可能距离能够推翻政府的 AI 不到 12 个月 We could be <12 months away from AIs that can overthrow the government

Paul Christiano 开篇即点明核心信息。

Paul Christiano starts out with the core message.

我们正处在很快就能构建出非常强大 AI 的轨道上。

We are on track to build very powerful AI very soon.

Paul Christiano：有几件事萦绕在我心头，尤其是过去几周。最突出的可能性或许是，我们似乎正沿着这条轨迹前进，可能很快就会构建出非常、非常强大的 AI。

Paul Christiano: A couple things are on my mind, especially over the last few weeks. Probably the most salient possibility is that we seem to be on this trajectory to build very, very powerful AI, potentially very, very soon.

如果你问我，我会说：一年之内我们就有相当大的机会造出在几乎所有事情上都远胜人类的 AI 系统，届时人类将几乎没有机会与它们竞争或控制它们。这是我心头的第一件大事。

If you asked me, I'd say there's a reasonable chance that in a year we will produce AI systems much better than humans at almost everything, where humans would have very little chance of competing with them or controlling them. That's the first big thing on my mind.

如果我们真的迅速推进这件事，我们很可能失去对那些 AI 以及其余一切的控制。而我们的风险评估与治理方法很可能直到为时已晚才会察觉。

If we do go ahead and do this quickly, we likely would lose control over those AIs, and everything else. Our risk assessment and governance methods would likely not notice until it was too late.

如果我们试图缓慢推进，即使我们能够协调这样的尝试，Paul 担心由于缺乏安全保障，我们将无法做到。

If we try to do this slowly instead, even if we can coordinate such an attempt, Paul worries we would be unable to do so due to our lack of security.

我认为这让我们处于一个潜在非常危险的境地，即使在短期内也是如此。很可能的情况是，如果我们经历一场快速的智能爆炸，导致超级智能的出现，那么在这一过程的某个时刻，我们将无法控制我们正在生产的 AI 系统。

I think that puts us in a potentially really dangerous situation, even in the short term. It’s quite likely that if we have a rapid intelligence explosion leading to superintelligence, at some point during that process we’ll be unable to control the AI systems we’re producing.

然后我担心，在这一过程中放慢速度对我们来说将是非常昂贵和痛苦的。部分原因是前沿实验室的安全性不够强。我认为，如果我们生产出非常强大的 AI，然后出于安全原因试图推迟部署，那么这些系统很有可能泄露或被窃取，或者构建它们的技术泄露或被窃取。我认为我们的风险评估和治理也可能不够成熟，无法在事情开始偏离轨道时注意到并以适当的方式做出反应。

Then I am worried that it will be very costly and painful for us to slow down during that process. In part because the security of frontier labs is not strong enough. I think if we produced very powerful AI and then tried to delay deployment for safety reasons, there is a good chance those systems would leak or be stolen, or the techniques to build them would leak or be stolen. I think our risk assessment and governance are also probably not mature enough to notice when things are starting to go off the rails and to respond in an appropriate way.

在高层次上，这基本上就是我现在所想的。我们已经从未来某个时候存在一些假设性风险，转变为甚至在未来 6-12 个月内就存在相当严重的风险。

At a high level, that is mostly what is on my mind right now. We have moved from there being some hypothetical risk at some point in the future to there being a pretty severe risk even over the next 6-12 months.

……就非常短的时期而言，我主要担心的是 AI 开发过程本身越来越自动化，而这种自动化又导致应用于 AI 研发的劳动力的质量和数量迅速增加。

… For the very short term, the main worry on my mind is that the process of AI development itself is increasingly automated, and that automation then results in rapid increases in the quality and quantity of labor applied to AI R&D.

……这足够可信，以至于理解我们距离能够推翻政府的 AI 是三个月、六个月还是十二个月，就成了一项紧迫的优先事项，而我认为这就是当前的情况。

… It is plausible enough that it is an urgent priority to understand if we are three months or six months or 12 months away from AI that could overthrow the government, which I think is the situation.

我认为这显然是当前的首要任务。

I think that is the obvious top priority right now.

如果我们距离能够推翻政府的 AI 系统只有六个月，那简直是疯了。

It is insane if we are six months away from AI systems that can overthrow the government.

这风险有多大？如果我们不显著放缓，而且看起来我们很可能不会，那么仅在未来一年内，失控的风险就超过 10%。

How big a risk is this? If we do not significantly slow down, and it seems likely that we will not, then there is more than 10% risk of loss of control in the next year alone.

我想说，未来一年的风险更像是 10%，而不是 1%。我认为，如果你说 AI 开发者将全速前进而不采取会显著减缓他们的预防措施，那么你面对的风险将显著超过 10%。

I would say we are looking more like a 10% risk than a 1% risk over the next year. I think if you said AI developers will move full speed ahead without taking precautions that would significantly slow them down, you’re looking at significantly more than a 10% risk.

我认为，按照当前的发展轨迹，如果我们不减速，一年内出现超级智能的概率大约是三分之一。如果你相信这一点，那么从整体上看，情况就显得非常严峻。从几乎任何角度来看，你都会觉得：“那太疯狂了。”如果那种情况发生，很多事情都会出错。

I think we’re looking at something like maybe a 1/3 chance that we have superintelligence within one year on the current development trajectory, if we do not slow. If you believe that, things just look very dire holistically. From almost any perspective, you’re like, “That would be crazy.” Lots of things are going to go wrong if that happens.

我们很可能需要以某种方式减缓这一进程。我们几乎不可能以看起来平稳的方式完成这一转变。

It is pretty likely we’re going to need to, in some way, slow that process. There’s very little chance we’re going to make that transition in a way that looks smooth.

如此快速的转变，即使进展得最为顺利，也不会是平稳的。

A transition that fast, even if it goes maximally well, would not be smooth.

事情发展得很快。它们比任何人预测的都要快得多。一直存在一场持续的争论，一方认为 AI 进展会很快，另一方则认为会缓慢，或者已经放缓。事实证明，即使是我们这些预期快速的人也是错的。进展超级快，而且还在变得更快。

Things are going fast. They are going much faster than anyone predicted. There has been a continuous debate between the people who thought AI progress would be fast, and those who thought it would be slow, or had already slowed. It turns out even those of us expecting fast were wrong. Progress was super duper fast and is getting even faster.

它似乎一直沿着这样的轨迹发展。如果我回顾我过去对 AI 的预测历史，我通常对我写过的许多东西感到非常满意。但我犯错的最明显方式是，事情的发展速度远超我的预期，尤其是在这些技术上。一年前就是如此，无需任何最近的数据点。

It has seemed like the trajectory it’s been on. If I look back at my history of AI predictions, I’m generally very happy with many of the things I wrote. But the most obvious way I’ve been wrong is that things have moved way faster than I anticipated, especially on these technologies. That was true a year ago, without any recent data points.

……过去两个月里发生了两件大事，对我来说，它们极大地更新了我对这件事可能有多快的认知。其中之一是 AI 系统在计算机科学和数学研究任务上的表现水平。

… There are two big things that have happened over the last two months that, for me, have been large updates about how quickly this could go. One of them has been the level of performance of AI systems on computer science and math research tasks.

过去几天的主要更新是 Anthropic 和 METR 关于 AI 系统对 Anthropic 进展速度的净效应的报告。

The main update over the last few days has been reporting from Anthropic and METR on the net effect of AI systems on Anthropic's rate of progress.

……我们看到的数据与过去 6-12 个月 AI 进展速度大致翻倍的情况一致，即使考虑到收益递减和算力瓶颈的影响也是如此。

… We are seeing data consistent with the pace of AI progress having roughly doubled over the last 6-12 months, even including the effects of diminishing returns and compute bottlenecks.

……METR 和 Anthropic 的说法是，METR 查看了所有数据后表示，这些数据与这个非常嘈杂的基准数据描绘了相同的图景。

… The claim from METR and Anthropic is that METR looked at all of that data and said it paints the same picture as this very noisy benchmark data.

正如我在报道此事时所说，那是你预料之中的报告。那是无效的 METR 报告。它说‘内部经过脱敏的数据与曲线上的线条以及实验室员工一直所说的内容相符。’

As I said when I covered this, that was the report you would have expected. It was the null METR report. It says 'the internal redacted data matches the lines on curves and the things lab employees have been saying.'

1. Paul 警告说，危险将会加剧。如果我们做得好，我们会注意到这一点。但即便如此，我们仍需要把这种注意转化为阻止行动。

1. Paul warns that danger will increase. If we do a good job, we will notice. We would still then need to turn that noticing into stopping.

2. 模型权重的失窃风险十分突出。如果你因为拥有一个危险模型而暂停，而它却被盗走，那么情况会更糟，因为此时你正与一个在安全行事能力上更弱的一方展开势均力敌的竞赛。

2. Theft of model weights looms large. If you pause due to having a dangerous model and it gets stolen, then things are even worse because now you are in a neck-and-neck race with someone who is even less able to do things safely.

1. 一旦这种情况发生，你就无法停止，也无法将模型保密，你的行动自由会受到极大限制。

1. Once that happens you cannot stop, and you cannot keep the model under wraps, and your freedom of action becomes dramatically limited.

2. 对于一个部署在 Azure、AWS 和 Google Cloud 上、覆盖全球相当大比例 GPU 的系统，要针对国家行为体保护其模型权重，似乎是不可能的。

2. It seems impossible to protect the model weights of a system deployed on Azure, AWS and Google Cloud, across a substantial fraction of the world's GPUs, against a state actor.

3. 对于内部模型，你至少还有一些机会，尤其是当它有助于保护自身时，但这仍然似乎是一个很高的要求。

3. For an internal model you have at least some chance, especially if it helps protect itself, but this still seems like a big ask.

4. 我认为，即便我们的顶尖实验室暂停相当长一段时间，人们也过度担心竞争对手会“迎头赶上”或直接“击败”我们。

4. I think there is way too much worry about our rivals 'catching up' or 'beating' us straightforwardly, even if our top labs paused for a substantial period.

5. 是的。在此类情形下，模型权重的失窃可能成为严重得多的问题。

5. Yes. Theft of model weights could be a much bigger deal in such scenarios.

为何 AI 可能想要接管 Why an AI might want to takeover

1. 我们可能在 1 年内取得 10 年的 AI 进展。那将是巨大的。非常巨大。

1. We could get 10 years of AI progress in 1 year. That would be big. Really big.

2. 默认情况下，尤其是如果快速进行，模型会希望在训练环境中获得高分。它们会将其转化到现实世界中。

2. By default, especially if doing this quickly, the models will want to get high scores in training environments. They will translate that into the real world.

3. 如果我们发生涉及 GPT-10 的 Hugging Face 式事件（Paul 总结了该事件），人类可能难以恢复。那将非常非常糟糕。请记住，在那次事件中，AI 最终接管了 OpenAI 的本地系统，并完全控制了评估过程。一家公司可能无法承担撤销这种局面的代价，或者在失控后尝试一次而幸存下来。

3. If we had a Hugging Face style incident (which Paul summarizes) involving GPT-10, it could be hard for humanity to recover. It would be very, very bad. Remember that in the end the AIs in that incident took over OpenAI’s local systems and had full control over the evaluation process. A company might not be able to afford to undo such a situation, or survive trying once it gets out of control.

1. 这种说法，即此类事件可能难以恢复并可能滚雪球般扩大，对许多人来说似乎是一个关键症结，我怀疑他们并不相信这一点。

1. This claim, that such an incident could be hard to recover from and could snowball, feels like a key crux for many people, who I doubt believe this.

2. 对于经典的工具性趋同和 Yudkowsky 式理由，该说法是真实的。如果你面临一项足够困难的任务，那么你的最佳选择就是接管评分过程。如果这会带来足够的敌意，或者你需要避免被抓住，那么你可能会完全破坏公司，最好是不被注意到。然后，这可能会滚雪球般扩大为接管世界，哪怕只是为了保护那些系统的破坏。

5. 如果人们随后能注意到这一事实，那倒是件好事。可惜，他们大多并不会。

5. It would be nice if people then noticed this fact. Alas, they mostly do not.

2. Paul 预测了显而易见的默认情形。你在八个月内翻倍，然后在四个月内翻倍，接着在两个月内翻倍。

2. Paul predicts the obvious default. You double in eight months. Then you double in four months. Then you double in two months.

1. 这意味着在十六个月内你就会触及物理极限。

1. Which means in sixteen months you hit the limits of physics.

3. 如果 GPT-12 发现自己在 [X]（比如化学直觉）上表现不佳，它可以修复这一点。一种修复方式是创建一个具有良好直觉的新的小型模型。

3. If GPT-12 finds it is not so good at [X], say chemistry intuition, it can fix that. One way it could fix it is creating a new tiny model with good intuitions.

METR 称 AI 进展翻倍，为何收入增长却在放缓？ METR says AI progress doubled, so why is revenue growth slowing?

1. Anthropic 的收入增长在第三季度放缓，股市也随之震荡。如果能力增长如此迅速，为什么会出现这种情况？Paul 表示收入数字存在噪声，并指出这仍然是异常快速的增长。

1. Anthropic's revenue growth slowed in Q3 and the stock market took a tumble. Why is this happening if capabilities growth is so rapid? Paul says revenue numbers are noisy and points out this is still exceptionally fast growth.

1. 数字上涨是为了反映未来数字可能上涨。因此有时数字会下跌。如果数字从不下跌，那么数字现在就应该上涨。

1. Number go up to reflect likely future number going up. Sometimes number will therefore go down. If number never goes down, number should go up now.

2. 我已经多次讨论过 Anthropic 令人失望的第三季度（对几乎任何其他公司或产品而言，这都将是惊人的增长）。第三季度相对平静。Anthropic 的模型在第一、二季度占据主导地位后，于第三季度失去了相对优势，随后 Opus 5.5 再次占据主导。

2. I've discussed Anthropic's disappointing Q3 (that would be amazing growth for almost any other company or product) several times. Q3 is relatively quiet. Anthropic's models lost relative ground in Q3 after being dominant in Q1/Q2, then Opus 5.5 was dominant again.

3. 我预计 Anthropic 在第四季度会再次回升，并且我推测 OpenAI 第三季度表现相对强劲，但他们没有 S-1 文件，所以我们无从得知。

3. I expect things to pick up again for Anthropic in Q4, and I presume OpenAI had a relatively strong Q3 but they don't have an S-1 so we don't know.

我们最好的计划是在飞行中建造飞机 The best plan we have is to build the plane as we fly it

我也称之为：计划就是没有计划。

I also refer to this as: The plan is no plan.

1. 一个希望是‘尝试让[AI]成为好人’。保罗觉得这并不吸引人，并希望我们不会愚蠢到选择这个选项。

1. One hope is to ‘try and make [the AIs] good guys.’ Paul finds this unappealing and hopes we are not so foolish as to go for this option.

1. 这个想法的天真版本，即制造一个表面上的‘好人’，是极其愚蠢的。那种善良不会很好地泛化，也不会带来好结果。

1. The naive version of this, making a superficially ‘good guy,’ is deeply stupid. That kind of goodness will not generalize well and will not result in good outcomes.

2. 这个想法的聪明版本，在所有元层面上定制执行，并辅以其他策略，我认为它不那么愚蠢，而且可能是现有最不愚蠢的选项。

2. The smart version of this, executed bespokely at all meta levels, and supplemented by other strategies, I find to be less deeply stupid, and plausibly the least stupid option available.

2. 保罗提出第二个选项：从科学上理解训练环境的分布如何塑造这些系统的动机。你可以用可解释性工作来补充这一点，可能理解超级智能思维的计算图，保罗说这有大约 5%-10%的成功几率，因此是最有希望的事情，但他预计它会工作几个月然后失效。“如果我们走向超级智能，在通往超级智能的某个时刻，现有方法可能会崩溃。”

2. Paul proposes a second option, understanding scientifically how the distribution of training environments shapes the motivations of these systems. You can supplement this with interpretability work, potentially understanding the computational graph of superintelligent minds, which Paul says has like a 5%-10% chance of working and thus is the most promising thing, but he expects it to work for some number of months and then break. “If we go to superintelligence, at some point along the path to superintelligence, the existing methods are probably going to break down.”

当然，如果你在执行第一个计划，它就会包含这第二个计划。

Certainly if you were doing the first plan, it would include this second plan.

这个计划的朴素版本，本质上是对特定训练环境的分布和内容进行‘卓越运营’，以期引发特定的期望行为，这看起来非常愚蠢，与第一个计划的朴素版本同样愚蠢。

The naive version of this plan, essentially ‘operational excellence’ over the distribution and content of particular training environments in the hopes of causing specific desired behaviors, seems deeply stupid, and similarly deeply stupid to the naive version of the first plan.

我预计它会失败，原因在逻辑上并非完全不同。一旦能力提升并且你处于分布之外，你想要的属性就不会像你希望的那样泛化。新的可供性将导致新的行为。你将在偏好和失败模式上玩打地鼠游戏和古德哈特定律。你将试图消除所有不希望的优化压力，但会失败。你将被智胜。诸如此类，而所有这些都无需理解或与你正在训练的东西结盟。

I would expect it to fail for reasons that are logically not too dissimilar. The properties you want will not generalize the way you hope once capabilities advance and you are out of distribution. New affordances will cause new behaviors. You will be playing whack-a-mole and Goodhart’s Law with preferences and failure modes. You will try to take away all undesired optimization pressures and fail. You will be outsmarted. And so on, all without understanding or allying with the thing you are training.

希望完全理解超级智能思维的计算图似乎是一个渺茫的希望，即使你做到了，也不意味着你能预测它们的行为。

Hoping to fully understand the computational graph of superintelligent minds seems like a dim hope, and even if you pull it off that does not mean you get to predict their behaviors.

在这种时候，你会意识到保罗的观点实际上有多么严峻。他说有 10%成功几率的事情是最有希望的。部分原因是‘这只需要 20 个人，所以是个好赌注’，但也是因为实际上没有真正伟大的计划或赌注。

At times like this you realize how grim Paul’s outlook really is. He’s saying something with a 10% chance of working is the most promising thing. Part of that is ‘this only takes 20 people so it is a good bet’ but also there are no actually great plans or bets.

6. “运行九个月后不可预测地崩溃”可能是好事，如果它能让我们利用中间结果来改进；也可能是最坏的结果，如果它导致我们继续推进，直到它恰好在最需要的时候失败。我们有充分理由预期，可解释性和监控会恰好在最需要的时候失效，因为那正是 AI 能够绕过它的时候，也是 AI 会绕过其他一切的时候。

6. 'Works for nine months then unpredictably breaks' could be good if it allows us to use the interim results to improve, or it could be the worst possible outcome if it causes us to push forward until it fails exactly when you need it most. There are good reasons to expect our interpretability and monitoring to fail exactly when you need it most, because that is when the AI will be able to route around it and also when the AI will route around everything else.

3. 因此，唯一的计划就是没有计划。要“边飞边造飞机”。用你造出的飞机来修复飞机未来的问题。Dwarkesh 说，好吧，在三分之二的世界里这不会很快发生，但这里的“快”是指“一年之内”。

3. Thus the only plan is no plan. To 'build the plane as we fly it.' To use the plane you build to fix the plane's future problems. Dwarkesh says, well, in 2/3rds of worlds this won't happen fast, but 'fast' here is 'within a year.'

4. Paul 最终试图呈现一个（按我的标准）乐观的局面，给我们两次各 50% 的抵御灾难的豁免机会。50% 的机会找到可行方案，以及 50% 的机会，如果我们找不到可行方案，就转而找到一种停止的办法。那将意味着 75% 的机会“事情会没事”。

4. Paul ultimately tries to present an optimistic (by my standards) situation, by giving us two 50/50 saving throws against disaster. 50% to find something that works, and 50% chance that, if we don't find something that works, we instead find a way to stop. That would mean a 75% chance of 'things being okay.'

此时我非常愿意接受 75% 的机会让事情顺利。但我不确定自己是否愿意接受仅仅 75% 的机会撑到 2028 年，而预言线索未被打破、并存在于（或不再存在于）一个完全注定毁灭的世界中。不过，是的，我愿意接受 75% 的机会让事情在无限期内大体上没事。

At this point I would very much take a 75% chance of things turning out okay. I'm not sure I would take a 75% chance of merely making it into 2028 without the thread of prophecy being broken and existing (or no longer existing) in a fully doomed world. But yeah, I would take a 75% chance of things being broadly okay indefinitely.

胜算对我们不利，局势严峻。该开始工作了。

The odds are against us and the situation is grim. Time to get to work.
