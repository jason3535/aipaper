摘要 · Abstract

越来越多的领域表明，深度学习模型可以使用相对较大的批量大小进行训练，而不会牺牲数据效率。然而，这种大规模数据并行的限制似乎因领域而异，从 ImageNet 的数万批量到 Dota 2 游戏智能体的数百万批量。据我们所知，对于这些批量大小限制为何不同，或者如何在新领域中选择正确的批量大小，目前缺乏概念上的理解。在本文中，我们证明了一个简单且易于测量的统计量——梯度噪声尺度——可以预测许多领域和应用中最大有用的批量大小，包括多个监督学习数据集（MNIST、SVHN、CIFAR-10、ImageNet、Billion Word）、强化学习领域（Atari 和 Dota），甚至生成模型训练（SVHN 上的自编码器）。我们发现，随着训练过程中损失的降低，噪声尺度会增加，并且模型大小主要通过改进模型性能来影响噪声尺度。我们的经验驱动理论还描述了计算效率和时间效率之间的权衡，并提供了自适应批量大小训练益处的粗略模型。

In an increasing number of domains it has been demonstrated that deep learning models can be trained using relatively large batch sizes without sacrificing data efficiency. However the limits of this massive data parallelism seem to differ from domain to domain, ranging from batches of tens of thousands in ImageNet to batches of millions in RL agents that play the game Dota 2. To our knowledge there is limited conceptual understanding of why these limits to batch size differ or how we might choose the correct batch size in a new domain. In this paper, we demonstrate that a simple and easy-to-measure statistic called the gradient noise scale predicts the largest useful batch size across many domains and applications, including a number of supervised learning datasets (MNIST, SVHN, CIFAR-10, ImageNet, Billion Word), reinforcement learning domains (Atari and Dota), and even generative model training (autoencoders on SVHN). We find that the noise scale increases as the loss decreases over a training run and depends on the model size primarily through improved model performance. Our empirically-motivated theory also describes the tradeoff between compute-efficiency and time-efficiency, and provides a rough model of the benefits of adaptive batch-size training.

1 引言 1 Introduction

过去几年，用于训练深度学习模型的计算量迅速增加[AH18]。这种增长的一个主要推动因素和限制因素是并行性——即训练过程可以在多大程度上有效地分布在多个设备上。无论总计算量有多少，如果模型训练无法充分并行化，那么它可能需要过多的串行时间，从而在实践中不可行。

The last few years have seen a rapid increase in the amount of computation used to train deep learning models [AH18]. A major enabler as well as a limiting factor in this growth has been parallelism – the extent to which a training process can be usefully spread across multiple devices. Regardless of how much total computation is available, if model training cannot be sufficiently parallelized, then it may take too much serial time and therefore may be practically infeasible.

深度学习中一个非常常见的并行化来源是数据并行，它涉及将一批数据分割到多个设备上，然后聚合并应用得到的梯度。数据并行需要设备之间的快速通信，但也要求大批量在加速学习方面具有算法上的有效性。最近，许多论文在特定数据集或任务上经验性地表明，大批量大小可以在训练中实现几乎线性的加速，而不会显著损害样本效率或泛化能力。例如，8000[GDG+17]、16000[SKYL17]、32000[YGG17、YZH+17、ASF17]甚至 64000[JSH+18]个样本的批量大小已被有效用于训练 ImageNet，而数千的批量大小已用于语言模型和生成模型[OEGA18、PKYC18、BDS18]。这种现象不仅限于监督学习：在强化学习中，超过一百万个时间步的批量大小（并行运行数万个环境）已被用于 Dota 游戏智能体[BCD+18]，甚至在简单的 Atari 环境中，数千个时间步的批量大小也被证明是有效的[AAG+18、HQB+18、SA18]。这些发现使得大量数据和计算能够在合理的时间内高效地注入模型，从而在监督学习、强化学习和其他领域实现更强大的模型。

A very common source of parallelism in deep learning has been data parallelism, which involves splitting a batch of data across multiple devices and then aggregating and applying the resulting gradients. Data parallelism requires fast communication between devices, but also requires that large batches are algorithmically effective in accelerating learning. Recently, a number of papers have shown empirically that on specific datasets or tasks, large batch sizes can achieve almost linear speed-ups in training without substantially harming sample efficiency or generalization. For example, batch sizes of 8 thousand [GDG+17], 16 thousand [SKYL17], 32 thousand [YGG17, YZH+17, ASF17], and even 64 thousand [JSH+18] examples have been effectively employed to train ImageNet, and batch sizes of thousands have been effective for language models and generative models [OEGA18, PKYC18, BDS18]. This phenomenon is not confined to supervised learning: in reinforcement learning, batch sizes of over a million timesteps (with tens of thousands of environments running in parallel) have been used in a Dota-playing agent [BCD+18], and even in simple Atari environments batch sizes of several thousand timesteps have proved effective [AAG+18, HQB+18, SA18]. These discoveries have allowed massive amounts of data and computation to be productively poured into models in a reasonable amount of time, enabling more powerful models in supervised learning, RL, and other domains.

然而，对于给定的数据集和模型，几乎没有指导我们预测可以实际使用多大的批量大小，为什么该数字取特定值，或者如果我们使用不同的数据集或模型，它会如何变化。例如，为什么在训练 Dota 智能体时我们可以使用超过一百万的批量大小，而在训练图像识别模型时只能使用数千或数万？在实践中，研究人员倾向于简单地通过实验来尝试批量大小并观察效果，但这有一个缺点：大批量大小通常需要仔细调整才能有效（例如，它们可能需要预热期或不寻常的学习率调度），因此使用大批量大小的可能性可能长期未被发现。例如，Atari 和 ImageNet 任务在传统上多年来都使用比现在已知可能更小的批量大小。提前知道我们期望有效的批量大小将在训练新模型时具有重要的实际优势。

However, for a given dataset and model, there is little to guide us in predicting how large a batch size we can feasibly use, why that number takes a particular value, or how we would expect it to differ if we used a different dataset or model. For example, why can we apparently use a batch size of over a million when training a Dota agent, but only thousands or tens of thousands when training an image recognition model? In practice researchers tend to simply experiment with batch sizes and see what works, but a downside of this is that large batch sizes often require careful tuning to be effective (for example, they may require a warmup period or an unusual learning rate schedule), so the fact that it is possible to use a large batch size can remain undiscovered for a long time. For example, both the Atari and ImageNet tasks were for several years conventionally run with a substantially smaller batch size than is now understood to be possible. Knowing ahead of time what batch size we expect to be effective would be a significant practical advantage in training new models.

在概念方面，我们推导了一个框架，该框架在基本假设下预测，训练应该几乎线性地并行化，直到批量大小等于噪声尺度，之后应该平滑但相对快速地过渡到进一步并行化收益最小的区域。此外，我们预计噪声尺度在训练过程中会随着模型变得更准确而增加，并且对于更复杂的任务应该更大，但不应与模型大小本身有强依赖关系。我们还分析了在训练过程中根据噪声尺度动态调整批量大小所预期的效率提升。最后，我们预测，在其他条件相同的情况下，由于环境的随机性和信用分配问题引入的额外方差，复杂强化学习任务中的噪声尺度往往更大。

On the conceptual side, we derive a framework which predicts, under some basic assumptions, that training should parallelize almost linearly up to a batch size equal to the noise scale, after which there should be a smooth but relatively rapid switch to a regime where further parallelism provides minimal benefits. Additionally, we expect that the noise scale should increase during training as models get more accurate, and should be larger for more complex tasks, but should not have a strong dependence on model size per se. We also provide an analysis of the efficiency gains to be expected from dynamically adjusting the batch size according to noise scale during training. Finally, we predict that, all else equal, the noise scale will tend to be larger in complex RL tasks due to the stochasticity of the environment and the additional variance introduced by the credit assignment problem.

On the empirical side, we verify these predictions across 8 tasks in supervised learning, RL, and generative models, including ImageNet, CIFAR-10, SVHN, MNIST, BillionWord, Atari, OpenAI’s Dota agent [BCD+18], and a variational autoencoder for images. 2.1 直观图景 2.1 Intuitive Picture

在详细讨论梯度噪声尺度和批量大小之前，先呈现直观图景是有用的。假设我们有一个希望通过随机梯度下降（SGD）优化的函数。存在一个潜在的真实优化景观，对应于整个数据集上的损失（或者更抽象地说，对应于其抽取分布的损失）。当我们使用有限批量大小执行 SGD 更新时，我们是在近似这个真实损失的梯度。我们应该如何决定使用多大的批量大小？

Before working through the details of the gradient noise scale and the batch size, it is useful to present the intuitive picture. Suppose we have a function we wish to optimize via stochastic gradient descent (SGD). There is some underlying true optimization landscape, corresponding to the loss over the entire dataset (or, more abstractly, the loss over the distribution it is drawn from). When we perform an SGD update with a finite batch size, we’re approximating the gradient to this true loss. How should we decide what batch size to use?

当批量大小非常小时，近似将具有非常高的方差，得到的梯度更新主要是噪声。连续应用这些 SGD 更新将平均掉方差并推动我们整体朝着正确的方向前进，但参数的单个更新不会非常有帮助，我们几乎可以通过并行聚合这些更新并一次性应用它们（即使用更大的批量大小）来获得相同效果。关于大批量和小批量训练的说明性比较，见图 2。

When the batch size is very small, the approximation will have very high variance, and the resulting gradient update will be mostly noise. Applying a bunch of these SGD updates successively will average out the variance and push us overall in the right direction, but the individual updates to the parameters won’t be very helpful, and we could have done almost as well by aggregating these updates in parallel and applying them all at once (in other words, by using a larger batch size). For an illustrative comparison between large and small batch training, see Figure 2.

相比之下，当批量大小非常大时，批量梯度将几乎完全匹配真实梯度，相应地，两个随机采样的批次将具有几乎相同的梯度。因此，将批量大小加倍几乎不会改善更新——我们将使用两倍的计算量而收益甚微。

By contrast, when the batch size is very large, the batch gradient will almost exactly match the true gradient, and correspondingly two randomly sampled batches will have almost the same gradient. As a result, doubling the batch size will barely improve the update – we will use twice as much computation for little gain.

直观上，从第一个区域（增加批量大小导致几乎完全线性的加速）到第二个区域（增加批量大小主要浪费计算）的过渡应该大致发生在梯度的噪声和信号平衡的地方——即梯度的方差与梯度本身处于同一尺度。形式化这一启发式观察引出了噪声尺度。

Intuitively, the transition between the first regime (where increasing the batch size leads to almost perfectly linear speedups) and the second regime (where increasing the batch size mostly wastes computation) should occur roughly where the noise and signal of the gradient are balanced – where the variance of the gradient is at the same scale as the gradient itself222Note that these considerations are completely agnostic about the size of the dataset itself.. Formalizing this heuristic observation leads to the noise scale.

这种情况在图 1 中以图形方式展示。对于给定的模型，我们希望以尽可能少的挂钟时间（x 轴）进行训练，同时使用尽可能少的总计算量（y 轴）——这是并行化的通常目标。改变批量大小使我们沿着两者之间的权衡曲线移动。最初，我们可以增加批量大小而不会大幅增加总计算量，然后有一个“转折点”，两者之间存在实质性权衡，最后当批量大小很大时，我们无法在训练时间上取得进一步进展。在下面的概念和实验结果中，我们形式化了这些概念，并表明曲线弯曲处（从而近似最大的有效批量大小）实际上大致由噪声尺度设定。

The situation is shown pictorially in Figure 1. For a given model, we’d like to train it in as little wall time as possible (x-axis) while also using as little total computation as possible (y-axis) – this is the usual goal of parallelization. Changing the batch size moves us along a tradeoff curve between the two. Initially, we can increase the batch size without much increase in total computation, then there is a “turning point” where there is a substantive tradeoff between the two, and finally when the batch size is large we cannot make further gains in training time. In the conceptual and experimental results below, we formalize these concepts and show that the bend in the curve (and thus the approximate largest effective batch size) is in fact set roughly by the noise scale.

2.2 梯度、批次与梯度噪声尺度 2.2 Gradients, Batches, and the Gradient Noise Scale

2.4 假设与注意事项 2.4 Assumptions and Caveats

前面几节的数学论证依赖于几个假设和注意事项，有必要将它们列在一起，以帮助阐明我们预期方程 2.8 和 2.9 中的量在哪些方面以及为何与训练相关：

The mathematical argument in the previous sections depends on several assumptions and caveats, and it is useful to list these all in one place, in order to help clarify where and why we might expect the quantities in equations 2.8 and 2.9 to be relevant to training:

短视偏差：第 2.2 节的描述是严格局部的——它告诉我们如何在下一步梯度中最好地改进损失。贪婪地选择最佳局部改进通常不是全局优化损失的最佳方式（例如参见[WRLG18]）。例如，在存在不良局部最小值或条件不良的景观中，贪婪优化可能表现不佳。临界批次大小会因噪声有益的程度而减小。

Short-horizon bias: The picture in Section 2.2 is a strictly local picture – it tells us how to best improve the loss on the next gradient step. Greedily choosing the best local improvement is generally not the best way to globally optimize the loss (see e.g. [WRLG18]). For example, greedy optimization might perform poorly in the presence of bad local minima or when the landscape is ill-conditioned. The critical batch size would then be reduced by the extent to which noise is beneficial.

条件不良：在条件不良的优化问题中，参数值通常沿大曲率方向振荡，而不是以可预测的方式减小（例如参见[Goh17]和附录 E.1）。这意味着方程 2.7 不会完美反映每步的优化进展。然而，我们将看到，通过得到的权衡方程 2.11，它仍然能准确预测不同批次大小下训练的相对速度。

Poor conditioning: In poorly conditioned optimization problems, parameter values often oscillate along the large-curvature directions rather than decreasing in a predictable way (see e.g. [Goh17] and Appendix E.1). This means that Equation 2.7 will not perfectly reflect the amount of optimization progress made per step. Nevertheless, we will see that it still accurately predicts the relative speed of training at different batch sizes via the resulting tradeoff Equation 2.11.

简化噪声尺度：如第 2.2 节所述，当我们使用简化噪声尺度（公式 2.9）而非精确噪声尺度（公式 2.8）时，若 Hessian 矩阵条件数不佳，该数值可能不准确。梯度的不同分量可能具有非常不同的噪声尺度。

Simplified noise scale: As noted in Section 2.2, whenever we use the simplified noise scale (Equation 2.9) rather than the exact noise scale (Equation 2.8), this number may be inaccurate to the extent that the Hessian is not well-conditioned. Different components of the gradient can have very different noise scales.

学习率调优：第 2.2 节的论证假设我们采用最优步长并最大化损失的期望改进（公式 2.6）。在实践中，学习率不太可能完美调优，因此损失的实际改进（以及训练随批量大小的缩放）可能不完全符合公式 2.7。然而，通过尝试选择最佳学习率调度（或简单地进行网格搜索），我们可以减少这一误差来源。此外，噪声尺度通过训练的“温度”强烈依赖于学习率，但只要学习率接近最优，这一误差来源就很小。我们在附录 C 中提供了关于这种依赖关系的更详细讨论。

Learning rate tuning: The arguments in Section 2.2 assume that we take the optimal step size and maximize the expected improvement in loss, Equation 2.6. In practice learning rates are unlikely to be perfectly tuned, so that the actual improvement in loss (and thus the scaling of training with batch size) may not perfectly reflect Equation 2.7. However, by trying to choose the best learning rate schedules (or by simply doing a grid search) we can reduce this source of error. In addition, the noise scale depends strongly on the learning rate via a ‘temperature’ of training, though this source of error is small as long as the learning rate is reasonably close to optimal. We provide a more detailed discussion of this dependence in Appendix C.

二次近似：公式 2.4 中的泰勒展开仅到二阶，因此如果三阶项在梯度样本分布或优化景观中很重要，则可能导致偏离我们的概念模型，特别是偏离公式 2.7。直观上，由于参数更新是局部的且通常很小，我们怀疑前两个误差来源比第三个更重要。

Quadratic approximation: The Taylor expansion in Equation 2.4 is only to second order, so if third order terms are important, in either the distribution of gradient samples or the optimization landscape, then this may introduce deviations from our conceptual model, and in particular deviations from Equation 2.7. Intuitively, since parameter updates are local and often quite small we suspect that the previous two sources of error will be more important than this third one.

泛化：第 2.2 节的描述未涉及泛化——它严格关于作为数学函数的训练损失优化。一些论文报告了“泛化差距”，即大批量导致良好的训练损失但造成测试损失下降，这似乎与过拟合无关[KMN+16, HHS17]。第 2.2 节的论证不排除这种可能性，但最近的工作[SLA+18]发现，当超参数适当调优时，没有泛化差距的证据。

Generalization: The picture in Section 2.2 says nothing about generalization – it is strictly about optimizing the training loss as a mathematical function. Some papers have reported a “generalization gap” in which large batch sizes lead to good training loss but cause a degradation in test loss, apparently unrelated to overfitting [KMN+16, HHS17]. The arguments in Section 2.2 don’t exclude this possibility, but recent work [SLA+18] has found no evidence of a generalization gap when hyperparameters are properly tuned.

尽管我们的概念模型存在这些潜在问题，我们将在第 3 节展示，噪声尺度总体上是一个良好的经验预测指标，用于预测临界批量大小。此外，我们将看到大多数训练运行与公式 2.11 拟合得非常好。

Despite these potential issues in our conceptual model, we’ll show in Section 3 that the noise scale is overall a good empirical predictor of the critical batch size. Furthermore, we will see that most training runs fit Equation 2.11 remarkably well.

Since |G|𝐺|G| decreases as we approach the minimum of a smooth loss, we would expect ℬℬ\mathcal{B} to increase during neural network training.

对模型大小的弱依赖性：神经网络中模型参数的数量在噪声尺度中抵消，因此我们不预期ℬ对模型大小（在固定损失下）表现出强依赖性。如上所述，实现更好损失的模型往往具有更高的噪声尺度，而更大的模型通常实现更好的损失，因此在实践中我们确实预期更大的模型具有更高的噪声尺度，但仅通过实现更好损失的机制。

Weak dependence on model size: The number of model parameters in the neural network cancels in the noise scale, so we do not expect ℬℬ\mathcal{B} to exhibit a strong dependence on model size (at fixed loss). As discussed above, models that achieve better loss will tend to have a higher noise scale, and larger models often achieve better loss, so in practice we do expect larger models to have higher noise scale, but only through the mechanism of achieving better loss.

学习率调优：如果学习率太小，由于附录 C 中描述的“温度”依赖性，噪声尺度将被人为放大。为了获得噪声尺度的有用测量，学习率需要适合参数空间中的当前点。

Learning rate tuning: The noise scale will be artificially inflated if the learning rate is too small, due to the ‘temperature’ dependence described in Appendix C. To get a useful measurement of the noise scale, the learning rate needs to be appropriate to the current point in parameter space.

第一点和最后一点可以在玩具模型中解析地展示（见附录 C），但我们不期望理论分析能提供超出上述直觉的大量见解。相反，我们将专注于通过实验确认这些预期。

The first and last points can be exhibited analytically in toy models (see Appendix C), but we do not expect theoretical analyses to provide a great deal of insight beyond the intuitions above. Instead, we will focus on confirming these expectations empirically.

On the small datasets MNIST, CIFAR10, and SVHN, overfitting makes measurement of test error more difficult, but we do measure the test error behavior in Appendix E.3, and both the Pareto fronts and critical batch size generally do not change much.

The fact that the noise scale is consistent between well-tuned training runs suggests that the corresponding optimization trajectories are similar in some sense. In Appendix C we investigate this idea further and relate the noise scale to a characteristic ‘temperature’ of the training process.

噪声尺度的定义并不显式依赖于模型中的参数数量。我们推测，在损失值固定的情况下，它们将大致独立于模型大小。

The definitions of the noise scales do not have any manifest dependence on the number of parameters in a model. We have conjectured that they will be roughly independent of model size at fixed values of the loss.

LSTM 语言模型提供了一个自然的测试案例，因为 LSTM 的大小可以缩放而不改变模型架构。如图 8 所示，在损失值固定的情况下，简单噪声尺度似乎大致独立于模型大小。然而，由于较大的模型性能更高且损失更低，它们最终会达到比小模型更大的噪声尺度。我们对于噪声尺度如何随模型架构变化没有具体的假设，但最近沿着这些方向获得了有趣的结果[SLA+18]。

LSTM language models provide a natural test case, since LSTM sizes can be scaled up without qualitatively altering model architecture. As shown in Figure 8, the simple noise scale appears to be roughly independent of model size at a fixed value of the loss. However, due to their higher performance and lower achieved loss, larger models eventually reach larger noise scales than smaller models. We do not have specific hypotheses for how the noise scale should vary with model architecture, but interesting results along these lines were recently obtained [SLA+18].

4 相关工作 4 Related Work

大量先前的工作研究了大批量训练，探讨了噪声尺度的各种版本，探索了自适应批次大小和学习率调度，并证明了大批量训练在特定数据集上的有效性。我们试图在下面总结这些工作。

A great deal of prior work has studied large-batch training, investigated versions of the noise scale, explored adaptive batch size and learning rate schedules, and demonstrated that large batch training can be effective on specific datasets. We attempt to summarize this work below.

最近的论文从经验上探索了大批量训练的极限，特别是对于 ImageNet [GDG+17, YZH+17, JSH+18]，在某些情况下使用了逐层自适应学习率[YGG17]。更近的工作表明，大批量训练也可以应用于强化学习[AAG+18, BCD+18, SA18, HQB+18]。使用二阶优化方法[BGM17]可能会进一步提高数据并行的效用。一项与本文同时进行的非常优秀的实证研究[SLA+18]对大批量训练及其泛化潜在问题进行了全面回顾。[GVY+18]也系统地研究了大批量训练，尽管它没有为每个批次大小单独调整学习率。

Recent papers have probed the limits of large batch training empirically, especially for ImageNet [GDG+17, YZH+17, JSH+18], in some cases using layer-wise adaptive learning-rates [YGG17]. More recent work has demonstrated that large batch training can also be applied to RL [AAG+18, BCD+18, SA18, HQB+18]. The use of second order optimization methods [BGM17] might increase the utility of data parallelism even further. A thorough review of large batch training and potential issues with generalization was provided in a very nice recent empirical study [SLA+18] done in parallel with this work. [GVY+18] also systematically studied large-batch training, though it did not tune the learning rate separately for each batch size.

其他近期工作探讨了梯度噪声对优化速度和批次大小选择的影响。[SZL13]将梯度噪声与局部最优步长联系起来，以确定自适应学习率。[MHB17]推导了 SGD 的采样分布，激发了我们定义“温度”。[SL17]将这个温度与临界批次大小联系起来，尽管他们预测了数据集大小的依赖性，但我们并未观察到。[SZT17]识别了训练的信号主导和噪声主导阶段。[SKYL17]表明，衰减学习率和增加批次大小具有相同的效果，这是由 SGD 训练温度驱动的。（[DNG17]也建议同时增加学习率和批次大小，但动机不同。）[IES+18]从经验上研究了梯度噪声在强化学习中的作用。

Other recent work has explored the impact of gradient noise on optimization speed and batch size selection. [SZL13] connected gradient noise and the locally optimal step size to identify an adaptive learning rate. [MHB17] derived a sampling distribution for SGD, motivating our definition of ‘temperature’. [SL17] connected this temperature to the critical batch size, though they predict a dependence on dataset size which we do not observe. [SZT17] identified a signal-dominated and noise-dominated phase of training. [SKYL17] showed that decaying the learning rate and increasing the batch size have the same effect, motivated by the SGD training temperature. ([DNG17] also suggested increasing learning rate and batch size together, but with different motivation.) [IES+18] empirically investigated the role of gradient noise in reinforcement learning.

梯度噪声尺度在早期工作中也被研究以帮助选择批次大小。噪声尺度本身隐式地用于基本的样本量选择统计技术（例如，参见[Wik, NIS]）。[BCNW12]隐式地使用梯度噪声尺度进行批次大小选择的理论分析。[BCN16, DYJG16, BRH16]在神经网络优化背景下提出了基于梯度噪声尺度的自适应采样方法。[YPL+17]针对一类特定函数分析了梯度噪声尺度，并将其与临界批次大小联系起来，尽管它预测了学习速度随批次大小的急剧变化，而不是我们观察到的平滑变化。[CWZ+18]从理论上分析了浅层或线性网络中梯度噪声尺度对网络宽度的依赖性，尽管他们在神经网络上发现了不一致的经验结果。[MBB17]找到了一个类似于我们的批次大小优化加速公式，尽管他们的临界批次大小取决于损失的平滑性参数，而不是直接取决于梯度噪声。

The gradient noise scale in particular has also been studied in earlier work to aid in batch size selection. The noise scale itself is used implicitly in basic statistical techniques for sample size selection (see e.g. [Wik, NIS]). [BCNW12] implicitly uses the gradient noise scale for a theoretical analysis of batch size selection. [BCN16, DYJG16, BRH16] propose adaptive sampling methods based on the gradient noise scale in the context of neural network optimization. [YPL+17] analyzed the gradient noise scale for a particular class of functions and related it to the critical batch size, though it predicts a sharp change in learning speed with batch size rather than the smooth change we observe. [CWZ+18] theoretically analyzed the dependence of the gradient noise scale on network width for shallow or linear networks, though they find inconsistent empirical results on neural networks. [MBB17] found a formula for the optimization speedup in terms of batch size resembling ours, though their critical batch size depends on smoothness parameters of the loss rather than directly gradient noise.

已有多种工作研究神经网络损失景观，并利用它得出关于最优训练的结论。损失景观的局部性质不一定是整体最优训练的良好指南[WRLG18]。在训练开始和结束之间插值时，损失往往相当平滑[GVS14]。但噪声可能在训练早期有用[NVL+15, YPL+17]，也许是因为它导致泛化更好的最小值[KMN+16]。

There has been a variety of work studying the Neural Network loss landscape and using it to draw conclusions about optimal training. Local properties of the loss landscape are not necessarily a good guide to overall optimal training [WRLG18]. The loss tends to be fairly smooth when interpolating between the start and end of training [GVS14]. But noise may be useful early in training [NVL+15, YPL+17], perhaps because it leads to minima that generalize better [KMN+16].

我们工作的一个宏观动机是更好地理解学习随计算和数据资源的缩放；这个问题在[HNA+17]中从模型大小缩放的角度得到了解决。

A big-picture motivation for our work was to better understand the scaling of learning with computational and data resources; this question was addressed from the perspective of scaling the model size in [HNA+17].

我们的关键贡献包括：通过一个简单模型将梯度噪声尺度与优化速度联系起来，以及系统地测量各种任务的临界批次大小和噪声尺度。我们还阐明了训练温度在 SGD 中的作用，并提出了一个最优批次大小调度。

Our key contributions include connecting the gradient noise scale to the speed of optimization with a simple model, as well as systematically measuring the critical batch size and noise scale for a variety of tasks. We also clarify the role of the training temperature in SGD and propose an optimal batch size schedule.

What does the validity of this theory mean, and in what way is it useful? At the level of a given task, it allows us to use the noise scale from a single run (even an only partially complete run with much smaller batch size, though see caveats about learning rate tuning in the appendix) to estimate the largest useful batch size, and thus reduces the extensive hyperparameter searches that are necessary to find this batch size by trial and error. It also tells us to expect that larger batch sizes will show diminishing returns in a predictable way that has the same form regardless of the task.

Due to its crucial role in data-parallelism, we have focused on the batch size B𝐵B, presuming that the learning rate or effective ‘temperature’ will be optimized after B𝐵B has been chosen. 在我们的实验中，我们测量并报告了在具有良好优化学习率的单次运行中训练期间的噪声尺度。请注意，虽然噪声尺度测量在不同批量大小的运行之间是一致的，但在不同学习率下并不一致（见附录 C）。因此，为了获得有意义的噪声尺度测量，使用具有良好调整学习率的运行很重要。

In our experiments we measure and report the noise scale during training for a single run with a well-optimized learning rate. Note that while the noise scale measurement is consistent between runs at different batch sizes, it is not consistent at different learning rates (see Appendix C). So, it is important to use a run with a well-tuned learning rate in order to get a meaningful noise scale measurement.

E.1 确定性训练表现不佳 E.1 Deterministic Training Performs Poorly

从第 2 节关于噪声尺度的讨论中，我们预期在大批量 B≫ℬ_noise 时，我们可以获得梯度的非常好的估计。这将建议一种最小随机性的训练方法，即在每一步对真实梯度方向上的真实损失进行线搜索，并相应更新参数。

From the discussion of the noise scale in Section 2, we expect that at large batch size B≫ℬnoisemuch-greater-than𝐵subscriptℬnoiseB\gg\mathcal{B}_{\rm noise} we can obtain a very good estimate of the gradient. This would then suggest a minimally stochastic approach to training, where at each step one performs a line search of the true loss in the direction of the true gradient, and updates parameters accordingly.

这种近乎确定性的“贪婪下降”方法在实践中表现不佳[WRLG18]。虽然它的前几步往往显著降低损失，但随后的步长迅速减小，仅提供最小的进一步训练进展。事实上，当使用固定学习率训练时，我们观察到训练往往趋向于一个状态，其中最优步长（通过在参数更新方向上的线搜索确定）几乎正好是实际更新幅度的一半。我们发现无论学习率如何（扫描几个数量级），这一现象都会发生，并且似乎普遍存在于各种不同的模型中，如图 17 和 18 所示。对这些结果的一个自然解释是，大 Hessian 方向主导了更新[GARD18]，因此训练涉及快速振荡，如图 18 所示（参见[Goh17]对这些振荡的直观描述）。因此，线搜索似乎对确定完整训练运行的最优步长没有帮助。

This nearly deterministic ‘greedient descent’ method performs poorly in practice [WRLG18]. While its first few steps tend to decrease the loss significantly, subsequent step sizes decrease rapidly and provide minimal further training progress. In fact, when training with a fixed learning rate, we have observed that training often tends towards a regime where the optimal step size (determined by a line search in the direction of the parameter update) is almost exactly half of the actual update magnitude. We have found that this phenomenon occurs regardless of the learning rate (scanning over several orders of magnitude), and seems common to a variety of different models, as shown in Figures 17 and 18. A natural interpretation of these results is that large Hessian directions are dominating the update [GARD18], so that training involves rapid oscillations, as seen in Figure 18 (see [Goh17] for an intuitive picture of these oscillations). Because of this, line searches do not appear to be useful in determining the optimal step size for a full training run.
