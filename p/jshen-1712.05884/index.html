Natural TTS Synthesis by Conditioning WaveNet on Mel Spectrogram Predictions

摘要 · Abstract

本文描述了 Tacotron 2，一种直接从文本进行语音合成的神经网络架构。该系统由一个循环序列到序列特征预测网络组成，该网络将字符嵌入映射到梅尔频谱，随后是一个改进的 WaveNet 模型作为声码器，从这些频谱合成时域波形。我们的模型达到了 4.53 的平均意见得分（MOS），与专业录制语音的 4.58 MOS 相当。为了验证我们的设计选择，我们进行了系统关键组件的消融研究，并评估了使用梅尔频谱作为 WaveNet 输入而非语言、时长和 F0 特征的影响。我们进一步证明，使用紧凑的声学中间表示可以显著简化 WaveNet 架构。

This paper describes Tacotron 2, a neural network architecture for speech synthesis directly from text. The system is composed of a recurrent sequence-to-sequence feature prediction network that maps character embeddings to mel-scale spectrograms, followed by a modified WaveNet model acting as a vocoder to synthesize timedomain waveforms from those spectrograms. Our model achieves a mean opinion score (MOS) of $4.53$ comparable to a MOS of $4.58$ for professionally recorded speech. To validate our design choices, we present ablation studies of key components of our system and evaluate the impact of using mel spectrograms as the input to WaveNet instead of linguistic, duration, and $F_0$ features. We further demonstrate that using a compact acoustic intermediate representation enables significant simplification of the WaveNet architecture.

局限 · Limitations

  • 系统偶尔产生发音错误，尤其对于域外文本如人名。
    The system occasionally produces mispronunciations, especially for out-of-domain text like names.
  • 韵律建模仍不完美，某些情况下出现不自然的强调或音高。
    Prosody modeling remains imperfect, with unnatural emphasis or pitch in some cases.
  • 端点预测在极长输入句子上失败。
    End-point prediction fails on very long input sentences.
  • 训练需要匹配的数据分布；在域外文本上性能下降。
    Training requires matched data distribution; performance degrades on out-of-domain text.
  • 两个组件分开训练，使用真实特征训练 WaveNet 在推理使用预测特征时质量下降。
    The two components are trained separately, and using ground truth features for WaveNet training degrades quality when inference uses predicted features.

摘要 Abstract

本文描述了 Tacotron 2，一种直接从文本进行语音合成的神经网络架构。该系统由一个循环序列到序列特征预测网络组成，该网络将字符嵌入映射到梅尔尺度语谱图，随后是一个改进的 WaveNet 模型作为声码器，从这些语谱图合成时域波形。我们的模型达到了 4.53 的平均意见得分（MOS），与专业录制语音的 4.58 MOS 相当。为了验证我们的设计选择，我们展示了系统关键组件的消融研究，并评估了使用梅尔语谱图作为 WaveNet 条件输入（而非语言、时长和 F0 特征）的影响。我们进一步表明，使用这种紧凑的声学中间表示可以显著减小 WaveNet 架构的规模。

This paper describes Tacotron 2, a neural network architecture for speech synthesis directly from text. The system is composed of a recurrent sequence-to-sequence feature prediction network that maps character embeddings to mel-scale spectrograms, followed by a modified WaveNet model acting as a vocoder to synthesize time-domain waveforms from those spectrograms. 1 引言 1 Introduction

从文本生成自然语音（文本到语音合成，TTS）尽管经过数十年的研究，仍然是一项具有挑战性的任务[1]。随着时间的推移，不同的技术主导了该领域。基于单元选择的拼接合成——将预录制波形的小单元拼接在一起的过程[2, 3]——多年来一直是主流技术。统计参数语音合成[4, 5, 6, 7]直接生成平滑的语音特征轨迹，由声码器合成，解决了拼接合成中边界伪影的许多问题。然而，与人类语音相比，这些系统生成的音频通常听起来沉闷且不自然。

Generating natural speech from text (text-to-speech synthesis, TTS) remains a challenging task despite decades of investigation [1]. Over time, different techniques have dominated the field. Concatenative synthesis with unit selection, the process of stitching small units of pre-recorded waveforms together [2, 3] was the state-of-the-art for many years. Statistical parametric speech synthesis [4, 5, 6, 7], which directly generates smooth trajectories of speech features to be synthesized by a vocoder, followed, solving many of the issues that concatenative synthesis had with boundary artifacts. However, the audio produced by these systems often sounds muffled and unnatural compared to human speech.

WaveNet [8]是一种时域波形的生成模型，其生成的音频质量开始与真实人类语音相媲美，并已用于一些完整的 TTS 系统[9, 10, 11]。然而，WaveNet 的输入（语言特征、预测的对数基频（F0）和音素时长）需要大量的领域专业知识来生成，涉及复杂的文本分析系统以及健壮的词典（发音指南）。

WaveNet [8], a generative model of time domain waveforms, produces audio quality that begins to rival that of real human speech and is already used in some complete TTS systems [9, 10, 11]. The inputs to WaveNet (linguistic features, predicted log fundamental frequency (F0subscript𝐹0F_{0}), and phoneme durations), however, require significant domain expertise to produce, involving elaborate text-analysis systems as well as a robust lexicon (pronunciation guide).

Tacotron [12]是一种序列到序列架构[13]，用于从字符序列生成幅度语谱图，通过用单个从数据中训练的神经网络替代这些语言和声学特征的生成，简化了传统的语音合成流程。为了对生成的幅度语谱图进行声码化，Tacotron 使用 Griffin-Lim 算法[14]进行相位估计，随后进行逆短时傅里叶变换。正如作者所指出的，这仅仅是未来神经声码器方法的占位符，因为 Griffin-Lim 会产生特征性伪影，并且音频质量低于 WaveNet 等方法。

Tacotron [12], a sequence-to-sequence architecture [13] for producing magnitude spectrograms from a sequence of characters, simplifies the traditional speech synthesis pipeline by replacing the production of these linguistic and acoustic features with a single neural network trained from data alone. To vocode the resulting magnitude spectrograms, Tacotron uses the Griffin-Lim algorithm [14] for phase estimation, followed by an inverse short-time Fourier transform. As the authors note, this was simply a placeholder for future neural vocoder approaches, as Griffin-Lim produces characteristic artifacts and lower audio quality than approaches like WaveNet.

在本文中，我们描述了一种统一的、完全神经网络的语音合成方法，它结合了先前方法的最佳部分：一个生成梅尔语谱图的序列到序列 Tacotron 风格模型[12]，随后是一个改进的 WaveNet 声码器[10, 15]。我们的模型直接在归一化的字符序列和对应的语音波形上训练，学习合成难以与真实人类语音区分的自然语音。

In this paper, we describe a unified, entirely neural approach to speech synthesis that combines the best of the previous approaches: a sequence-to-sequence Tacotron-style model [12] that generates mel spectrograms, followed by a modified WaveNet vocoder [10, 15]. Trained directly on normalized character sequences and corresponding speech waveforms, our model learns to synthesize natural sounding speech that is difficult to distinguish from real human speech.

Deep Voice 3 [11]描述了一种类似的方法。然而，与我们的系统不同，其自然度尚未被证明能与人类语音相媲美。Char2Wav [16]描述了另一种类似的端到端 TTS 方法，使用神经声码器。然而，它们使用不同的中间表示（传统声码器特征），并且模型架构差异显著。

Deep Voice 3 [11] describes a similar approach. However, unlike our system, its naturalness has not been shown to rival that of human speech. Char2Wav [16] describes yet another similar approach to end-to-end TTS using a neural vocoder. However, they use different intermediate representations (traditional vocoder features) and their model architecture differs significantly.

2 模型架构 2 Model Architecture

我们提出的系统由两个组件组成，如图 1 所示：（1）一个带有注意力的循环序列到序列特征预测网络，从输入字符序列预测梅尔语谱图帧序列；（2）一个改进版本的 WaveNet，以预测的梅尔语谱图帧为条件生成时域波形样本。

Our proposed system consists of two components, shown in Figure 1: (1) a recurrent sequence-to-sequence feature prediction network with attention which predicts a sequence of mel spectrogram frames from an input character sequence, and (2) a modified version of WaveNet which generates time-domain waveform samples conditioned on the predicted mel spectrogram frames.

2.1 中间特征表示 2.1 Intermediate Feature Representation

在这项工作中，我们选择了一种低级声学表示：梅尔频率语谱图，来桥接两个组件。使用易于从时域波形计算的表示使我们能够分别训练两个组件。这种表示也比波形样本更平滑，并且由于它对每帧内的相位不变，因此更容易使用平方误差损失进行训练。

In this work we choose a low-level acoustic representation: mel-frequency spectrograms, to bridge the two components. Using a representation that is easily computed from time-domain waveforms allows us to train the two components separately. This representation is also smoother than waveform samples and is easier to train using a squared error loss because it is invariant to phase within each frame.

梅尔频率语谱图与线性频率语谱图（即短时傅里叶变换（STFT）幅度）相关。它通过将非线性变换应用于 STFT 的频率轴获得，灵感来自人类听觉系统的测量响应，并以更少的维度总结频率内容。使用这种听觉频率尺度具有强调低频细节的效果，这些细节对语音清晰度至关重要，同时弱化高频细节，这些细节主要由摩擦音和其他噪声爆发主导，通常不需要高保真建模。由于这些特性，基于梅尔尺度的特征已被用作语音识别的底层表示数十年[17]。

A mel-frequency spectrogram is related to the linear-frequency spectrogram, i.e., the short-time Fourier transform (STFT) magnitude. It is obtained by applying a nonlinear transform to the frequency axis of the STFT, inspired by measured responses from the human auditory system, and summarizes the frequency content with fewer dimensions. Using such an auditory frequency scale has the effect of emphasizing details in lower frequencies, which are critical to speech intelligibility, while de-emphasizing high frequency details, which are dominated by fricatives and other noise bursts and generally do not need to be modeled with high fidelity. Because of these properties, features derived from the mel scale have been used as an underlying representation for speech recognition for many decades [17].

虽然线性语谱图丢弃了相位信息（因此是有损的），但 Griffin-Lim[14]等算法能够估计这些丢弃的信息，从而通过逆短时傅里叶变换实现时域转换。梅尔语谱图丢弃了更多信息，提出了一个具有挑战性的逆问题。然而，与 WaveNet 中使用的语言和声学特征相比，梅尔语谱图是音频信号更简单、更低级的声学表示。因此，以梅尔语谱图为条件的类似 WaveNet 模型应该能够直接生成音频，本质上作为一个神经声码器。事实上，我们将展示使用改进的 WaveNet 架构从梅尔语谱图生成高质量音频是可能的。

While linear spectrograms discard phase information (and are therefore lossy), algorithms such as Griffin-Lim [14] are capable of estimating this discarded information, which enables time-domain conversion via the inverse short-time Fourier transform. Mel spectrograms discard even more information, presenting a challenging inverse problem. However, in comparison to the linguistic and acoustic features used in WaveNet, the mel spectrogram is a simpler, lower-level acoustic representation of audio signals. It should therefore be straightforward for a similar WaveNet model conditioned on mel spectrograms to generate audio, essentially as a neural vocoder. Indeed, we will show that it is possible to generate high quality audio from mel spectrograms using a modified WaveNet architecture.

2.2 频谱预测网络 2.2 Spectrogram Prediction Network

与 Tacotron 一样，梅尔语谱图通过短时傅里叶变换（STFT）计算，使用 50 毫秒帧大小、12.5 毫秒帧移和汉宁窗函数。我们尝试了 5 毫秒帧移以匹配原始 WaveNet 中条件输入的频率，但相应的时间分辨率增加导致了显著的发音问题。

As in Tacotron, mel spectrograms are computed through a short-time Fourier transform (STFT) using a 50 ms frame size, 12.5 ms frame hop, and a Hann window function. We experimented with a 5 ms frame hop to match the frequency of the conditioning inputs in the original WaveNet, but the corresponding increase in temporal resolution resulted in significantly more pronunciation issues.

我们使用 80 通道的梅尔滤波器组将 STFT 幅度转换为梅尔尺度，频率范围从 125 Hz 到 7.6 kHz，然后进行对数动态范围压缩。在对数压缩之前，滤波器组输出幅度被裁剪到最小值 0.01，以限制对数域中的动态范围。

We transform the STFT magnitude to the mel scale using an 80 channel mel filterbank spanning 125 Hz to 7.6 kHz, followed by log dynamic range compression. Prior to log compression, the filterbank output magnitudes are clipped to a minimum value of 0.01 in order to limit dynamic range in the logarithmic domain.

该网络由一个编码器和一个带有注意力的解码器组成。编码器将字符序列转换为隐藏特征表示，解码器利用该表示预测语谱图。输入字符使用学习的 512 维字符嵌入表示，通过 3 个卷积层堆叠，每层包含 512 个形状为 5×1 的滤波器（即每个滤波器跨越 5 个字符），随后进行批归一化[18]和 ReLU 激活。与 Tacotron 一样，这些卷积层对输入字符序列中的更长上下文（例如 N 元语法）进行建模。最后一个卷积层的输出传递到一个包含 512 个单元（每个方向 256 个）的双向[19] LSTM [20]层，以生成编码特征。

The network is composed of an encoder and a decoder with attention. The encoder converts a character sequence into a hidden feature representation which the decoder consumes to predict a spectrogram. Input characters are represented using a learned 512-dimensional character embedding, which are passed through a stack of 3 convolutional layers each containing 512 filters with shape 5×1515\times 1, i.e., where each filter spans 5 characters, followed by batch normalization [18] and ReLU activations. As in Tacotron, these convolutional layers model longer-term context (e.g., N𝑁N-grams) in the input character sequence. The output of the final convolutional layer is passed into a single bi-directional [19] LSTM [20] layer containing 512 units (256 in each direction) to generate the encoded features.

编码器输出由注意力网络使用，该网络将完整的编码序列总结为每个解码器输出步骤的固定长度上下文向量。我们使用[21]中的位置敏感注意力，它扩展了加性注意力机制[22]，将先前解码器时间步的累积注意力权重作为附加特征。这鼓励模型在输入中一致地向前移动，减轻某些子序列被解码器重复或忽略的潜在失败模式。注意力概率在将输入和位置特征投影到 128 维隐藏表示后计算。位置特征使用 32 个长度为 31 的一维卷积滤波器计算。

The encoder output is consumed by an attention network which summarizes the full encoded sequence as a fixed-length context vector for each decoder output step. We use the location-sensitive attention from [21], which extends the additive attention mechanism [22] to use cumulative attention weights from previous decoder time steps as an additional feature. This encourages the model to move forward consistently through the input, mitigating potential failure modes where some subsequences are repeated or ignored by the decoder. Attention probabilities are computed after projecting inputs and location features to 128-dimensional hidden representations. Location features are computed using 32 1-D convolution filters of length 31.

解码器是一个自回归循环神经网络，它从编码输入序列逐帧预测梅尔语谱图。来自前一个时间步的预测首先通过一个小型预网，包含 2 个全连接层，每层 256 个隐藏 ReLU 单元。我们发现预网作为信息瓶颈对于学习注意力至关重要。预网输出和注意力上下文向量被拼接，并通过一个包含 2 个单向 LSTM 层的堆叠，每层 1024 个单元。LSTM 输出和注意力上下文向量的拼接通过线性变换投影，以预测目标语谱图帧。最后，预测的梅尔语谱图通过一个 5 层卷积后网，该后网预测一个残差添加到预测中，以改善整体重建。每个后网层包含 512 个形状为 5×1 的滤波器，带有批归一化，除最后一层外所有层后跟 tanh 激活。

The decoder is an autoregressive recurrent neural network which predicts a mel spectrogram from the encoded input sequence one frame at a time. The prediction from the previous time step is first passed through a small pre-net containing 2 fully connected layers of 256 hidden ReLU units. We found that the pre-net acting as an information bottleneck was essential for learning attention. The pre-net output and attention context vector are concatenated and passed through a stack of 2 uni-directional LSTM layers with 1024 units. The concatenation of the LSTM output and the attention context vector is projected through a linear transform to predict the target spectrogram frame. Finally, the predicted mel spectrogram is passed through a 5-layer convolutional post-net which predicts a residual to add to the prediction to improve the overall reconstruction. Each post-net layer is comprised of 512 filters with shape 5×1515\times 1 with batch normalization, followed by tanh\tanh activations on all but the final layer.

我们最小化后网前后均方误差（MSE）的总和，以帮助收敛。我们还尝试了通过混合密度网络[23, 24]对输出分布建模的对数似然损失，以避免假设随时间恒定的方差，但发现这些更难训练，并且没有导致更好的样本音质。

We minimize the summed mean squared error (MSE) from before and after the post-net to aid convergence. We also experimented with a log-likelihood loss by modeling the output distribution with a Mixture Density Network [23, 24] to avoid assuming a constant variance over time, but found that these were more difficult to train and they did not lead to better sounding samples.

与语谱图帧预测并行，解码器 LSTM 输出和注意力上下文的拼接被投影到一个标量，并通过 sigmoid 激活以预测输出序列是否完成的概率。这个“停止令牌”预测在推理时使用，允许模型动态决定何时终止生成，而不是总是生成固定时长。具体来说，当该概率超过 0.5 的阈值时，在第一个帧处完成生成。

In parallel to spectrogram frame prediction, the concatenation of decoder LSTM output and the attention context is projected down to a scalar and passed through a sigmoid activation to predict the probability that the output sequence has completed. This “stop token” prediction is used during inference to allow the model to dynamically determine when to terminate generation instead of always generating for a fixed duration. Specifically, generation completes at the first frame for which this probability exceeds a threshold of 0.5.

网络中的卷积层使用概率为 0.5 的 dropout[25]进行正则化，LSTM 层使用概率为 0.1 的 zoneout[26]进行正则化。为了在推理时引入输出变化，概率为 0.5 的 dropout 仅应用于自回归解码器预网中的层。

The convolutional layers in the network are regularized using dropout [25] with probability 0.5, and LSTM layers are regularized using zoneout [26] with probability 0.1. In order to introduce output variation at inference time, dropout with probability 0.5 is applied only to layers in the pre-net of the autoregressive decoder.

与原始 Tacotron 相比，我们的模型使用更简单的构建块，在编码器和解码器中使用普通 LSTM 和卷积层，而不是“CBHG”堆叠和 GRU 循环层。我们不使用“缩减因子”，即每个解码器步骤对应一个语谱图帧。

In contrast to the original Tacotron, our model uses simpler building blocks, using vanilla LSTM and convolutional layers in the encoder and decoder instead of “CBHG” stacks and GRU recurrent layers. We do not use a “reduction factor”, i.e., each decoder step corresponds to a single spectrogram frame.

2.3 WaveNet 声码器 2.3 WaveNet Vocoder

我们使用文献[8]中 WaveNet 架构的修改版，将梅尔频谱图特征表示逆变换为时域波形样本。与原始架构一样，有 30 个扩张卷积层，分为 3 个扩张周期，即第 k 层（k=0…29）的扩张率为 2^(k mod 10)。为了适应频谱图帧的 12.5 毫秒帧跳跃，条件堆栈中仅使用 2 个上采样层，而不是 3 层。

We use a modified version of the WaveNet architecture from [8] to invert the mel spectrogram feature representation into time-domain waveform samples. As in the original architecture, there are 30 dilated convolution layers, grouped into 3 dilation cycles, i.e., the dilation rate of layer k (k=0​…​29𝑘0…29k=0\ldots 29) is 2k(mod10)superscript2annotated𝑘pmod102^{k\pmod{10}}. To work with the 12.5 ms frame hop of the spectrogram frames, only 2 upsampling layers are used in the conditioning stack instead of 3 layers.

我们没有使用 softmax 层预测离散桶，而是遵循 PixelCNN++[27]和 Parallel WaveNet[28]，使用 10 分量逻辑分布混合（MoL）以 24 kHz 生成 16 位样本。为了计算逻辑混合分布，WaveNet 堆栈输出经过 ReLU 激活，然后进行线性投影，以预测每个混合分量的参数（均值、对数尺度、混合权重）。损失计算为真实样本的负对数似然。

Instead of predicting discretized buckets with a softmax layer, we follow PixelCNN++ [27] and Parallel WaveNet [28] and use a 10-component mixture of logistic distributions (MoL) to generate 16-bit samples at 24 kHz. To compute the logistic mixture distribution, the WaveNet stack output is passed through a ReLU activation followed by a linear projection to predict parameters (mean, log scale, mixture weight) for each mixture component. The loss is computed as the negative log-likelihood of the ground truth sample.

3.1 训练设置 3.1 Training Setup

我们的训练过程首先单独训练特征预测网络，然后独立训练修改后的 WaveNet，使用第一个网络生成的输出。

Our training process involves first training the feature prediction network on its own, followed by training a modified WaveNet independently on the outputs generated by the first network.

为了训练特征预测网络，我们在单个 GPU 上应用标准最大似然训练过程（在解码器侧馈入正确输出而非预测输出，也称为教师强制），批大小为 64。我们使用 Adam 优化器[29]，β1=0.9，β2=0.999，ϵ=10^{-6}，学习率为 10^{-3}，从 50,000 次迭代后开始指数衰减至 10^{-5}。我们还应用 L2 正则化，权重为 10^{-6}。

To train the feature prediction network, we apply the standard maximum-likelihood training procedure (feeding in the correct output instead of the predicted output on the decoder side, also referred to as teacher-forcing) with a batch size of 64 on a single GPU. 然后，我们在特征预测网络的真实对齐预测上训练修改后的 WaveNet。也就是说，预测网络在教师强制模式下运行，其中每个预测帧以编码输入序列和真实频谱图中对应的前一帧为条件。这确保了每个预测帧与目标波形样本精确对齐。

We then train our modified WaveNet on the ground truth-aligned predictions of the feature prediction network. That is, the prediction network is run in teacher-forcing mode, where each predicted frame is conditioned on the encoded input sequence and the corresponding previous frame in the ground truth spectrogram. This ensures that each predicted frame exactly aligns with the target waveform samples.

我们使用批大小 128，分布在 32 个 GPU 上，进行同步更新训练，使用 Adam 优化器，β1=0.9，β2=0.999，ϵ=10^{-8}，固定学习率为 10^{-4}。对最近的更新平均模型权重有助于提高质量。因此，我们维护网络参数的指数加权移动平均，衰减率为 0.9999——此版本用于推理（另见[29]）。为了加速收敛，我们将波形目标缩放因子 127.5，使逻辑混合层的初始输出更接近最终分布。

我们在内部美国英语数据集[12]上训练所有模型，该数据集包含来自一位专业女性说话者的 24.6 小时语音。我们数据集中的所有文本都拼写出来，例如，“16”写作“sixteen”，即我们的模型都在规范化文本上训练。

We train all models on an internal US English dataset[12], which contains 24.6 hours of speech from a single professional female speaker. All text in our datasets is spelled out. e.g., “16” is written as “sixteen”, i.e., our models are all trained on normalized text.

3.2 评估 3.2 Evaluation

在推理模式下生成语音时，真实目标未知。因此，解码过程中馈入前一步的预测输出，这与训练时使用的教师强制配置相反。

When generating speech in inference mode, the ground truth targets are not known. Therefore, the predicted outputs from the previous step are fed in during decoding, in contrast to the teacher-forcing configuration used for training.

我们从内部数据集的测试集中随机选择 100 个固定示例作为评估集。在此集上生成的音频被发送到类似 Amazon Mechanical Turk 的人工评分服务，每个样本由至少 8 名评分员按 1 到 5 分（0.5 分增量）评分，从中计算主观平均意见得分（MOS）。每次评估独立进行，因此评分员对两个不同模型的输出进行评分时不会直接比较。

We randomly selected 100 fixed examples from the test set of our internal dataset as the evaluation set. Audio generated on this set are sent to a human rating service similar to Amazon’s Mechanical Turk where each sample is rated by at least 8 raters on a scale from 1 to 5 with 0.5 point increments, from which a subjective mean opinion score (MOS) is calculated. Each evaluation is conducted independently from each other, so the outputs of two different models are not directly compared when raters assign a score to them.

请注意，虽然评估集中的实例从未出现在训练集中，但两个集之间存在一些重复模式和常见词。虽然这可能导致 MOS 比由随机单词组成的句子构成的评估集更高，但使用此集使我们能够与真实情况进行比较。由于我们比较的所有系统都在相同数据上训练，相对比较仍然有意义。

Note that while instances in the evaluation set never appear in the training set, there are some recurring patterns and common words between the two sets. While this could potentially result in an inflated MOS compared to an evaluation set consisting of sentences generated from random words, using this set allows us to compare to the ground truth. Since all the systems we compare are trained on the same data, relative comparisons are still meaningful.

我们还对我们的系统合成的音频与真实音频进行了并排评估。对于每对话语，评分员被要求给出从-3（合成比真实差得多）到 3（合成比真实好得多）的分数。总体平均分数为-0.270±0.155，表明评分员对真实情况有微小但统计上显著的偏好。详细分解见图 2。评分员的评论表明，我们系统偶尔的发音错误是这种偏好的主要原因。

3.3 消融研究 3.3 Ablation Studies

虽然我们模型的两个组件是分开训练的，但 WaveNet 组件依赖于预测特征进行训练。另一种方法是独立训练 WaveNet，使用从真实音频中提取的梅尔频谱图。我们在表 2 中探讨了这一点。

While the two components of our model were trained separately, the WaveNet component depends on the predicted features for training. An alternative is to train WaveNet independently on mel spectrograms extracted from ground truth audio. We explore this in Table 2.

正如预期，当用于训练的特征与用于推理的特征匹配时，性能最佳。然而，当在真实特征上训练并从预测特征合成时，结果比相反情况更差。这是由于预测频谱图倾向于过度平滑且不如真实详细——这是特征预测网络优化的平方误差损失的结果。当在真实频谱图上训练时，网络不会学习从过度平滑的特征生成高质量语音波形。

As expected, the best performance is obtained when the features used for training match those used for inference. However, when trained on ground truth features and made to synthesize from predicted features, the result is worse than the opposite. This is due to the tendency of the predicted spectrograms to be oversmoothed and less detailed than the ground truth – a consequence of the squared error loss optimized by the feature prediction network. When trained on ground truth spectrograms, the network does not learn to generate high quality speech waveforms from oversmoothed features.

我们尝试训练预测线性频率频谱图而不是梅尔频谱图，从而可以使用 Griffin-Lim 逆变换频谱图。

Instead of predicting mel spectrograms, we experiment with training to predict linear-frequency spectrograms instead, making it possible to invert the spectrogram using Griffin-Lim.

如文献[10]所述，WaveNet 比 Griffin-Lim 产生更高质量的音频。然而，使用线性尺度或梅尔尺度频谱图之间没有太大差异。因此，使用梅尔频谱图似乎是严格更好的选择，因为它是一种更紧凑的表示。在未来工作中探索梅尔频率箱数量与音频质量之间的权衡将是有趣的。

As noted in [10], WaveNet produces much higher quality audio compared to Griffin-Lim. However, there is not much difference between the use of linear-scale or mel-scale spectrograms. As such, the use of mel spectrograms seems to be a strictly better choice since it is a more compact representation. It would be interesting to explore the trade-off between the number of mel frequency bins versus audio quality in future work.

由于在解码之前无法使用预测的未来帧的信息，我们使用卷积后处理网络在解码后结合过去和未来帧来改进特征预测。然而，因为 WaveNet 已经包含卷积层，人们可能想知道当 WaveNet 用作声码器时后处理网络是否仍然必要。为了回答这个问题，我们比较了有和没有后处理网络的模型，发现没有后处理网络时，我们的模型仅获得 4.429±0.071 的 MOS 分数，而有后处理网络时为 4.526±0.066，这意味着经验上后处理网络仍然是网络设计的重要组成部分。

Since it is not possible to use the information of predicted future frames before they have been decoded, we use a convolutional post-processing network to incorporate past and future frames after decoding to improve the feature predictions. However, because WaveNet already contains convolutional layers, one may wonder if the post-net is still necessary when WaveNet is used as the vocoder. To answer this question, we compared our model with and without the post-net, and found that without it, our model only obtains a MOS score of 4.429±0.071plus-or-minus4.4290.0714.429\pm 0.071, compared to 4.526±0.066plus-or-minus4.5260.0664.526\pm 0.066 with it, meaning that empirically the post-net is still an important part of the network design.

WaveNet 的一个定义性特征是使用扩张卷积，以随层数指数级增加感受野。我们评估了具有不同感受野大小和层数的模型，以检验我们的假设：由于梅尔频谱图比语言特征更接近波形的表示，并且已经捕获了帧间的长期依赖关系，因此一个具有小感受野的浅层网络可能足以令人满意地解决该问题。

A defining feature of WaveNet is its use of dilated convolution to increase the receptive field exponentially with the number of layers. We evaluate models with varying receptive field sizes and number of layers to test our hypothesis that a shallow network with a small receptive field may solve the problem satisfactorily since mel spectrograms are a much closer representation of the waveform than linguistic features and already capture long-term dependencies across frames.

如表 4 所示，我们发现我们的模型仅使用 12 层、感受野为 10.5 毫秒即可生成高质量音频，而基线模型需要 30 层和 256 毫秒的感受野。这些结果证实了文献[9]中的观察结果，即大的感受野大小并非音频质量的关键因素。然而，我们假设正是选择以梅尔频谱图为条件才使得这种复杂性降低成为可能。

As shown in Table 4, we find that our model can generate high-quality audio using as few as 12 layers with a receptive field of 10.5 ms, compared to 30 layers and 256 ms in the baseline model. These results confirm the observations in [9] that a large receptive field size is not an essential factor for audio quality. However, we hypothesize that it is the choice to condition on mel spectrograms that allows this reduction in complexity.

另一方面，如果我们完全消除扩张卷积，感受野将比基线小两个数量级，即使堆叠深度与基线模型相同，质量也会显著下降。这表明模型需要波形样本时间尺度上的足够上下文才能生成高质量的声音。

On the other hand, if we eliminate dilated convolutions altogether, the receptive field becomes two orders of magnitude smaller than the baseline and the quality degrades significantly even though the stack is as deep as the baseline model. This indicates that the model requires sufficient context at the time scale of waveform samples in order to generate high quality sound.

4 结论 4 Conclusion

本文描述了 Tacotron 2，一个完全神经网络的 TTS 系统，它结合了序列到序列的循环网络与注意力机制，以预测梅尔频谱图，并使用改进的 WaveNet 声码器。由此产生的系统以 Tacotron 级别的韵律和 WaveNet 级别的音频质量合成语音。该系统可以直接从数据中训练，无需依赖复杂的特征工程，并实现了接近自然人类语音的最先进音质。

This paper describes Tacotron 2, a fully neural TTS system that combines a sequence-to-sequence recurrent network with attention to predicts mel spectrograms with a modified WaveNet vocoder. The resulting system synthesizes speech with Tacotron-level prosody and WaveNet-level audio quality. This system can be trained directly from data without relying on complex feature engineering, and achieves state-of-the-art sound quality close to that of natural human speech.
