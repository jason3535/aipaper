宇宙世界基础模型平台：面向物理 AI

Cosmos World Foundation Model Platform for Physical AI

迪特·福克斯 Dieter Fox · · 2025-01-07 · arXiv:2501.03575 ↗

摘要 · Abstract

物理 AI 需要先进行数字化训练。它需要自身的数字孪生（策略模型）以及世界的数字孪生（世界模型）。在本文中，我们介绍了宇宙世界基础模型平台，以帮助开发者为其物理 AI 设置构建定制化的世界模型。我们将世界基础模型定位为通用世界模型，可以微调为适用于下游应用的定制世界模型。我们的平台涵盖视频整理流水线、预训练的世界基础模型、预训练世界基础模型的后训练示例以及视频分词器。为了帮助物理 AI 构建者解决社会最关键的问题，我们将 Cosmos 开源，并通过 https://github.com/nvidia-cosmos/cosmos-predict1 提供具有宽松许可证的开放权重模型。

Physical AI needs to be trained digitally first. It needs a digital twin of itself, the policy model, and a digital twin of the world, the world model. In this paper, we present the Cosmos World Foundation Model Platform to help developers build customized world models for their Physical AI setups. We position a world foundation model as a general-purpose world model that can be fine-tuned into customized world models for downstream applications. Our platform covers a video curation pipeline, pre-trained world foundation models, examples of post-training of pre-trained world foundation models, and video tokenizers. To help Physical AI builders solve the most critical problems of our society, we make Cosmos open-source and our models open-weight with permissive licenses available via https://github.com/nvidia-cosmos/cosmos-predict1.

核心贡献 · Key contributions

  • 提出了面向物理 AI 的 Cosmos 世界基础模型平台，包括视频整理、分词器、预训练和后训练世界模型。
    Introduces Cosmos World Foundation Model Platform for Physical AI, including video curation, tokenizers, pre-trained and post-trained world models.
  • 开发了可扩展的视频整理流程，从 2000 万小时视频中提取 1 亿个高质量片段，包括过滤、标注和去重。
    Develops a scalable video curation pipeline that extracts 100M high-quality clips from 20M hours of video, with filtering, annotation, and deduplication.
  • 提出了 Cosmos 分词器系列，具有因果性，重建质量优越，推理速度提升高达 12 倍。
    Presents Cosmos Tokenizer, a family of causal video tokenizers with superior reconstruction quality and up to 12x faster inference.
  • 探索了两种可扩展的 WFM 范式：基于 Transformer 的扩散模型和自回归模型，模型规模高达 140 亿参数。
    Explores two scalable WFM paradigms: transformer-based diffusion and autoregressive models, with model sizes up to 14B.
  • 展示了后训练应用，包括相机姿态控制、人形机器人和自动驾驶，证明了微调的优势。
    Demonstrates post-training applications for camera pose control, humanoid robots, and autonomous driving, showing fine-tuning benefits.
  • 以宽松许可证开源模型和分词器，加速物理 AI 研究。
    Open-sources models and tokenizers with permissive licenses to accelerate Physical AI research.

局限 · Limitations

  • 世界基础模型问题远未解决，需要进一步研究以推进最先进水平。
    World foundation model problem is far from solved; additional research needed for state-of-the-art advancement.
  • 论文未包含策略评估、初始化、训练、规划或合成数据生成的实证结果。
    The paper does not include empirical results for policy evaluation, initialization, training, planning, or synthetic data generation.
  • 自回归模型因重度压缩而产生失真，通过扩散解码器缓解但未完全解决。
    Autoregressive models suffer from distortions due to heavy compression, mitigated by a diffusion decoder but not fully resolved.
  • 训练需要大量算力（1 万块 H100 GPU 运行 3 个月），限制了较小研究团队的可及性。
    Training requires massive compute (10,000 H100 GPUs for 3 months), limiting accessibility for smaller research groups.
  • 护栏系统阻止有害输入/输出，但可能无法覆盖所有边缘情况或对抗性攻击。
    Guardrail system blocks harmful inputs/outputs but may not cover all edge cases or adversarial attacks.
