ALOHA Unleashed：机器人灵巧性的简单配方

ALOHA Unleashed: A Simple Recipe for Robot Dexterity

切尔西·芬恩 Chelsea Finn · · 2024-10-17 · arXiv:2410.13126 ↗

摘要 · Abstract

近期研究在利用模仿学习训练端到端机器人策略方面取得了有前景的成果。本文探讨了在具有挑战性的灵巧操作任务中，模仿学习能推进到何种程度。我们表明，在 ALOHA 2 平台上进行大规模数据收集，结合扩散策略等表达力强的模型，这一简单配方能有效学习涉及可变形物体和复杂接触动力学的挑战性双手操作任务。我们在 5 个真实世界任务和 3 个模拟任务上验证了该配方，并展示了相较于最先进基线的性能提升。项目网站和视频可在 aloha-unleashed.github.io 查看。

Recent work has shown promising results for learning end-to-end robot policies using imitation learning. In this work we address the question of how far can we push imitation learning for challenging dexterous manipulation tasks. We show that a simple recipe of large scale data collection on the ALOHA 2 platform, combined with expressive models such as Diffusion Policies, can be effective in learning challenging bimanual manipulation tasks involving deformable objects and complex contact rich dynamics. We demonstrate our recipe on 5 challenging real-world and 3 simulated tasks and demonstrate improved performance over state-of-the-art baselines. The project website and videos can be found at aloha-unleashed.github.io.

核心贡献 · Key contributions

  • 提出了 ALOHA Unleashed，一种将 ALOHA 2 平台上的大规模数据收集与扩散策略相结合的简单方法，用于灵巧的双臂操作。
    Introduces ALOHA Unleashed, a simple recipe combining large-scale data collection on ALOHA 2 with Diffusion Policies for dexterous bimanual manipulation.
  • 在 5 个真实世界任务和 3 个模拟任务上展示了最先进的性能，包括系鞋带和挂衬衫，这些是端到端策略的首次实现。
    Demonstrates state-of-the-art performance on 5 real-world and 3 simulated tasks, including tying shoelaces and hanging shirts, which are firsts for end-to-end policies.
  • 收集了超过 26,000 个真实世界演示和 2,000 个模拟演示，使用了可扩展的遥操作协议，涉及 35 位非专家操作员。
    Collects over 26,000 real-world demonstrations and 2,000 simulated ones, using a scalable teleoperation protocol with 35 non-expert operators.
  • 表明基于 Transformer 的扩散策略在灵巧任务上优于 L1 回归基线（如 ACT），尤其是在处理多模态动作分布方面。
    Shows that Transformer-based Diffusion Policies outperform L1 regression baselines (like ACT) on dexterous tasks, especially in handling multimodal action distributions.
  • 展示了对未见过的衬衫、机器人和环境的泛化能力，以及对物体初始状态变化的鲁棒性。
    Demonstrates generalization to unseen shirts, robots, and environments, and robustness to variations in object initial states.
  • 提供了关于数据规模、数据过滤以及数据多样性对学习恢复行为重要性的见解。
    Provides insights into data scaling, filtering, and the importance of data diversity for learning recovery behaviors.

局限 · Limitations

  • 策略每次仅针对单一任务训练，缺乏多任务或语言条件能力。
    Policies are trained for a single task at a time, lacking multi-task or language-conditioned capabilities.
  • 策略每 1 秒重新规划一次，对于需要更快反馈的高反应性任务可能太慢。
    The policy replans every 1 second, which may be too slow for highly reactive tasks requiring faster feedback.
  • 每个任务需要大量人工演示（数千个），收集耗时且成本高。
    Requires a large number of human demonstrations per task (thousands), which is time-consuming and costly to collect.
  • 泛化仅限于训练分布内的状态；对于分布外的配置（如翻转的衬衫或缠绕的鞋带）会失败。
    Generalization is limited to states within the training distribution; fails on out-of-distribution configurations like flipped shirts or tangled laces.
  • 该方法仅依赖视觉反馈，没有力-扭矩传感，这可能限制在需要精确力控制的任务中的性能。
    The approach relies on visual feedback only, without force-torque sensing, which may limit performance in tasks requiring precise force control.
