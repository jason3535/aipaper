摘要 · Abstract

生成式 AI 的最新进展彻底改变了视觉内容创作，但将模型输出与人类偏好对齐仍是一个关键挑战。虽然强化学习已成为微调生成模型的一种有前景的方法，但现有方法如 DDPO 和 DPOK 存在根本性局限——特别是在扩展到大规模和多样化提示集时无法保持稳定优化，严重限制了其实用性。本文提出 DanceGRPO 框架，通过创新性地将群体相对策略优化（GRPO）应用于视觉生成任务来解决这些局限。我们的关键洞察是，GRPO 固有的稳定机制使其能够克服先前基于强化学习方法在视觉生成中面临的优化挑战。DanceGRPO 取得了多项重要进展：首先，它在多种现代生成范式（包括扩散模型和整流流）中展示了持续稳定的策略优化。其次，在扩展到包含三个关键任务和四个基础模型的复杂真实场景时，它保持了稳健性能。第三，它在优化由五种不同奖励模型（评估图像/视频美学、文本-图像对齐、视频运动质量和二元反馈）捕获的多样化人类偏好方面表现出显著的多功能性。我们的综合实验表明，DanceGRPO 在多个既定基准（包括 HPS-v2.1、CLIP Score、VideoAlign 和 GenEval）上比基线方法高出高达 181%。我们的结果确立了 DanceGRPO 作为在视觉生成中扩展基于人类反馈的强化学习任务的稳健且多功能的解决方案，为协调强化学习和视觉合成提供了新见解。

Recent advances in generative AI have revolutionized visual content creation, yet aligning model outputs with human preferences remains a critical challenge. While Reinforcement Learning (RL) has emerged as a promising approach for fine-tuning generative models, existing methods like DDPO and DPOK face fundamental limitations - particularly their inability to maintain stable optimization when scaling to large and diverse prompt sets, severely restricting their practical utility. This paper presents DanceGRPO, a framework that addresses these limitations through an innovative adaptation of Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) for visual generation tasks. Our key insight is that GRPO's inherent stability mechanisms uniquely position it to overcome the optimization challenges that plague prior RL-based approaches on visual generation. DanceGRPO establishes several significant advances: First, it demonstrates consistent and stable policy optimization across multiple modern generative paradigms, including both diffusion models and rectified flows. Second, it maintains robust performance when scaling to complex, real-world scenarios encompassing three key tasks and four foundation models. Third, it shows remarkable versatility in optimizing for diverse human preferences as captured by five distinct reward models assessing image/video aesthetics, text-image alignment, video motion quality, and binary feedback. Our comprehensive experiments reveal that DanceGRPO outperforms baseline methods by up to 181\% across multiple established benchmarks, including HPS-v2.1, CLIP Score, VideoAlign, and GenEval. Our results establish DanceGRPO as a robust and versatile solution for scaling Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) tasks in visual generation, offering new insights into harmonizing reinforcement learning and visual synthesis.

1 引言 1 Introduction

生成模型的最新进展——特别是扩散模型[1, 2, 3, 4]和整流流[5, 6, 7]——通过提高图像和视频生成中的输出质量和多样性，改变了视觉内容创作。虽然预训练建立了基础数据分布，但在训练过程中整合人类反馈对于将输出与人类偏好和美学标准对齐至关重要[8]。现有方法面临显著限制：ReFL [9, 10, 11]依赖可微奖励模型，这在视频生成中引入 VRAM 低效并需要大量工程努力，而 DPO 变体（Diffusion-DPO [12, 13]、Flow-DPO [14]、OnlineVPO [15]）仅实现边际视觉质量改进。基于强化学习（RL）的方法[16, 17]将奖励作为黑盒目标进行优化，提供了潜在解决方案，但引入了三个未解决的挑战：（1）整流流模型的基于常微分方程（ODE）的采样与马尔可夫决策过程公式冲突；（2）先前的策略梯度方法（DDPO [18]、DPOK [19]）在扩展到小数据集（例如<100 个提示）之外时表现出不稳定性；（3）现有方法尚未在视频生成任务中得到验证。

Recent advances in generative models—particularly diffusion models [1, 2, 3, 4] and rectified flows [5, 6, 7]—have transformed visual content creation by improving output quality and versatility in image and video generation. While pretraining establishes foundational data distributions, integrating human feedback during training proves critical for aligning outputs with human preferences and aesthetic criteria [8]. Existing methods face notable limitations: ReFL [9, 10, 11] relies on differentiable reward models, which introduce VRAM inefficiency in video generation and require several extensive engineering efforts, while DPO variants (Diffusion-DPO [12, 13], Flow-DPO [14], OnlineVPO [15]) achieve only marginal visual quality improvements. Reinforcement learning (RL)-based methods [16, 17], which optimize rewards as black-box objectives, offer potential solutions but introduce three unresolved challenges: (1) the Ordinary Differential Equations (ODEs)-based sampling of rectified flow models conflict with Markov Decision Process formulations; (2) prior policy gradient approaches (DDPO [18], DPOK [19]) show instability when scaling beyond small datasets (e.g., &lt;100 prompts); and (3) existing methods remain unvalidated for video generation tasks.

本文通过将扩散模型和整流流的采样过程重新表述为随机微分方程（SDE），并应用群体相对策略优化（GRPO）[20, 21]来稳定训练过程，解决了这些差距。GRPO 是最近的一项突破，它增强了大型语言模型（LLM）在数学和编码等复杂任务上的性能。在本文中，我们率先通过 DanceGRPO 框架将 GRPO 适配到视觉生成任务，建立了 GRPO 与视觉生成任务之间的“和谐之舞”。

This work addresses these gaps by reformulating the sampling of diffusion models and rectified flows via Stochastic Differential Equations (SDEs) and applying Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) [20, 21] to stabilize the training process. GRPO is a recent breakthrough that enhances the performance of large language models (LLMs) on complex tasks such as mathematics and coding. In this paper, we pioneer the adaptation of GRPO to visual generation tasks through the DanceGRPO framework, establishing a "harmonious dance" between GRPO and visual generation tasks.

我们提出了一个算法 DanceGRPO 的全面研究，涵盖两种生成范式（扩散/整流流）、三个任务（文生图、文生视频、图生视频）、四个基础模型（Stable Diffusion [2]、HunyuanVideo [22]、FLUX [23]、SkyReels-I2V [24]）和五个奖励模型（图像/视频美学、文本-图像对齐、视频运动质量和二元阈值）。

We present a comprehensive study of one algorithm, DanceGRPO across two generative paradigms (diffusion/rectified flows), three tasks (text-to-image, text-to-video, image-to-video), four foundation models (Stable Diffusion [2], HunyuanVideo [22], FLUX [23], SkyReels-I2V [24]), and five reward models (image/video aesthetics, text-image alignment, video motion quality, and binary thresholds).

Due to reward sparsity in practice, we apply the same reward signal across all timesteps during optimization. While traditional GRPO formulations employ KL-regularization to prevent reward over-optimization, we empirically observe minimal performance differences when omitting this component. So, we omit the KL-regularization item by default. The full algorithm can be found in Algorithm 1. We also introduce how to train with Classifier-Free Guidance (CFG) [35] in Appendix 8. In summary, we formulate the sampling processes of diffusion model and rectified flow as MDPs, use SDE sampling equations, adopt a GRPO-style objective, and generalize to text-to-image, text-to-video, and image-to-video generation tasks.

初始化噪声。在 DanceGRPO 框架中，初始化噪声是一个关键组成部分。之前基于 RL 的方法如 DDPO 使用不同的噪声向量来初始化训练样本。然而，如附录 11 中图 8 所示，对相同提示的样本分配不同的噪声向量总是导致视频生成中的奖励破解现象，包括训练不稳定。因此，在我们的框架中，我们对来自相同文本提示的样本分配共享的初始化噪声。

Initialization Noise. In the DanceGRPO framework, the initialization noise constitutes a critical component. Previous RL-based approaches like DDPO use different noise vectors to initialize training samples. However, as shown in Figure 8 in Appendix 11, assigning different noise vectors to samples with the same prompts always leads to reward hacking phenomena in video generation, including training instability. Therefore, in our framework, we assign shared initialization noise to samples originating from the same textual prompts.

时间步选择。虽然去噪过程可以在 MDP 框架内严格形式化，但经验观察表明，去噪轨迹中的子集时间步可以省略而不影响性能。这种计算步骤的减少提高了效率，同时保持了输出质量，如第 3.6 节进一步分析。

Timestep Selection. While the denoising process can be rigorously formalized within the MDP framework, empirical observations reveal that subsets of timesteps within a denoising trajectory can be omitted without compromising performance. This reduction in computational steps enhances efficiency while maintaining output quality, as further analyzed in Section 3.6.

结合多个奖励模型。在实践中，我们使用不止一个奖励模型以确保更稳定的训练和更高质量的视觉结果。如附录中图 9 所示，仅使用 HPS-v2.1 奖励[25]训练的模型倾向于生成不自然（“油腻”）的输出，而加入 CLIP 分数有助于保留更真实的图像特征。我们不直接组合奖励，而是聚合优势函数，因为不同的奖励模型通常在不同的尺度上运行。这种方法稳定了优化并导致更平衡的生成。

Incorporating Multiple Reward Models. In practice, we employ more than one reward model to ensure more stable training and higher-quality visual results. As illustrated in Figure 9 in Appendix, models trained exclusively with HPS-v2.1 rewards [25] tend to generate unnatural ("oily") outputs, whereas incorporating CLIP scores helps preserve more realistic image characteristics. Rather than directly combining rewards, we aggregate advantage functions, as different reward models often operate on different scales. This approach stabilizes optimization and leads to more balanced generations.

Best-of-N 推理 Scaling 的扩展。如第 3.6 节所述，我们的方法优先使用高效样本——具体来说，是由 Best-of-N 采样选择的前 k 和后 k 候选样本。这种选择性采样策略通过关注解空间中的高奖励和关键低奖励区域来优化训练效率。我们使用暴力搜索生成这些样本。虽然其他方法如树搜索或贪心搜索仍然是进一步探索的有前景的途径，但我们将它们的系统集成留待未来研究。

Extension on Best-of-N Inference Scaling. As outlined in Section 3.6, our methodology prioritizes the use of efficient samples—specifically, those associated with the top k and bottom k candidates selected by the Best-of-N sampling. This selective sampling strategy optimizes training efficacy by focusing on high-reward and critical low-reward regions of the solution space. We use brute-force search to generate these samples. While alternative approaches, such as tree search or greedy search, remain promising avenues for further exploration, we defer their systematic integration to future research.

2.3 不同任务与不同奖励的应用 2.3 Application to Different Tasks with Different Rewards

我们在两种生成范式（扩散/整流流）和三个任务（文本到图像、文本到视频、图像到视频）上验证了我们算法的有效性。为此，我们选择了四个基础模型（Stable Diffusion [2]、HunyuanVideo [22]、FLUX [23]、SkyReels-I2V [24]）进行实验。所有这些方法在其采样过程中都可以精确地构建在 MDP 框架内。这使我们能够统一这些任务的理论基础，并通过 DanceGRPO 改进它们。据我们所知，这是首次将统一框架应用于多样化的视觉生成任务的工作。

We verify the effectiveness of our algorithm in two generative paradigms (diffusion/rectified flow) and three tasks (text-to-image, text-to-video, image-to-video). For this, we choose four fundamental models (Stable Diffusion [2], HunyuanVideo [22], FLUX [23], SkyReels-I2V [24]) for the experiment. All of these methods can be precisely constructed within the framework of MDP during their sampling process. This allows us to unify the theoretical bases across these tasks and improve them via DanceGRPO. To our knowledge, this is the first work to apply the unified framework to diverse visual generation tasks.

我们使用五个奖励模型来优化视觉生成质量：（1）图像美学使用在人类评分数据上微调的预训练模型量化视觉吸引力[25]；（2）文本-图像对齐使用 CLIP [26]最大化提示与输出之间的跨模态一致性；（3）视频美学质量使用 VLM [14, 29]将图像评估扩展到时间域，评估帧质量和连贯性；（4）视频运动质量通过物理感知的 VLM [14]分析轨迹和变形来评估运动真实性；（5）阈值化二元奖励采用受[20]启发的二元机制，其中奖励通过固定阈值离散化（超过阈值的值获得 1，否则为 0），专门设计用于评估生成模型在基于阈值的优化下学习突然奖励分布的能力。

We use five reward models to optimize visual generation quality: (1) Image Aesthetics quantifies visual appeal using a pretrained model fine-tuned on human-rated data [25]; (2) Text-image Alignment employs CLIP [26] to maximize cross-modal consistency between prompts and outputs; (3) Video Aesthetics Quality extends image evaluation to temporal domains using VLMs [14, 29], assessing frame quality and coherence; (4) Video Motion Quality evaluates motion realism through physics-aware VLM[14] analysis of trajectories and deformations; (5) Thresholding Binary Reward employs a binary mechanism motivated by [20], where rewards are discretized via a fixed threshold (values exceeding the threshold receive 1, others 0), specifically designed to evaluate generative models’ ability to learn abrupt reward distributions under threshold-based optimization.

3.3 文生视频 3.3 Text-to-Video Generation

HunyuanVideo。优化文本到视频生成模型相比文本到图像框架面临显著更大的挑战，主要由于训练和推理期间计算成本升高以及收敛速度较慢。在预训练协议中，我们始终采用渐进策略：初始训练聚焦于文本到图像生成，随后是低分辨率视频合成，最后是高分辨率视频精炼。然而，经验观察表明，仅依赖图像中心优化会导致次优的视频生成结果。为解决此问题，我们的实现使用以 480×480 像素分辨率合成的训练视频样本，但我们可以可视化更大像素的样本。

HunyuanVideo. Optimizing text-to-video generation models presents significantly greater challenges compared to text-to-image frameworks, primarily due to elevated computational costs during training and inference, as well as slower convergence rates. In the pretraining protocol, we always adopt a progressive strategy: initial training focuses on text-to-image generation, followed by low-resolution video synthesis, and culminates in high-resolution video refinement. However, empirical observations reveal that relying solely on image-centric optimization leads to suboptimal video generation outcomes. To address this, our implementation employs training video samples synthesized at a resolution of 480×480 pixels, but we can visualize the samples with larger pixels.

此外，构建有效的视频奖励模型用于训练对齐面临巨大困难。我们的实验评估了几个候选模型：Videoscore [40]模型表现出不稳定的奖励分布，使其不适用于优化；而 Visionreward-Video [41]（一个 29 维度量）产生语义连贯的奖励，但在各个维度上存在不准确性。因此，我们采用了 VideoAlign [14]，这是一个多维框架，评估三个关键方面：视觉美学质量、运动质量和文本-视频对齐。值得注意的是，文本-视频对齐维度表现出显著不稳定性，导致其被排除在最终分析之外。我们还增加了 VideoAlign 每秒采样帧数以提高训练稳定性。如图 2(a)和图 2(b)所示，我们的方法在视觉和运动质量指标上分别实现了 56%和 181%的相对改进。扩展的定性结果见表 3.1。

Furthermore, constructing an effective video reward model for training alignment poses substantial difficulties. Our experiments evaluated several candidates: the Videoscore [40] model exhibited unstable reward distributions, rendering it impractical for optimization, while Visionreward-Video [41], a 29-dimensional metric, yielded semantically coherent rewards but suffered from inaccuracies across individual dimensions. Consequently, we adopted VideoAlign [14], a multidimensional framework evaluating three critical aspects: visual aesthetics quality, motion quality, and text-video alignment. Notably, the text-video alignment dimension demonstrated significant instability, prompting its exclusion from our final analysis. We also increase the number of sampled frames per second for VideoAlign to improve the training stability. As illustrated in Figure 2(a) and Figure 2(b), our methodology achieves relative improvements of 56% and 181% in visual and motion quality metrics, respectively. Extended qualitative results are provided in the Table 3.1.

3.4 图生视频 3.4 Image-to-Video Generation

SkyReels-I2V。SkyReels-I2V 代表了最先进的开源图像到视频（I2V）生成框架，截至本研究开始时的 2025 年 2 月已建立。该模型源自 HunyuanVideo 架构，通过将图像条件集成到输入拼接过程中进行微调。我们研究的一个核心发现是，I2V 模型仅允许优化运动质量，包括运动连贯性和美学动态，因为视觉保真度和文本-视频对齐本质上受输入图像属性而非模型参数空间的约束。因此，我们的优化协议利用 VideoAlign 奖励模型中的运动质量度量，在该维度上实现了 91%的相对改进，如图 2(c)所示。在 RLHF 训练过程中，我们必须启用 CFG 训练以确保采样质量。

SkyReels-I2V. SkyReels-I2V represents a state-of-the-art open-source image-to-video (I2V) generation framework, established as of February 2025 at the inception of this study. Derived from the HunyuanVideo architecture, the model is fine-tuned by integrating image conditions into the input concatenation process. A central finding of our investigation is that I2V models exclusively allow optimization of motion quality, encompassing motion coherence and aesthetic dynamics, since visual fidelity and text-video alignment are inherently constrained by the attributes of the input image rather than the parametric space of the model. Consequently, our optimization protocol leverages the motion quality metric from the VideoAlign reward model, achieving a 91% relative improvement in this dimension as shown in Figure 2(c). We must enable the CFG-training to ensure the sampling quality during the RLHF training process.

3.6 消融实验 3.6 Ablation Study

时间步选择的消融研究。如第 2.2 节所述，我们研究了时间步选择对 HunyuanVideo-T2I 模型训练动态的影响。我们在五个实验条件下进行了消融研究：(1) 仅在从噪声开始的前 30%时间步上训练，(2) 在随机采样的 30%时间步上训练，(3) 在输出前的最后 40%时间步上训练，(4) 在随机采样的 60%时间步上训练，(5) 在采样的 100%时间步上训练。如图 4(b)所示，实证结果表明，初始 30%的时间步对于学习基础生成模式至关重要，因为它们对模型性能的贡献不成比例。然而，仅在此区间训练会导致性能相比全序列训练下降，可能是由于对后期精炼动态的暴露不足。

Ablation on Timestep Selection. As detailed in Section 2.2, we investigate the impact of timestep selection on the training dynamics of the HunyuanVideo-T2I model. We conduct an ablation study across three experimental conditions: (1) training exclusively on the first 30% of timesteps from noise, (2) training on randomly sampled 30% of timesteps, (3) training on the final 40% of timesteps before outputs, (4) training on randomly sampled 60% of timesteps, and (5) training on sampled 100% of timesteps. As shown in Figure 4(b), empirical results indicate that the initial 30% of timesteps are critical for learning foundational generative patterns, as evidenced by their disproportionate contribution to model performance. However, restricting training solely to this interval leads to performance degradation compared to full-sequence training, likely due to insufficient exposure to late-stage refinement dynamics.

为了在计算效率和模型保真度之间取得平衡，我们始终在训练中实施 40%的随机时间步丢弃策略。该方法随机掩盖所有阶段中 40%的时间步，同时保持潜在扩散过程中的时间连续性。研究结果表明，策略性时间步子采样可以优化基于流的生成框架中的资源利用。

To reconcile this trade-off between computational efficiency and model fidelity, we always implement a 40% stochastic timestep dropout strategy during training. This approach randomly masks 40% of timesteps across all phases while preserving temporal continuity in the latent diffusion process. The findings suggest that strategic timestep subsampling can optimize resource utilization in flow-based generative frameworks.

噪声水平εt 的消融研究。我们系统研究了训练期间噪声水平εt 对 FLUX 的影响。我们的分析表明，降低εt 会导致显著的性能下降，如图 4(c)定量所示。值得注意的是，使用其他噪声衰减调度（例如 DDPM 中使用的）的实验在输出质量上与我们的基线配置相比没有统计学显著差异。此外，大于 0.3 的噪声水平有时会导致 RLHF 训练后出现噪声图像。

Ablation on Noise Level εt\varepsilon_{t}. We systematically investigate the impact of noise level εt\varepsilon_{t} during training on FLUX. Our analysis reveals that reducing εt\varepsilon_{t} leads to a significant performance degradation, as quantitatively demonstrated in Figure 4(c). Notably, experiments with alternative noise decay schedules (e.g., those used in DDPM) show no statistically significant differences in output quality compared to our baseline configuration. Futhermore, the noise level larger than 0.3 sometimes leads to noisy images after RLHF training.

4 相关工作 4 Related Work

对齐大型语言模型。大型语言模型（LLM）[42, 43, 44, 45, 46]通常通过基于人类反馈的强化学习（RLHF）[47, 46, 45, 48, 49, 50]进行对齐。RLHF 涉及基于模型输出的比较数据训练奖励函数以捕捉人类偏好，然后将其用于强化学习以对齐策略模型。虽然一些方法利用策略梯度方法，其他方法则关注直接策略优化（DPO）[51]。策略梯度方法已被证明有效，但计算成本高且需要大量超参数调优。相比之下，DPO 提供了更具成本效益的替代方案，但始终不如策略梯度方法。

Aligning Large Language Models. Large Language Models (LLMs) [42, 43, 44, 45, 46] are typically aligned with Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) [47, 46, 45, 48, 49, 50]. RLHF involves training a reward function based on comparison data of model outputs to capture human preferences, which is then utilized in reinforcement learning to align the policy model. While some approaches leverage policy gradient methods, others focus on Direct Policy Optimization (DPO) [51]. Policy gradient methods have proven effective but are computationally expensive and require extensive hyperparameter tuning. In contrast, DPO offers a more cost-efficient alternative but consistently underperforms compared to policy gradient methods.

最近，DeepSeek-R1 [20]证明，应用大规模强化学习与格式化和仅结果奖励函数可以引导 LLM 自我涌现思维过程，实现类似人类的复杂思维链推理。该方法在复杂推理任务中取得了显著优势，展示了在大型语言模型中推进推理能力的巨大潜力。

Recently, DeepSeek-R1 [20] demonstrated that the application of large-scale reinforcement learning with formatting and result-only reward functions can guide LLMs toward the self-emergence of thought processes, enabling human-like complex chain-of-thought reasoning. This approach has achieved significant advantages in complex reasoning tasks, showcasing immense potential in advancing reasoning capabilities within Large Language Models.

对齐扩散模型和整流流。扩散模型和整流流也可以从与人类反馈的对齐中显著受益，但与 LLM 相比，探索仍处于初级阶段。该领域的关键方法包括：(1) 直接策略优化（DPO）风格[52, 14, 41, 53, 12]方法，(2) 使用奖励信号直接反向传播[54]，如 ReFL [9]，以及(3) 基于策略梯度的方法，包括 DPOK [19]和 DDPO [18]。然而，生产级模型主要依赖 DPO 和 ReFL，因为先前的策略梯度方法在大规模设置中表现出不稳定性。我们的工作解决了这一限制，提供了增强稳定性和可扩展性的稳健解决方案。我们也希望我们的工作能为其在不同模态（如图像和文本）[55, 56]中统一优化范式的潜力提供见解。

Aligning Diffusion Models and Rectified Flows. Diffusion models and rectified flows can also benefit significantly from alignment with human feedback, but the exploration remains primitive compared with LLMs. Key approaches in this area include: (1) Direct Policy Optimization (DPO)-style [52, 14, 41, 53, 12] methods, (2) direct backpropagation with reward signals [54], such as ReFL [9], and (3) policy gradient-based methods, including DPOK [19] and DDPO [18]. However, production-level models predominantly rely on DPO and ReFL, as previous policy gradient methods have demonstrated instability when applied to large-scale settings. Our work addresses this limitation, providing a robust solution to enhance stability and scalability. We also hope our work offers insights into its potential to unify optimization paradigms across different modalities (e.g. image and text) [55, 56].

5 结论与展望 5 Conclusion and Future Work

本工作开创性地将组相对策略优化（GRPO）集成到视觉生成中，建立了 DanceGRPO 作为统一框架，用于增强文本到图像、文本到视频和图像到视频任务中的扩散模型和整流流。通过弥合语言和视觉模态之间的差距，我们的方法解决了先前方法的关键局限性，通过高效对齐人类偏好和稳健扩展到复杂多任务设置实现了卓越性能。实验表明，在视觉保真度、运动质量和文本-图像对齐方面有显著改进。未来工作将探索 GRPO 在多模态生成中的扩展，进一步统一生成式 AI 中的优化范式。

This work pioneers the integration of Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) into visual generation, establishing DanceGRPO as a unified framework for enhancing diffusion models and rectified flows across text-to-image, text-to-video, and image-to-video tasks. By bridging the gap between language and visual modalities, our approach addresses critical limitations of prior methods, achieving superior performance through efficient alignment with human preferences and robust scaling to complex, multi-task settings. Experiments demonstrate substantial improvements in visual fidelity, motion quality, and text-image alignment. Future work will explore GRPO’s extension to multimodal generation, further unifying optimization paradigms across Generative AI.
