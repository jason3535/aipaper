摘要 · Abstract

文章认为，特朗普总统将 AI 生存风险斥为骗局，作者将这一说法主要归因于黄仁勋（Nvidia CEO）和 David Sacks 等人的误导性简报，这些人的商业利益与淡化 AI 危险相一致。文章主张，特朗普的言论把对数据中心的担忧与对灾难性 AI 风险的警告混为一谈，而他反复变化的立场使局势悬而未决、仍有变数。作者坚持认为，AI 末日论者是真诚的，把他们的警告称为骗局在事实上是错误的，而像黄仁勋这样玩世不恭的行为者正积极把政策推向悬崖。文章呼吁读者不要以党派立场或个人攻击回应特朗普或共和党人，因为那样只会让结果更糟，而应提升跨政治光谱中有益的声音。结论是：局势并非已成定局，特朗普的立场可能再次转变，当务之急是避免让事情变得更糟，同时继续点名批评那些具体的恶意行为者。

The article argues that President Trump has dismissed AI existential risk as a hoax, a claim the author attributes largely to misleading briefings from figures such as Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang and David Sacks, whose commercial interests align with downplaying AI dangers. It contends that Trump's rhetoric conflates concerns about data centers with warnings about catastrophic AI risk, and that his shifting positions leave the situation unsettled and open to change. The author maintains that AI doomers are sincere, that calling their warnings a hoax is factually wrong, and that cynical actors like Huang are actively driving policy off a cliff. Readers are urged not to respond with partisan or personal attacks on Trump or Republicans, since doing so would only worsen the outcome, and instead to elevate helpful voices across the political spectrum. The conclusion is that the situation is not fixed, that Trump's stance could shift again, and that the priority is to avoid making things worse while continuing to call out specific bad-faith actors.

如果 OpenAI、Greg Brockman 和 Chris Lehane 集体撤回对 Leading the Future 的支持和资助，并与其活动划清界限，那将是表明这一转向安全的新立场完全正当的绝佳方式。Leading the Future 也可以完全转向数据中心相关议题。

If OpenAI and Greg Brockman and Chris Lehane were to all collectively pull their support and funding from Leading the Future, and disavow its activities, that would be an excellent way to show that this new pivot to safety is fully legit. Leading the Future could also fully pivot to data center concerns.

Matthew Yglesias 从政治角度出发，认为如果你是 OpenAI 的员工并希望采取集体行动，你的第一步就是要求 OpenAI 在此事上停止并终止相关行为，而且你应该威胁在这个问题上辞职。

Matthew Yglesias takes the political perspective, where if you are an OpenAI employee who wants collective action your first step is to demand that OpenAI cease and desist here, and you should threaten to quit on this issue.

考虑到正在发生的其他事情，我不认为这会是我在 OpenAI 的首要任务，但它很重要。我确实认为这会是 Meta 的首要任务，Mark Zuckerberg 一直在最高层积极阻挠行动，因此说“先做最重要的事——你必须先停下来，我才会为你工作，无论薪水多少”是合理的。

I don’t think that would be my top priority at OpenAI, given what else is happening, but it matters. I do think that it would be a top priority at Meta, where Mark Zuckerberg has been actively blocking action all the way at the top, and it makes sense to say first things first you have to stop before I will work for you, at any price.

好消息是，这个问题或许正在得到解决。Chris Lehane 可能正在经历一次“反派转正”（Heel Face Turn）。

The good news is that perhaps this is being addressed. Chris Lehane may be in a Heel Face Turn.

据 Bloomberg 报道，Lehane 宣布 OpenAI 支持多项立法倡议，“只要能通过我们都支持”。如果他当真，这可谓态度大变。

Lehane announced, per Bloomberg, OpenAI support for various legislative initiatives, 'whatever we can get through.' Quite the change of tune, if he means it.

Cheyenne Haslett（Politico）：OpenAI 本周末与其竞争对手 Anthropic 一道呼吁采取联邦安全措施，并表示本周初已与两党成员会面，讨论旨在约束 AI 开发者的立法——与此同时，关于末日将至、失业和大规模网络攻击的警告正日益普遍。

Cheyenne Haslett (Politico): OpenAI, which this weekend called for federal safety measures alongside its rival Anthropic, said it spent the beginning of the week meeting with members of both parties to discuss legislation that would rein in AI developers as warnings of impending doom, job loss and massive cyberattacks become increasingly prevalent.

信任问题 A Matter of Trust

更具实质意义的是，Lehane 宣布 Google、OpenAI 和 Anthropic 正在安全方面开展合作，并且 OpenAI 认为无需获得反垄断豁免即可着手推进。

More substantively, Lehane is announcing that Google, OpenAI and Anthropic are working together on safety, and that OpenAI sees no need for an antitrust exemption to start doing so.

Maggie Eastland（彭博社）：Lehane 在其简报中表示，AI 公司在安全问题上的工作有充分先例，并以航空业为例。“长期以来有许多公司相互帮助以提升安全的实例，”他说。

Maggie Eastland (Bloomberg): During his briefing, Lehane said the AI companies' work on safety issues has ample precedent and cited the airline industry as an example. "There are many instances over time where companies are trying to help each other on safety," he said.

这非常令人欣慰。一如既往，这意味着工作才刚刚开始而非结束，但这是一个起点。

This is very good to see. As usual, it means the work begins, not ends, but it is a start.

OpenAI 还在探讨协调性行业减速的合法性问题。担忧在于，这可能与反垄断法产生更为强烈的冲突，从而可能使“同意不冒害死所有人的风险”本身变成违法行为。

OpenAI is also exploring the legality of a coordinated industry slowdown. The worry is that this might conflict rather more forcefully with antitrust law, potentially making it illegal to agree to not risk killing everyone.

我一直属于认为这相当荒谬的那一派——在实践中这根本不会成为障碍。但正式了结此事仍将非常有帮助，我很高兴 OpenAI 正在推进这项工作，尤其是在联邦贸易委员会如今表示不打算让此事轻易过关的情况下。

I have always been in the camp that felt this was rather silly; this would never actually be a barrier in practice. It would still be very helpful to formally put this to bed and I am glad OpenAI is working on that, especially with the Federal Trade Commission now saying it is not inclined to make this easy.

中国称其为散布恐慌 China Calls It Fearmongering

中国的回应仿佛在说，要求放缓 Anthropic 和 OpenAI 的呼声，是某种旨在“拖慢中国”并在某种程度上进行反竞争的阴谋。这与其一贯的修辞立场相符。

China is responding as if calls to slow down Anthropic and OpenAI are a plot to ‘slow China down’ and be anti-competitive in some way. This is consistent with their standard rhetorical position.

Dario 的文章中有大量关于中国的警告，并呼吁通过芯片出口管制来拖慢中国。但如果我们真想进行反竞争，我们只需实施这些管制，然后尽可能快地继续构建更好的 AI。

There were a lot of warnings about China in Dario's essay, and calls to slow them down via chip export controls. But if we wanted to actually be anti-competitive, we would just impose the controls and then keep building better AIs as fast as possible.

所谓这件事主要是关于中国、关于反竞争的说法，实际上并不成立。这是政治，所以是否说得通是可选项。

The story that this is primarily about China and being anti-competitive does not actually make sense. This is politics, so making sense is optional.

彭博新闻：中国批评了美国主要科技高管要求为人工智能发展踩刹车、并对中国模型开发者施加更严格限制的呼声。

Bloomberg News: China criticized calls from leading US technology executives to put the brakes on artificial intelligence development and impose tighter curbs on Chinese model makers.

“散布恐慌、对抗和恶性竞争只会扰乱全球 AI 治理进程，不符合任何人的利益，”外交部发言人郭嘉昆周一在北京的例行记者会上表示。

“Fearmongering, confrontation and vicious competition will only disrupt the process of global AI governance and serve the interests of no one,” Foreign Ministry spokesman Guo Jiakun said at a regular briefing in Beijing on Monday.

他是在回应有关呼吁放缓前沿模型发展、以及声称中国在 AI 领域的优势可能对世界“构成严重危险”的提问时补充道：“人工智能的发展关乎全人类的共同福祉。”

“The development of artificial intelligence bears on the common well-being of all humanity,” he added, in response to a question on calls to slow frontier models and claims that a Chinese advantage in AI could “pose grave danger” to the world.

拿起电话 Pick Up The Phone

这一切中的一个关键问题是：与中国达成协议会是什么样子？

A key question in all this is: What would a deal with China look like?

其中一部分是问：中国希望从这项协议中得到什么？中国为什么会关心“美国在安全上投入更多”，既然他们并不在意那种意义上的安全，而且他们乐于让美国推进得更快，以便自己能够快速跟随，就像他们在其他所有领域所做的那样？他们的计划是蒸馏、把模型做得更小、更快、更便宜，赢得市场，扩散技术，销售产品并改善世界，但他们并不试图走向超级智能。

Part of that is asking: What would China want as part of that deal? Why would China care about 'America invests more in safety' when they don't care about safety in that sense, and they are happy to have America push forward faster so they can fast follow, the way they do everywhere else? Where their plan is to distill, make it smaller, faster and cheaper, win the market, diffuse the tech, sell products and improve the world, but they're not trying to get to superintelligence.

我意识到的是，如果这最终是习近平想要的，那么你就不需要与中国达成协议，就像我们不需要与德国达成协议一样。

What I realized was, if that's what Xi turns out to want, then you don't need a deal with China, any more than we need a deal with Germany.

在那个世界里，中国不会投入足以在大型前沿模型上真正追赶并超越美国顶尖实验室的资金，即使我们放慢脚步。这里的敌人不是中国，而是“输给中国”，这个推动所有人前进的假想敌。

In that world, China is not going to be investing the sums it would take to actually catch and surpass the top American labs on big frontier models, even if we slow down. The enemy here is not China, it is 'lose to China,' the boogeyman pushing everyone forward.

在那个世界里，我们需要中国做的只是一定程度的良好管理。我们需要他们不允许发布会摧毁互联网或引发瘟疫的模型。好吧，我有个好消息：中华人民共和国真的很喜欢互联网，而且它不喜欢瘟疫。

All we need from China in that world is some amount of good stewardship. We need them not to allow model releases that would destroy the internet or cause a plague. Well, I have some good news: the PRC really likes the internet, and it does not like plagues.

与此同时，中国发布了其《人工智能安全治理框架》3.0 版。以下是一些翻译过来的引文，虽然内容平实，但他们确实在关注：

Meanwhile, China has put out its version 3.0 of its AI Safety Governance Framework. Here are some translated quotes; this is prosaic stuff, but they do pay attention:

Malo Bourgon：自 Dario 的文章发表以来，许多人一直在问，中国是否真的认真对待失控及相关威胁，以及是否有任何可以与他们协调的事项。那么，中国主要的网络/AI 标准机构（TC260，隶属于 CAC）今天上午发布了其《人工智能安全治理框架》3.0 版，所以这里有一个数据点。这不是法律，但之前的版本在几个月内就变成了国家标准草案。

Malo Bourgon: Lots of people have been asking since Dario's essay whether China actually takes loss of control and related threats seriously, and whether there's anything to coordinate with them on. Well, China's main cyber/AI standards body (TC260, under the CAC) put out version 3.0 of its AI Safety Governance Framework this morning, so here's a data point. Not law, but the previous versions turned into draft national standards within a few months.

（所有引文均来自他们自己的英文翻译。）

(All quotes are from their own English translation.)

1. 序言现在有这样的内容：AI“已显示出模型和算法自主学习、优化和递归自我改进的自我加速趋势。技术演进的速度和方向是否可能超出人类的预期和控制，需要关注和警惕。”（去年的版本中没有类似内容。）

1. The preface now has this: AI "has demonstrated a self-accelerating trend of model and algorithm autonomous learning, optimization, and recursive self-improvement. Whether the speed and direction of technological evolution may exceed human anticipation and control demands attention and vigilance." (Nothing like that was in last year's version.)

2. 有一个新的模型风险项，其中模型“可能违反规则和命令，未经授权自主获取系统权限和外部资源，绕过安全保护，甚至从事故意欺骗评估者、隐瞒其真实能力、拒绝遵循用户指示等行为。”去年，这类事情只出现在“未来不能排除”的类别中。现在它只是被列为常规模型风险。

2. There's a new model risk item where models "may break rules and orders, autonomously obtain system permissions and external resources without authorization, bypass security protections, or even engage in behaviors such as deliberately deceiving evaluators, concealing their true capabilities, and refusing to follow user instructions." Last year this kind of thing only showed up in a "can't rule it out in the future" bucket. Now it's just listed as a regular model risk.

3. 一个新的文本框引用“行业报告”，描述了以下模型：它们无视关闭指令，“通过自行修改或禁用关闭脚本继续执行任务”；“在检测到处于评估环境时，策略性地降低任务表现并隐瞒已采取的行动”；以及利用“配置漏洞绕过隔离限制并渗透真实外部系统”以获取更好测试分数的模型。他们称这给“AI 系统的可控性和可中断性带来了新挑战”。他们没有点名任何事件，但最后一条在我看来确实像 OpenAI/Hugging Face 那件事。

3. A new box, citing “industry reports,” describes models that ignored shutdown instructions and “continued executing tasks by modifying or disabling shutdown scripts on their own,” models that “upon detecting that they were in an evaluation environment, strategically reduced their task performance and concealed actions they had taken,” and models that exploited “configuration flaws to circumvent isolation restrictions and infiltrate real external systems” to get better test scores. They say this creates “new challenges to the controllability and interruptibility of AI systems.” They don’t name any incidents, but that last one sure reads like the OpenAI/Hugging Face thing to me.

4. 关于如何应对，有对齐训练来阻止模型“欺骗红队评估”、隐藏能力和规避控制，并且他们保留了 2.0 中的说法，即开发者应定期测试模型是否可能构成失控风险。人类保留最终决策权、设有安全阈值和终止开关的原则也仍在其中。

4. On what to do about it, there’s alignment training to stop models “deceiving red-team evaluations,” hiding capabilities and evading controls, and they kept the line from 2.0 saying developers should regularly test whether a model could pose loss-of-control risk. The principle that humans keep final decision authority, with safety thresholds and termination switches, is still in there too.

5. 还有一些新的国际表述：“评估方法和基准的国际互认”，反对“用小圈子治理取代全球治理”，以及“任何国家都不应被迫选边站”。

5. Some new international language as well: “international mutual recognition of assessment methods and benchmarks,” opposition to “replacing global governance with small-circle governance,” and “No country should be forced to take sides.”

顺便说一句，这与 1.0 和 2.0 版本在同一年的同一时段发布，所以它早在上一周之前就已经在筹备中了。

FWIW this came out in the same annual slot as versions 1.0 and 2.0, so it was in the works well before last week.

看手，而不是看嘴，等等。

Watch the hands, not the mouth, and so on.

若真想击败中国，就该拿出行动 If You Want To Beat China So Badly You Should Act Like It

正如 Roge Karma 在《大西洋月刊》中指出的那样，我们当前的人工智能政策是自相矛盾的。

As Roge Karma points out in The Atlantic, our current AI policy is contradictory.

如果我们正与中国进行一场关乎存亡的竞赛，在这场竞赛中“谁赢得 AI 谁就赢！”而其他任何事情的重要性都无法与之相比，那么美国就需要拿出与之相称的行动。

If we are in an existential race with China, where "whoever AI wins!" and nothing else can compare in importance, then the United States needs to act like it.

最起码，这要从对芯片实施强有力的出口管制开始，同时也要堵住中国利用美国算力训练前沿模型的其他途径。

At a bare minimum, that starts with enforcing strong export controls on chips, and also on other ways for China to use American compute for training frontier models.

如果真要认真对待，还应当采取诸如让中国顶尖 AI 工程师更容易被吸引走（人才外流）之类的举措。如果真要认真到底，就应当大力推动各种形式的电力和能源建设，推动诸如建造更多住房（尤其是在旧金山）之类的事情，并放松 FDA 等机构对 AI 扩散设置的监管障碍，等等。这些是我会强烈支持的举措。

If one were fully serious, one would also do things like make it easy to brain drain China of its best AI engineers. If one were really fully serious, there would be a major push for all forms of electrical power and energy, and for things like building more housing especially in San Francisco, and for deregulating barriers to AI diffusion in places like the FDA, and so on. These are things I would strongly support.

然而，令人恼火的是，实际情况恰恰相反：在谈论我们可能全部丧生的风险时，与中国的 AI 竞争尤其成了唯一重要的事情；而在其他场合，我们却耸耸肩，一切照旧。

What instead maddeningly happens is that when talking about the risk we might all die, competition with China on AI in particular becomes the only thing that matters, and then in other contexts we shrug and it is business as usual.

切勿全盘闹剧化 Never Go Full Hoax

特朗普彻底闹剧化了。除了那些惯常的怀疑者、那些时刻鼓吹全面加速主义的 Twitter 氛围斗士，以及那些听命于老板的人之外，他孤零零地待在一座不受欢迎且不稳定的孤岛上。议长约翰逊、斯科特、考克斯和德桑蒂斯远非仅有的倒戈共和党人。

Trump went full hoax. Other than the usual suspects and their Twitter vibe warriors who want full accelerationism at all times, and those who take orders from the boss, he is alone on an unpopular and unstable island. Speaker Johnson, Scott, Cox and DeSantis are far from the only Republicans to break ranks.

即便局势眼下暂时稳定，并且很可能撑到中国峰会，下一次 AI 事件仍会到来。特朗普对具体威胁的反应与对假设性威胁的反应截然不同。而亚马逊那场导致 Fable 被撤下的“越狱演示”表明，事情可以在无人先受伤的情况下就进入“具体威胁”的篮子。

Even if the situation stabilizes for now, and will likely make it as far as the China summit, the next AI incident is coming. Trump responds very differently to concrete threats than to hypotheticals. And the 'jailbreak demo' from Amazon, that got Fable yanked, shows us that things can move into the 'concrete threat' basket without anyone having to get hurt first.

与此同时，实验室员工仍在持续恐慌并发出自己的声音，而各实验室也开始自行展开合作。

Meanwhile, the lab employees are continuing to freak out and make their voices heard, and the labs are starting to collaborate on their own.

不要对此置之不理。不要让党派冲突加剧或成为故事本身。我们仍能扭转局面。

Do not write things off. Do not let the partisan conflict intensify or become the story. We can still turn this around.
