摘要 · Abstract

我们提出了一种替代的生成对抗网络生成器架构，借鉴了风格迁移文献。新架构自动学习并实现了生成图像中高层属性（例如，在人类面部训练时的姿态和身份）与随机变化（例如，雀斑、头发）的无监督分离，并实现了直观的、尺度特定的合成控制。新生成器在传统分布质量指标上提升了最先进水平，展示了更好的插值特性，并更好地解耦了潜在因素的变化。为了量化插值质量和解耦程度，我们提出了两种新的自动化方法，适用于任何生成器架构。最后，我们引入了一个新的、高度多样化和高质量的人脸数据集。

We propose an alternative generator architecture for generative adversarial networks, borrowing from style transfer literature. The new architecture leads to an automatically learned, unsupervised separation of high-level attributes (e.g., pose and identity when trained on human faces) and stochastic variation in the generated images (e.g., freckles, hair), and it enables intuitive, scale-specific control of the synthesis. The new generator improves the state-of-the-art in terms of traditional distribution quality metrics, leads to demonstrably better interpolation properties, and also better disentangles the latent factors of variation. To quantify interpolation quality and disentanglement, we propose two new, automated methods that are applicable to any generator architecture. Finally, we introduce a new, highly varied and high-quality dataset of human faces.

摘要 Abstract

我们提出了一种生成对抗网络的替代生成器架构，借鉴了风格迁移文献。新架构能够自动学习、无监督地分离生成图像中的高级属性（例如，在训练人脸时，姿态和身份）与随机变化（例如，雀斑、头发），并实现了直观的、尺度特定的合成控制。新的生成器在传统分布质量指标方面提升了现有技术水平，展现出明显更好的插值特性，并且更好地解开了潜在变化因素。为了量化插值质量和解缠结程度，我们提出了两种新的自动化方法，适用于任何生成器架构。最后，我们引入了一个新的、高度多样且高质量的人脸数据集。

We propose an alternative generator architecture for generative adversarial networks, borrowing from style transfer literature. The new architecture leads to an automatically learned, unsupervised separation of high-level attributes (e.g., pose and identity when trained on human faces) and stochastic variation in the generated images (e.g., freckles, hair), and it enables intuitive, scale-specific control of the synthesis. The new generator improves the state-of-the-art in terms of traditional distribution quality metrics, leads to demonstrably better interpolation properties, and also better disentangles the latent factors of variation. To quantify interpolation quality and disentanglement, we propose two new, automated methods that are applicable to any generator architecture. 1 引言 1 Introduction

生成方法——尤其是生成对抗网络（GAN）[22]——产生的图像分辨率和质量最近得到了快速提升[30, 45, 5]。然而，生成器仍然作为黑箱运行，尽管最近有了一些努力[3]，但对图像合成过程各个方面的理解，例如随机特征的起源，仍然缺乏。潜在空间的特性也知之甚少，通常展示的潜在空间插值[13, 52, 37]没有提供定量方法来比较不同生成器。

The resolution and quality of images produced by generative methods — especially generative adversarial networks (GAN) [22] — have seen rapid improvement recently [30, 45, 5]. Yet the generators continue to operate as black boxes, and despite recent efforts [3], the understanding of various aspects of the image synthesis process, e.g., the origin of stochastic features, is still lacking. The properties of the latent space are also poorly understood, and the commonly demonstrated latent space interpolations [13, 52, 37] provide no quantitative way to compare different generators against each other.

受风格迁移文献[27]的启发，我们重新设计了生成器架构，以暴露控制图像合成过程的新方法。我们的生成器从一个学习到的常数输入开始，并根据潜在代码调整每个卷积层的图像“风格”，从而直接控制不同尺度上图像特征的强度。结合直接注入网络的噪声，这种架构变化导致生成图像中高级属性（例如，姿态、身份）与随机变化（例如，雀斑、头发）的自动、无监督分离，并实现了直观的尺度特定混合和插值操作。我们没有以任何方式修改判别器或损失函数，因此我们的工作与当前关于 GAN 损失函数、正则化和超参数的讨论是正交的[24, 45, 5, 40, 44, 36]。

Motivated by style transfer literature [27], we re-design the generator architecture in a way that exposes novel ways to control the image synthesis process. Our generator starts from a learned constant input and adjusts the “style” of the image at each convolution layer based on the latent code, therefore directly controlling the strength of image features at different scales. Combined with noise injected directly into the network, this architectural change leads to automatic, unsupervised separation of high-level attributes (e.g., pose, identity) from stochastic variation (e.g., freckles, hair) in the generated images, and enables intuitive scale-specific mixing and interpolation operations. We do not modify the discriminator or the loss function in any way, and our work is thus orthogonal to the ongoing discussion about GAN loss functions, regularization, and hyper-parameters [24, 45, 5, 40, 44, 36].

我们的生成器将输入潜在代码嵌入到一个中间潜在空间中，这对网络中变化因素的表示方式产生了深远影响。输入潜在空间必须遵循训练数据的概率密度，我们认为这会导致一定程度的不可避免的纠缠。我们的中间潜在空间不受此限制，因此可以解缠结。由于先前估计潜在空间解缠结程度的方法不直接适用于我们的情况，我们提出了两种新的自动化指标——感知路径长度和线性可分离性——用于量化生成器的这些方面。使用这些指标，我们表明，与传统生成器架构相比，我们的生成器允许对不同变化因素进行更线性、更少纠缠的表示。

Our generator embeds the input latent code into an intermediate latent space, which has a profound effect on how the factors of variation are represented in the network. The input latent space must follow the probability density of the training data, and we argue that this leads to some degree of unavoidable entanglement. Our intermediate latent space is free from that restriction and is therefore allowed to be disentangled. As previous methods for estimating the degree of latent space disentanglement are not directly applicable in our case, we propose two new automated metrics — perceptual path length and linear separability — for quantifying these aspects of the generator. Using these metrics, we show that compared to a traditional generator architecture, our generator admits a more linear, less entangled representation of different factors of variation.

最后，我们提出了一个新的人脸数据集（Flickr-Faces-HQ, FFHQ），其质量远高于现有高分辨率数据集，并且涵盖了更广泛的变化（附录 A）。我们已经公开了这个数据集，以及我们的源代码和预训练网络。111https://github.com/NVlabs/stylegan 相关视频可在同一链接下找到。

Finally, we present a new dataset of human faces (Flickr-Faces-HQ, FFHQ) that offers much higher quality and covers considerably wider variation than existing high-resolution datasets (Appendix A). We have made this dataset publicly available, along with our source code and pre-trained networks.111https://github.com/NVlabs/stylegan The accompanying video can be found under the same link.

2.2 现有技术 2.2 Prior art

关于 GAN 架构的许多工作都集中在改进判别器上，例如，使用多个判别器[18, 47, 11]、多分辨率判别[60, 55]或自注意力[63]。生成器方面的工作主要集中在输入潜在空间中的精确分布[5]或通过高斯混合模型[4]、聚类[48]或鼓励凸性[52]来塑造输入潜在空间。

Much of the work on GAN architectures has focused on improving the discriminator by, e.g., using multiple discriminators [18, 47, 11], multiresolution discrimination [60, 55], or self-attention [63]. The work on generator side has mostly focused on the exact distribution in the input latent space [5] or shaping the input latent space via Gaussian mixture models [4], clustering [48], or encouraging convexity [52].

最近的条件生成器通过单独的嵌入网络将类别标识符馈送到生成器中的大量层[46]，而潜在代码仍然通过输入层提供。一些作者考虑了将部分潜在代码馈送到多个生成器层[9, 5]。在并行工作中，Chen 等人[6]使用 AdaIN 对生成器进行“自调制”，类似于我们的工作，但没有考虑中间潜在空间或噪声输入。

Recent conditional generators feed the class identifier through a separate embedding network to a large number of layers in the generator [46], while the latent is still provided though the input layer. A few authors have considered feeding parts of the latent code to multiple generator layers [9, 5]. In parallel work, Chen et al. [6] “self modulate” the generator using AdaINs, similarly to our work, but do not consider an intermediate latent space or noise inputs.

3 基于样式的生成器的特性 3 Properties of the style-based generator

我们的生成器架构使得通过尺度特定的风格修改来控制图像合成成为可能。我们可以将映射网络和仿射变换视为从学习到的分布中为每种风格抽取样本的方式，而合成网络则是基于一组风格生成新图像的方式。每种风格的效果在网络中是局部化的，即修改特定的风格子集可以预期只影响图像的某些方面。

Our generator architecture makes it possible to control the image synthesis via scale-specific modifications to the styles. We can view the mapping network and affine transformations as a way to draw samples for each style from a learned distribution, and the synthesis network as a way to generate a novel image based on a collection of styles. The effects of each style are localized in the network, i.e., modifying a specific subset of the styles can be expected to affect only certain aspects of the image.

为了理解这种局部化的原因，我们考虑 AdaIN 操作（公式 1）首先将每个通道归一化为零均值和单位方差，然后仅根据风格应用缩放和偏置。由风格决定的新的逐通道统计量修改了特征对于后续卷积操作的相对重要性，但由于归一化，它们不依赖于原始统计量。因此，每个风格在下一个 AdaIN 操作覆盖之前仅控制一个卷积。

To see the reason for this localization, let us consider how the AdaIN operation (Eq. 1) first normalizes each channel to zero mean and unit variance, and only then applies scales and biases based on the style. The new per-channel statistics, as dictated by the style, modify the relative importance of features for the subsequent convolution operation, but they do not depend on the original statistics because of the normalization. Thus each style controls only one convolution before being overridden by the next AdaIN operation.

3.1 样式混合 3.1 Style mixing

为了进一步鼓励风格局部化，我们采用了混合正则化，即在训练过程中，给定百分比的图像使用两个随机潜码而非一个生成。生成此类图像时，我们在合成网络中的随机选择点从一个潜码切换到另一个——我们称之为风格混合的操作。具体来说，我们将两个潜码𝐳₁,𝐳₂通过映射网络，并让对应的𝐰₁,𝐰₂控制风格，使得𝐰₁在交叉点之前应用，𝐰₂在之后应用。这种正则化技术防止网络假设相邻风格是相关的。

To further encourage the styles to localize, we employ mixing regularization, where a given percentage of images are generated using two random latent codes instead of one during training. When generating such an image, we simply switch from one latent code to another — an operation we refer to as style mixing — at a randomly selected point in the synthesis network. To be specific, we run two latent codes 𝐳1,𝐳2subscript𝐳1subscript𝐳2{\bf z}_{1},{\bf z}_{2} through the mapping network, and have the corresponding 𝐰1,𝐰2subscript𝐰1subscript𝐰2{\bf w}_{1},{\bf w}_{2} control the styles so that 𝐰1subscript𝐰1{\bf w}_{1} applies before the crossover point and 𝐰2subscript𝐰2{\bf w}_{2} after it. This regularization technique prevents the network from assuming that adjacent styles are correlated.

3.2 随机变化 3.2 Stochastic variation

人像中有许多方面可以被视为随机的，例如头发、胡茬、雀斑或皮肤毛孔的精确位置。只要遵循正确的分布，这些都可以随机化而不影响我们对图像的感知。

There are many aspects in human portraits that can be regarded as stochastic, such as the exact placement of hairs, stubble, freckles, or skin pores. Any of these can be randomized without affecting our perception of the image as long as they follow the correct distribution.

让我们考虑传统生成器如何实现随机变化。鉴于网络的唯一输入是通过输入层，网络需要发明一种方法，在需要时从早期激活生成空间变化的伪随机数。这会消耗网络容量，并且隐藏生成信号的周期性很困难——而且并不总是成功，正如生成图像中常见的重复模式所证明的那样。我们的架构通过在每次卷积后添加逐像素噪声完全避免了这些问题。

Let us consider how a traditional generator implements stochastic variation. Given that the only input to the network is through the input layer, the network needs to invent a way to generate spatially-varying pseudorandom numbers from earlier activations whenever they are needed. This consumes network capacity and hiding the periodicity of generated signal is difficult — and not always successful, as evidenced by commonly seen repetitive patterns in generated images. Our architecture sidesteps these issues altogether by adding per-pixel noise after each convolution.

我们发现有趣的是，噪声的影响在网络中似乎高度局部化。我们假设，在生成器的任何点，都存在尽快引入新内容的压力，而我们的网络创建随机变化的最简单方式是依赖提供的噪声。每一层都有新的噪声集可用，因此没有动机从早期激活生成随机效果，从而导致局部化效果。

We find it interesting that the effect of noise appears tightly localized in the network. We hypothesize that at any point in the generator, there is pressure to introduce new content as soon as possible, and the easiest way for our network to create stochastic variation is to rely on the noise provided. A fresh set of noise is available for every layer, and thus there is no incentive to generate the stochastic effects from earlier activations, leading to a localized effect.

3.3 全局效果与随机性的分离 3.3 Separation of global effects from stochasticity

前面的部分以及随附的视频表明，虽然对风格的改变具有全局效果（改变姿势、身份等），但噪声仅影响无关紧要的随机变化（不同梳理的头发、胡须等）。这一观察与风格迁移文献一致，其中已确定空间不变统计量（Gram 矩阵、通道均值、方差等）可靠地编码图像的风格[20, 39]，而空间变化特征编码特定实例。

The previous sections as well as the accompanying video demonstrate that while changes to the style have global effects (changing pose, identity, etc.), the noise affects only inconsequential stochastic variation (differently combed hair, beard, etc.). This observation is in line with style transfer literature, where it has been established that spatially invariant statistics (Gram matrix, channel-wise mean, variance, etc.) reliably encode the style of an image [20, 39] while spatially varying features encode a specific instance.

在我们的基于风格的生成器中，风格影响整个图像，因为完整的特征图使用相同的值进行缩放和偏置。因此，全局效果如姿势、光照或背景风格可以连贯地控制。同时，噪声独立地添加到每个像素，因此非常适合控制随机变化。如果网络试图使用噪声控制姿势，那将导致空间不一致的决策，然后被判别器惩罚。因此，网络学会适当地使用全局和局部通道，无需明确指导。

In our style-based generator, the style affects the entire image because complete feature maps are scaled and biased with the same values. Therefore, global effects such as pose, lighting, or background style can be controlled coherently. Meanwhile, the noise is added independently to each pixel and is thus ideally suited for controlling stochastic variation. If the network tried to control, e.g., pose using the noise, that would lead to spatially inconsistent decisions that would then be penalized by the discriminator. Thus the network learns to use the global and local channels appropriately, without explicit guidance.

4 解耦研究 4 Disentanglement studies

解缠结有多种定义[54, 50, 2, 7, 19]，但一个共同目标是潜空间由线性子空间组成，每个子空间控制一个变化因子。然而，𝒵中每个因子组合的采样概率需要与训练数据中的相应密度匹配。如图 6 所示，这阻止了因子在典型数据集和输入潜分布下完全解缠结。²²为解缠结研究设计的少数人工数据集（例如[43, 19]）以均匀频率列出所有预定义变化因子的组合，从而隐藏了问题。

There are various definitions for disentanglement [54, 50, 2, 7, 19], but a common goal is a latent space that consists of linear subspaces, each of which controls one factor of variation. However, the sampling probability of each combination of factors in 𝒵𝒵\mathcal{Z} needs to match the corresponding density in the training data. As illustrated in Figure 6, this precludes the factors from being fully disentangled with typical datasets and input latent distributions.222The few artificial datasets designed for disentanglement studies (e.g., [43, 19]) tabulate all combinations of predetermined factors of variation with uniform frequency, thus hiding the problem.

我们生成器架构的一个主要好处是中间潜空间𝒲不必支持根据任何固定分布采样；其采样密度由学习到的分段连续映射 f(𝐳)诱导。这个映射可以适应“解扭曲”𝒲，使得变化因子变得更加线性。我们假设生成器有压力这样做，因为基于解缠结表示生成真实图像应该比基于缠结表示更容易。因此，我们期望训练在无监督设置下产生更少缠结的𝒲，即当变化因子事先未知时[10, 35, 49, 8, 26, 32, 7]。

A major benefit of our generator architecture is that the intermediate latent space 𝒲𝒲\mathcal{W} does not have to support sampling according to any fixed distribution; its sampling density is induced by the learned piecewise continuous mapping f​(𝐳)𝑓𝐳f({\bf z}). This mapping can be adapted to “unwarp” 𝒲𝒲\mathcal{W} so that the factors of variation become more linear. We posit that there is pressure for the generator to do so, as it should be easier to generate realistic images based on a disentangled representation than based on an entangled representation. As such, we expect the training to yield a less entangled 𝒲𝒲\mathcal{W} in an unsupervised setting, i.e., when the factors of variation are not known in advance [10, 35, 49, 8, 26, 32, 7].

不幸的是，最近提出的用于量化解缠结的度量[26, 32, 7, 19]需要将输入图像映射到潜码的编码器网络。这些度量不适合我们的目的，因为我们的基线 GAN 缺乏这样的编码器。虽然可以为此添加额外的网络[8, 12, 15]，但我们希望避免将精力投入到不是实际解决方案一部分的组件上。为此，我们描述了两种量化解缠结的新方法，这两种方法都不需要编码器或已知的变化因子，因此对于任何图像数据集和生成器都是可计算的。

Unfortunately the metrics recently proposed for quantifying disentanglement [26, 32, 7, 19] require an encoder network that maps input images to latent codes. These metrics are ill-suited for our purposes since our baseline GAN lacks such an encoder. While it is possible to add an extra network for this purpose [8, 12, 15], we want to avoid investing effort into a component that is not a part of the actual solution. To this end, we describe two new ways of quantifying disentanglement, neither of which requires an encoder or known factors of variation, and are therefore computable for any image dataset and generator.

As noted by Laine [37], interpolation of latent-space vectors may yield surprisingly non-linear changes in the image. 4.2 线性可分性 4.2 Linear separability

如果潜空间足够解缠结，应该可以找到一致对应于各个变化因子的方向向量。我们提出另一个度量，通过测量潜空间点可以通过线性超平面分离成两个不同的集合的程度来量化这种效果，使得每个集合对应于图像的特定二元属性。

If a latent space is sufficiently disentangled, it should be possible to find direction vectors that consistently correspond to individual factors of variation. We propose another metric that quantifies this effect by measuring how well the latent-space points can be separated into two distinct sets via a linear hyperplane, so that each set corresponds to a specific binary attribute of the image.

为了对生成的图像进行标注，我们针对多个二元属性训练了辅助分类网络，例如区分男性和女性面孔。在我们的测试中，分类器采用了与判别器相同的架构（即与[30]相同），并使用 CelebA-HQ 数据集进行训练，该数据集保留了原始 CelebA 数据集中的 40 个属性。为了衡量一个属性的可分离性，我们生成 200,000 张图像，其中𝐳∼P(𝐳)，并使用辅助分类网络对其进行分类。然后根据分类器的置信度对样本进行排序，并移除置信度最低的一半，得到 100,000 个带标签的潜空间向量。

In order to label the generated images, we train auxiliary classification networks for a number of binary attributes, e.g., to distinguish male and female faces. In our tests, the classifiers had the same architecture as the discriminator we use (i.e., same as in [30]), and were trained using the CelebA-HQ dataset that retains the 40 attributes available in the original CelebA dataset. To measure the separability of one attribute, we generate 200,000 images with 𝐳∼P​(𝐳)similar-to𝐳𝑃𝐳{\bf z}\sim P({\bf z}) and classify them using the auxiliary classification network. We then sort the samples according to classifier confidence and remove the least confident half, yielding 100,000 labeled latent-space vectors.

对于每个属性，我们拟合一个线性 SVM，根据潜空间点（传统方法为𝐳，基于风格的方法为𝐰）预测标签，并通过该平面对点进行分类。然后计算条件熵 H(Y|X)，其中 X 是 SVM 预测的类别，Y 是由预训练分类器确定的类别。这告诉我们，在已知样本位于超平面哪一侧的情况下，确定其真实类别还需要多少额外信息。低值表明相应的变化因素在潜空间中具有一致的方向。

5 结论 5 Conclusion

基于我们的结果以及 Chen 等人[6]的并行工作，越来越清楚的是，传统的 GAN 生成器架构在各个方面都逊色于基于风格的设计。这在既定的质量指标上是成立的，我们进一步相信，我们对高级属性和随机效应分离以及中间潜空间线性度的研究，将有助于提高对 GAN 合成的理解和可控性。

Based on both our results and parallel work by Chen et al. [6], it is becoming clear that the traditional GAN generator architecture is in every way inferior to a style-based design. This is true in terms of established quality metrics, and we further believe that our investigations to the separation of high-level attributes and stochastic effects, as well as the linearity of the intermediate latent space will prove fruitful in improving the understanding and controllability of GAN synthesis.

我们注意到，我们的平均路径长度指标可以很容易地在训练过程中用作正则化项，也许线性可分离性指标的某种变体也可以。总的来说，我们期望在训练过程中直接塑造中间潜空间的方法将为未来的工作提供有趣的途径。

We note that our average path length metric could easily be used as a regularizer during training, and perhaps some variant of the linear separability metric could act as one, too. In general, we expect that methods for directly shaping the intermediate latent space during training will provide interesting avenues for future work.

这些数据集使用与 FFHQ 相同的设置进行训练，Bedroom 和 Cats 的训练时长为 7000 万张图像，Cars 为 4600 万张。我们怀疑 Bedroom 的结果开始接近训练数据的极限，因为在许多图像中，最令人反感的问题是继承自低质量训练数据的严重压缩伪影。Cars 具有更高质量的训练数据，也允许更高的空间分辨率（512×384 而不是 256^2），而 Cats 由于姿势、缩放级别和背景的高度内在变化，仍然是一个困难的数据集。

These datasets were trained using the same setup as FFHQ for the duration of 70M images for Bedroom and Cats, and 46M for Cars. We suspect that the results for Bedroom are starting to approach the limits of the training data, as in many images the most objectionable issues are the severe compression artifacts that have been inherited from the low-quality training data.
