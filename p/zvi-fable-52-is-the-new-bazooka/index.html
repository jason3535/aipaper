摘要 · Abstract

本文评测了 Claude Fable 5.2，将其定位为应对最艰难技术任务的高端“火箭筒”模型，而非日常默认主力。核心论点是：在许多日常基准测试中，Fable 5.2 相较 Opus 5.5 仅有小幅提升，但在困难、复杂或长周期工作上带来大幅改进，涵盖远程劳动指数、科学与编程基准、机器人控制以及网络安全等领域。它还比 Fable 5.1 更便宜，速度最高快 30%，并提供可选的高价模式将速度翻倍。作者结论是：多数用户应默认使用中等投入程度的 Opus 5.5，将 Fable 5.2 留给雄心勃勃的技术项目，并根据个人能等待回复的时长来选择模型与投入程度。

This article reviews Claude Fable 5.2, positioning it as a high-end "bazooka" model for the hardest technical tasks rather than a default daily driver. The core argument is that Fable 5.2 offers only modest gains over Opus 5.5 on many everyday benchmarks, but delivers large improvements on difficult, complex, or long-horizon work, including the Remote Labor Index, science and coding benchmarks, robotics control, and cybersecurity. It is also cheaper and up to 30% faster than Fable 5.1, with an optional higher-price mode that doubles speed. The author concludes that most users should default to Opus 5.5 at medium effort, reserve Fable 5.2 for ambitious technical projects, and choose model and effort level based on how long a person can wait for a reply.
