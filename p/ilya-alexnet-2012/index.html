使用深度卷积神经网络的ImageNet分类

ImageNet Classification with Deep Convolutional Neural Networks

伊利亚·苏茨克维尔 Ilya Sutskever · NeurIPS (2012) · 2012-12-01 · NeurIPS 2012 ↗

摘要 · Abstract

我们训练了一个大型深度卷积神经网络，将ImageNet LSVRC-2010竞赛中的120万张高分辨率图像分类到1000个不同的类别中。在测试数据上，我们取得了top-1和top-5错误率分别为37.5%和17.0%，这远优于之前的最佳水平。该神经网络拥有6000万个参数和65万个神经元，由五个卷积层（其中一些后面跟着最大池化层）和三个全连接层组成，最后是一个1000路的softmax层。为了加速训练，我们使用了非饱和神经元和非常高效的GPU卷积实现。为了减少全连接层中的过拟合，我们采用了一种最近开发的正则化方法“dropout”，该方法被证明非常有效。我们还将该模型的一个变体参加了ILSVRC-2012竞赛，并以15.3%的获胜top-5测试错误率取得了胜利，而第二名的最佳成绩为26.2%。

We trained a large, deep convolutional neural network to classify the 1.2 million high-resolution images in the ImageNet LSVRC-2010 contest into the 1000 dif-ferent classes. On the test data, we achieved top-1 and top-5 error rates of 37.5% and 17.0% which is considerably better than the previous state-of-the-art. The neural network, which has 60 million parameters and 650,000 neurons, consists of five convolutional layers, some of which are followed by max-pooling layers, and three fully-connected layers with a final 1000-way softmax. To make train-ing faster, we used non-saturating neurons and a very efficient GPU implemen-tation of the convolution operation. To reduce overfitting in the fully-connected layers we employed a recently-developed regularization method called “dropout” that proved to be very effective. We also entered a variant of this model in the ILSVRC-2012 competition and achieved a winning top-5 test error rate of 15.3%, compared to 26.2% achieved by the second-best entry.

核心贡献 · Key contributions

  • 训练了大型深度卷积神经网络，在ImageNet上达到最佳性能。
    Trained a large deep CNN achieving state-of-the-art on ImageNet.
  • 引入ReLU激活函数大幅加速训练。
    Introduced ReLU activation to speed up training significantly.
  • 使用GPU并行在两块显卡上处理6000万参数。
    Used GPU parallelism to handle 60M parameters across two GPUs.
  • 采用数据增强和Dropout减少过拟合。
    Employed data augmentation and dropout to reduce overfitting.
  • 在ILSVRC-2012中以15.3%的top-5错误率获胜，比第二名低10.9%.
    Won ILSVRC-2012 with 15.3% top-5 error, outperforming second best by 10.9%.

局限 · Limitations

  • 需要昂贵的GPU和5-6天训练时间。
    Requires expensive GPUs and 5-6 days of training time.
  • 尽管使用Dropout和数据增强，过拟合仍是挑战。
    Overfitting remains a challenge despite dropout and data augmentation.
  • 架构大小受限于GPU内存（每块3GB）。
    Architecture size limited by GPU memory (3GB per GPU).
  • 移除任何卷积层都会使top-1性能下降约2%。
    Removing any convolutional layer degrades top-1 performance by 2%.
  • 未使用无监督预训练，完全依赖监督学习。
    No unsupervised pre-training used; relies purely on supervised learning.
