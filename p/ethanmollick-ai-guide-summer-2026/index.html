An opinionated guide to which AI to use to do stuff (Summer 2026)

核心贡献 · Key contributions

  • 指出智能体式系统是主要转变，将AI模型与工具结合以完成数小时工作。
    Marks agentic systems as the main shift, combining AI models with tools to complete hours of work.
  • 推荐ChatGPT和Claude作为实际工作的仅有两个实用选择，并给出具体模式与模型。
    Recommends ChatGPT and Claude as the only two practical choices for real work, with specific modes and models.
  • 强调权限设置和默认审批对于防止错误及提示注入攻击至关重要。
    Highlights permission settings and approval defaults as essential to prevent mistakes and prompt injection attacks.
  • 展示智能体在校对、电脑修复及操作电脑方面的能力，同时强调需人工审查。
    Demonstrates agent capability in proofreading, computer fixes, and computer use, while stressing human review.
  • 指出Google的NotebookLM和Gemini Omni是有用的特定工具，而Copilot和开放权重模型落后。
    Identifies Google's NotebookLM and Gemini Omni as useful niche tools, while Copilot and open-weights lag behind.
  • 建议支付20美元，委派一个真实任务，并像管理人一样与智能体迭代。
    Advises paying 20 dollars, delegating one real task, and iterating with the agent like managing a person.

局限 · Limitations

  • 20美元档的智能体用量有限，更贵的套餐主要是购买更多工时而非更智能的AI。
    Agent usage at the $20 tier is limited, and costlier plans buy more hours rather than smarter AI.
  • 提示注入和不受限的电脑操作仍是安全担忧，尽管模型抗性有所提升。
    Prompt injection and unfettered computer use remain security concerns despite improved model resistance.
  • 这些系统文档匮乏且命名混乱，使选择和导航变得不必要地复杂。
    Systems are badly documented and confusingly named, making selection and navigation unnecessarily complicated.
  • 人工监督至关重要，但在长期自主任务中可能成为瓶颈。
    Human oversight is essential but may become a bottleneck for long-horizon autonomous tasks.
  • Google、Copilot和开放权重模型在智能体式能力上有明显差距；开放权重需要专业知识才能有效使用。
    Google, Copilot, and open-weight models have notable gaps in agentic abilities; open weights require expertise.
