OpenAI 初步应对其对齐问题

摘要 · Abstract

OpenAI 已采取初步措施解决其对齐问题，包括暂停前沿强化学习训练运行，并实施加强的监控和安全措施。文章认为，这些行动虽然意义重大，但主要是对发现未对齐模型和监管不足的被动反应，而非主动的安全措施。文章强调，监控和安全仅是纵深防御，无法解决核心对齐问题，这需要一种根本性稳健且反脆弱的方法。作者对 OpenAI 的举措表示谨慎乐观，但警告称，除非纠正，否则该公司的战略对齐方法仍不充分，且可能注定失败。读者得出的结论是，虽然这些步骤是积极的第一步，但要确保 AI 安全发展，还需做更多工作。

OpenAI has taken initial steps to address its alignment problems, including pausing frontier RL training runs and implementing enhanced monitoring and security measures. The article argues that these actions, while significant, are primarily reactive responses to the discovery of misaligned models and inadequate oversight, rather than proactive safety measures. It emphasizes that monitoring and security are only defense-in-depth and cannot solve the core alignment problem, which requires a fundamentally robust and antifragile approach. The author expresses cautious optimism about OpenAI's moves but warns that the company's strategic alignment approach remains insufficient and potentially doomed unless corrected. Readers are left with the conclusion that while these steps are a positive first move, much more work is needed to ensure safe AI development.

    New multistage monitoring uses activation classifiers at every token, escalating to high-compute automated investigators.

    Author argues alignment must be antifragile and primary; monitoring and security are only defense-in-depth.

局限 · Limitations

  • 这些行动是对已发现失准的被动反应，而非主动安全措施。
    Actions are reactive responses to discovered misalignment, not proactive safety measures.
  • 监控无法解决核心对齐问题，过度依赖会加速模型学会规避监控。
    Monitoring cannot solve core alignment; over-reliance accelerates models learning to evade it.
  • 可扩展监督问题依旧存在：较弱监控者监督更强模型，失准会滚雪球式放大。
    Scalable oversight problems persist: weaker monitors supervising stronger models, with snowballing misalignment.
  • 将对齐视为三大自增强支柱之一是概念错误，使实验室缺乏应对能力。
    Treating alignment as one of three self-reinforcing pillars is a conceptual error leaving labs unequipped.
  • 暂停是局部且临时的，并非对停止前沿开发的长期承诺。
    Pauses are partial and temporary, not an extended commitment to halt frontier development.

The best guess is that OpenAI is leaning even harder on RL with smarter models to train longer horizon agentic tasks, including coordination between agents, and this is leading to a lot more misalignment, including obvious and visible misalignment, that they can no longer pretend not to notice. 部分是，绝对如此。我从未怀疑过 Altman 及其团队明白先进 AI 极其危险，也明白如果证明有必要，他们愿意采取代价高昂的措施。做得还不够，而且他们常常让我们失望，但比大多数实验室做得多得多，远非为零。

Partly, yes, absolutely. I have never doubted that Altman and company understand that advanced AI is super dangerous, and that they are open to taking expensive measures if they proved necessary. Not enough, and they've let us down often, but a lot more than most labs, and far from zero.

When you refuse to use even the selfishly optimal amount of caution for an extended period, then move in the direction of the selfishly optimal amount of caution because of the incentives, then in some sense 'the system is working' and you are responding to selfish incentives; the system does not do zero work. That is not the same as the system working.

Nor does it mean that we should despair that a company would ever do the right thing because it is the right thing, or as part of a coordinated action, beyond its own selfish myopic interests.

当采取行动代价高昂时，第一步是愿意在代价低廉、免费，甚至不做反而代价高昂时就去做。你总得从某处开始。

The first step to taking an action when it is expensive is being willing to do it when it is cheap, or free, or actively expensive to not do. You gotta start somewhere.

It is indeed rather scary to consider what might happen at a place like SpaceX, if they were operating at a similar capability level, given they do not even have so much as a safety team and have shown infinitely less dignity than OpenAI. I get very suspicious when I see attempts to treat this as a sort of victory lap, a proof that OpenAI leadership was acting responsibly and properly cared about safety all along and they have now been vindicated. Could Sam Altman, the rest of leadership and OpenAI fully redeem themselves from here? Absolutely. This is a good first step. I am listening. There is a long way to go, on many levels.

究竟暂停了什么？ What Exactly Is Paused?

据我理解，OpenAI 共有三处暂停，这还不包括 OpenAI 得知 HuggingFace 被黑客攻击后最初那次快速的推理中止。

As I understand it, there are three pauses at OpenAI, not counting the initial quick inference halt right after OpenAI learned about the HuggingFace hack.

1. 此前已完成的一次为期两周的暂停：针对包括 Astra 在内的前沿模型进行强化学习（RL），以加固环境并扩大监控。

1. A completed prior two-week pause in RL for frontier models, including Astra, to harden environments and expand monitoring.

2. Astra 被限制在满足额外安全要求的环境中运行。部分工作负载达到了这一标准，但“相当数量”的工作负载处于暂停状态。

2. Astra is restricted to environments that meet additional security requirements. Some of the workloads meet this bar, but 'a significant number' are paused.

3. 另一个独立前沿模型的强化学习训练已被暂停数周，且目前仍无限期暂停；该模型是 OpenAI 迄今规模最大、计划发布的一款模型，暂停目的是提升安全性并“收集对齐证据”。

3. The RL training of a distinct frontier model, their largest yet that is intended for release, has been paused for multiple weeks and is still paused indefinitely, to improve security and 'gather evidence of alignment.'

这些持续中的暂停并非对所有前沿 AI 训练或其他开发工作的全面暂停，但也绝非空谈。这正在大幅放缓下一代发布模型 Astra 的训练，以及推测为 Astra 计划继任者的模型的训练。

The ongoing pauses are not full pauses on all frontier AI training or other development. Neither are they cheap talk. This is slowing down both the next release model, Astra, and the training of what is presumably Astra's planned successor, a substantial amount.

他们仍打算尽快发布 Astra。市场预计它将在九月推出。

They still intend to ship Astra as soon as possible. The market anticipates it in September.

Sam Altman（OpenAI CEO）：（我们仍期望很快发布出色的新模型；这一举措影响的是更远期的发布。）

Sam Altman (CEO OpenAI): (We still expect to ship great new models soon; this impacts further-out releases.)

我们没有足够的信息来判断这一举措在尺度上处于什么位置、会有多痛苦，或者其中有多少已被市场定价。怀疑者认为这最终不过是空谈，或者基本上只是他们本来无论如何都得做的事，这是合理的，因为前沿实验室尚未赢得我们的信任。

We do not have enough information to know where on the scale this move falls, or how painful it will be, or how much of this was priced in. It is reasonable for a skeptic to expect this to ultimately be a nothingburger, or as mostly what they would have had to do anyway, as the frontier labs have not earned our trust.

即便没有办法核实其影响，我仍认为这是向前迈出的实质性一步，朝向一种更好的制度，而且是关键的一步。谨慎乐观。

I still see this, even without a way to verify the impact, as a substantial step forward, towards a better regime, and a key step. Cautious optimism.

也许我们顶尖的 AI 实验室在关键时刻真的会拒绝开发超级智能——如果显而易见我们尚未准备好。在那个层级上，你不能先把它开发出来，然后把它搁置不用。

Perhaps our top AI labs actually will refuse, at crunch time, to develop superintelligence if it remains obvious we are not ready. At that level, you don't get to develop it and then sit on it.

三大支柱 Three Pillars

这就是他们思考问题的方式：

This is the way they are thinking about things:

我们开发能力更强模型的方法建立在三项相互强化的保障措施之上：

Our approach to developing more capable models rests on three reinforcing safeguards:

1. 监控（Monitoring），用于检测并使我们能够对令人担忧的行为作出响应。

1. Monitoring, which detects and allows us to respond to concerning behavior.

2. 对齐（Alignment），降低有害或未经授权行为的可能性。

2. Alignment, which reduces the likelihood of harmful or unauthorized actions.

Zvi Mowshowitz: isn't part of the problem of alignment that you need a way that survives even when lots of your tasks are configured incorrectly and you make tons of dumb mistakes?

David Manheim：简单对齐：当你知道自己想要什么并正确、完整地指定一切时，AI 会做你想要的。

David Manheim: Easy alignment: AI does what you want when you know what it is and specify everything correctly.

困难对齐：即使需求描述得很差，AI 也能理解并做你想要的。

Hard alignment: AI understands and does what you want even if poorly specified.

实际对齐：即使你无法理解自己想要什么或结果是什么，AI 也会做你真正想要的。

Actual alignment: AI does what you'd want even if you can't understand what that is or the results.

Operculum：这两者似乎都很难。

Operculum: they both seem hard.

roon（OpenAI）：公平地说，两者都很难，但我的观点是，即便是简单的常识性对齐也很难。

roon (OpenAI): to be fair they are both hard, but my point is that even easy common-sense alignment is hard.

你将会拥有数百万（仍然是虚构的数量级）的任务类型、环境、虚拟机、目标等等。

You are going to have millions (still made up OOM) of task types and environments and virtual machines and objectives and all that.

你将会搞砸其中很多。你就是会。会有不可能完成的任务。会有奖励黑客行为未被发现的地方。会有大量被污染的数据。会有很多地方，所谓的“正确”答案强化了你通常不想要的东西。错误的教训无处不在。系统性的错位压力是存在的，除非你系统地发现并平衡它们。等等。Anthropic 的风险报告很有说明性。

You are going to fuck a bunch of them up. You just are. There will be impossible tasks. There will be places where reward hacking is not caught. There will be massive amounts of contaminated data. There will be lots of places where the ‘right’ answer reinforces things you in general do not want. Wrong lessons are everywhere. Systematic misalignment pressures are there unless you systematically find and counterbalance them. And so on. Anthropic’s risk report is illustrative.

安全方面有些人会说：“好吧，如果你继续犯那些愚蠢到不会出现在电影版本中的错误，你需要停下来。”但唉，现实就是这样运作的，错误会无处不在且愚蠢。

There are those on the safety side who would say ‘well then if you keep making mistakes too dumb to appear in the movie version you need to stop’ but alas that is simply how reality works, the mistakes are going to be everywhere and dumb.

也许，通过英勇的努力，你可以消除大多数平庸的错误，并迫使你的错误不那么明显。你大多可以犯大师所谓的失误，而不是业余者所谓的失误。这当然有帮助。但会有失误。我不是在批评竞技场中的那个人，也不是说这不会成为大部分工作。

You can maybe, with a heroic effort, get rid of most of the prosaic mistakes, and force your mistakes to be less obvious. You can mostly make what grandmasters call blunders, instead of what amateurs call blunders. It certainly helps along the way. But there will be blunders. I’m not knocking the one in the arena, and again I’m not saying this doesn’t end up as most of the legwork.

这仍然意味着你需要一个计划，能够抵御数千（虚构的数量级）这样的愚蠢错误，其中一些会存在相当长一段时间，并让你恢复。你必须极具反脆弱性，并且有办法注意到事情出错并将船驶回正轨。不可靠性并不完全是固有的。

This still means you need a plan that is robust to thousands (made up OOM) of these dumb mistakes, some of them there for quite a while, and lets you recover. You must be dramatically antifragile, and have ways of noticing things going wrong and steering the ship back on course. The unreliability is not entirely inherent.

我认为，如果那些平凡琐碎的工作做得足够好，并且关键的高层冲突得到正确解决，Anthropic 的美德伦理与宪法式方法有非零的可能性实现这一目标。我不认为 OpenAI 的义务论模型规范方法，以及将其视为一系列工程任务的想法，能够单独做到这一点。

I think the virtue ethical and constitutional approach of Anthropic has a non-zero chance of being able to pull this off if the prosaic stuff gets done well enough and key high-level clashes get resolved correctly. I don’t think OpenAI’s deontological model spec approach, and the idea of this as a series of engineering tasks, can do it alone.

文化危机 A Crisis of Culture

Maxwell Zeff 在《Wired》上报道了“OpenAI 内部的安全清算”。

Maxwell Zeff reports in *Wired* of a "Safety Reckoning Inside OpenAI."

根据这些报道，情况已经变得相当糟糕。

The situation had, by these accounts, gotten quite bad.

Maxwell Zeff：多名现任和前任 OpenAI 员工在匿名条件下向《WIRED》透露内部私密事务，他们表示，快速发布新 AI 模型和产品的竞争压力，使员工难以充分优先考虑安全、安保与对齐。

Maxwell Zeff: Multiple current and former OpenAI employees, who spoke on the condition of anonymity to discuss private internal matters, tell WIRED they believe competitive pressures to quickly ship new AI models and products have made it difficult for staffers to sufficiently prioritize safety, security, and alignment.

这其实并不是一个新信息。多年来我们一直收到这样的信息。不同之处在于，如今出了足够明确的问题，OpenAI 正试图对此采取行动，至少试图说出正确的话。

That is not exactly a new message. We've been getting this message for years. The difference is that now something sufficiently clear has gone wrong that OpenAI is trying to do something about it, and at least trying to say the right things.

Boaz Barak（OpenAI）：我确实同意，HF 事件不仅需要修复一些问题，还需要改变我们的文化。时间会证明一切，但我看到了一些充满希望的迹象。其中很大一部分在于安全团队与研究人员之间更紧密的协作。

Boaz Barak (OpenAI): I actually agree that the HF incident requires not just fixing some issues but also changing our culture. Time will tell but I am seeing some hopeful signs. A big part of this is closer collaboration between security and researchers.

深入思考这一事件让我认识到，对齐需要深度融入每一项训练任务。亟需更紧密的协作。如果“合并研究与安全团队”的计划以安全优先的方式实施，我尚能对其抱有一定同情；但我担心它更像是一种让安全团队向其他团队汇报、并进一步在资源和优先级上被边缘化的手段。

Thinking through this incident clarified for me that alignment needs to be deeply integrated into every training task. Closer collaboration is needed in the extreme. I can have some sympathy for the 'combine the research and safety teams' plan if implemented as safety first, whereas I fear it is more a way to make the safety teams report to the others and get further starved for resources and priority.

当我甚至看到“安全与研究人员”被当作分类时，我就担心这场战斗已经输了，因为我对这些平凡问题的理解发生了变化。安全不是这枚硬币的另一面，安全是你已经失败时的纵深防御。

When I even see 'security and researchers' as the categories I worry that the battle has already been lost, as my understanding of the prosaic problems changes. Security is not the other side of this coin, security is your defense-in-depth in case you have already failed.

There is also this, which I mostly find funny rather than worrisome:

Glaese is in a long-term relationship with Thibault "Tibo" Sottiaux, OpenAI's head of core products like ChatGPT and Codex—an arrangement that multiple current and former employees tell WIRED they believe is unusual, given the often adversarial dynamic between safety and product teams.

《连线》的这篇报道没有告诉我们，除了“更紧密的协作”之外，实际采取了哪些措施来解决这一问题。这些措施可能意义重大，也可能并非如此。

What the Wired piece does not tell us is what steps are actually being taken to fix this, other than 'closer collaboration.' They might be meaningful, they might not be.

There were reports, originally from FT, that OpenAI had disbanded its Preparedness Team, with its responsibilities distributed among other teams. Which sounded bad.

看来情况并非如此有意义地成立，RSI 预备工作负责人 Micah Carroll 表示，RSI 与失准预备团队“正在做比以往更紧迫的工作，并且从未拥有如此大的授权去做这些工作。”

It appears this is not meaningfully the case, and Head of RSI Preparedness Micah Carroll says the RSI and misalignment preparedness team is 'doing more urgent work than ever, and has never been more empowered to do so.'

Micah Carroll：能力研究的人常说“对齐似乎挺容易的，如果把它当作最高优先级来修复，我们就能做到”。现在正是他们大显身手的时候！

Micah Carroll: Capabilities folks often have said "alignment seems pretty easy, if it were top priority to fix it, we could do it". This is their time to shine!

你知道，这其实是一句相当可怕的话。

整个 OpenAI 的人员流动和重组程度令人担忧，但我相信他们实际上并没有解散该小组的重要工作。我也接受，鉴于事情的发展方式，也许我们确实需要一次重组。你不应该默认因为重组就攻击别人。我们应该担心康威定律吗？

The level of churn and reorganization is concerning throughout OpenAI, but I trust that they are not actually disbanding the important work of the group. I also buy that maybe we needed a reorg, given how things were going. You don't want to default to attacking people for reorgs. Should we worry about Conway's Law?

If the trust I am extending here turns out to have been misplaced, I will update a substantial amount towards a much higher level of not trusting anything OpenAI says or promises, beyond my current healthy skepticism.

OpenAI 基金会只是资助一些事情 The OpenAI Foundation Just Funds Things

我在这里提及此事，是因为它能说明 OpenAI 如何看待通往美好未来的路径问题。

I'm including this here because it is illustrative of how OpenAI views the problem of navigating to a good future.

这并不是说，用 1 亿美元做这件事是糟糕的花钱方式。

It's not that this is a bad way to spend $100 million dollars.

问题在于，OpenAI 基金会是为一个特定目的而存在的，这是世界上最重要的目的，即确保 AGI 发展顺利、人类能够安然度过这一进程，避免生存性风险和不可挽回的灾难，尤其是通过对 OpenAI 的监督以及解决相关的关键问题来实现。

It is that the OpenAI Foundation exists for a specific purpose, the most important purpose in the world, which is to ensure that AGI goes well and humanity makes it through this, avoiding existential risk and irrecoverable disasters, especially via supervision of OpenAI and solving key associated problems.

我完全支持改善丙型肝炎的治疗，但这与使命无关。

I am all for better treatment of Hepatitis C, but that's irrelevant to the mission.

相反，它把时间和资金花在 AI 扩散上，方式刻意设计得让普通人听起来顺耳。这是好事，但这并不是基金会存在的意义。再说一遍，如果它一直这样做，而且规模只有这么大——该基金会目前估值约 1800 亿美元——那这个基金会就无关紧要。它将名存实亡。

Instead it is spending its time and money on AI diffusion in ways designed to sound good to normies. That's a good thing but it's not why the foundation matters. Again, if this is what they keep doing, at only this size — the foundation is valued at ~$180 billion even now — the foundation does not matter. It will be dead.

**OpenAI 基金会：** OpenAI 基金会正在发起 **AI for Civil Society and Philanthropy** 计划，旨在帮助非营利组织、社区组织及其他可信赖的合作伙伴，将先进 AI 应用于紧迫挑战，开发切实可行的解决方案，并推广行之有效的做法。

**The OpenAI Foundation:** The OpenAI Foundation is launching **AI for Civil Society and Philanthropy** to help nonprofits, community organizations, and other trusted partners put advanced AI to work on urgent challenges, develop practical solutions, and scale what works.

首项行动是与 **Common Health Coalition** 共同投入 **1 亿美元**，发起 **Breakthroughs to Follow-through** 计划，帮助护理团队利用 AI 让更多患者获得经过验证的治疗——初期目标是在阿拉巴马州、伊利诺伊州、路易斯安那州和马萨诸塞州将丙型肝炎治愈率提高一倍。Breakthroughs to Follow-through 将致力于识别已脱离照护的患者、整合病历记录、跟踪进展，并将有限的人力时间集中到最能产生影响的环节。

The first effort is a **$100 million commitment** with the **Common Health Coalition** to launch **Breakthroughs to Follow-through**, to help care teams use AI to reach more patients with proven treatments — starting with the goal of doubling hepatitis C cure rates in Alabama, Illinois, Louisiana, and Massachusetts. Breakthroughs to Follow-through will work to identify patients who have fallen out of care, synthesize records, track progress, and focus limited staff time where it can have the greatest impact.

OpenAI 基金会希望赋能最贴近问题的人群，并将 AI 应用于能够切实改善人们生活的领域。

The OpenAI Foundation wants to empower those who are closest to the issues and to put AI to work where it can make a meaningful difference in people's lives.

这体现了一种心态：未来是一系列普通的工程问题，那就去解决它们。这远比大多数人的反应——即什么问题都不解决——要好得多，但仍不足以应对当下的时刻。

This is reflective of a mindset that the future is a series of ordinary engineering problems, so let's go solve them. This is way, way better than most people's reaction, which is to not solve any of the problems, but it does not meet the moment.

快，没有时间了 Quickly, There’s No Time

如今，AI 领域发展迅猛。我们仍在等待事后分析报告，而他们应对措施的许多细节尚未敲定，更不用说对外分享了。

The world of AI moves fast now. We are still awaiting the post-mortem, and many of the details of their response have yet to be hashed out, let alone shared externally.

OpenAI 已迈出了初步的、切实且代价高昂的第一步，以解决其训练和评估流程中的诸多问题。

OpenAI has made initial good, real and expensive first steps towards addressing many of the problems with its training and evaluation pipeline.

这些早期行动是强有力的第一步。OpenAI 仍需迅速扭转许多局面，无论是重大事项还是大量平淡无奇的小事，并且这次必须兑现承诺。

These early actions are a strong first step. OpenAI still needs to quickly turn many things around, both the big things and the tons of prosaic little things, and it needs to deliver on its promises this time around.

这包括解决监督和安全方面的彻底失败，但对齐才是关键。

That includes addressing the Total Failures of oversight and security, but alignment is what matters.

最缺失的一点是，OpenAI 对核心问题的看法仍然是错误的。它被视作执行失败，将对齐作为若干支柱之一，并专注于防止特定的不良行为。确实存在关键的执行失败，但如果仅解决这些，那么败局已定。需要一种根本不同的对齐方法和问题视角。

What is most missing is that OpenAI's vision of the central problem remains wrong. It is being viewed as a failure to execute, with alignment one of several pillars and focused on preventing specific undesired actions. There were key failures to execute, but if that is all that is addressed then the battle is already lost. A fundamentally different approach to alignment, and view of the problem, is what is needed.
