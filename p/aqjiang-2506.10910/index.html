摘要 · Abstract

我们介绍了 Magistral，这是 Mistral 的第一个推理模型，也是我们自己的可扩展强化学习（RL）管线。我们没有依赖现有实现或从先前模型中提取的 RL 轨迹，而是从头开始，完全依靠我们自己的模型和基础设施。值得注意的是，我们展示了一个能够探索 LLM 纯 RL 训练极限的堆栈，提出了一种强制模型使用推理语言的简单方法，并表明仅对文本数据进行 RL 训练能保持初始检查点的大部分能力。我们发现，文本上的 RL 能保持或提升多模态理解、指令遵循和函数调用能力。我们发布了 Magistral Medium，它在 Mistral Medium 3 之上仅通过 RL 训练推理能力，并开源了 Magistral Small（Apache 2.0），其中进一步包含了来自 Magistral Medium 的冷启动数据。

We introduce Magistral, Mistral's first reasoning model and our own scalable reinforcement learning (RL) pipeline. Instead of relying on existing implementations and RL traces distilled from prior models, we follow a ground up approach, relying solely on our own models and infrastructure. Notably, we demonstrate a stack that enabled us to explore the limits of pure RL training of LLMs, present a simple method to force the reasoning language of the model, and show that RL on text data alone maintains most of the initial checkpoint's capabilities. We find that RL on text maintains or improves multimodal understanding, instruction following and function calling. We present Magistral Medium, trained for reasoning on top of Mistral Medium 3 with RL alone, and we open-source Magistral Small (Apache 2.0) which further includes cold-start data from Magistral Medium.

局限 · Limitations

  • 强化学习训练依赖可验证奖励，限制了其在需要客观正确性任务之外的适用性。
    RL training relies on verifiable rewards, limiting applicability to tasks with objective correctness.
  • 由于语言约束，多语言性能相比英语下降 4-10%。
    Multilingual performance degrades 4-10% compared to English due to language constraints.
  • 该方法需要大量数据过滤和两阶段难度筛选。
    The method requires extensive data filtering and two-stage difficulty curation.
  • 代码任务的比例奖励导致最终性能略低于二元奖励。

2.1 强化学习算法 2.1 Reinforcement learning algorithm

我们使用组相对策略优化（GRPO）[Shao et al., 2024] 作为我们的 RL 算法。与 PPO [Schulman et al., 2017] 不同，GRPO 消除了对“评论家模型”的需求，而是使用策略对每个提示的多次生成的平均奖励来计算优势的基线。具体来说，GRPO 优化策略 πθ\pi_{\theta} 以最大化以下目标：

Unlike PPO [Schulman et al., 2017], GRPO eliminates the need for a ‘critic model’, and instead uses the average reward from multiple generations per prompt from the policy to compute a baseline for advantage calculation. 消除 KL 散度。KL 散度惩罚约束在线策略不要偏离参考策略太远，有助于保持与初始模型的对齐。然而，在 GRPO 中，策略无论如何都会大幅偏离，并且为 KL 计算维护参考模型的副本会带来我们认为不合理的计算成本。我们完全移除了 KL 惩罚。

Eliminating KL divergence. The KL divergence penalty constrains the online policy from deviating too much from a reference policy, helping to maintain alignment with the initial model. However, in GRPO, the policy diverges substantially regardless, and maintaining a copy of the reference model for KL computation incurs a compute cost we find unjustified. We remove the KL penalty entirely.

损失归一化。为避免在组内不同生成之间引入长度偏差，我们首先计算组内所有生成的所有 token 的逐 token 损失之和，然后除以组内生成的总长度 ∑i=1G|oi|\sum_{i=1}^{G}|o_{i}| 来归一化损失。

Loss normalization. To avoid introducing length biases between generations in one group, we normalize the loss by first adding token-wise loss for all tokens and all generations and then dividing by the total length of generations in the group ∑i=1G|oi|\sum_{i=1}^{G}|o_{i}|.

优势归一化。我们简单地将每个 token 的优势估计为 A^i,t=A^i=ri−μ\hat{A}_{i,t}=\hat{A}_{i}=r_{i}-\mu，其中 μ\mu 是组内奖励的均值。遵循 Andrychowicz 等人 [2020] 的做法，我们进一步在每个小批量中将优势归一化为 A^i,tnorm=(A^i−A^mean)/A^std\hat{A}_{i,t}^{\text{norm}}=(\hat{A}_{i}-\hat{A}^{\text{mean}})/\hat{A}^{\text{std}}，其中 A^mean\hat{A}^{\text{mean}} 和 A^std\hat{A}^{\text{std}} 是小批量中优势 A^i\hat{A}_{i} 的序列级均值和标准差。

Advantage normalization. We estimate the advantage of each token simply as A^i,t=A^i=ri−μ\hat{A}_{i,t}=\hat{A}_{i}=r_{i}-\mu, where μ\mu is the mean of rewards within a group. Following Andrychowicz et al. [2020], we additionally normalize the advantages in each minibatch as A^i,tnorm=(A^i−A^mean)/A^std\hat{A}_{i,t}^{\text{norm}}=(\hat{A}_{i}-\hat{A}^{\text{mean}})/\hat{A}^{\text{std}} where A^mean\hat{A}^{\text{mean}} and A^std\hat{A}^{\text{std}} are the sequence-wise mean and standard deviation of the advantages A^i\hat{A}_{i} in a minibatch.

放宽信任区域的上界。我们允许模型探索罕见但可能具有洞察力的推理步骤，防止策略变得确定性。我们采用 Clip-Higher [Yu et al., 2025] 策略来解决熵崩溃问题。在标准 GRPO 中，ε\varepsilon 裁剪通过限制低似然 token 概率的增加来限制探索，阻碍了罕见但重要的推理路径的强化。通过将上裁剪阈值提高到 εhigh\varepsilon_{\text{high}}，低概率 token 有更多增长空间，从而增强输出的熵和多样性，并改善推理探索。我们发现，仔细调整 εhigh\varepsilon_{\text{high}} 对于保持 RL 运行的稳定性至关重要。我们在训练期间将其调整在 0.26 到 0.28 之间，以保持组熵稳定。

Relaxing the trust region’s upper bound. We allow the model to explore rare but potentially insightful reasoning steps, preventing deterministic policies. We adopt the Clip-Higher [Yu et al., 2025] strategy to address entropy collapse. In standard GRPO, ε\varepsilon-clipping limits exploration by restricting the increase in probability of low-likelihood tokens, hindering the reinforcement of rare but important reasoning paths. By increasing the upper clipping threshold to εhigh\varepsilon_{\text{high}}, low-probability tokens have more room to grow, enhancing entropy and diversity in outputs, and improving reasoning exploration. We found that careful tuning of εhigh\varepsilon_{\text{high}} is crucial to maintaining stability in the RL run. We adjusted it between 0.26 and 0.28 during the training to keep the group entropy stable.

消除非多样化组。所有生成要么完全正确要么完全错误的组具有零优势，因此对批量损失没有贡献。这会导致梯度变小且对噪声更敏感。为解决此问题，我们在形成训练批次时过滤掉所有优势为零的组。

Eliminating non-diverse groups. Groups where all generations are either entirely correct or wrong have zero advantage and therefore contribute nothing to the batch loss. This results in smaller gradients with increased noise sensitivity. To address this, we filter out all groups with zero advantage when forming training batches.

2.2 奖励塑形 2.2 Reward shaping

选择合适的奖励对于 RL 算法的有效工作至关重要。在训练期间，模型生成沿四个维度进行评估：格式、正确性、长度和语言一致性，我们将在下面描述。

Choosing the appropriate reward is crucial for the RL algorithm to work effectively. During training, model generations are evaluated along four axes: formatting, correctness, length, and language consistency, which we describe below.

对于数学和代码问题，我们指示模型遵循特定格式，以便于提取模型的答案：

For both math and code problems, we instruct the model to follow a specific format, which facilitates the extraction of the model’s answer:

标签要求：(i) 模型响应必须以 <think> 标签开头，并且必须包含对应的 </think> 标签。(ii) 响应中应恰好存在一组这样的标签。

Tag requirements: (i) The model response must start with a &lt;think&gt; tag and must include a corresponding &lt;/think&gt; tag. (ii) There should be exactly one set of these tags present in the response.

数学响应：对于数学输出，响应必须在答案部分（</think> 标签之后）包含用 \boxed{} 括起来的最终答案。

Mathematical responses: For mathematical outputs, the response must include the final answer enclosed in \boxed{} within the answer section, following the &lt;/think&gt; tag.

代码响应：对于代码输出，响应必须在答案部分包含至少一个 Markdown 代码块，格式为三重反引号后跟编程语言说明。

Code responses: For code outputs, the response must include at least one markdown block, formatted with triple backticks followed by the programming language specification, in the answer section.

未能满足这些条件中的任何一个将导致奖励为 0，并且响应将不再进行评分。否则，响应将获得 0.1 的奖励并进入评分阶段。

Failure to meet any of these conditions results in a reward of 0, and the response will not be graded further. Otherwise, the response gets a reward of 0.1 and proceeds to grading.

如果生成的答案符合要求的格式，我们提取模型解决方案并使用验证器评估其正确性。

If the generated answer follows the required formatting, we extract the model solution and use a verifier to assess its correctness.

数学正确性：最终答案从解决方案中最后一个 \boxed{} 内提取，并使用基于规则的验证器与参考答案进行比较。该验证器会标准化真实答案和生成的答案，以正确奖励语义相同但语法不同的响应。我们结合使用不同的解析器和 SymPy（https://www.sympy.org/en/index.html）来评估输出并将其与原始真实答案进行比较。如果答案正确，则额外奖励 0.9，使总奖励达到 1.0。

Math correctness. The final answer is extracted from inside the last \boxed{} in the solution and compared against the reference answer using a rule-based verifier. It normalizes both the ground-truth and the generated answer to correctly reward semantically identical responses with different syntaxes. We leverage a combination of different parsers and SymPy222https://www.sympy.org/en/index.html to evaluate outputs and compare them to the original ground truth. An additional reward of 0.9 is given if the answer is correct, making the total reward 1.0.

代码正确性：代码从答案部分中的第一个 Markdown 代码块中提取。如果代码是用 C++ 编写的，则使用 C++20 标准编译，超时时间为 10 秒。我们预编译了竞赛编程中常用的 bits/stdc++.h 标准库头文件，以加快编译过程。我们从可用的测试用例中随机选择 20 个测试，确保在给定的响应组内使用相同的测试。然后对代码执行这些测试，每个测试的超时时间为 4 秒，内存限制为 300 MB。如果代码成功通过所有测试，则额外奖励 0.9。

Code correctness. Code is extracted from the first markdown code block in the answer section. If the code is written in C++, it is compiled with a timeout of 10 seconds, using the C++20 standard. 3 基础设施 3 Infrastructure

在本节中，我们介绍用于在线训练的基础设施。我们采用类似于先前多项工作中提出的分布式 RL 训练系统 [Espeholt et al., 2018, Hu et al., 2024, Noukhovitch et al., 2024, Sheng et al., 2024, Wu et al., 2025]，该系统协调三种工作节点：

In this section, we present our infrastructure for online training. We adopt a distributed RL training system similar to those proposed in several prior works [Espeholt et al., 2018, Hu et al., 2024, Noukhovitch et al., 2024, Sheng et al., 2024, Wu et al., 2025] that coordinates three kinds of workers:

训练器：维护模型权重的主副本并执行梯度更新。

Trainers maintain the main copy of the model weights and perform gradient updates.

生成器：执行“roll-outs”，使用最新策略从训练提示中返回带有对数概率的补全。

Generators perform ‘roll-outs’, using the latest policy to return completions with log-probabilities from the training prompts.

验证器：评估生成器产生的补全并返回奖励（详见第 2.2 节）。

Verifiers evaluate the completions produced by the generators and return a reward (see Section 2.2 for details).

生成器是总算力的重要组成部分，也是在线 RL 特有的部分。它们的工作负载高度异构且难以预测，因为序列长度的分布高度偏斜并在训练过程中变化：最长的补全可能比最短的补全多花费 5 倍的时间。系统的主要约束之一是不引入序列长度偏差：补全长度的分布必须与训练数据的分布完全相同，即使较短的补全完成得更快。另一个竞争目标是尽快更新生成器权重。我们希望生成尽可能在策略上，但我们也希望生成器无需相互等待或等待训练器即可运行。

Generators are a significant part of the total compute and the part that’s unique to online RL. Their workload is highly heterogeneous and hard to predict as the distribution of sequence lengths is highly skewed and changes over the course of training: the longest completions can take up to 5 times longer than the shortest. One of the main constraints of the system is to introduce no bias on sequence lengths: the distribution of completion lengths must be exactly that of the training data, even though shorter completions finish more quickly. A competing goal is to update the generator weights as soon as possible. We want the generations to be as on-policy as possible, but we also want the generators to operate without waiting for each other or the trainers.

为了在没有近似的情况下进行训练，我们可以顺序处理批次：在批次上启动生成器，等待所有序列完成，更新训练器和生成器的模型权重，然后重复。然而，由于补全时间异构，这种方法会导致生成器空闲和流水线效率低下。相反，我们优先考虑效率，并以最大吞吐量连续运行生成器，无需等待训练器。我们不断从生成器收集组，验证它们，并更新训练器。在这些更新之后，训练器通过 NCCL 将新权重发送给生成器，而不丢弃当前正在生成的在途序列。从 GPU 到 GPU 广播权重至关重要，因为它将单次更新的时间减少到 5 秒以下，即使对于大型模型和大型世界规模也是如此。我们在图 3 中说明了这一过程。

In order to train without any approximation, we could process batches sequentially: start generators on a batch, wait for all sequences to complete, update the model weights for both trainers and generators, and repeat. However, this approach leads to idle generators and low pipeline efficiency due to heterogeneous completion times. Instead, we prioritize efficiency and operate the generators continuously at maximum throughput without ever waiting for the trainers. We constantly gather groups from the generators, verify them, and update the trainers. After these updates, the trainers send the new weights to the generators via NCCL, without discarding the in-flight sequences currently being generated. Broadcasting weights from GPUs to GPUs is crucial as it reduces the time required for a single update to below 5 seconds, even with large models and large world sizes. We illustrate this process in Figure 3.

当为单个提示生成解决方案时，模型权重可能会经历多次更新，反映出训练器的最新改进。到它被完全处理并发送给训练器时，模型权重可能已经更新了多次，但最新的词元始终是在策略上生成的。更新模型权重时，之前存储在键值缓存中的隐藏状态会略微过时，因为它们是由先前版本的模型计算的。出于性能考虑，我们发现重新计算键值缓存并非必要，这可能是由于损失函数中固有的离策略校正 [Schulman et al., 2017]。

As a solution is generated for a single prompt, it may experience multiple updates to the model weights, reflecting the latest improvements from the trainers. By the time it is fully processed and sent to the trainers, the model weights may have been updated several times, but the latest tokens are always generated on-policy. When updating the model weights, the hidden states previously stored in the key-value cache become slightly outdated because they were computed by previous versions of the model. For performance, we find that recomputing the key-value cache is not necessary, potentially due to off-policy corrections inherent to the loss function [Schulman et al., 2017].

我们将批次定义为固定数量的生成完成序列，而非固定数量的词元。生成器根据预设的排列将每个完成的序列发送给一个随机的训练器等级。当每个数据并行等级收到足够多的完成序列以构成一个批次时，执行一次梯度更新。如果训练器是瓶颈（例如在早期训练中生成序列仍然较短时），我们将传入的生成序列累积到一个具有固定大小限制的阻塞队列中，该限制控制离策略程度。一个批次可以划分为多个小批次以执行多次优化步骤（见第 6.3 节）。每个小批次有固定数量的完成序列但词元数量可变，因此进一步划分为固定词元大小的微批次。由于我们在微批次上累积梯度，样本的顺序无关紧要。我们利用这一特性实现了一种贪婪的整理算法，按降序对序列进行排序，并尝试将它们逐个放入空闲的微批次中（如果有），否则开始一个新的微批次。这确保了每个小批次中训练工作负载的均匀性，将填充减少了 19%。

We define a batch as a fixed number of generated completions, rather than a fixed number of tokens. Generators send each finished completion to a random trainer rank according to a pre-set permutation. A gradient update is performed when each data parallel rank has received enough completions to make a batch. If the trainers are the bottleneck, as is the case in early training when the generations are still short, we accumulate incoming generations into a blocking queue with a fixed size limit that controls off-policy degree. A batch may be partitioned into minibatches to perform several optimization steps (see Section 6.3). Each minibatch has a fixed number of completions but a variable number of tokens, so it is further divided into microbatches of a fixed token size. Since we accumulate the gradient over microbatches, the order of samples does not matter. We take advantage of this property to implement a greedy collation algorithm, sorting the sequences by descending size and trying to fit them one by one into a free microbatch if there is one or starting a new otherwise. This ensures a homogeneous workload across training workers for each minibatch, reducing padding by 19%.

We limit ourselves to problems with verifiable solutions; we use mathematical problems whose solution is a numerical answer or expression, and code problems with associated tests. We apply extensive filtering, which we describe here.

4.1 数学 4.1 Math

我们从大约 70 万个样本的大型但嘈杂的问题集开始。我们首先对数据进行全面的预处理和过滤，以确保所有问题都是完整的，并且最终答案准确且可通过基于规则的系统验证。特别是，我们过滤了基于证明和多部分的问题，因为这类问题难以验证正确性。此外，我们将多项选择题重新表述为基于陈述的问题，以实现更稳健的验证并增加难度。

We started with a large but noisy problem set of around 700k samples. We first perform comprehensive pre-processing and filtering of the data to ensure all the problems are complete and that the final answers were accurate and verifiable with a rule-based system. Particularly, we filter proof-based and multi-part problems for which it is difficult to verify correctness. Furthermore, we reformulate multiple-choice problems into statement-based problems for more robust verification and increased difficulty.

我们实现了一个两阶段过滤流程，以策划一个难度适中的问题数据集，既不太容易也不太难以供模型学习。首先，我们使用 Mistral Large 2 [MistralAI, 2024] 进行初始难度评估，为每个问题采样 16 个解决方案，并移除那些从未被解决或成功率很高的问题。这个初始策划的问题集随后通过我们的在线强化学习流程用于训练一个 24B 模型，产生了一个小型但能力强大的检查点，我们仅将其用于评分。

We implemented a two-stage filtering pipeline to curate a dataset of problems at a ‘goldilocks’ difficulty level, neither too easy nor too hard for the model to learn from. First, we performed an initial difficulty assessment using Mistral Large 2 [MistralAI, 2024], by sampling 1616 solutions for each problem and removing the ones that are either never solved or solved with a high success rate. This initial, curated set of problems was then used to train a 24B model via our online RL pipeline, resulting in a small but capable checkpoint which we use solely for grading.

在第二阶段，这个经过强化学习训练的更强模型被用于重新评分整个原始数据集。我们再次为每个问题采样 16 个响应，过滤掉最简单和仍未解决的问题。然后，我们进一步过滤掉可能不正确的问题，即大多数样本具有相同最终答案但与“真实”答案不一致的问题。这是因为当模型持续达成与参考答案相矛盾的共识时，问题本身更可能具有错误的真实答案。

In the second stage, this stronger, RL-trained model was used to re-grade the entire original dataset. We again sampled 16 responses for each problem, filtering out the easiest and the still-unsolved problems. We then further filter out potentially incorrect problems where a majority of samples have the same final answer but disagree with the “ground-truth” answer. This is because when the model consistently reaches a consensus that contradicts the reference solution, the problems themselves are more likely to have wrong ground-truth answers.

这种两阶段方法至关重要，因为仅使用初始较弱的 Mistral Large 2 模型进行一次通过是不够的。其推理能力可能导致它错误地将许多真正困难的问题归类为不可解而丢弃。通过使用更强的强化学习训练模型进行第二次通过，我们确保这些有价值的、具有挑战性的训练示例被准确评估并保留。

This two-stage methodology was crucial because a single pass with the initial, weaker Mistral Large 2 model would have been insufficient. Its reasoning capabilities would likely have caused it to discard many genuinely difficult problems by incorrectly classifying them as unsolvable. By using a stronger, RL-trained model for a second pass, we ensured that these valuable and challenging training examples were accurately assessed and retained.

4.2 代码 4.2 Code

我们从各种来源收集了代码竞赛数据。每个数据点包括一个问题陈述，以及（如果可用）正确的解决方案和相关测试。对于训练过程，我们希望每个问题都有问题陈述和大量正确的测试。为此，我们首先移除任何没有解决方案且没有足够测试的问题。然后，每个解决方案在所有可用测试上执行，我们丢弃一致性不足的测试。对于具有足够一致性但没有解决方案成功的测试，我们假设该测试不正确，并更新它以反映解决方案输出中最常见的结果。在代码问题缺乏测试的情况下，我们生成额外的测试并对它们进行相同的评估过程。

We gathered code contest data from various sources. Each data point includes a problem statement and, when available, correct solutions and related tests. For the training process, we want problem statements and a large number of correct tests per problem. In order to achieve this, we first remove any problems without solutions and without enough tests. Each solution is then executed on all available tests, and we discard tests with insufficient agreement. For tests with sufficient agreement but where no solution succeeded, we assume that the test is incorrect and update it to reflect the most common result among the solutions’ outputs. In cases where code problems lack tests, we generate additional tests and subject them to the same evaluation process.

最后，在适用的情况下，问题陈述被复制以要求使用 Python 或 C++编写代码，这是竞争性编程中常用的两种语言。这个过程产生了一个包含 3.5 万个代码问题的数据集。

Finally, where applicable, problem statements are duplicated to require code in Python or C++, two commonly used languages in competitive programming. This process resulted in a dataset of 35k code problems.

5.3 Magistral Small——基于推理 SFT 引导的 RL 5.3 Magistral Small – RL on top of reasoning SFT bootstrapping

鉴于 Magistral Medium 是一个强大的“教师”模型，我们接下来探索如何训练最强大的学生模型。为此，我们训练了 Magistral Small，它通过来自 Magistral Medium 的 SFT 轨迹进行“冷启动”。

Given a strong ‘teacher’ model in Magistral Medium, we next explore how one can train the strongest possible student model. To do so, we train Magistral Small, which is ‘cold-started’ with SFT traces from Magistral Medium.

We begin by extracting traces with correct answers from the RL training of Magistral Medium, excluding those from early steps with short CoTs. We also maintain a mixed difficulty level of the problems by limiting number of generations per problem to avoid biasing the collected traces towards easier problems and also upsampling problems with lower pass rates.

我们通过从 Magistral Medium 对大量多样化提示生成响应来扩充这个 SFT 冷启动数据，这些提示来自 OpenThoughts [Guha et al., 2025] 和 OpenR1 [Hugging Face, 2025, Penedo et al., 2025] 的代码子集。我们在此基础上进行额外的过滤，并保留了一部分提示。这为我们提供了一个混合难度的推理数据集。我们还包含了 10%的通用指令微调数据点，以保留非推理能力。我们对 Mistral Small 3 Instruct（一个 240 亿参数的模型）进行了 4 个周期的微调，并选择了 AIME'24 上最好的检查点作为后续强化学习阶段的初始检查点。

We augment this SFT cold-start data by generating responses from our Magistral Medium on a large set of diverse prompts, sourced from OpenThoughts [Guha et al., 2025] and the code subset of OpenR1 [Hugging Face, 2025, Penedo et al., 2025]. We perform additional filtering on top and kept a subset of the prompts. This gives us a reasoning dataset with mixed difficulty. We also include 10% of datapoints for general instruction tuning in order to preserve non-reasoning capabilities. We finetuned Mistral Small 3 Instruct (a 24-billion parameter model) for 4 epochs, and chose the best checkpoint on AIME’24 as the initial checkpoint for the following RL stage.

然后，我们使用批量大小为 2048 个序列、最大无惩罚完成长度 l_max - l_cache 为 32k 的 RL 训练这个 SFT 检查点。我们使用 1.0 的采样温度进行生成，因为这能在避免低温下缺乏多样性和高温下产生不连贯输出之间取得最佳平衡。我们使用ε_high 为 0.3 来鼓励探索，因为冷启动模型产生的响应熵值要低得多。

We then trained this SFT checkpoint with RL using a batch size of 2048 sequences, and a maximum non-penalized completion length lmax−lcachel_{\text{max}}-l_{\text{cache}} of 32k. We used a sampling temperature of 1.0 for our generations, as it provided the best balance between avoiding the lack of diversity seen at lower temperatures and the incoherent outputs generated at higher temperatures. We use a εhigh\varepsilon_{\text{high}} of 0.3, to encourage exploration, as the cold-started model yielded responses with far lower entropy.

6.3 批次与小批量大小 6.3 Batch and minibatch size

像 PPO 或 GRPO 这样的强化学习（RL）算法引入了两种不同的批尺度。批大小，记为 n_batch，指的是在更新生成器权重之前收集的序列数量。小批量大小，n_minibatch，表示用于计算梯度并执行单次优化步骤的序列数量。需要注意的是，n_minibatch 必须能整除 n_batch。此外，在异步 RL 流水线中，引入了第三个尺度：并发序列数 n_async，表示并行生成的序列数量。如果并发生成的序列数 n_async 远大于批大小 n_batch，那么一个典型序列是由 n_async/n_batch 个不同策略生成的，可能过于离策略。当每批进行多于一次小批量更新时，这种影响会变得更糟。

Reinforcement learning (RL) algorithms like PPO or GRPO introduce two distinct batch scales. The batch size, denoted as nbatchn_{\text{batch}}, refers to the number of sequences collected before updating the generator’s weights. The minibatch size, nminibatchn_{\text{minibatch}}, indicates the number of sequences used to compute the gradient and perform a single optimization step. It is important to note that nminibatchn_{\text{minibatch}} must divide nbatchn_{\text{batch}}. Additionally, in an asynchronous RL pipeline, a third scale is introduced: the number of concurrent sequences, nasyncn_{\text{async}}, which represents the number of sequences being generated in parallel. If the number of concurrently generated sequences nasyncn_{\text{async}} is much larger than the batch size nbatchn_{\text{batch}}, a typical sequence was generated with nasync/nbatchn_{\text{async}}/n_{\text{batch}} different policies and could be too off-policy. The effect becomes worse as we do more than one minibatch update per one batch.

为了验证这一假设，我们准备了一个强大的 3B 模型，从 Ministral 3B 开始使用 SFT，然后使用 GRPO 在仅数学数据上以恒定学习率、固定的 n_async=4096 以及不同的 n_batch 和 n_minibatch 值（来自{1024,2048,4096,8192}）进行训练。

To test this hypothesis we prepared a strong 3B model using SFT starting from Ministral 3B, and then trained it using GRPO on math-only data with a constant learning rate, a fixed nasync=4096n_{\text{async}}=4096, and different values of nbatchn_{\text{batch}} and nminibatchn_{\text{minibatch}} in {1024,2048,4096,8192}\{1024,2048,4096,8192\}.

我们观察到，只要保持 n_batch=n_minibatch 且 n_batch 足够大，性能在按处理提示数绘制时非常相似，如图 6(a)所示。另一方面，当 n_minibatch 减小而 n_batch 保持不变时，性能突然下降，即使与 n_batch 减小到相同 n_minibatch 的情况相比也是如此，如图 6(b)所示。当 n_batch≤1024 时，训练变得不太稳定，因此我们在最终训练和进一步消融中选择保持比率 n_async/n_batch≤2 且 n_batch=n_minibatch。

We observe that as long as we keep nbatch=nminibatchn_{\text{batch}}=n_{\text{minibatch}} and that nbatchn_{\text{batch}} is large enough, the performance is very similar when plotted depending on the number of processed prompts, as can be seen in Figure 6 (a). On the other hand, when nminibatchn_{\text{minibatch}} is decreased while keeping nbatchn_{\text{batch}} constant, the performance suddenly degrades, even when compared to nbatchn_{\text{batch}} reduced to the same nminibatchn_{\text{minibatch}}, as highlighted in Figure 6 (b). 7 分析 7 Analysis

在本节中，我们研究 RL 训练的动力学，并提出证据表明增加完成长度是提高模型性能的主要资源。这些动力学不会破坏先前的能力，推理能力甚至可以泛化：多模态推理得到免费改进，函数调用和指令遵循保持不变甚至略有提升。此外，我们讨论了两个对我们无效的想法：基于测试完成率在代码任务中提供更细粒度的奖励，以及通过损失中的熵奖励项控制熵。

In this section, we investigate the dynamics of RL training and present evidence that increasing completion length is the main resource that improves the performance of the model. Those dynamics are not destructive to previous capabilities, and the reasoning capabilities can even generalize: multimodal reasoning gets improved for free, and function calling and instruction following remain unchanged or even get a small boost. Additionally, we discuss two ideas that didn’t work for us - giving more fine-grained rewards in code tasks based on test completion rate and controlling entropy via entropy bonus term in the loss.

7.4 不成功的方法 7.4 Unsuccessful approaches

在本节中，我们介绍了各种尝试过但最终未采用的方法，因为它们没有带来性能提升。

In this section, we present various approaches that we tried but ultimately did not adopt, as they did not yield any performance improvements.

竞争性编程的严格要求，在正确性和复杂度约束方面，导致奖励稀疏，通常由于奖励多样性有限，许多代码生成被丢弃。

The strict requirements of competitive programming, in terms of correctness and adherence to complexity constraints, result in sparse rewards, often causing many code generations to be discarded due to limited reward diversity.

为了解决这个问题，我们尝试了比例奖励：基于通过的测试比例，而不是第 2.2.2 节中讨论的二元奖励。在使用 24B 模型进行 250 步的消融实验中，我们发现使用比例奖励训练更快，丢弃的数据少三倍。然而，这种方法导致基准上的最终性能略低，LiveCodeBench 下降 2%（图 11a），生成长度增长较慢（图 11b）。

To address this, we experimented with a proportional reward: based on the fraction of tests passed, as opposed to the binary reward discussed in Section 2.2.2. In an ablation with a 24B model over 250 steps, we found that training with proportional rewards was faster, discarding three times less data. However, this approach led to slightly lower final performance on benchmarks, with a 2% decrease on LiveCodeBench (Figure 11a), and slower growth in generation length (Figure 11b).

希望基于通过测试比例的比例奖励能为 RL 训练提供比简单通过/失败更丰富的信号。然而，潜在的问题是部分奖励也可能为不正确的解决方案提供错误信号，并且对实现之间的微小不一致更敏感，可能导致训练批次的意义降低。

The hope was that a reward based on the fraction of tests passed should provide a richer signal than a simple pass/fail for RL training. However, the potential issue is that partial rewards could also provide false signal to incorrect solutions and be more sensitive to minor inconsistencies between implementations, potentially leading to less meaningful training batches.

为了鼓励探索并防止 RL 训练中熵崩溃，文献中常见策略[Schulman 等人，2017]是添加熵奖励损失项。然而，我们发现这一策略不稳定，因为熵奖励的效果随数据集变化显著。对于纯数学数据集，熵随熵奖励下降，而较高的εhigh 维持熵，增强探索（图 12a）。在数学和代码数据集上，熵随熵奖励过度增加（即使系数与纯数学运行相同），而较高的εhigh 允许熵下降，改善利用（图 12b）。

To encourage exploration and prevent entropy collapse during RL training, a common strategy in the literature [Schulman et al., 2017] is to add an entropy bonus loss term. However, we found this strategy to be unstable as the effect of the entropy bonus varies significantly depending on the dataset. For a math-only dataset, entropy drops with the entropy bonus, while a higher εhigh\varepsilon_{\text{high}} maintains entropy, enhancing exploration (Figure 12a). On a math and code dataset, entropy increases excessively with the entropy bonus (even with the same coefficient as in the math-only run), while a higher εhigh\varepsilon_{\text{high}} allows entropy to decrease, improving exploitation (Figure 12b).

相反，我们发现依赖εhigh 更有效，正如文献[Yu 等人，2025，Wang 等人，2025]所指出的。这种方法避免了与熵奖励相关的不稳定性问题。

Instead, we found it more effective to depend on εhigh\varepsilon_{\text{high}}, as also noted in literature [Yu et al., 2025, Wang et al., 2025]. This method avoids the instability issues associated with entropy bonuses.

另一种控制熵的方法是在 PPO 损失中添加 KL 项。然而，由于生成分布预计会与原始模型显著偏离，我们发现使用 KL 惩罚主要阻碍训练，这与先前发现[Yu 等人，2025]一致。我们尝试在训练中使用权重的指数移动平均作为 KL 的参考，但发现手动调整εhigh 更简单。

Another approach for controlling entropy is adding a KL term to the PPO loss. However, as the generation distribution is expected to deviate significantly from the original model, we found that using a KL penalty primarily hinders training, consistent with previous findings [Yu et al., 2025]. We attempted using an exponential moving average of the weights during training as a reference for KL, but found it simpler to manually adjust εhigh\varepsilon_{\text{high}}.

This included a total of about 1.3M generations. We then run RL on top of this finetuned checkpoint using our most difficult subset of the data. As shown in Figure 13, applying RL yields substantial performance gains over the SFT checkpoint. Notably, the RL model improves by over 10 points on AIME’25 and 5 points on LiveCodeBench, achieving a final performance level on par with Deepseek-R1 on code and math benchmarks.

9 结论 9 Conclusion

Magistral 是我们迈向具有强化学习的通用能力系统的第一步。我们期待面前的下一个研究问题：哪些损失和优化算法最合适，通过让模型自举其自身的推理轨迹可以解锁多少收益，或者如何将算力扩展到下一个数量级。展望未来，我们也兴奋地推动 RL 在广泛的应用领域中的边界，包括工具使用、集成多模态和智能体。在探索这一前沿时，我们仍然致力于以透明和乐观的方式为科学做出贡献。

Magistral is our first step towards generally capable systems with reinforcement learning. We look forward to the next research problems ahead of us: what loss and optimization algorithms are the most appropriate, how much gain can be unlocked by bootstrapping a model on its own reasoning traces, or how to scale to the next order of magnitude of compute. Looking ahead, we are also excited to push the boundaries of RL across a whole range of applications, with tool-use, integrated multimodality, and agents. As we explore this frontier, we remain committed to contributing to science in a transparent and optimistic manner.
