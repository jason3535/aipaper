摘要 · Abstract

全球中期天气预报对许多社会和经济领域的决策至关重要。传统的数值天气预报通过增加计算资源来提高预报精度，但无法直接利用历史天气数据改进模型。我们提出了一种基于机器学习的方法“GraphCast”，它可以直接从再分析数据中训练。该方法能在不到一分钟内，以 0.25 度分辨率在全球范围内预测数百个天气变量，时间跨度达 10 天。我们证明，GraphCast 在 1380 个验证目标中的 90%上显著优于最准确的业务确定性系统，其预报支持更好的极端事件预测，包括热带气旋、大气河流和极端温度。GraphCast 是准确高效天气预报的关键进展，有助于实现机器学习在复杂动力系统建模中的潜力。

Global medium-range weather forecasting is critical to decision-making across many social and economic domains. Traditional numerical weather prediction uses increased compute resources to improve forecast accuracy, but cannot directly use historical weather data to improve the underlying model. We introduce a machine learning-based method called "GraphCast", which can be trained directly from reanalysis data. It predicts hundreds of weather variables, over 10 days at 0.25 degree resolution globally, in under one minute. We show that GraphCast significantly outperforms the most accurate operational deterministic systems on 90% of 1380 verification targets, and its forecasts support better severe event prediction, including tropical cyclones, atmospheric rivers, and extreme temperatures. GraphCast is a key advance in accurate and efficient weather forecasting, and helps realize the promise of machine learning for modeling complex dynamical systems.

核心贡献 · Key contributions

  • 提出 GraphCast，一种直接从再分析数据训练的全球中期天气预报机器学习方法。
    Introduces GraphCast, a machine learning method for global medium-range weather forecasting trained directly from reanalysis data.
  • 在单个 TPU v4 设备上不到一分钟内实现全球 0.25°分辨率 10 天预报。
    Achieves 10-day forecasts at 0.25° resolution globally in under one minute on a single TPU v4 device.
  • 在 1380 个验证目标中，90%优于最准确的操作确定性系统（HRES）。
    Outperforms the most accurate operational deterministic system (HRES) on 90% of 1380 verification targets.
  • 展示了对热带气旋、大气河流和极端温度等极端事件预测的改进。
    Demonstrates improved prediction of severe events including tropical cyclones, atmospheric rivers, and extreme temperatures.
  • 采用编码-处理-解码图神经网络架构，共 3670 万个参数。
    Uses an encode-process-decode graph neural network architecture with 36.7 million parameters.
  • 表明机器学习天气预报可以补充传统数值天气预报方法。
    Shows that machine learning weather prediction can complement traditional numerical weather prediction methods.

局限 · Limitations

  • 由于训练数据和硬件限制，以 0.25°分辨率和 6 小时时间步长运行。
    Operates at 0.25° resolution and 6-hour time steps due to training data and hardware constraints.
  • 专注于确定性预报，不如集合方法能捕捉不确定性。
    Focuses on deterministic forecasts, not capturing uncertainty as well as ensemble methods.
  • MSE 训练目标在较长预报时效鼓励空间模糊，限制了某些应用的价值。
    MSE training objective encourages spatial blurring at longer lead times, limiting value for some applications.
  • 关键依赖于来自数值天气预报系统的高质量再分析数据。
    Depends critically on high-quality reanalysis data from numerical weather prediction systems.
  • 不能替代传统方法；而是补充传统方法，并具有进一步规模扩张的潜力。
    Not a replacement for traditional methods; complements them with potential for further scaling.

引言 Introduction

2022 年 10 月中旬，意大利博洛尼亚时间 05:45 UTC，欧洲中期天气预报中心（ECMWF）的新高性能计算设施刚刚开始运行。在过去的几个小时里，综合预报系统（IFS）一直在进行复杂的计算，以预测未来几天和几周的地球天气，其首批预测刚刚开始分发给用户。这一过程每六小时重复一次，每天如此，为世界提供最准确的天气预报。

It is 05:45 UTC in mid-October, 2022, in Bologna, Italy, and the European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts (ECMWF)’s new High-Performance Computing Facility has just started operation. For the past several hours the Integrated Forecasting System (IFS) has been running sophisticated calculations to forecast Earth’s weather over the next days and weeks, and its first predictions have just begun to be disseminated to users. This process repeats every six hours, every day, to supply the world with the most accurate weather forecasts available.

IFS 以及更广泛的现代天气预报是科学和工程的胜利。天气系统的动力学是地球上最复杂的物理现象之一，每天，个人、行业和政策制定者做出的无数决策都依赖于准确的天气预报，从决定是否穿夹克到躲避危险风暴。当今天气预报的主要方法是“数值天气预报”（NWP），它涉及使用超级计算机求解天气的控制方程。NWP 的成功在于严谨且持续的研究实践，这些实践提供了对天气现象日益详细的描述，以及 NWP 如何通过增加算力资源来扩展以提升精度[benjamin2019100, bauerQuietRevolution]。因此，天气预报的准确性逐年提高，以至于地表温度或飓风路径可以提前多天预测——这在几十年前是不可想象的。

The IFS, and modern weather forecasting more generally, are triumphs of science and engineering. The dynamics of weather systems are among the most complex physical phenomena on Earth, and each day, countless decisions made by individuals, industries, and policymakers depend on accurate weather forecasts, from deciding whether to wear a jacket or to flee a dangerous storm. The dominant approach for weather forecasting today is “numerical weather prediction” (NWP), which involves solving the governing equations of weather using supercomputers. The success of NWP lies in the rigorous and ongoing research practices that provide increasingly detailed descriptions of weather phenomena, and how well NWP scales to greater accuracy with greater computational resources [benjamin2019100, bauerQuietRevolution]. As a result, the accuracy of weather forecasts have increased year after year, to the point where the surface temperature, or the path of a hurricane, can be predicted many days ahead—a possibility that was unthinkable even a few decades ago.

但是，虽然传统的 NWP 随着算力的增加而扩展良好，但其精度并不随历史数据量的增加而提高。存在大量的天气和气候数据档案，例如 ECMWF 的 MARS [mars_userdoc]，但直到最近，几乎没有实际方法可以利用这些数据直接改进预报模型的质量。相反，NWP 方法是通过训练有素的专家创新更好的模型、算法和近似来改进的，这可能是一个耗时且昂贵的过程。

But while traditional NWP scales well with compute, its accuracy does not improve with increasing amounts of historical data. There are vast archives of weather and climatological data, e.g. ECMWF’s MARS [mars_userdoc], but until recently there have been few practical means for using such data to directly improve the quality of forecast models. Rather, NWP methods are improved by highly trained experts innovating better models, algorithms, and approximations, which can be a time-consuming and costly process.

基于机器学习的天气预报（MLWP）为传统 NWP 提供了一种替代方案，其中预报模型直接从历史数据中训练。这有可能通过捕捉数据中不易用显式方程表示的模式和尺度来提高预报精度。MLWP 还通过利用现代深度学习硬件而非超级计算机，以及实现更有利的速度-精度权衡，提供了更高效率的机会。最近，MLWP 在传统 NWP 相对较弱的领域帮助改进了基于 NWP 的预报，例如次季节热浪预测[lopez2022global]和基于雷达图像的降水临近预报[shi2017deep, sonderby2020metnet, ravuri2021skilful, espeholt2022deep]，在这些领域，精确的方程和稳健的数值方法不那么可用。

Machine learning-based weather prediction (MLWP) offers an alternative to traditional NWP, where forecast models are trained directly from historical data. This has potential to improve forecast accuracy by capturing patterns and scales in the data which are not easily represented in explicit equations. MLWP also offers opportunities for greater efficiency by exploiting modern deep learning hardware, rather than supercomputers, and striking more favorable speed-accuracy trade-offs. Recently MLWP has helped improve on NWP-based forecasting in regimes where traditional NWP is relatively weak, for example sub-seasonal heat wave prediction [lopez2022global] and precipitation nowcasting from radar images [shi2017deep, sonderby2020metnet, ravuri2021skilful, espeholt2022deep], where accurate equations and robust numerical methods are not as available.

在中期天气预报中，即预测未来 10 天的大气变量，像 IFS 这样的基于 NWP 的系统仍然是最准确的。世界上顶级的确定性业务系统是 ECMWF 的高分辨率预报（HRES），它是 IFS 的一个组成部分，以 0.1°的经纬度分辨率生成全球 10 天预报，耗时约一小时[rasp2020weatherbench]。然而，在过去几年中，用于中期预报的 MLWP 方法一直在稳步推进，这得益于 WeatherBench 等基准测试[rasp2020weatherbench]。基于卷积神经网络[weyn2019can, weyn2020improving, rasp2021data]和 Transformer[nguyen2023climax]的深度学习架构在粗于 1.0°的经纬度分辨率上显示出有希望的结果，而最近的工作——使用图神经网络（GNN）[keisler2022forecasting]、傅里叶神经算子[pathak2022fourcastnet, kurth2022fourcastnet]和 Transformer[bi2022pangu]——报告的性能开始接近 IFS 在 1.0°和 0.25°分辨率下对少数变量和长达七天的预报时效的表现。

In medium-range weather forecasting, i.e., predicting atmospheric variables up to 10 days ahead, NWP-based systems like the IFS are still most accurate. The top deterministic operational system in the world is ECMWF’s High RESolution forecast (HRES), a component of IFS which produces global 10-day forecasts at 0.1​°0.1° latitude/longitude resolution, in around an hour [rasp2020weatherbench]. However, over the past several years, MLWP methods for medium-range forecasting have been steadily advancing, facilitated by benchmarks such as WeatherBench [rasp2020weatherbench]. Deep learning architectures based on convolutional neural networks [weyn2019can, weyn2020improving, rasp2021data] and Transformers [nguyen2023climax] have shown promising results at latitude/longitude resolutions coarser than 1.0​°1.0°, and recent works—which use graph neural networks (GNN) [keisler2022forecasting], Fourier neural operators [pathak2022fourcastnet, kurth2022fourcastnet], and Transformers [bi2022pangu]—have reported performance that begins to approach IFS’s at 1.0​°1.0° and 0.25​°0.25° for a handful of variables, and lead times up to seven days.

GraphCast 模型 GraphCast

这里我们介绍一种新的用于全球中期天气预报的 MLWP 方法，称为“GraphCast”，它在单个 Google Cloud TPU v4 设备上不到一分钟内生成准确的 10 天预报，并支持预测热带气旋路径、大气河流和极端温度等应用。

Here we introduce a new MLWP approach for global medium-range weather forecasting called “GraphCast”, which produces an accurate 10-day forecast in under a minute on a single Google Cloud TPU v4 device, and supports applications including predicting tropical cyclone tracks, atmospheric rivers, and extreme temperatures.

GraphCast 将地球天气的两个最新状态（当前时间和六小时前）作为输入，并预测六小时后的下一个天气状态。单个天气状态由 0.25°经纬度网格（721×1440）表示，对应赤道附近约 28×28 公里的分辨率（图 1a），其中每个网格点代表一组地表和大气变量（列于表 1）。与传统 NWP 系统类似，GraphCast 是自回归的：可以通过将其自身预测作为输入反馈来“展开”，以生成任意长的天气状态轨迹（图 1b–c）。

GraphCast takes as input the two most recent states of Earth’s weather—the current time and six hours earlier—and predicts the next state of the weather six hours ahead. A single weather state is represented by a 0.25​°0.25° latitude/longitude grid (721×14407211440721\times 1440), which corresponds to roughly 28×28282828\times 28 kilometer resolution at the equator (Figure 1a), where each grid point represents a set of surface and atmospheric variables (listed in Table 1). Like traditional NWP systems, GraphCast is autoregressive: it can be “rolled out” by feeding its own predictions back in as input, to generate an arbitrarily long trajectory of weather states (Figure 1b–c).

GraphCast 实现为一个神经网络架构，基于“编码-处理-解码”配置中的 GNN [battaglia2018relational]，总共有 3670 万个参数。先前基于 GNN 的学习模拟器[sanchez2020learning, pfaff2021learning]在学习由偏微分方程建模的流体和其他系统的复杂动力学方面非常有效，这支持了它们对天气动力学建模的适用性。

GraphCast is implemented as a neural network architecture, based on GNNs in an “encode-process-decode” configuration [battaglia2018relational], with a total of 36.7 million parameters. Previous GNN-based learned simulators [sanchez2020learning, pfaff2021learning] have been very effective at learning the complex dynamics of fluid and other systems modeled by partial differential equations, which supports their suitability for modeling weather dynamics.

编码器（图 1d）使用单个 GNN 层将变量（归一化为零均值单位方差）表示为输入网格上的节点属性，映射到内部“多网格”表示上的学习节点属性。

The encoder (Figure 1d) uses a single GNN layer to map variables (normalized to zero-mean unit-variance) represented as node attributes on the input grid to learned node attributes on an internal “multi-mesh” representation.

多网格（图 1g）是一个空间均匀的图，在全球具有高空间分辨率。它通过迭代六次细化正二十面体（12 个节点，20 个面，30 条边）来定义，每次细化将每个三角形分成四个较小的三角形（导致面数和边数增加四倍），并将节点重新投影到球面上。多网格包含来自最高分辨率网格的 40,962 个节点，以及中间图中创建的所有边的并集，形成具有不同长度的边的扁平层次结构。

The multi-mesh (Figure 1g) is a graph which is spatially homogeneous, with high spatial resolution over the globe. It is defined by refining a regular icosahedron (12 nodes, 20 faces, 30 edges) iteratively six times, where each refinement divides each triangle into four smaller ones (leading to four times more faces and edges), and reprojecting the nodes onto the sphere. The multi-mesh contains the 40,962 nodes from the highest resolution mesh, and the union of all the edges created in the intermediate graphs, forming a flat hierarchy of edges with varying lengths.

处理器（图 1e）使用 16 个非共享的 GNN 层在多网格上执行学习到的消息传递，从而能够以少量消息传递步骤实现高效的局部和远程信息传播。

The processor (Figure 1e) uses 16 unshared GNN layers to perform learned message-passing on the multi-mesh, enabling efficient local and long-range information propagation with few message-passing steps.

解码器（图 1f）将最终处理器层的学习特征从多网格表示映射回经纬度网格。它使用单个 GNN 层，并将输出预测为对最近输入状态的残差更新（输出归一化以实现目标残差的单位方差）。更多架构细节见补充材料第 3 节。

The decoder (Figure 1f) maps the final processor layer’s learned features from the multi-mesh representation back to the latitude-longitude grid. It uses a single GNN layer, and predicts the output as a residual update to the most recent input state (with output normalization to achieve unit-variance on the target residual). See Supplements Section 3 for further architectural details.

在模型开发过程中，我们使用了来自 ECMWF 的 ERA5 [hersbach2020era5]再分析档案的 39 年（1979–2017）历史数据。作为训练目标，我们平均了按垂直层加权的均方误差（MSE）。误差是在 GraphCast 的预测状态与对应的 ERA5 状态之间，在 N 个自回归步骤上计算的。在训练过程中，N 的值从 1 逐步增加到 12（即从六小时到三天）。GraphCast 通过梯度下降和反向传播进行训练，以最小化训练目标。训练 GraphCast 在 32 个 Cloud TPU v4 设备上使用批量并行大约需要四周。更多训练细节见补充材料第 4 节。

During model development, we used 39 years (1979–2017) of historical data from ECMWF’s ERA5 [hersbach2020era5] reanalysis archive. As a training objective, we averaged the mean squared error (MSE) weighted by vertical level. Error was computed between GraphCast’s predicted state and the corresponding ERA5 state over N𝑁N autoregressive steps. The value of N𝑁N was increased incrementally from 1 to 12 (i.e., six hours to three days) over the course of training. GraphCast was trained to minimize the training objective using gradient descent and backpropagation. Training GraphCast took roughly four weeks on 32 Cloud TPU v4 devices using batch parallelism. See Supplements Section 4 for further training details.

与真实部署场景一致（其中未来信息不可用于模型开发），我们在 2018 年及以后的保留数据上评估了 GraphCast（见补充材料第 5.1 节）。

Consistent with real deployment scenarios, where future information is not available for model development, we evaluated GraphCast on the held out data from the years 2018 onward (see Supplements Section 5.1).

验证方法 Verification methods

我们通过将 GraphCast 的精度与 HRES 在大量变量、层和预报时效上进行比较，全面验证了 GraphCast 的预报技能。我们使用两个技能指标量化 GraphCast、HRES 和 ML 基线的各自技能：均方根误差（RMSE）和异常相关系数（ACC）。

We verify GraphCast’s forecast skill comprehensively by comparing its accuracy to HRES’s on a large number of variables, levels, and lead times. We quantify the respective skills of GraphCast, HRES, and ML baselines with two skill metrics: the root mean square error (RMSE) and the anomaly correlation coefficient (ACC).

在 GraphCast 在每个网格点预测的 227 个变量和层组合中，我们评估了其与 HRES 在 69 个组合上的技能，对应于 WeatherBench [rasp2020weatherbench]的 13 个层和 ECMWF 评分卡[haiden2018evaluation]中的变量；见表 1 中的粗体变量和层，以及补充材料第 1.2 节中评估期间 HRES 循环的业务版本。注意，我们将总降水量排除在评估之外，因为 ERA5 降水数据存在已知偏差[lavers2022evaluation]。除了正文中报告的总体性能外，补充材料第 7 节提供了更详细的评估，包括其他变量、区域性能、纬度和气压层效应、谱特性、模糊化、与其他基于 ML 的预报的比较以及模型设计选择的影响。

Of the 227 variable and level combinations predicted by GraphCast at each grid point, we evaluated its skill versus HRES on 69 of them, corresponding to the 13 levels of WeatherBench[rasp2020weatherbench] and variables from the ECMWF Scorecard [haiden2018evaluation]; see boldface variables and levels in Table 1 and Supplements Section 1.2 for which HRES cycle was operational during the evaluation period. Note, we exclude total precipitation from the evaluation because ERA5 precipitation data has known biases [lavers2022evaluation]. In addition to the aggregate performance reported in the main text, Supplements Section 7 provides further detailed evaluations, including other variables, regional performance, latitude and pressure level effects, spectral properties, blurring, comparisons to other ML-based forecasts, and effects of model design choices.

在进行这些比较时，确定技能的两个关键选择是：(1) 用于比较的基准事实的选择，以及(2) 仔细考虑用于将数据与观测值关联的数据同化窗口。我们使用 ERA5 作为评估 GraphCast 的基准事实，因为它经过训练以接收 ERA5 数据作为输入并预测 ERA5 数据作为输出。然而，将 HRES 预报与 ERA5 进行比较会导致初始预报步骤上的非零误差。相反，我们构建了一个“HRES 预报第 0 步”（HRES-fc0）数据集作为 HRES 的基准事实。HRES-fc0 包含未来初始时刻 HRES 预报的输入（见补充材料第 1.2 节），确保每个数据点由最近的观测值支撑，并且 HRES 预报的第零步误差为零。

In making these comparisons, two key choices underlie how skill is established: (1) the selection of the ground truth for comparison, and (2) a careful accounting of the data assimilation windows used to ground data with observations. We use ERA5 as the ground truth for evaluating GraphCast, since it was trained to take ERA5 data as input and predict ERA5 data as outputs. However, evaluating HRES forecasts against ERA5 would result in non-zero error on the initial forecast step. Instead, we constructed an “HRES forecast at step 0” (HRES-fc0) dataset to use as ground truth for HRES. HRES-fc0 contains the inputs to HRES forecasts at future initializations (see Supplements Section 1.2), ensuring that each data point is grounded by recent observations and that the zeroth step of HRES forecasts will have zero error.

方法之间的公平比较要求任何方法都不应拥有其他方法无法获得的特权信息。由于天气预报数据的性质，这需要仔细控制 ERA5 和 HRES 数据同化窗口之间的差异。每天，HRES 使用以 00z、06z、12z 和 18z（其中 18z 表示 18:00 UTC）为中心的四个±3 小时窗口同化观测数据，而 ERA5 使用以 00z 和 12z 为中心的两个+9h/-3h 窗口，或等效地以 06z 和 18z 为中心的两个+3h/-9h 窗口。我们选择从 06z 和 18z 初始时刻评估 GraphCast 的预报，确保其输入包含来自未来+3h 观测的信息，与 HRES 的输入匹配。我们没有从 00z 和 12z 初始时刻评估 GraphCast，以避免 ERA5 输入中+9h 前瞻与 HRES 输入中+3h 前瞻之间的不匹配。在选择目标预报时效时，我们应用了相同的逻辑，并仅每 12 小时评估一次目标，以确保地面真值 ERA5 和 HRES 具有相同的+3h 前瞻（详见补充材料第 5.2 节）。

Fair comparisons between methods require that no method should have privileged information not available to the other. Because of the nature of weather forecast data, this requires careful control of the differences between the ERA5 and HRES data assimilation windows. Each day, HRES assimilates observations using four +/-3h windows centered on 00z, 06z, 12z and 18z (where 18z means 18:00 UTC), while ERA5 uses two +9h/-3h windows centered on 00z and 12z, or equivalently two +3h/-9h windows centered on 06z and 18z. We chose to evaluate GraphCast’s forecasts from the 06z and 18z initializations, ensuring its inputs carry information from +3h of future observations, matching HRES’s inputs. We did not evaluate GraphCast from 00z and 12z initializations, avoiding a mismatch between a +9h lookahead in ERA5 inputs versus +3h lookahead for HRES inputs. We applied the same logic when choosing target lead times and evaluate targets only every 12h to ensure that the ground truth ERA5 and HRES have the same +3h lookahead (see Supplements Section 5.2).

HRES 在 06z 和 18z 初始化的预报仅运行 3.75 天的预报时长（HRES 的 00z 和 12z 初始化运行 10 天）。因此，我们的图表将用虚线表示过渡，其中虚线前 3.5 天是与 HRES 在 06z 和 18z 初始化的比较，虚线后是与 00z 和 12z 初始化的比较。补充材料第 5 节包含进一步的验证细节。

HRES’s forecasts initialized at 06z and 18z are only run for a horizon of 3.75 days (HRES’s 00z and 12z initializations are run for 10 days). Therefore, our figures will indicate a transition with dashed line, where the 3.5 days before the line are comparisons with HRES initialized at 06z and 18z, and after the line are comparisons with initializations at 00z and 12z. Supplements Section 5 contains further verification details.

预报验证结果 Forecast verification results

我们发现，在 0.25°经纬度水平分辨率和 13 个垂直层次上评估 10 天预报时，GraphCast 的天气预报技能优于 HRES。

We find that GraphCast has greater weather forecasting skill than HRES when evaluated on 10-day forecasts at a horizontal resolution of 0.25​°0.25° for latitude/longitude and at 13 vertical levels.

HRES 表现优于 GraphCast 的大气区域（记分卡中顶部的红色行）不成比例地位于平流层，并且具有最低的训练损失权重（详见补充材料第 7.2.2 节）。当排除 50 hPa 层次时，GraphCast 在剩余 1280 个目标中的 96.9%上显著优于 HRES。当排除 50 和 100 hPa 层次时，GraphCast 在剩余 1180 个目标中的 99.7%上显著优于 HRES。在进行分区评估时，我们发现先前的结果在全球范围内普遍成立，详见补充材料图 16、17 和 18。

The regions of the atmosphere in which HRES had better performance than GraphCast (top rows in red in the scorecards), were disproportionately localized in the stratosphere, and had the lowest training loss weight (see Supplements Section 7.2.2). When excluding the 50 hPahPa\mathrm{h}\mathrm{P}\mathrm{a} level, GraphCast significantly outperforms HRES on 96.9%percent96.996.9\% of the remaining 1280 targets. When excluding levels 50 and 100 hPahPa\mathrm{h}\mathrm{P}\mathrm{a}, GraphCast significantly outperforms HRES on 99.7%percent99.799.7\% of the 1180 remaining targets. When conducting per region evaluations, we found the previous results to generally hold across the globe, as detailed in Supplements Figures 16, 17 and 18.

我们发现，在 MSE 损失中增加自回归步数可以提高 GraphCast 在较长预报时效的性能（详见补充材料第 7.3.2 节），并鼓励其通过预测空间平滑输出来表达不确定性，从而导致较长预报时效的预报更模糊（详见补充材料第 7.5.3 节）。然而，HRES 的底层物理方程不会导致模糊预测。为了评估如果允许 HRES 也模糊其预报，GraphCast 相对于 HRES 在 RMSE 技能上的优势是否保持不变，我们通过最小化相对于模型各自地面真值的 RMSE，为 GraphCast 和 HRES 拟合了模糊滤波器。我们发现，最优模糊的 GraphCast 在 1380 个验证目标中的 88.0%上比最优模糊的 HRES 具有更高的技能，这与我们上述结论基本一致（详见补充材料第 7.4 节）。

We found that increasing the number of auto-regressive steps in the MSE loss improves GraphCast performance at longer lead time (see Supplements Section 7.3.2) and encourages it to express its uncertainty by predicting spatially smoothed outputs, leading to blurrier forecasts at longer lead times (see Supplements Section 7.5.3). HRES’s underlying physical equations, however, do not lead to blurred predictions. To assess whether GraphCast’s relative advantage over HRES on RMSE skill is maintained if HRES is also allowed to blur its forecasts, we fit blurring filters to GraphCast and to HRES, by minimizing the RMSE with respect to the models’ respective ground truths. We found that optimally blurred GraphCast has greater skill than optimally blurred HRES on 88.0%percent88.088.0\% of our 1380 verification targets which is generally consistent with our above conclusions (see Supplements Section 7.4).

我们还将 GraphCast 的性能与顶级竞争 ML 天气模型 Pangu-Weather [bi2022pangu]进行了比较，发现 GraphCast 在其提出的 252 个目标中的 99.2%上优于 Pangu-Weather（详见补充材料第 6 节）。

We also compared GraphCast’s performance to the top competing ML-based weather model, Pangu-Weather [bi2022pangu], and found GraphCast outperformed it on 99.2%percent99.299.2\% of the 252 targets they presented (see Supplements Section 6 for details).

极端事件预报结果 Severe event forecasting results

除了在广泛的变量和预报时效上评估 GraphCast 相对于 HRES 的预报技能外，我们还评估了其预报如何支持预测极端事件，包括热带气旋、大气河流和极端温度。这些是关键的下游应用，GraphCast 并未专门针对这些进行训练，但对人类活动非常重要。

Beyond evaluating GraphCast’s forecast skill against HRES’s on a wide range of variables and lead times, we also evaluate how its forecasts support predicting severe events, including tropical cyclones, atmospheric rivers, and extreme temperature. These are key downstream applications for which GraphCast is not specifically trained, but which are very important for human activity.

热带气旋路径 Tropical cyclone tracks

提高热带气旋预报的准确性有助于避免伤害和生命损失，并减少经济损失 [martinez2020forecast]。气旋的存在、强度和轨迹是通过将追踪算法应用于位势（z）、水平风（10u/10v、u/v）和平均海平面气压（msl）的预报来预测的。我们基于 ECMWF 公布的协议 [magnusson2021tropical] 实现了一个追踪算法，并将其应用于 GraphCast 的预报，以生成气旋路径预测（详见补充材料第 8.1 节）。作为比较基线，我们使用了从 HRES 的 0.1°预报中获得的业务路径，这些路径存储在 TIGGE 档案中 [bougeault2010thorpex, swinbank2016]，并测量了两个模型相对于 IBTrACS [knapp2010international, ibtracs-dataset-2018] 路径的误差，IBTrACS 是一个独立的、从各种分析和观测来源汇总的气旋路径再分析数据集。与热带气旋预测的既定评估一致 [magnusson2021tropical]，我们在 GraphCast 和 HRES 都检测到气旋时评估所有路径，确保两个模型在相同事件上评估，并验证每个模型的真阳性率相似。

Improving the accuracy of tropical cyclone forecasts can help avoid injury and loss of life, as well as reducing economic harm [martinez2020forecast]. A cyclone’s existence, strength, and trajectory is predicted by applying a tracking algorithm to forecasts of geopotential (z), horizontal wind (10u/10v, u/v), and mean sea-level pressure (msl). We implemented a tracking algorithm based on ECMWF’s published protocols [magnusson2021tropical] and applied it to GraphCast’s forecasts, to produce cyclone track predictions (see Supplements Section 8.1). As a baseline for comparison, we used the operational tracks obtained from HRES’s 0.1​°0.1° forecasts, stored in the TIGGE archive [bougeault2010thorpex, swinbank2016], and measured errors for both models against the tracks from IBTrACS [knapp2010international, ibtracs-dataset-2018], a separate reanalysis dataset of cyclone tracks aggregated from various analysis and observational sources. Consistent with established evaluation of tropical cyclone prediction [magnusson2021tropical], we evaluate all tracks when both GraphCast and HRES detect a cyclone, ensuring that both models are evaluated on the same events, and verify that each model’s true-positive rates are similar.

大气河流 Atmospheric rivers

大气河流是大气中的狭窄区域，负责中纬度地区大部分向极地水汽输送，并在美国西海岸产生 30%-65%的年降水量 [chapman2019improving]。其强度可以通过垂直积分水汽输送 ivt [neiman2008meteorological, moore2012physical] 来表征，指示事件将提供有益降水还是与灾难性破坏相关 [corringham2019atmospheric]。ivt 可以从水平风速（u 和 v）和比湿（q）的非线性组合计算得出，GraphCast 预测这些变量。我们在寒冷月份（10 月至 4 月）评估了北美沿海和东太平洋地区的 GraphCast 预报，此时大气河流最为频繁。尽管没有专门针对大气河流表征进行训练，但图 3c 显示，与 HRES 相比，GraphCast 改进了 ivt 的预测，从短预报时效的 25%到较长预报时效的 10%（详见补充材料第 8.2 节）。

Atmospheric rivers are narrow regions of the atmosphere which are responsible for the majority of the poleward water vapor transport across the mid-latitudes, and generate 30%-65% of annual precipitation on the U.S. West Coast [chapman2019improving]. Their strength can be characterized by the vertically integrated water vapor transport ivt [neiman2008meteorological, moore2012physical], indicating whether an event will provide beneficial precipitation or be associated with catastrophic damage [corringham2019atmospheric]. ivt can be computed from the non-linear combination of the horizontal wind speed (u and v) and specific humidity (q), which GraphCast predicts. We evaluate GraphCast forecasts over coastal North America and the Eastern Pacific during cold months (Oct–Apr), when atmospheric rivers are most frequent. Despite not being specifically trained to characterize atmospheric rivers, Figure 3c shows that GraphCast improves the prediction of ivt compared to HRES, from 25% at short lead time, to 10% at longer horizons (see Supplements Section 8.2 for details).

极端高温与低温 Extreme heat and cold

极端高温和低温的特征是相对于典型气候学的大异常 [magnusson2014verification, lopez2022global, ecmwf2022heatwaveuk]，这可能很危险并扰乱人类活动。我们评估了 HRES 和 GraphCast 在预测超过位置、一天中的时间和月份的气候学前 2%事件方面的技能，针对 2t 在 12 小时、5 天和 10 天预报时效，覆盖北半球和南半球夏季月份的大陆区域。我们绘制了精确率-召回率曲线 [saito2015precision] 以反映减少假阳性（高精确率）和减少假阴性（高召回率）之间不同的可能权衡。对于每个预报，我们通过改变一个“增益”参数来获得曲线，该参数缩放 2t 预报相对于中位气候学的偏差。

Extreme heat and cold are characterized by large anomalies with respect to typical climatology [magnusson2014verification, lopez2022global, ecmwf2022heatwaveuk], which can be dangerous and disrupt human activities. We evaluate the skill of HRES and GraphCast in predicting events above the top 2% climatology across location, time of day, and month of the year, for 2t at 12-hour, 5-day, and 10-day lead times, for land regions across northern and southern hemisphere over summer months. We plot precision-recall curves [saito2015precision] to reflect different possible trade-offs between reducing false positives (high precision) and reducing false negatives (high recall). For each forecast, we obtain the curve by varying a “gain” parameter that scales the 2t forecast’s deviations with respect to the median climatology.

训练数据时效性影响 Effect of training data recency

GraphCast 可以定期用最新数据重新训练，这原则上使其能够捕捉随时间变化的天气模式，如 ENSO 循环和其他振荡，以及气候变化的影响。我们训练了四个 GraphCast 变体，数据始终从 1979 年开始，但分别结束于 2017 年、2018 年、2019 年和 2020 年（我们将结束于 2017 年的变体标记为“GraphCast:<<2018”等）。我们将它们的性能与 HRES 在 2021 年测试数据上进行了比较。

GraphCast can be re-trained periodically with recent data, which in principle allows it to capture weather patterns that change over time, such as the ENSO cycle and other oscillations, as well as effects of climate change. We trained four variants of GraphCast with data that always began in 1979, but ended in 2017, 2018, 2019, and 2020, respectively (we label the variant ending in 2017 as “GraphCast:&lt;&lt;2018”, etc). We compared their performances to HRES on 2021 test data.

结论 Conclusions

GraphCast 相对于 HRES 的预报技能和效率表明，MLWP 方法现在与传统天气预报方法具有竞争力。此外，GraphCast 在极端事件预报上的性能（它并非直接针对此训练）证明了其稳健性和下游价值潜力。我们相信这标志着天气预报的一个转折点，有助于开辟新的途径，通过使廉价预测更准确、更易获取且适用于特定应用，来加强个人和行业对天气依赖决策的广度。

GraphCast’s forecast skill and efficiency compared to HRES shows MLWP methods are now competitive with traditional weather forecasting methods. Additionally, GraphCast’s performance on severe event forecasting, which it was not directly trained for, demonstrates its robustness and potential for downstream value. We believe this marks a turning point in weather forecasting, which helps open new avenues to strengthen the breadth of weather-dependent decision-making by individuals and industries, by making cheap prediction more accurate, more accessible, and suitable for specific applications.

GraphCast 拥有 3670 万个参数，按现代 ML 标准是一个相对较小的模型，选择它是为了保持内存占用可控。虽然 HRES 以 0.1°分辨率、137 个层次和高达 1 小时时间步长发布，但 GraphCast 以 0.25°经纬度分辨率、37 个垂直层次和 6 小时时间步长运行，这是因为 ERA5 训练数据的原生 0.25°分辨率，以及将更高分辨率数据适配到硬件上的工程挑战。总的来说，GraphCast 应被视为一个模型家族，当前版本是我们在当前工程约束下实际能容纳的最大版本，但未来随着更大的计算资源和更高分辨率的数据，它有潜力进一步扩展。

With 36.7 million parameters, GraphCast is a relatively small model by modern ML standards, chosen to keep the memory footprint tractable. And while HRES is released on 0.1​°0.1° resolution, 137 levels, and up to 1 hour time steps, GraphCast operated on 0.25​°0.25° latitude-longitude resolution, 37 vertical levels, and 6 hour time steps, because of the ERA5 training data’s native 0.25​°0.25° resolution, and engineering challenges in fitting higher resolution data on hardware. Generally GraphCast should be viewed as a family of models, with the current version being the largest we can practically fit under current engineering constraints, but which have potential to scale much further in the future with greater compute resources and higher resolution data.

我们方法的一个关键局限性在于如何处理不确定性。我们专注于确定性预报并与 HRES 进行比较，但 ECMWF 的 IFS 的另一支柱——集合预报系统 ENS——对于 10 天以上的预报尤其重要。天气动力学的非线性意味着在较长的预报时效上不确定性增加，这很难被单一的确定性预报捕捉。ENS 通过生成多个随机预报来解决这个问题，这些预报模拟未来天气的经验分布，但生成多个预报成本高昂。相比之下，GraphCast 的 MSE 训练目标鼓励其通过空间模糊预测来表达不确定性，这可能限制了其在某些应用中的价值。构建更明确建模不确定性的系统是至关重要的下一步。

One key limitation of our approach is in how uncertainty is handled. We focused on deterministic forecasts and compared against HRES, but the other pillar of ECMWF’s IFS, the ensemble forecasting system, ENS, is especially important for 10+ day forecasts. The non-linearity of weather dynamics means there is increasing uncertainty at longer lead times, which is not well-captured by a single deterministic forecast. ENS addresses this by generating multiple, stochastic forecasts, which model the empirical distribution of future weather, however generating multiple forecasts is expensive. By contrast, GraphCast’s MSE training objective encourages it to express its uncertainty by spatially blurring its predictions, which may limit its value for some applications. Building systems that model uncertainty more explicitly is a crucial next step.

需要强调的是，数据驱动的 MLWP 关键依赖于通过 NWP 同化的大量高质量数据，而像 ECMWF 的 MARS 档案这样的丰富数据源是无价的。因此，我们的方法不应被视为传统天气预报方法的替代品，这些方法已经发展了几十年，在许多现实世界环境中经过严格测试，并提供了许多我们尚未探索的功能。相反，我们的工作应被解释为 MLWP 能够应对现实世界预报问题挑战的证据，并具有补充和改进当前最佳方法的潜力。

It is important to emphasize that data-driven MLWP depends critically on large quantities of high-quality data, assimilated via NWP, and that rich data sources like ECMWF’s MARS archive are invaluable. Therefore, our approach should not be regarded as a replacement for traditional weather forecasting methods, which have been developed for decades, rigorously tested in many real-world contexts, and offer many features we have not yet explored. Rather our work should be interpreted as evidence that MLWP is able to meet the challenges of real-world forecasting problems, and has potential to complement and improve the current best methods.

除了天气预报，GraphCast 可以为其他重要的地理时空预报问题开辟新方向，包括气候与生态、能源、农业以及人类和生物活动，以及其他复杂动力系统。我们相信，基于丰富真实世界数据训练的学习模拟器将在推进机器学习在物理科学中的作用方面至关重要。

Beyond weather forecasting, GraphCast can open new directions for other important geo-spatiotemporal forecasting problems, including climate and ecology, energy, agriculture, and human and biological activity, as well as other complex dynamical systems. We believe that learned simulators, trained on rich, real-world data, will be crucial in advancing the role of machine learning in the physical sciences.
