摘要 · Abstract

本文评测了 Anthropic 的 Fable 5.1 模型，重点介绍其能力、定价变化及安全措施的减少。该模型在编程、科学研究和智能体任务上较前代有适度改进，在 Terminal-Bench-Science 和 CursorBench 等基准测试中表现显著。Anthropic 通过降低缓存读取价格来降低实际成本，并为符合条件的客户提供零数据保留。文章指出，用户反应不一，有人称赞其编程能力和个性，但也有人担忧其 token 消耗和成本。总体而言，Fable 5.1 被定位为强劲的竞争者，尽管部分基准测试出现倒退或被 GPT-6 Astra 超越。

This article reviews Anthropic's Fable 5.1 model, highlighting its capabilities, pricing changes, and reduced safeguards. The model shows modest improvements over previous versions in coding, scientific research, and agentic tasks, with notable gains in benchmarks like Terminal-Bench-Science and CursorBench. Anthropic has lowered effective costs by reducing cache read prices, and offers zero data retention for eligible customers. The article notes mixed reactions, with praise for coding and personality but concerns about token usage and cost. Overall, Fable 5.1 is positioned as a strong competitor, though some benchmarks show regressions or are outperformed by GPT-6 Astra.

核心贡献 · Key contributions

  • Fable 5.1 在编程、智能体式任务与长时运行任务上有所提升，在 Terminal-Bench-Science 与 CursorBench 上进步显著。
    Fable 5.1 improves coding, agentic work, and long-running tasks, with strong gains on Terminal-Bench-Science and CursorBench.
  • 缓存读取价格从每百万 token 1 美元降至 0.25 美元，使智能体式工作的有效成本最多降低约 45%。
    Cache read price cut from $1 to $0.25 per million tokens lowers effective cost up to ~45% for agentic work.
  • 生物学防护措施对良性请求的干预减少 85%；Claude Code 每会话的网络干预减少约 60%。
    Biology safeguards intervene 85% less on benign requests; cyber interventions per Claude Code session drop ~60%.
  • 符合条件的客户现可使用零数据保留，消除了企业采用的一大障碍。
    Zero data retention now available for eligible customers, removing a major enterprise adoption barrier.
  • 写作质量与语气改善，减少“Claude 腔”，在长多步任务中更易读。
    Writing quality and tone improve, with reduced Claude-speak and better readability over long multi-step tasks.
  • 在 Artificial Analysis 指数与 Vals 综合榜领先，但相对 Fable 5 提升有限，部分基准出现回退。
    Leads Artificial Analysis Index and Vals composite, though gains over Fable 5 are modest with some regressions.

局限 · Limitations

  • token 消耗偏高推高实际成本；订阅限额未变，重度使用更快耗尽配额。
    Token-hungry behavior raises real costs; subscription limits unchanged, so heavy use exhausts quotas faster.
  • 部分基准回退：Toolathon、HealthBench、BioMysteryBench，以及 AA-Briefcase 的呈现质量。
    Some benchmarks regress: Toolathon, HealthBench, BioMysteryBench, and presentation quality in AA-Briefcase.
  • 更高努力等级导致多余的非请求编辑，拉低 FrontierCode 分数；努力等级可使 token 用量波动 11 倍。
    Higher effort levels cause extra unrequested edits, hurting FrontierCode scores; effort level swings token use 11x.
  • 在 FrontierMath、ARC-AGI 与 Epoch ECI 上落后于 GPT-6 Astra；计算机使用与智能体式设计已被超越。
    Trails GPT-6 Astra on FrontierMath, ARC-AGI, and Epoch ECI; computer use and agentic design now eclipsed.
  • 安全分类器仍会误判并将用户降级到旧模型，在某些智能体式提示注入场景下不安全。
    Safety classifiers still misfire and can punt users to older models, unsafe in some agentic prompt-injection contexts.

我们的价格便宜 Our Price Cheap

Anthropic 在 Fable 5.1 的定价上做了一个奇怪的举动。标价与 Fable 5 相比没有变化，但实际价格更低，因为他们将缓存读取的价格从每百万 token 1 美元降低到了 0.25 美元。

Anthropic is doing a strange thing with Fable 5.1 pricing. The headline price is unchanged from Fable 5, but the effective price is lower because they are reducing the pricing on cache reads from $1 to $0.25 per million tokens.

这将使典型的每 token 成本降低 25%，而高度智能体式工作的成本降低“最高约 45%”，这与 Boris 所说的典型 Claude Code 使用成本降低 38% 相符：

This will lower typical per-token costs by 25% and highly agentic work costs by ‘up to approximately 45%,’ consistent with Boris’s 38% cheaper for typical Claude Code use:

我认为这通过与实际成本对齐来使激励一致，但我倾向于将部分折扣放在标价上。人都是简单的生物，有时候你得站在他们的角度说话。

I presume this aligns incentives, by lining up with actual costs, but I would have been inclined to put some of the discount in headline costs instead. People are simple creatures, you have to talk to them on their level sometimes.

零数据保留与减少的保障措施 Zero Data Retention and Reduced Safeguards

尽管 Fable 5 显然是市面上最好的模型，但它在 Ramp 上占 Anthropic 美元支出的比例从未超过约 11%。

Fable 5 never got above about 11% of Anthropic dollar spend on Ramp, despite being the clearly best model out there.

Two of the big objections were the safeguards having a large blast radius that stopped ordinary work, and companies, often due to various regulatory concerns, failing to abide Anthropic’s data retention policy, where they required records be kept for 30 days.

能够遵守规定的个人 Dude 拥有巨大的编码优势。

The individual Dude, who could abide, had huge coding edge.

Anthropic 已听取了你的意见，并花了一些时间适应，政府也比之前冷静了下来——之前政府非常紧张，因一个所谓的‘越狱’而迫使 Fable 下线。Anthropic 正在开发一个新系统，将允许‘符合条件的客户’通过让客户存储数据的方式，在零外部数据保留的情况下使用 Fable 5.1。在此之前，这些客户可以享受完全的零数据保留。

Anthropic has heard you, and had some time to get more comfortable, and for the government to calm down versus when it was so nervous it forced Fable offline over a supposed ‘jailbreak.’ Anthropic is working on a new system that will allow ‘eligible customers’ to use Fable 5.1 with zero outside data retention, via letting the customer store the data. Until then, full zero data retention is available for those customers.

We should see massive adoption of Fable 5.1 within the Anthropic ecosystem, far more than the 11% for Fable 5. If we do not, then people really are purely balking at the headline price without thinking it through.
