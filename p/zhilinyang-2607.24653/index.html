Kimi K3：开放前沿智能

Kimi K3: Open Frontier Intelligence

摘要 · Abstract

我们推出了 Kimi K3，这是一个 2.8T 参数的混合专家模型，激活参数为 1040 亿，具备原生视觉能力和 100 万 token 的上下文窗口。Kimi K3 基于 Kimi Delta Attention 和 Attention Residuals 构建，这些技术改善了序列长度和模型深度上的信息流动。结合 Stable LatentMoE（每个 token 有效激活 896 个路由专家中的 16 个）以及经过优化的训练和数据方案，这些进展使得整体扩展效率相较于 Kimi K2 提高了约 2.5 倍。后训练阶段重点包括在通用、智能体（agentic）和编程领域的强化学习，以及多种推理努力水平，从而实现了组合泛化和稳健的长周期执行能力。在 2.8T 规模下，Kimi K3 得到了多个领域基础设施进展的支持：针对 KDA 的算法-系统协同设计、完美平衡的专家并行训练与高效内存管理、支持持久化回放和沙盒状态的百万 token 智能体强化学习，以及部署创新。广泛的评估表明，Kimi K3 在长周期编程、智能体、知识、推理和视觉任务上达到了前沿水平。虽然其整体性能仍落后于最强大的专有模型（即 Claude Fable 5 和 GPT-5.6 Sol），但在我们评估的套件中，Kimi K3 持续优于其他开源和专有模型。我们发布了完整的 Kimi K3 模型权重，以促进未来研究并加速前沿智能的更广泛部署和采用。

We introduce Kimi K3, a 2.8T parameter Mixture-of-Experts model with 104 billion activated parameters, native vision capabilities, and a 1-million-token context window. Kimi K3 is built on Kimi Delta Attention and Attention Residuals, which improve information flow across sequence length and model depth. Together with Stable LatentMoE, which effectively activates 16 of 896 routed experts per token, and refined training and data recipes, these advances yield an approximately 2.5x improvement in overall scaling efficiency over Kimi K2. Post-training highlights reinforcement learning across general, agentic, and coding domains and multiple reasoning-effort levels, enabling compositional generalization and robust long-horizon execution. At 2.8T scale, Kimi K3 is supported by infrastructure advances in multiple areas: algorithm-system co-design for KDA, perfectly balanced expert-parallel training with efficient memory management, million-token agentic RL with persistent rollout and sandbox states, and deployment innovations. Extensive evaluations show that Kimi K3 achieves frontier-level performance across long-horizon coding, agentic, knowledge, reasoning, and vision tasks. While its overall performance still trails the most powerful proprietary models, namely Claude Fable 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol, Kimi K3 consistently outperforms other open and proprietary models evaluated in our suite. We release the full Kimi K3 model weights to facilitate future research and accelerate the broader deployment and adoption of frontier intelligence.

核心贡献 · Key contributions

  • 提出了Kimi K3：一个2.8T参数的混合专家（MoE）模型，激活参数104B，原生视觉能力和100万token上下文窗口。
    Introduce Kimi K3, a 2.8T-parameter Mixture-of-Experts model with 104B active parameters, native vision, and a 1M-token context window.
  • 提出Kimi Delta Attention与注意力残差机制，改善序列长度和模型深度的信息流动，并结合Stable LatentMoE。
    Propose Kimi Delta Attention and Attention Residuals, improving information flow across sequence length and model depth, combined with Stable LatentMoE.
  • 通过架构与训练数据配方，整体规模扩张效率相比Kimi K2提升约2.5倍。
    Achieve approximately 2.5x improvement in overall scaling efficiency over Kimi K2 through architecture and training data recipes.
  • 在通用、智能体式和编码领域应用后训练强化学习，并设置多种推理努力等级，实现组合泛化与稳健的长程执行。
    Apply post-training reinforcement learning across general, agentic, and coding domains with multiple reasoning-effort levels, enabling compositional generalization and robust long-horizon execution.
  • 提出覆盖算法-系统协同设计、均衡专家并行训练以及带持久rollout与沙盒状态的百万token智能体式强化学习的基础设施进展。
    Present infrastructure advances spanning algorithm-system co-design, balanced expert-parallel training, and million-token agentic RL with persistent rollout and sandbox states.
  • 在长程编码、智能体式、知识、推理和视觉任务上展现前沿级性能，并开放完整模型权重。
    Show frontier-level performance across long-horizon coding, agentic, knowledge, reasoning, and vision tasks, and release full model weights.

局限 · Limitations

  • 整体性能仍落后于最强的专有模型，即Claude Fable 5与GPT-5.6 Sol。
    Overall performance still trails the most powerful proprietary models, namely Claude Fable 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol.
  • 评估结果基于作者自有的测试套件，而非外部或第三方基准。
    Evaluation results are based on the authors' own test suite, not on external or third-party benchmarks.
  • 对比仅覆盖评估套件所选模型，并非所有现有开放与专有系统。
    Comparisons include only the models selected in the evaluation suite, not all existing open and proprietary systems.
  • 在2.8T规模下，Kimi K3在训练和部署上都需要大量的基础设施支持。
    At 2.8T scale, Kimi K3 requires substantial infrastructure support across training and deployment.
