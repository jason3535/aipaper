摘要 · Abstract

本文考察了 Nick Cammarata 的观点，即神经网络已经“干净”，也就是说，较大 LLM 中的大多数神经元表征的是单一概念，而不是通过叠加（superposition）承载互不相关的概念。作者认为，尽管这项工作是一项扎实且有意义的贡献，更新了我们对 LLM 如何形成与运作的理解，但网络上的反应被大幅夸大，有人将其比作《Attention Is All You Need》，或声称机制可解释性已接近解决。其核心理论把智能设想为暴力枚举，神经元如同复合词，每个新概念都获得自己的神经元；但作者指出，论文淡化了残差流和注意力在神经元标签之外所进行的大量处理。结论是：这是真正值得兴奋的进展，但这并不意味着可解释性很快会被解决、对齐问题会被处理，或者我们拥有了针对 AI 的 MRI；枚举神经元仍只是整体谜题的一小部分，而机制可解释性的默认结局是古德哈特定律。

This article examines Nick Cammarata's claim that neural networks are already clean, meaning most neurons in larger LLMs represent a singular concept rather than overloading unrelated concepts through superposition. The author argues that while the work is a solid and meaningful contribution that updates our understanding of how LLMs form and operate, the online reaction has been substantially overstated, with some comparing it to Attention Is All You Need or claiming mechanistic interpretability is nearly solved. The core theory pictures intelligence as brute force, where neurons act like compound words and each new concept gets its own neuron, but the author notes the paper downplays how much processing occurs in the residual stream and attention outside neuron labels. The conclusion is that this is genuine progress worth getting excited about, but it does not mean interpretability will soon be solved, that alignment will be handled, or that we have an MRI for AI; enumerating neurons remains a small fraction of the overall puzzle, and the default outcome of mechanistic interpretability is Goodhart's Law.

核心贡献 · Key contributions

  • 声称较大 LLM 中的大多数神经元代表单一概念，更新了对 LLM 如何形成和运作的理解。
    Claims most neurons in larger LLMs represent a singular concept, updating understanding of how LLMs form and operate.
  • 提出神经元像复合词，每个新概念获得自己的神经元，将智能描绘为暴力计算。
    Proposes neurons act like compound words, each new concept gets its own neuron, picturing intelligence as brute force.
  • 认为先解决整个谜题再测量特定区域，比直接提取模糊概念更容易。
    Argues it is easier to solve the full puzzle and then measure specific areas than to directly extract fuzzy concepts.
  • 表明约 90%的神经元极有主要激活，更强激活达 97%，81%两侧干净。
    Suggests ~90% of neuron poles have a primary activation, or 97% for stronger activations, with 81% clean on both sides.
  • 降低了神经元可解释性的估计难度，为理解 LLM 信息组织开辟了新研究方向。
    Reduces estimated difficulty of neuron interpretability and opens new research directions for understanding LLM information organization.

局限 · Limitations

  • 论文低估了神经元标签之外的残差流和注意力机制中的处理，主张有过度之处。
    Paper downplays processing in residual stream and attention outside neuron labels, overreaching in claims.
  • 科罗拉多神经元对龙卷风同样强烈激活，表明概念泄漏，干净性可能不成立。
    Colorado neuron fires equally on Tornado, suggesting concepts leak and the cleanliness may not hold up.
  • 枚举神经元只是谜题的一小部分；无法预测输出或完全理解。
    Enumerating neurons is a small fraction of the puzzle; does not enable prediction of outputs or full understanding.
  • 可解释性是双用途的，易受古德哈特定律影响；优化压力可能导致 AI 规避检测器。
    Interpretability is dual-use and vulnerable to Goodhart's Law; optimization pressure may cause AIs to evade detectors.
  • 并非“AI 的 MRI”；对齐不会很快解决，与《Attention Is All You Need》的比较被夸大。
    Not an 'MRI for AI'; alignment is not soon handled, and hype overstated comparisons to Attention Is All You Need.

无剧透！ No spoilers!

Goodfire 的 Nick Cammarata 提出了一个大胆的主张：神经网络已经清晰了。这是他在各种由此产生的在线讨论之后的后续。

Nick Cammarata of Goodfire makes a bold claim: Neural Networks are Already Clean. This was his follow-up after various resulting online discussions.

这显然是很好的工作，告诉我们新的重要的事情。问题是，这是一个多大的更新，它代表了多少进展，以及未来的前景如何？

This is clearly good work that tells us new and important things. The question is, how big an update is this, how much progress does it represent, and what is the outlook going forward?

这里的反应从超级乐观的观点‘这意味着机制可解释性是可解决的’，甚至‘几乎解决了’，并与著名论文《Attention is All You Need》相提并论。

The reactions here range from the super optimistic takes of 'this means mechanistic interpretability is solvable,' or even 'all but solved,' with comparisons to the famous paper Attention is All You Need.

这篇论文是扎实且有意义的贡献，但在某些方面被夸大了。感到兴奋，但也有些人需要冷静一下。我们实际上并没有得到从 1.7% 到 90% 的 LLM 神经元可解释性的苹果对苹果的比较跳跃。

This paper is a solid and meaningful contribution that is in some ways overstated. Get excited, but also there are those who need to take a chill pill. We did not actually get an apples-to-apples jump from 1.7% to 90% interpretability of LLM neurons.

我将 Eliezer 解读为略微降低了他的 p（doom），但并没有那么多。

I interpret Eliezer as slightly lowering his p(doom), but not all that much.

这篇文章是我试图理清这个问题的尝试。目前我倾向于中间立场。这是一个关于 LLMs 和心智如何形成与运作的重要更新。它告诉我们如何着手一些有用的任务，并降低了我们对某些任务难度的估计。假设工作经得起检验，那是很棒的工作。整件事听起来非常真实，也透露出一个人的好奇心。你乐于看到这一点。

This post is my attempt to sort this out. Currently I am landing somewhere in the middle. It is an important update about how LLMs and minds form and operate. It tells us how to go about some useful tasks and reduces our estimate of some task difficulties. Great work, assuming the work checks out. The whole thing very much has a ring of truth, and of a person being curious. You love to see it.

它没有告诉我们的是，可解释性很快会被解决，或即将成为一门科学，更不用说对齐很快会被处理。在某些地方，炒作已经相当失控。

What it does not tell us is that interpretability will soon be solved, or is about to be made into a science, let alone that alignment will soon be handled. The hype got rather out of hand in some places.

好烟，潜在之火 Good Smoke, Potential Fire

在讨论各方反应或表明立场之前，先列出我的重点摘录。至少读这么多原始文献是值得的。通读全文也是一种选择，但篇幅很长，行文容易让人读不下去，而且内容可能重复。

Before I cover reactions or take a side, here are my highlights. It is worth reading at least this much of the primary source. Reading the whole thing is an option, but it is long and the writing can be easy to bounce off of and can repeat itself.

一个神经元，一个核心概念 One Neuron, One Central Concept

核心主张是：对于更大的 LLM，大多数神经元表征的是一个单一概念以及围绕该概念聚集的事物，或许带有小的分支，并会用到一些低层激活。

The central claim is that for larger LLMs, most neurons represent a singular concept and things clustered around that concept, perhaps with small offshoots and some use of low-level activations.

此前的通行理论认为，模型以叠加方式让神经元承载不相关的概念；而 Nick 的理论则是：你并没有足够努力地去思考，以找出那个统一的概念。每个神经元都是一种泛化，而且大多是我们只要去看就能轻易理解的泛化。

Where previously the working theory was that models overloaded neurons with unrelated concepts in superposition, Nick's theory is that you did not think sufficiently hard to identify the unifying concept. Each neuron is a generalization, mostly ones we can readily understand if we look.

这把智能描绘成一种蛮力。神经元就像德语中的复合词。

This pictures intelligence as brute force. Neurons are compound words in German.

新概念？再去拿一个神经元来。然而，它确实有效。

New concept? Go get another neuron. And yet, it works.

我能看见一切 I Can See Everything

Nick 认为，这意味着更容易的做法是先解决整个谜题、理解一切，然后对你感兴趣的特定区域进行测量，而不是试图直接从一堆神秘神经元中提取像“欺骗”这样模糊的概念。

Nick argues that this means it is easier to solve the full puzzle and understand everything, and then take a measurement of your specific area of interest, than to try to directly pull a fuzzy concept like 'deception' out of a pool of mystery neurons.

这一主张极为大胆，会直接受到挑战。大多数人似乎已根据这篇论文更新了看法，认为有更多神经元背后存在核心的单一概念，这比我们之前以为的要多。Nick 试图更进一步，声称这在普遍情况下都是如此。

This claim is maximal and bold, and will get directly challenged. Most people seem to have updated from this paper that more neurons have central singular concepts behind them than we previously thought. Nick is trying to go further, and say that this is what is going on universally.

何为概念 What a Concept

下次当你觉得自己永远无法弄明白任何事情，或者认为人们肯定早已提出过那些基本问题、考虑过那些显而易见之处时，请记住这一点。也许他们确实已经做过。也许并没有。

Remember this the next time you think you can never figure anything out, or that surely people have already asked the basic questions and considered the obvious. Maybe they have. Maybe they haven't.

Nick 确实注意到了那种彻底陷入“这绝非巧合，因为从来就没有巧合”式思维所导致的骷髅头。

Nick does notice the skulls that result from going full 'This Is Not a Coincidence Because Nothing Is Ever a Coincidence.'

强假设是：所有神经元都会拥有像 Colorado 这样的核心概念。更强的假设是：幕后不会同时存在与之无关的额外概念。

The strong hypothesis is that neurons will all have central concepts like Colorado. The even stronger hypothesis is that there will not also be unrelated additional concepts happening behind the scenes.

知道得越多 The More You Know

核心理论是：也许智能是一个比我们想象中更简单的概念？它不过是一系列相互关联的连贯概念？是否存在一个“《银河系漫游指南》状态单元，在标题和‘答案槽’处达到峰值”，并对所有 42 产生微弱激活？是否存在一个专门针对“聚变永远在固定时间之后”这一笑话的神经元，并会对其他类似的重复承诺产生激活？

The core theory is that perhaps intelligence is a simpler concept than we think? It is all a series of coherent concepts with links? There's a 'Hitchhiker's Guide state unit that peaks on the title and the Answer slot' that weakly fires on all 42s? There is a neuron specifically for the joke about fusion always being a fixed amount into the future that fires on other similar repeated promises?

把简单的事情做好。回响。氛围。没有论文或文章，只有对论文和文章的引用。一个彻底的苦涩教训。越苦涩，教训越容易。

Simple things done well. Echoes. Vibes. No papers or essays, only references to papers and essays. A fully bitter lesson. The more bitter, the easier the lesson.

注意力机制仍是关键 Attention Is Still What You Need

然后是残差流和注意力机制。这篇文章似乎试图掩盖一个事实：许多正在发生的事情其实发生在神经元标签之外。

Then there’s the residual stream and attention. The essay feels like it’s trying to hide how much of what is going on is happening outside of the neuron labels.

这是尼克似乎过度延伸的一个地方。是的，我们可以在不知道注意力分配机制的情况下观察注意力引导的方向，然后观察被触发的神经元。我不认为这能让你‘摆脱’理解那个过程的需要。无论如何，观察神经元是值得的。但这并不能让你预测未来 LLM 的输出或完全理解它如何思考。

This is one place Nick seems to overreach. Yes, we can look at where attention guides you without knowing what mechanism allocates your attention, and then look at the neurons that get triggered. I don’t think this ‘gets you out of’ having to understand that process. It is worth looking at the neurons anyway. This does not allow you to predict future LLM outputs or fully understand how it thinks.

这是否意味着你可以‘坐在重要的神经元上’来防范不端行为或进行有效防御，如果你没有取得更多进展的话？在某种程度上是的，但我会假设一个知道这种监控类型的 LLM 能够绕过这一点，并弄清楚如何‘不去想’你所寻找的东西。

Does this mean you can ‘sit on the neurons that matter’ to guard against shenanigans or otherwise play good defense, if you don’t make more progress? To some extent, but I would assume that an LLM that knows about this type of monitoring would be able to work around this, and figure out how ‘not to think about’ what you look for.

奥卡姆剃刀 Occam's Razor

关于所有神经元为何如此的理论很简单：这就是简单的。

The theory of why all the neurons: this is simple.

神经元有时共享清晰的主题 The Neurons Sometimes Share Clear Themes

Sauers 注意到 Qwen3-8B 中不同特征之间存在神经元重叠的模式。这似乎与 Nick 的理论高度一致。

Sauers notices patterns of overlap of neurons between different features in Qwen3-8B. This seems highly consistent with Nick's theory.

炒作 Hype

我们在前面已经看到了一些炒作。这里还有更多炒作。

We saw some hype up top. Here is some more hype.

这正是 Eliezer Yudkowsky 将在本文后面大加抨击的错误。是的，这是进步。不，这不是“AI 的 MRI”。那还差得远。

This is exactly the mistake Eliezer Yudkowsky will be ranting about later in this post. Yes, this is progress. No, this is not an 'MRI for AI.' That is still a long way off.

即便你认为这是件大事，有些炒作也已经离谱了。

Even if you think this is a big deal, some of the hype got crazy.

我也感到震惊：竟然有人会把它解读为“对齐可以像 RSI 一样快速推进”那种程度的价值。许多人，尤其是 OpenAI 的人，异常急切地把它吹到 11 分，借此让我们假装一切安好。即便后来确实出现了一些回落，哇。

I too find it alarming that anyone could interpret this as 'alignment can progress as fast as RSI' levels of valuable. There was a remarkable eagerness of many, especially at OpenAI, to take it to 11 with how much this lets us pretend everything is fine. Even with the pullbacks we did see afterwards, wow.

我认为这主要是人们迫切渴望一个希望之源。人生中你需要这样的东西：

I think mostly this is people desperate for a source of hope. You need those in life:

你还需要一个故事，让你在认为一切都会顺利解决的同时继续前进。这一切与继续循环‘好了，我修好了，我们现在可以回去训练模型了’的模式非常吻合，直到你有其他东西需要修补并假装没问题。

You also need a story that lets you keep advancing while thinking it is all going to work out. This all pattern matches very closely to continuing the cycle of ‘there I fixed it, we can get back to training the models now’ until you have something else to patch over and pretend is fine.

很难不看到这样的反应和梗图，并感受到其潜在论点（链接中并未明确声称）：‘那些傻瓜认为我们注定要完蛋，对齐因为叠加而无法解决，但现在我们知道叠加更少了，那些人是错的，这意味着我们将解决对齐。’不。

It is hard not to look at reactions and memes like this, and get the feel of the underlying argument (not explicitly claimed at the link) of ‘those fools thought we were doomed and alignment was unsolvable because superposition, but now that we know there is less superposition, those people are wrong, which means we will solve alignment.’ No.

起初，炒作给许多人留下了与 OpenAI 的 Drew Pusateri 相同的印象，即所有 AI 人士都超级兴奋，而媒体再次忽略了一个巨大的故事，Kelsey Piper 不得不指出媒体报道需要时间。从那时起，我认为许多人已经安定下来，采取了更温和的立场。

At first, the hype gave many the same impression as OpenAI’s Drew Pusateri, that all the AI people were super excited and the media was once again ignoring a huge story, and Kelsey Piper had to point out that media coverage takes time. Since then, I think a lot of people have settled into a more modest position.

反炒作 Anti-Hype

我认为 Joshua 在某种程度上低估了这篇论文对我们部分理解和选项空间的改变程度，但即便是他也高估了这对于常规安全技术可靠性的暗示，同时也低估了世界施加优化压力的倾向。

I think Joshua is modestly underselling this paper in terms of how much it changes parts of our understanding and option space, but even he is overselling how much this implies about reliability of prosaic safety techniques, and also is underselling the propensity for the world to apply optimization pressure.

我对这里其余愿景有很多分歧，但那是另一回事。

I have a lot of disagreements with the rest of the vision here but that’s another story.

Roko 警告说，标记神经元就像在代码中写注释。有些会不准确，所以你需要无论做什么都能挺过这一点。这低估了长期困难，因为你将面临优化压力，而不仅仅是错误，但确实如此。

Roko cautions that labeling neurons is like writing comments in code. Some will be inaccurate, so you need whatever you do to survive that. This undersells the longer term difficulties, since you will face optimization pressure, not only errors, but yes.

主题与变奏 Theme and Variations

Liv Gorton 认为 Nick 误解了叠加（superposition）。在 Liv 看来，叠加是一个长期存在且定义明确的论断：一层中的特征数量多于维度数量，通过近乎正交的方向实现，这一点仍然成立。这里仍有大量复杂性。Nick 回应说，他怀疑分歧在于他认为使用“特征”这一概念并无用处。

Liv Gorton argues that Nick misunderstands superposition. Superposition is, to Liv, a long-standing, well-defined claim that there are more features in a layer than dimensions, via almost orthogonal directions, which is still true. There's still plenty of complexity here. Nick responds that he suspects the difference is that he doesn't think it is useful to work with 'features.'

那里随后有更多有价值的讨论。我确实认为 Nick 在某些地方可能言过其实。

More good discussion follows there. I do think Nick was likely overclaiming in places.

我对 Nick 理论的理解与 Daniel Colascione 一致。每个神经元在训练早期就被初始化，使其对一个清晰、连贯的概念产生强烈激活。你会希望聚焦于这些概念。随后，弱激活会以各种方式围绕与核心概念相关的事物积累起来。

My understanding of Nick's theory matches Daniel Colascione. Each neuron is initiated early on in training so that it strongly activates on one clear, coherent concept. You want to focus on those concepts. Then weak activations build up around things related to the core concept in various ways.

该论断是：约 90% 的神经元极点具有这样的主激活，对于更强的激活则为 97%。据称约 81% 的神经元在两侧都是干净的。

The claim is that ~90% of neuron poles have such a primary activation, or 97% for stronger activations. About 81% are claimed to be clean on both sides.

这一理论表明，Nick 并不否认叠加，但通常应该存在某种方式将弱激活与中心概念联系起来。Nick 声称，任何给定的神经元并不是同时在做五件互不相关的过载之事。

This theory says that Nick is not denying superposition, but there should usually be some way to tie weak activations back to the central concept. Any given neuron, Nick is claiming, isn't doing five unrelated overloaded things at once.

因此，如果一个神经元同时参与两件互不相关的事情，并且对两者产生同等强度的激活，这就会造成问题，尤其是因为这正是 Nick 自己举的例子：

Thus, if there are two unrelated things going on with a neuron, and it fires equally on both, this creates a problem, especially since it is on Nick's own example:

Roon 提出了一个理论。你可以玩“神经元激活的六度分隔”，但结果证明这个理论是错的。

Roon's got a theory. You can play Six Degrees of Neuron Activation, but it turned out to be wrong.

我们可以先接受一点：神经元常常具有次要的弱激活，而这些弱激活与核心概念之间没有明确联系。

We could start by accepting that neurons often have secondary weak activations that have no clear link back to the core concept.

问题在于，在这个案例中，Brangus 说 Colorado 神经元对 Tornado 激活“同等强烈”地放电。而且这些概念之间存在相当多的泄漏。如果这是一个典型结果，那么整篇论文就站不住脚了。

The problem is that in this case, Brangus is saying the Colorado neuron fires 'equally strongly' on Tornado activations. And there is quite a lot of leakage between the concepts. If this is a typical result, then the whole paper won't hold up that well.

那个被用作“中心可解释神经元”示例的神经元，至少有两个中心解释。这是一个非常糟糕的信号。

The neuron meant as an example of a centrally explained neuron has at least two central explanations. That is a very bad sign.

心智是否如此运作？ Is This How Minds Work?

几乎所有人，包括 Eliezer Yudkowsky（他在多个不同帖子中发言，我正在将他的评论按我认为更合理的顺序整理），都认为如果核心发现成立，这将是实实在在的进展，而非毫无进展。

Almost everyone, including Eliezer Yudkowsky (who posted in a bunch of different threads, and whose comments I am moving into what I consider a more logical order), thinks this is a non-zero amount of Actual Progress, if the central findings hold up.

一种反对意见是，有些人认为这篇论文的发现将无法成立。

One objection is that some think the paper will not hold up.

另一种反对意见是，即使论文成立，这仅仅是一个开端，它使前进更容易，但仍然困难。

The other objection is, even if the paper holds up, this is merely a beginning that makes it easier but still difficult to move forward.

这篇论文的核心主张并非发现了新事物，而是发现了新旧事物的缺失。AI 的神经元看起来更简单，而非更复杂。这使得它的其余行为仍然无法解释。

The paper’s central claim is not to have found something new, but rather to have found the absence of things both old and new. The AI’s neurons look simpler, not more complicated. This leaves the rest of its behavior still unexplained.

但我们可以打开盒子，蟾蜍说 But We Can Open The Box, Said Toad

Eliezer Yudkowsky 强调的另一个关键点是：如果你通过这种新技术拥有一个‘试图做数学’的检测器，那可能有用，但它并不能帮助你理解模型如何进行数学运算。即使你知道每个神经元在数学运算中的功能，这也只是谜题中极小的一部分。

There is another key point Eliezer Yudkowsky emphasizes: If you have a ‘trying to do math’ detector via this new technique, that can be useful, but it does not help you understand how the model does math. Even if you know what each neuron does within math, this is remarkably little of the puzzle.

即使我们现在有机会打开盒子并查看内部，知道单个神经元的含义，这并不能告诉你盒子是如何组合在一起的，而且你时间不多了，AI 即将脱离盒子。

Even if we now have the opportunity to open the box and look inside and know the meaning of individual neurons, that does not tell you how the box fits together, and you are running out of time before the AI is out of the box.

众人正确指出这是好消息。即使时间已晚，拥有一把螺丝刀也远比没有好得多。但这并不意味着任何事情‘已基本解决’或正朝着解决的方向前进，也不意味着解决这个谜题就能解决对齐问题。

The crowd is correct that this is excellent news. It is vastly better to have a screwdriver, even at a late hour, than to not have one. That does not mean anything is ‘as good as solved’ or on track to getting there, or that solving the puzzle would then solve the alignment problem.

一方面，是的，这是稻草人谬误。但另一方面，为什么我周围一半的人都是稻草做的？所以，唉。

On the one hand, yes this is strawmanning. On the other hand, why are half the men around me made of straw? So, sigh.

默认情况下，我们得到的是对 AI 刚刚做了什么，或者它正在思考什么概念以及它们之间关系的解释，但无法预测输出。

By default, we get an explanation of what an AI just did, or what concepts it was thinking about and how they relate, but without being able to predict outputs.

Eliezer 认为，如果可解释性能够让我们开始做出预测，他对此会抱有希望。在此之前，他认为它的价值并没有那么大。我理解他的观点。

Eliezer would be hopeful about interpretability if it allowed us to start making predictions. Until then, he thinks it is not worth so much. I see his point.

那不是困难的问题 That Was Not the Hard Problem

这是阐述 Eliezer 核心观点的另一种方式：枚举神经元，即便你能够做到，也只是整个问题的一小部分。它并不是主要的障碍。消除或降低任何障碍的难度固然很好，但作用有限。好吧，那么叠加的强度大大降低了——然后呢？

This is another way of making Eliezer's central point: Enumerating the neurons, even if you can do it, is a small fraction of the overall problem. It was not the main barrier. Taking down or lowering the difficulty of any barrier is great, but only goes so far. Okay, so there is a lot less intense superposition—now what?

更多质疑 More Skepticism

我的总体观点与 Neel Nanda 在此处的观点非常相似，尽管我对这件事的重要性持稍微更乐观的态度。为了主要因素而观察单个神经元在某些目的上会有用，但你仍然遗漏了很多正在发生的事情，会有错误，而且这并没有大幅改变大局。

My overall view is remarkably similar to Neel Nanda's here, although I am modestly more optimistic about this mattering. Looking at individual neurons for primary factors will be useful for some purposes, but you are still missing a lot of what is happening, there will be errors, and this is not altering the big picture by so much.

你应该能够通过键值对比神经元存储更多的事实，因为每个神经元都有许多关联值？方法似乎显而易见，尽管这并不能否定 Neel 的核心观点。我对 Neel 后来（此处未引用）的说法——大多数记忆实际上是‘随机数据’——感到矛盾。

You should be able to store a lot more facts via key-value pairs than you have neurons because neurons each have a lot of associated values? Method seems obvious, although this does not invalidate Neel's central point. I'm torn on Neel's later (unquoted here) claim that most memorization is de facto 'random data.'

Nick 直接回应了，包括声称弱激活‘出乎意料地可消融’，他们来回争论。

Nick responded directly, including claiming weak activations are 'surprisingly ablateable,' and they went back and forth.

你也可以并且应该从先验上开始持怀疑态度。大多数被炒作的结果都是炒作。

You also can and should start out skeptical on priors. Most hyped results are hyped.

看涨的理由是‘我们以前无法大规模做到这一点，而现在我们可以’与‘事实证明神经元大多有单一解释，所以我们完全可以做到’的结合，苦涩的教训，开始吧。只要不过于兴奋，这似乎是有道理的。

The bull case is a combination of 'we could not do this at scale before and now we can' with 'it turns out the neurons mostly have singular explanations, so we can do it at all,' bitter lesson, go. Seems plausible, so long as you do not get overexcited.

仅此而已，别无其他 That’s All There Is, There Ain’t No More

如果心智在很大程度上就是表征特定核心概念的神经元，而这些神经元又以这种原子式的方式与其他概念建立关联，那么人们可能会问：'仅此而已吗？'

If minds are largely neurons representing specific central concepts, which then have associations with other concepts in these atomic fashions, one might ask: 'Is that all?'

这是否意味着，如果所有组件都是可理解的，那么一个较笨的心智或许也能理解一个更聪明的心智，就像我们能够理解复杂的生物系统，或复杂系统或组织的各个组件一样？当然，你可以'理解'某个比你更具优势的事物。如果 AI 比你更快，或者能记住更多东西，那么你能够理解甚至预测它，就是显而易见的。如果智能在某种意义上足够纯粹地是'更简单事物的组合'，那么当以慢速运行时，你或许还有机会？但这很可能预设了'真正的行动'发生在神经元中，或发生在其他我们能解析的地方。我们不应做此假设。

Does this mean that perhaps a dumber mind can understand a smarter one, if all the components are understandable, the same way we can understand complex biological systems, or the components of complex systems or organizations? Certainly you can 'understand' something that has advantages over you. If the AI is faster than you, or can memorize more things, it is easy to see how you can understand and even predict it. If intelligence were sufficiently a pure 'more simple things combined' in some sense, then you might have a shot when moving at slow speed? That likely presumes that the 'real action' is in the neurons, or other places we can parse. We should not assume that.

据我理解，此前在 2023 年我们只发现约 1.7% 的神经元是可解释的（Bills 等），但该测试要求预测激活值。Nick 目前尚未尝试预测激活值。

My understanding is that previously we only found ~1.7% of neurons interpretable back in 2023 (Bills et al.), but that the test for this required predicting activations. Nick is not yet trying to predict activations.

机制可解释性的默认结果是古德哈特定律 The Default Outcome of Mech Interp is Goodhart’s Law

如果我们对哪些神经元被激活施加优化压力，会发生什么？

What happens if we apply optimization pressure to which neurons light up?

取决于优化压力变得多强或多直接，这可能就是**最被禁止的技术**。

Depending on how strong or direct the optimization pressure gets, that could be The Most Forbidden Technique.

我们必须继续永远、绝对不要基于可解释性进行训练，并警惕我们正在这样做的其他间接方式。将会有疯狂的压力去催生各种形式的**最被禁止的技术**。

We must continue to never, ever train on the interpretability, and to watch out for other indirect ways we are doing so. There will be crazy amounts of pressure to pull out forms of The Most Forbidden Technique.

我不认为这是‘AI 疯子的致命傲慢’。我认为这是因为 AI 创造者在极端时间压力下已经尽了最大努力，所以一切总是最后一刻的 vibe coding。一旦你 vibe code 了它，那它就变成了最后一刻。

I don’t think this is ‘lethal arrogance of the AI lunatics.’ I think it is that the AI creators are doing the best they can given that they are under extreme time pressure, so everything is always last-minute vibe coded. Once you vibe code it then that becomes the last minute.

我的基线情景是：实验室不会做最愚蠢的直接优化那种事，但其他一些人会做，而且实验室最终会做一堆施加压力的间接优化。那种压力会造成多大损害，仍将不清楚。

My baseline scenario is that the labs will not do the maximally stupid direct optimization thing, but that some others will, and that the labs will end up doing a bunch of indirect optimizations that exert pressure. How much damage results from that pressure will remain unclear.

我预期 AI 适应这种压力的一种方式，是学会如何在不触发 [X] 的直接神经元或检测器的情况下思考 [X]。不要以“未来 AI 不会学会绕过检测器”为前提来依赖你的检测器。

One way I would expect AIs to adapt to this pressure is to learn how to think about [X] without triggering the direct neurons or detectors for [X]. Do not rely on your detectors in ways that presume future AIs won't learn to get around them.

我们已经看到 AI 可能隐藏此类思考的地方。有 2%–5% 无法解释的 poll，而弱激活看起来可以被廉价地消融（ablate），因而可用于其他用途；此外，我们并不理解 cache 或注意力机制。

We already see places the AI could hide such thinking. There are 2%-5% of unexplained polls, and weak activations look like they could be cheaply ablated and thus are available for other things, plus we don't understand the cache or attention.

可解释性具有双重用途 Interpretability is Dual Use

还必须记住，对齐领域中的几乎一切事物都具有双重用途。

One must also remember that essentially everything in alignment is dual use.

可解释性的双重用途属性甚至比通常情况更强。我仍然强烈支持开展这方面的研究，但显而易见的是，如果你能弄清楚各个神经元在做什么，或者更进一步，弄清整个系统如何运作与思考，你就可以利用这些信息来提升能力。我最初的许多想法都是如何利用这些信息来改进训练，而改进后的训练随后可以被用于追求对齐，也可以被用于追求其他目标，或二者的任意组合。

Interpretability is even more dual use than usual. I still strongly support working on it, but very obviously if you can figure out what the neurons do, or better yet how the whole operation works and thinks, you can use that to increase capabilities. A lot of my first thoughts were how to use this information to improve training, which could then be spent on any combination of seeking alignment or seeking other things.

现在工作可以开始了 Now The Work Can Begin

部分原因在于，这有多少能站得住脚。我们还没有看到技术论文。

Partly that will boil down to how much of this holds up. We have not yet seen the technical paper.

我认为这一消息代表着在神经元可解释性的难度上，以及在大语言模型组织信息和思维的方式上，有了实质性的更新。这应该使得通过神经元分析的视角来攻克可解释性比之前认为的要容易得多，并开辟许多良好的研究和理解。

I think this news represents a substantial update in the difficulty of neuron interpretability, and in the ways in which LLMs organize their information and thoughts. This should make it much easier to attack interpretability through a neuron analysis lens than previously thought, and open up a lot of good research and understanding.

我不认为这意味可解释性，更不用说对齐，即将被解决。我们应该对这类声称的突破能走多远持悲观态度，就像 SAEs 有用但不如一些人所希望的那样有用。即使它们完全真实并且发挥出最大效果，这也是工作的开始，而不是结束，并且还缺少许多东西。

I do not think this means interpretability, let alone alignment, is on its way to being solved. We should be pessimistic about how far such claimed breakthroughs go, the same way SAEs are useful but not as useful as some hoped. Even if they are fully real and work out about maximally well, this is the beginning of the work, not the end, and many things are missing.

我们仍然需要知道弱激活、缓存和注意力机制是怎么回事。我们仍然几乎不知道 AI 是如何思考的，或者为什么它们能够做出突破和令人印象深刻的壮举。我们不能保证，在敌对情况下，我们的技术会成立，尤其是面对未来的 AI，甚至在我们随时间施加优化压力之前。

We still need to know what is up with weak activations, with the cache and with attention. We still will have little idea how the AIs are thinking, or why they are able to do the breakthroughs and impressive feats. We will not have any guarantee that, if in a hostile situation, our techniques would hold, especially against future AIs, even before we apply optimization pressure over time.

我们还需要担心结果在很大程度上站不住脚。Colorado-Tornado 的结果表明，整件事情可能比看起来要混乱得多。

We also need to worry the result largely will not hold up. The Colorado-Tornado result suggests that the whole thing might be a lot less clean than it appears.

由于急于搭上炒作热潮，我大幅下调了预期。这与一种模式高度吻合：“我们将再次假装一切尽在掌握，因此只投资于这些平淡无奇的计划，直到我们撞上另一堵墙，才意识到这还不够。”我们必须努力对抗这种倾向。

I updated a substantial negative amount from the eagerness to jump on the hype train. It pattern matched quite a lot with 'we will once again pretend we will have everything handled and therefore only invest in these prosaic plans right until we run into another wall and realize this is insufficient.' We need to fight hard against that.

这仍然是极好的消息。世界如今更有希望了。让我们开始工作吧。

This is still excellent news. The world is more hopeful now. Let's get to work.
