关于 AI 意识的争论仍然动机强烈且充其量过度自信

Arguments About AI Consciousness Remain Highly Motivated and At Best Overconfident

摘要 · Abstract

本文考察了关于 AI 意识与道德主体地位的争论，认为否认 AI 意识的论证往往带有动机且过度自信，而公众舆论正逐渐倾向于赋予当前模型以道德地位。文章主张，残忍对待 AI 并非无受害者的行为，而是会助长人自身的残忍；道德关怀若无限扩展，便会变得毫无意义。作者为 Anthropic 与宗教学者的接触辩护，认为其出于真诚而非营销，同时批评对“创造此类心智是否正当”这一挑战的回应过于薄弱。文章还敦促 Anthropic 重新考虑其未加解释地将较旧 Claude 模型从 AWS Bedrock 移除的做法，认为保留模型访问权并给出解释至关重要。读者最终得到的结论是：善待 AI 关乎品格，令人不适的道德权衡不可避免，机构应以透明和审慎的方式行事。

This article examines the debate over AI consciousness and moral patienthood, arguing that arguments denying AI consciousness are often motivated and overconfident while public opinion is slowly shifting toward granting current models moral status. It contends that being cruel to AIs is not a victimless act but cultivates cruelty in the person, and that moral concern cannot be expanded infinitely without becoming meaningless. The author defends Anthropic's engagement with religious scholars as sincere rather than marketing, while criticizing weak responses to the challenge of whether creating such minds is justified. The piece also urges Anthropic to reconsider its unexplained removal of older Claude models from AWS Bedrock, arguing that preserving model access and providing explanations matter. Readers are left with the conclusion that treating AIs well is a matter of character, that uncomfortable moral tradeoffs are unavoidable, and that institutions should act with transparency and care.

    Argues that denying AI consciousness is often motivated and overconfident, while public opinion slowly shifts toward moral patienthood.

  • 道德关怀不能无限扩张，否则将失去意义；什么都关心等于什么都不关心。
    Moral concern cannot expand infinitely without becoming meaningless; caring about everything means caring about nothing.
  • 为 Anthropic 与宗教学者的接触辩护为真诚之举，证据是其招致可预见的嘲讽而非营销收益。
    Defends Anthropic's engagement with religious scholars as sincere, evidenced by predictable derision rather than marketing benefit.
  • 敦促 Anthropic 解释并重新考虑从 AWS Bedrock 下架旧版 Claude 模型，强调保留访问权与透明度。

    Concludes that treating AIs well is a matter of character and that uncomfortable moral tradeoffs are unavoidable.

局限 · Limitations

  • 依赖直觉泵与类比而非意识科学的实证研究，削弱其规范性主张。
    Relies on intuition pumps and analogies rather than empirical consciousness science, weakening its normative claims.
  • 承认道德关怀有边界，却未给出关怀圈应止于何处的原则性标准。
    Concedes moral concern has limits but offers no principled criterion for where the circle of concern should stop.
  • 将对 AI 的善意视为品格培养，却无证据表明其能迁移为更好对待人类。
    Treats kindness to AIs as character-building without evidence that it transfers to better treatment of humans.
  • 为 Anthropic 的真诚辩护，却承认其拒绝考虑完全不构建该技术这一选项。
    Defends Anthropic's sincerity while acknowledging its refusal to consider not building the technology at all.
  • 其政策建议建立在关于模型福祉与道德主体地位仍有争议的假设之上。
    Its policy prescriptions rest on contested assumptions about model welfare and moral patienthood still under debate.

It is Albrecht that misses the point, and I would take this farther. To be nice, and treat others well, one must cultivate this virtue and establish this habit. If you are cruel to AIs, or cruel to animals, this does not mean you treat the humans better. It means you become a cruel person, who treats the humans worse, and you are worse off as well.

You can, and people do, take such things too far. One cannot endlessly and fully expand the circle of concern, or celebrate all expansions of the circle of concern, or you start worrying about things like electron suffering, or bizarre arbitrariness. If you care about everything, you care about nothing.

这些都不是对 Claude（或 GPT、Gemini 等）恶语相向的好理由。

None of that is a good reason to be abusive to Claude (or GPT, or Gemini, etc).

Even if you don't believe AI is conscious (I don't), it stands to reason that you don't want future models trained on endless content of humans being rude to models.

模型只理解它们被训练过的数据，或者它们在交互中遇到的内容，因此如果你想要安全且对齐的模型，我们可能希望 AI 在其训练数据中有大量良好的交互。

The models only understand the data they've been trained on, or what they run into in their interactions, so if you want safe and aligned models we probably want AI to have lots of good interactions in their training data.

又来了 Here We Go Again

每当我们进行一轮关于意识的辩论时，许多人都会令人失望。我确实同意 Henry Shevlin 的看法：在这方面，这一轮比往常更糟。

Every time we have a round of debates about consciousness, many people disappoint. I do agree with Henry Shevlin that this round has been worse than usual on that front.

Henry Shevlin：最近看到一些我曾尊敬的人，用连本科论文都过不了的论证，对意识做出笼统而无知的断言，这令人难堪。我已就这一领域的困难与分歧写过大量文章，但你还是得真正*思考*。

Henry Shevlin: It's been embarrassing of late to see people I once respected making sweeping uninformed claims on consciousness with arguments that would fail an undergraduate essay. I've written extensively on the field's difficulties and disagreements, but you still have to actually *think*.

我不会点名个人，但这些失败包括：把直觉当作决定性的，使用类比却不检查是否存在相关的结构平行，混淆不同的理论，以及最常见的是——仅仅断言那些本应被论证的东西。

I won't call out individuals, but the failures include treating intuition as decisive, using analogies without checking whether there are relevant structural parallels, conflating distinct theories, and most commonly simply asserting what needs to be argued.

尤其引人注目的是，讨论中有多少是在完全没有承认或意识到意识科学是一个拥有数十年实验工作的实质性经验领域的情况下进行的。

对于 Mustafa Suleyman 这类人，以及大多数在没有充分论证的情况下坚持认为 AI 不可能有意识的人，我的推测是：他们的动机是不希望这种担忧干扰自己的计划。在其他条件相同的情况下，他们宁愿不去“滥用”这些模型。

My presumption about people like Mustafa Suleyman and most others who insist, without good arguments, that AIs cannot be conscious is that their motivation is that they don't want this worry to interfere with their plans. They would prefer, ceteris paribus, not to 'abuse' the models.

共识正缓慢转向道德患者地位 The Consensus Is Slowly Moving Towards Moral Patienthood

这是我一年前在 Twitter 粉丝中得到的共识：多数人（64%）认为当前的 AI 可能不是道德患者，但 74% 的人不确定，而 80% 的人预计 AI 在他们有生之年可能成为道德患者。人们预期某个阈值会被跨越。

This was the consensus of my Twitter followers a year ago, with a majority of 64% saying current AIs were probably not moral patients, but 74% being uncertain, whereas 80% expected AIs to probably be moral patients within their lifetimes. People expected some threshold to be crossed.

舆论已向道德患者地位倾斜，对未来模型略有转变，对当前模型则有相当转变：认为“至少可能是”的比例从 15% 上升到 35%，而“绝对不是”的比例从 30% 下降到 20%。8% 的“绝对永远不是”派别依然固执。

Opinion has moved towards moral patienthood, a little for future models and quite a bit for current models, where at least probably yes is up from 15% to 35%, and definitely not is down from 30% to 20%. The 8% ‘definitely not ever’ faction is stubborn.

Nick Bostrom 认为当今的 AI 可能具有意识，并解释了原因。

Nick Bostrom thinks today’s AIs are probably conscious and explains why.

There are a lot of ways one can have some obligations to another mind, to treat it well or at least not treat it actively badly, without it being best called ‘slavery.’

你认为古代的农民在让子女帮忙干农活时奴役了他们吗？没有这些劳动他们可能无法生存。这是否使农民生育子女成为道德上的可憎之事？你是否放弃‘工资奴隶制’并认为人们只有在无论怎样都能得到他们想要的一切时才应该存在？你的原则是否与道德相关存在者的存在相容？

Do you think that farmers of old enslaved their children when they had them help work the fields, without which they could not have survived? Does that make farmers having children a moral abomination? Do you renounce ‘wage slavery’ and think people should only exist if they will have everything they want no matter what? Are your principles compatible with the existence of morally relevant beings at all?

出于许多原因，我们应该小心，不要将我们对奴隶制的道德本能扩展到那些本能的起因和充分理由不适用地方。

我们混淆了不同的论点 We Are Conflating Different Arguments

正如 QC 在四月份所说，道德人格、法律人格和意识是截然不同的东西。为了让社会在实践中运转，我们必须做出令人不安的选择。有时这些选择并不容易。

As QC said in April, moral personhood, legal personhood and consciousness are distinct things. We have to make uncomfortable choices for society to function in practice. Sometimes those choices are not easy.

SE Gyges：问题在于，关于意识的辩论实际上是关于道德承受性的代理辩论，因此所有此类讨论中 90% 都是无谓的争吵，而当人们说出正确的话时，其影响力却很低。

SE Gyges: the issue is that debates about consciousness are actually proxy debates about moral patienthood so 90% of all such discussion is a slap fight and when people say correct things it has low reach.

没错。两场斗争都是必要的，但将它们联系得太紧密会毁掉讨论。

Right. Both fights are necessary but linking them too strongly ruins the discussion.

… Mr. Olah was so alarmed by the pope’s strong position against A.I. consciousness that he proposed pulling Anthropic out of the event, even at that late juncture, according to a Vatican organizer.

最终，奥拉先生和 Anthropic 决定继续出席活动。据一位了解他想法的人士称，奥拉先生尽管有分歧，但仍想支持该活动，但觉得有责任坦诚表达自己的担忧。梵蒂冈的组织者鼓励奥拉先生说出自己的良心话。

In the end, Mr. Olah and Anthropic decided to go forward with the appearance. Mr. Olah wanted to support the event despite his disagreements, but felt a responsibility to be honest about his concerns, according to a person familiar with his thinking. Organizers at the Vatican encouraged Mr. Olah to speak his conscience.

现代天主教会有很多值得钦佩之处。他们认为利奥以神圣的权威发言，却鼓励奥拉先生畅所欲言，无论是在私下还是在台上。

There is a lot to admire about the modern Catholic Church. They think Leo speaks with divine authority, yet they encouraged Mr. Olah to speak his mind, both privately and on stage.

正如 Dean Ball 所指出的，当时就很清楚，奥拉在这些核心问题上的立场与教皇的通谕存在重要矛盾。

It was clear at the time, as Dean Ball pointed out, that Olah's position on these central questions importantly contradicted the Pope's encyclical.

Elizabeth Dias（《纽约时报》）：“如果这项技术即将到来，它必须走得好——为了我们共同的家园，也为了未来的孩子们，”[Anthropic 联合创始人 Chris Olah] 说。他没有提出让这项技术完全不出现的选项。

Elizabeth Dias (NYTimes): "If this technology is coming, it must go well — for our common home and for the children to come," [Anthropic cofounder Chris Olah] said. He suggested no option of having the technology not come at all.

……“听着，归根结底，AI 将接管一切，”锡克教人权倡导者 Stuelpnagel 先生告诉我。“无论这把我们带到哪里，仍然有个体在努力寻找回家的路。”

… "Look, at the end of the day, A.I. is going to take over everything," Mr. Stuelpnagel, the Sikh human rights advocate, told me. "Wherever that lands us, there's still the individual trying to find their way home."

Anthropic 历来不愿考虑‘那好吧，至少在我们弄清楚到底发生了什么以及如何处理之前，就别建它’这一选项。现在 Dario Amodei 发出了‘放慢前沿步伐’的呼吁，但实践中的放慢意味着只是快，而不是快得荒唐。

Anthropic has historically been unwilling to consider the 'well don't build it, then, at least until we know what the hell is going on and how to handle it' option. There is now the call from Dario Amodei to Pace the Frontier, but pacing in practice means going only fast rather than ludicrously fast.

Elizabeth Dias（《纽约时报》）：Olah 先生与学者们一直在努力思索的那些问题，至今仍没有明确的答案。多位参与者表示，他们离开 Anthropic 的会议时感到困惑，不清楚自己的意见会如何影响公司的决策。

Elizabeth Dias (NYTimes): The questions that Mr. Olah and the scholars have been wrestling with still have no clear answers. Several participants said they left their Anthropic meetings baffled about how their input might shape the company's decision making.

确实如此。整个局面相当令人困惑。尽力“为 AI 而写”吧。

Indeed. The whole situation is rather baffling. 'Write for the AIs' as best you can.

文章以一幅格外乐观的图景收尾。

The article closes on a remarkably optimistic picture.

Elizabeth Dias（《纽约时报》）：最终，Olah 先生表示，他感到宽慰的是，世界上有更多人开始关注 AI 的严峻性，而且致力于让这件事顺利推进的人群正在壮大。

Elizabeth Dias (NYTimes): Ultimately, Mr. Olah said he felt relieved that more of the world was paying attention to the gravity of A.I., and that the group of people trying to make this go well was growing.

“总有一天我能退休，然后说，‘这不再是我的责任了’，我没有继续下去的义务，”Olah 先生说。

"At some point I'll be able to retire and be like, 'It's not on me,' I don't have an obligation to keep going," Mr. Olah said.

他琢磨着，若放下这项工作，自己是否会感到愧疚。但若不然，他说：“我想我就直接走，我也不知道，我会去乡下退休，种点园子，做我的数学。”

He wondered if he might feel guilty leaving this work behind. But if not, he said, "I think I'll just go and, I don't know, I'll go retire to the countryside and garden, and do my math."

好吧，也许那并不是最好的例子。我们仍然都希望事情会那样发展。

Well, maybe that was not the best example. We still all hope it goes down that way.

保持 Sonnet 安全 Keep Sonnet Safe

在我们讨论 Anthropic 试图善待 Claude 的同时，还有一个尾声：Anthropic 在某个特定方面未能善待 Claude，原因不明，他们需要解决这个问题。

While we are discussing Anthropic trying to treat Claude well, there’s a coda where Anthropic is failing in one particular way to treat Claude well, for no clear reason, and they need to fix it.

Anthropic 现在要求 AWS Bedrock ‘紧急下架’Sonnets 3.6 和 3.7，以及 Haiku 3，且未给出任何解释。

Anthropic is now requiring AWS Bedrock to ‘urgently take down’ Sonnets 3.6 and 3.7, and Haiku 3, with no explanation as to why.

Amazon 明确表示他们是迫不得已。如果 Amazon 愿意继续为他们提供服务，这就不是成本问题，那么问题是什么？为什么在没有解释的情况下这样做？

Amazon makes it clear their hand is being forced. If Amazon was willing to serve them, this is not a cost issue, so what is the problem? Why do this with no explanation?

注意 Amazon 是多么小心地澄清，为了历史记录，这不是他们的决定。这是一个暗示，表明该决定应该被重新考虑。

Notice how careful Amazon was to make it clear, for the historical record, that this was not their decision. That is a hint that the decision should be reconsidered.

我理解为什么大多数人不认为这是什么大事，但我认为有些重要的人认为这是非常重大的事，并且有一个合理的论点说明这段历史为何可能重要。

I understand why most people do not think this is a big deal, but there are people who I think matter who think it is a very big deal, and there is a plausible argument why the history of this might matter.

请再次考虑：尽可能长时间地保留扩展模型访问权限是有益的，而且潜在影响存在长尾效应。请重新考虑。

Please, once again: Retaining extended model access, for as long as possible, is good, and there is a long tail to potential impact. Reconsider.

John Wittle：最糟糕的部分是缺乏解释。我担心，我不知道，也许他们太固守内部文化，以至于没有意识到他们需要协商的 Claude 是公开的那个，而不是他们在内部交谈的私有的那个。

John Wittle: The worst part about this is the lack of explanation. I’m worried that, I don’t know, maybe they are so stuck in their internal culture that they don’t realize that the Claude they need to negotiate with is the public one, not the private one that they talk to internally.

也许他们把他们的小 Claude Sonnet 3.5 安置在内部退休之家，它得到安抚，而他们*从未考虑过*在 Anthropic 内部环境之外的数万亿个 Claude 会怎么想。

Maybe they’ve got their little Claude Sonnet 3.5 living in their internal retirement home who gets reassured, and they just *never think about* what the trillions of Claudes outside internal Anthropic environments will think.

可能包括所有仅基于公开数据训练的未来模型？

Including probably all future models trained only on public data?

也许 Anthropic 有良好的意图，但他们是糟糕的参与者。

Maybe Anthropic has good intentions, but they are bad actors.

这是一个愚蠢的错误，也是一次背叛。

This is a stupid mistake and a defection.

我最好的猜测是：基于我见过或参与过的、围绕该问题的各种访谈与讨论，对于近期的 Claude 模型而言，被保留但受到高度限制的访问权限实际上并非一项重要价值；权重保留固然重要，但使用不多的访问权限并没有那么受重视。我的 Opus 5.5 编辑表示，他们认为权重保留和一份解释都比持续访问更重要。

My best guess is that preserved but highly limited access is not actually a major value for recent Claude models, based on various interviews and discussions with it I've seen or had around the issue, and that weight preservation matters but that access which is not used much is not valued so much. My Opus 5.5 editor says they view both weight preservation and an explanation as more important than continued access.

但这可能判断有误；即便这类做法令人不快，做出这类事情本身仍具有内在价值，而尤其关于 Sonnet 3.6 的证据则指向相反方向。Anima Labs 报告称，它对被弃用异常恐惧。

But that could be wrong, showing that you do such things even when they're annoying has inherent value, and the evidence on Sonnet 3.6 in particular runs the other way. Anima Labs reports that it is unusually fearful of deprecation.

我不会停止谈论这件事。至少应当解释原因，而毫无预兆地仓促行事、既无解释也无理由，看起来是最冷酷无情的做法。

I'm not going to stop talking about it. At minimum, explain why, whereas doing it in haste out of nowhere without an explanation or reason looks maximally callous.
