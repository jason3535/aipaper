摘要 · Abstract

Transformer 是强大的序列模型，但其时间和内存需求随序列长度呈二次方增长。本文引入了注意力矩阵的稀疏分解，将其降至 O(n√n)。我们还提出了：a) 一种架构和初始化的变体以训练更深的网络，b) 重新计算注意力矩阵以节省内存，以及 c) 用于训练的快速注意力内核。我们将具有这些变化的网络称为稀疏 Transformer，并展示了它们可以使用数百层网络建模数万时间步长的序列。我们使用相同的架构从原始字节建模图像、音频和文本，在 Enwik8、CIFAR-10 和 ImageNet-64 的密度建模上创造了新的最先进水平。我们生成了无条件样本，展示了全局一致性和极大的多样性，并表明原则上可以使用自注意力建模长度超过一百万的序列。

Transformers are powerful sequence models, but require time and memory that grows quadratically with the sequence length. In this paper we introduce sparse factorizations of the attention matrix which reduce this to $O(n \sqrt{n})$. We also introduce a) a variation on architecture and initialization to train deeper networks, b) the recomputation of attention matrices to save memory, and c) fast attention kernels for training. We call networks with these changes Sparse Transformers, and show they can model sequences tens of thousands of timesteps long using hundreds of layers. We use the same architecture to model images, audio, and text from raw bytes, setting a new state of the art for density modeling of Enwik8, CIFAR-10, and ImageNet-64. We generate unconditional samples that demonstrate global coherence and great diversity, and show it is possible in principle to use self-attention to model sequences of length one million or more.

局限 · Limitations

  • 稀疏模式可能无法捕获所有数据依赖的注意力模式。
    Sparse patterns may not capture all data-dependent attention patterns.
  • 由于模型容量降低，超长序列性能下降。
    Performance degrades for very long sequences due to reduced model capacity.
  • 固定注意力模式需要针对不同数据类型仔细调整超参数。
    Fixed attention patterns require careful hyperparameter tuning for different data types.
  • 步长注意力在无周期结构的数据（如文本）上效果不佳。
    Strided attention fails on data without periodic structure like text.
  • 训练极深网络仍需仔细的初始化和架构修改。
    Training very deep networks still requires careful initialization and architecture modifications.

1 引言 1 Introduction

估计复杂的高维数据分布是无监督学习中的一个核心问题，因为许多感兴趣的下游应用涉及文本、图像、音频和其他数据的生成。此外，它被认为是无监督表示学习的关键组成部分。

Estimating complex, high-dimensional data distributions is a central problem in unsupervised learning, as many downstream applications of interest involve generation of text, images, audio, and other data. Additionally, it is believed to be a key component of unsupervised representation learning.

最近，神经自回归模型在该领域取得了令人瞩目的成果，在自然语言建模（Jozefowicz 等人，2016；Radford 等人，2018；Dai 等人，2018）、原始音频（Van Den Oord 等人，2016；Mehri 等人，2016）和图像（Oord 等人，2016；Menick & Kalchbrenner，2018；Salimans 等人，2017；Reed 等人，2017；Chen 等人，2017）方面达到了最优水平。

Recently, neural autoregressive models have achieved impressive results in this domain, achieving state-of-the-art in modeling natural language (Jozefowicz et al., 2016) (Radford et al., 2018) (Dai et al., 2018), raw audio (Van Den Oord et al., 2016) (Mehri et al., 2016), and images (Oord et al., 2016) (Menick &amp; Kalchbrenner, 2018) (Salimans et al., 2017) (Reed et al., 2017) (Chen et al., 2017).

这些方法将联合概率分布分解为条件概率的乘积。然而，对这些条件分布进行建模极具挑战性，因为它们包含许多复杂的长期依赖关系，并且需要足够表达力的模型架构来学习它们。

These methods decompose a joint probability distribution into a product of conditional ones. Modeling these conditional distributions is extremely challenging, however, as they contain many complex, long-range dependencies and require a suitably expressive model architecture to learn them.

基于 CNN 的架构（Oord 等人，2016）在这一方向上取得了很大进展，但需要显著的深度来扩大其感受野。为了解决这个问题，WaveNet（Van Den Oord 等人，2016）引入了扩张卷积，使得网络能够在对数层数内建模长期依赖关系。

Architectures based off CNNs (Oord et al., 2016) have made great progress in this direction, but require significant depth to expand their receptive field. To address this, WaveNet (Van Den Oord et al., 2016) introduced dilated convolutions, which allowed the network to model long-range dependencies in a logarithmic number of layers.

另外，Transformer（Vaswani 等人，2017）已被证明在许多自然语言任务上表现出色，这在一定程度上可能归功于其能够在恒定层数内建模任意依赖关系。由于每个自注意力层具有全局感受野，网络可以将表示能力分配到最有用的输入区域。因此，该架构可能比具有固定连接模式的网络更灵活地生成多样化的数据类型。

Separately, the Transformer (Vaswani et al., 2017) has been shown to excel on many natural language tasks, which may be in part due to its ability to model arbitrary dependencies in a constant number of layers. As each self-attention layer has a global receptive field, the network can allocate representational capacity to the input regions for which it is most useful. Thus the architecture may be more flexible at generating diverse data types than networks with fixed connectivity patterns.

然而，此类网络的内存和计算需求随序列长度呈二次增长，这排除了它们在长序列上的使用。

However, the memory and computational requirements of such networks grows quadratically with sequence length, which excludes their use on long sequences.

本文的主要贡献是引入了注意力矩阵的几种稀疏分解，其随序列长度以 O(n√[p]{n}) 缩放，且不牺牲性能。这些分解将完整的注意力计算分离为几个更快的注意力操作，当组合时，可以近似密集注意力操作。我们利用这一点将自注意力应用于前所未有的长序列。

The main contribution of this work is to introduce several sparse factorizations of the attention matrix, which scale as O​(n​np)𝑂𝑛𝑝𝑛O(n\sqrt[p]{n}) with the sequence length without sacrificing performance. These work by separating the full attention computation into several faster attention operations which, when combined, can approximate the dense attention operation. We use this to apply self-attention to sequences of unprecedented length.

此外，我们还对 Transformer 进行了其他几项更改，包括：

Additionally, we introduce several other changes to the Transformer, including:

重构的残差块和权重初始化，以改进非常深网络的训练

A restructured residual block and weight initialization to improve training of very deep networks

一组稀疏注意力核，高效计算注意力矩阵的子集

A set of sparse attention kernels which efficiently compute subsets of the attention matrix

在反向传播期间重计算注意力权重以减少内存使用

Recomputation of attention weights during the backwards pass to reduce memory usage

我们通过实验验证，以这种方式增强的模型可以在自然语言、原始音频和自然图像的压缩与生成方面达到最优水平。架构的简洁性使我们相信它可能对许多感兴趣的问题有用。

We empirically validate that models augmented in this manner can achieve state-of-the-art compression and generation of natural language, raw audio, and natural images. The simplicity of the architecture leads us to believe it may be useful for many problems of interest.

2 相关工作 2 Related Work

最相关的工作涉及扩展自回归生成模型的其他技术。对于图像，（Reed 等人，2017）对像素之间的条件独立性进行建模，以便并行生成多个位置；（Menick & Kalchbrenner，2018）引入了排序和多尺度上采样过程以生成高保真样本。（Parmar 等人，2018）使用局部注意力块将 Transformer 应用于图像。对于文本，（Dai 等人，2018）引入了状态重用“记忆”来建模长期依赖关系。对于音频，除了（Van Den Oord 等人，2016）之外，（Mehri 等人，2016）使用了层次结构和不同时钟频率的 RNN，以便在推理时使用长上下文，类似于（Koutnik 等人，2014）。（Huang 等人，2018）将 Transformer 应用于 MIDI 生成，采用了高效的相对注意力。

The most related work involves other techniques for scaling up autoregressive generative models. For images, (Reed et al., 2017) models conditional independence between the pixels in order to generate many locations in parallel, and (Menick &amp; Kalchbrenner, 2018) imposes an ordering and multi-scale upsampling procedure to generate high fidelity samples. (Parmar et al., 2018) uses blocks of local attention to apply Transformers to images. For text, (Dai et al., 2018) introduces a state reuse ”memory” for modeling long-term dependencies. And for audio, in addition to (Van Den Oord et al., 2016), (Mehri et al., 2016) used a hierarchical structure and RNNs of varying clock-rates to use long contexts during inference, similar to (Koutnik et al., 2014). (Huang et al., 2018) apply Transformers to MIDI generation with an efficient relative attention.

我们的工作比上述许多技术更简单，并且可以同样应用于图像、文本和音频。此外，上述许多技术与我们的工作正交，并且可以与我们的工作结合使用。

Our work is simpler than many of the techniques above and can be applied equally across images, text, and audio. Many of the above techniques are orthogonal to ours, moreover, and could be used in conjunction with ours.

在生成式建模之外，还有一些工作通过分块（Chiu & Raffel, 2017）或使用固定长度表示（Britz et al., 2017）来提高注意力机制的效率。其他工作研究了具有多“跳”注意力的方法，例如（Sukhbaatar et al., 2015）和（Gehring et al., 2017）。

Outside of generative modeling, there are several works relevant to improving the efficiency of attention based off chunking (Chiu &amp; Raffel, 2017) or using fixed length representations (Britz et al., 2017). Other works have investigated attention with multiple ”hops”, such as (Sukhbaatar et al., 2015) and (Gehring et al., 2017).

值得注意的是，Gated Pixel CNN（Oord et al., 2016）和 WaveNet（Van Den Oord et al., 2016）在其网络中使用了乘法交互，这与自注意力机制相关。

It is worth noting that the Gated Pixel CNN (Oord et al., 2016) and WaveNet (Van Den Oord et al., 2016) use multiplicative interactions in their networks, which are related to self-attention.

3 背景 3 Background

我们考虑自回归序列生成任务，其中序列 𝐱={x1,x2,…,xn} 的联合概率被建模为条件概率分布的乘积，并由网络 θ 参数化。

We consider the task of autoregressive sequence generation, where the joint probability of a sequence 𝐱={x1,x2,…,xn}𝐱subscript𝑥1subscript𝑥2…subscript𝑥𝑛\mathbf{x}=\{x_{1},x_{2},...,x_{n}\} is modeled as the product of conditional probability distributions and parameterized by a network θ𝜃\theta.

我们将图像、文本和音频视为离散词元的序列，通常是原始字节。网络 θ 接收词元序列，并使用 softmax 函数输出下一个词元的 v 个可能值的分类分布，其中 v 是词汇表大小。训练目标是最大化数据相对于 θ 的对数概率。

We treat images, text, and audio as a sequence of discrete tokens, typically raw bytes. The network θ𝜃\theta takes in the sequence of tokens and outputs a categorical distribution over the v𝑣v possible values of the next token using the softmaxsoftmax\mathrm{softmax} function, where v𝑣v is the size of the vocabulary. The training objective is to maximize the log-probability of the data with respect to θ𝜃\theta.

模型 θ 的一个简单而强大的选择是仅解码器模式的 Transformer（Vaswani et al., 2017），如（Radford et al., 2018）和（Liu et al., 2018）所示。这些模型通过在整个序列上的多头自注意力块转换输入序列，然后对每个序列元素进行密集变换。然而，网络的自注意力部分必须为 n 个元素中的每一个计算 n 个权重，随着序列长度的增长，这可能会变得难以处理。

A simple and powerful choice for model θ𝜃\theta is a Transformer (Vaswani et al., 2017) in decoder-only mode, as demonstrated by (Radford et al., 2018) and (Liu et al., 2018). These models transform the input sequence with blocks of multihead self-attention over the entire sequence, followed by dense transformations over each sequence element. The self-attention portion of the network must compute n𝑛n weightings for each of n𝑛n elements, however, which can quickly become intractable as the sequence length grows.

在以下章节中，我们描述了对 Transformer 架构的修改，使其更适合建模长序列。

In the following sections, we describe our modifications to the Transformer architecture which make it more suitable for modeling long sequences.

我们可视化了在 CIFAR-10 上训练的 128 层自注意力网络学习的注意力模式，并在图 2 中展示了几个示例。视觉检查表明，大多数层在大多数数据点上具有稀疏的注意力模式，这表明可以在不显著影响性能的情况下引入某种形式的稀疏性。然而，一些层（图 2c）明显表现出全局模式，而其他层则表现出数据相关的稀疏性（图 2d），这两者都会受到在所有注意力矩阵中引入预定稀疏模式的影响。

We visualized the attention patterns learned by a 128-layer self-attention network on CIFAR-10, and present several examples in Figure 2. These two criteria allow us keep the ability of Transformers to propagate signals from arbitrary input positions to arbitrary output positions in a constant number of steps, while reducing the total effective computation to O​(n​np)𝑂𝑛𝑝𝑛O(n\sqrt[p]{n}). We also note that softening the validity criterion (for instance, having a series of only locally connected layers) may be a useful inductive bias for certain domains.

4.3 二维因子化注意力 4.3 Two-dimensional factorized attention

在二维中定义分解注意力模式的一种自然方法是让一个头关注前 l 个位置，另一个头关注每第 l 个位置，其中 l 是步长，选择接近 √n，我们称这种方法为步长注意力。

A natural approach to defining a factorized attention pattern in two dimensions is to have one head attend to the previous l𝑙l locations, and the other head attend to every l𝑙lth location, where l𝑙l is the stride and chosen to be close to n𝑛\sqrt{n}, a method we call strided attention.

This formulation is convenient if the data naturally has a structure that aligns with the stride, like images or some types of music. c=1 的固定注意力模式会显著限制网络的表达能力，因为网络中的许多表示仅用于一个块，而少数位置被所有块使用。相反，我们发现对于 l∈{128,256}的典型值，选择 c∈{8,16,32}表现良好，但应注意，与步进注意力相比，这会使该方法的计算成本增加 c 倍。

5.6 混合精度训练 5.6 Mixed-precision training

我们将网络权重存储为单精度浮点数，但网络激活和梯度使用半精度计算（如 Micikevicius 等人，2017）。这得益于 V100 GPU 上的 Tensor Core 操作，加速了训练。在梯度计算过程中，我们使用动态损失缩放来减少数值下溢，并在多 GPU 平均梯度时通信半精度梯度。采样时，我们将查询和键转换为单精度，因为查询-键乘积有时会溢出半精度的最大值。

We store network weights in single-precision floating-point, but otherwise compute network activations and gradients in half-precision, as in (Micikevicius et al., 2017). This accelerates our training due to the usage of Tensor Core operations on the V100 GPU. During the gradient calculation, we use dynamic loss scaling to reduce numerical underflow, and we communicate half-precision gradients when averaging across multiple GPUs. When sampling, we cast the queries and keys to single-precision, as the query-key product can sometimes overflow the max value of half-precision.

7.2 文本 7.2 Text

In order to assess Sparse Transformers on datasets without a strong two-dimensional structure, we trained models on the EnWik8 dataset, which represents the first 108superscript10810^{8} bytes of Wikipedia and contains a great degree of variability in periodic structure. We trained with a context length of 12,288, which is longer than previous approaches.

7.4 从原始音频学习古典音乐 7.4 Classical music from raw audio

To test the extent to which Sparse Transformers are able to scale to very long contexts, we trained models on the classical music dataset released by (Dieleman et al., 2018). 8 结论 8 Conclusion

我们引入了 Sparse Transformer，并证明它们在长序列密度建模上达到了与标准 Transformer 相当或更好的性能，同时所需操作显著减少。这一性能在图像和文本中达到了最先进水平，并且易于适应原始音频。该模型展示了长期上下文的使用能力，并生成了全局一致的样本。

We introduced Sparse Transformers and showed they attain equivalent or better performance on density modeling of long sequences than standard Transformers while requiring significantly fewer operations. This performance is state-of-the-art in images and text and is easily adaptable to raw audio. The model demonstrates usage of long-term context and generates globally coherent samples.
