Deep Double Descent: Where Bigger Models and More Data Hurt

摘要 · Abstract

我们展示了多种现代深度学习任务存在“双重下降”现象：随着模型规模增大，性能先变差后变好。此外，双重下降不仅随模型规模变化，也随训练轮数变化。我们通过定义一种新的复杂度度量——有效模型复杂度，统一了上述现象，并推测了关于该度量的广义双重下降。此外，我们的模型复杂度概念使我们能够识别出某些增加（甚至翻两番）训练样本反而损害测试性能的区域。

We show that a variety of modern deep learning tasks exhibit a "double-descent" phenomenon where, as we increase model size, performance first gets worse and then gets better. Moreover, we show that double descent occurs not just as a function of model size, but also as a function of the number of training epochs. We unify the above phenomena by defining a new complexity measure we call the effective model complexity and conjecture a generalized double descent with respect to this measure. Furthermore, our notion of model complexity allows us to identify certain regimes where increasing (even quadrupling) the number of train samples actually hurts test performance.

    Provides extensive empirical validation across architectures, datasets, and optimizers.

局限 · Limitations

  • 双重下降在标签噪声下最显著；无噪声设置中不太明显。
    Double descent is most pronounced with label noise; less clear in noiseless settings.
  • 有效模型复杂度是启发式定义的，缺乏 epsilon 的原则性选择。
    Effective Model Complexity is defined heuristically with no principled choice of epsilon.
  • 深度网络中双重下降的理论理解仍不完整。
    Theoretical understanding of double descent in deep networks remains incomplete.
  • 最优早停可能缓解双重下降，但其相互作用尚未充分探索。
    Optimal early stopping may mitigate double descent, but its interaction is not fully explored.
  • 结果是经验性的；缺乏'充分小/大'的形式化规范。
    Results are empirical; formal specification of 'sufficiently smaller/larger' is lacking.

1 引言 1 Introduction

偏差-方差权衡是经典统计学习理论中的一个基本概念（例如，Hastie 等人，2005）。其思想是，复杂度越高的模型偏差越低但方差越高。根据这一理论，一旦模型复杂度超过某个阈值，模型就会“过拟合”，方差项主导测试误差，因此从这一点开始，增加模型复杂度只会降低性能（即增加测试误差）。因此，经典统计学的传统观点是，一旦超过某个阈值，“更大的模型更差”。

The bias-variance trade-off is a fundamental concept in classical statistical learning theory (e.g., Hastie et al. (2005)). The idea is that models of higher complexity have lower bias but higher variance. According to this theory, once model complexity passes a certain threshold, models “overfit” with the variance term dominating the test error, and hence from this point onward, increasing model complexity will only decrease performance (i.e., increase test error). Hence conventional wisdom in classical statistics is that, once we pass a certain threshold, “larger models are worse.”

然而，现代神经网络并未表现出这种现象。这些网络拥有数百万个参数，足以拟合甚至随机标签（Zhang 等人，2016），但在许多任务上它们比小模型表现更好。实际上，从业者的传统观点是“更大的模型更好”（Krizhevsky 等人，2012；Huang 等人，2018；Szegedy 等人，2015；Radford 等人，2019）。训练时间对测试性能的影响也存在争议。在某些情况下，“早停”会提高测试性能，而在其他情况下，将神经网络训练到零训练误差只会提高性能。最后，如果说经典统计学家和深度学习从业者有一致意见的话，那就是“更多数据总是更好”。

However, modern neural networks exhibit no such phenomenon. Such networks have millions of parameters, more than enough to fit even random labels (Zhang et al. (2016)), and yet they perform much better on many tasks than smaller models. Indeed, conventional wisdom among practitioners is that “larger models are better’’ (Krizhevsky et al. (2012), Huang et al. (2018), Szegedy et al. (2015), Radford et al. (2019)). The effect of training time on test performance is also up for debate. In some settings, “early stopping” improves test performance, while in other settings training neural networks to zero training error only improves performance. Finally, if there is one thing both classical statisticians and deep learning practitioners agree on is “more data is always better”.

在本文中，我们提供的经验证据既调和又挑战了上述一些“传统观点”。我们表明，许多深度学习设置有两个不同的阶段。在欠参数化阶段，模型复杂度相对于样本数量较小，测试误差随模型复杂度的变化遵循经典偏差/方差权衡预测的 U 形行为。然而，一旦模型复杂度足够大以实现插值（即达到接近零的训练误差），增加复杂度只会降低测试误差，遵循“更大模型更好”的现代直觉。类似的行为先前在 Opper（1995；2001）、Advani & Saxe（2017）、Spigler 等人（2018）和 Geiger 等人（2019b）中观察到。这一现象首先由 Belkin 等人（2018）普遍提出，并命名为“双下降”，并在决策树、随机特征和具有ℓ2 损失的 2 层神经网络上，针对包括 MNIST 和 CIFAR-10 在内的多种学习任务进行了演示。

In this paper, we present empirical evidence that both reconcile and challenge some of the above “conventional wisdoms.” We show that many deep learning settings have two different regimes. In the under-parameterized regime, where the model complexity is small compared to the number of samples, the test error as a function of model complexity follows the U-like behavior predicted by the classical bias/variance tradeoff. However, once model complexity is sufficiently large to interpolate i.e., achieve (close to) zero training error, then increasing complexity only decreases test error, following the modern intuition of “bigger models are better”. Similar behavior was previously observed in Opper (1995; 2001), Advani &amp; Saxe (2017), Spigler et al. (2018), and Geiger et al. (2019b). This phenomenon was first postulated in generality by Belkin et al. (2018) who named it “double descent”, and demonstrated it for decision trees, random features, and 2-layer neural networks with ℓ2subscriptℓ2\ell_{2} loss, on a variety of learning tasks including MNIST and CIFAR-10.

我们表明双下降是一种稳健的现象，出现在多种任务、架构和优化方法中（见图 1 和第 5 节；我们的实验总结在表 A 中）。此外，我们提出了一个更一般的“双下降”概念，超越了参数数量的变化。我们将训练过程的有效模型复杂度（EMC）定义为它能达到接近零训练误差的最大样本数量。EMC 不仅取决于数据分布和分类器架构，还取决于训练过程——特别是增加训练时间会增加 EMC。

We show that double descent is a robust phenomenon that occurs in a variety of tasks, architectures, and optimization methods (see Figure 1 and Section 5; our experiments are summarized in Table A). Moreover, we propose a much more general notion of “double descent” that goes beyond varying the number of parameters. We define the effective model complexity (EMC) of a training procedure as the maximum number of samples on which it can achieve close to zero training error. The EMC depends not just on the data distribution and the architecture of the classifier but also on the training procedure—and in particular increasing training time will increase the EMC.

我们假设，对于许多自然模型和学习算法，双下降作为 EMC 的函数发生。实际上，当我们保持模型固定并增加训练时间时，我们观察到“轮次维度的双下降”，在欠拟合阶段（当 EMC 小于样本数量时）性能遵循经典的 U 形曲线，然后一旦 EMC 足够大于样本数量，性能随训练时间提高（见图 2）。作为推论，早停仅在临界参数化模型的相对狭窄参数区域中有帮助。

We hypothesize that for many natural models and learning algorithms, double descent occurs as a function of the EMC. Indeed we observe “epoch-wise double descent” when we keep the model fixed and increase the training time, with performance following a classical U-like curve in the underfitting stage (when the EMC is smaller than the number of samples) and then improving with training time once the EMC is sufficiently larger than the number of samples (see Figure 2). As a corollary, early stopping only helps in the relatively narrow parameter regime of critically parameterized models.

最后，我们的结果揭示了测试性能随训练样本数量的变化。由于测试误差在 EMC 与样本数量匹配的点（从欠参数化到过参数化的过渡）附近达到峰值，增加样本数量会将这个峰值向右移动。虽然在大多数情况下增加样本数量会降低误差，但这种偏移效应有时会导致更多数据反而更差的情况！例如，图 3 展示了将样本数量增加 4.5 倍导致测试性能变差的案例。

Finally, our results shed light on test performance as a function of the number of train samples. Since the test error peaks around the point where EMC matches the number of samples (the transition from the under- to over-parameterization), increasing the number of samples has the effect of shifting this peak to the right. While in most settings increasing the number of samples decreases error, this shifting effect can sometimes result in a setting where more data is worse! For example, Figure 3 demonstrates cases in which increasing the number of samples by a factor of 4.54.54.5 results in worse test performance.

模型维度的双重下降。在第 5 节中，我们研究了在固定大量优化步数下，规模递增的模型的测试误差。我们表明，对于各种现代数据集（CIFAR-10、CIFAR-100、IWSLT'14 德-英，带有不同数量的标签噪声）、模型架构（CNN、ResNet、Transformer）、优化器（SGD、Adam）、训练样本数量和训练过程（数据增强和正则化），都会出现“模型维度的双重下降”。此外，测试误差的峰值系统地出现在插值阈值处。特别地，我们展示了实际场景中更大的模型反而更差。

Model-wise Double Descent. In Section 5, we study the test error of models of increasing size, for a fixed large number of optimization steps. We show that “model-wise double-descent” occurs for various modern datasets (CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100, IWSLT‘14 de-en, with varying amounts of label noise), model architectures (CNNs, ResNets, Transformers), optimizers (SGD, Adam), number of train samples, and training procedures (data-augmentation, and regularization). Moreover, the peak in test error systematically occurs at the interpolation threshold. In particular, we demonstrate realistic settings in which bigger models are worse.

周期维度的双重下降。在第 6 节中，我们研究了固定的大规模架构在训练过程中的测试误差。我们在类似设置下证明，当模型训练到刚好达到≈0 训练误差时，测试性能会出现相应的峰值。大模型的测试误差首先下降（训练开始时），然后上升（临界区域附近），最后再次下降（训练结束时）——也就是说，更长时间的训练可以纠正过拟合。

Epoch-wise Double Descent. In Section 6, we study the test error of a fixed, large architecture over the course of training. We demonstrate, in similar settings as above, a corresponding peak in test performance when models are trained just long enough to reach ≈0absent0~{}\approx 0 train error. The test error of a large model first decreases (at the beginning of training), then increases (around the critical regime), then decreases once more (at the end of training)—that is, training longer can correct overfitting.

样本维度的非单调性。在第 7 节中，我们研究了固定模型和训练过程在不同训练样本数量下的测试误差。与我们广义的双重下降假设一致，我们在“临界区域”观察到不同的测试行为，此时样本数量接近模型能拟合的最大值。这通常表现为一个长平台区域，在此区域中，训练到完成时增加更多数据可能无济于事（如 CIFAR-10 上的 CNN）。此外，我们展示了某些设置（IWSLT'14 英-德上的 Transformer）中，这表现为一个峰值——对于固定的架构和训练过程，更多数据实际上有害。

Sample-wise Non-monotonicity. In Section 7, we study the test error of a fixed model and training procedure, for varying number of train samples. Consistent with our generalized double-descent hypothesis, we observe distinct test behavior in the “critical regime”, when the number of samples is near the maximum that the model can fit. This often manifests as a long plateau region, in which taking significantly more data might not help when training to completion (as is the case for CNNs on CIFAR-10). Moreover, we show settings (Transformers on IWSLT‘14 en-de), where this manifests as a peak—and for a fixed architecture and training procedure, more data actually hurts.

关于标签噪声的评论。我们观察到所有形式的双重下降在训练集带有标签噪声（现实世界中收集训练数据时常见）的设置中最强烈。然而，我们也展示了几个即使没有标签噪声也存在测试误差峰值的实际设置：CIFAR-100 上的 ResNet（图 4(a)）和 CNN（图 20）；IWSLT'14 上的 Transformer（图 8）。此外，我们所有的实验都表明，在临界区域测试行为明显不同——在无噪声情况下通常表现为测试误差的“平台”，随着添加标签噪声而发展为峰值。进一步讨论见第 8 节。

Remarks on Label Noise. We observe all forms of double descent most strongly in settings with label noise in the train set (as is often the case when collecting train data in the real-world). However, we also show several realistic settings with a test-error peak even without label noise: ResNets (Figure 4(a)) and CNNs (Figure 20) on CIFAR-100; Transformers on IWSLT‘14 (Figure 8). Moreover, all our experiments demonstrate distinctly different test behavior in the critical regime— often manifesting as a “plateau” in the test error in the noiseless case which develops into a peak with added label noise. See Section 8 for further discussion.

模型维度的双重下降最早由 Belkin 等人（2018）作为一般现象提出。类似行为在 Opper（1995；2001）、Advani & Saxe（2017）、Spigler 等人（2018）和 Geiger 等人（2019b）中也有观察到。随后，大量工作研究了双重下降现象。在可处理的线性最小二乘回归设置中对其进行理论分析的论文越来越多，包括 Belkin 等人（2019）；Hastie 等人（2019）；Bartlett 等人（2019）；Muthukumar 等人（2019）；Bibas 等人（2019）；Mitra（2019）；Mei & Montanari（2019）。此外，Geiger 等人（2019a）提供了在 CIFAR-10 上训练的卷积网络中模型维度双重下降的初步结果。我们的工作与上述论文在两个关键方面不同：首先，我们将双重下降的思想从参数数量扩展到在统一概念“有效模型复杂度”下纳入训练过程，从而产生新的见解，如周期维度的双重下降和样本非单调性。训练时间增加对应复杂度增加的概念也在 Nakkiran 等人（2019）中提出。其次，我们提供了对现代实践（涵盖各种架构、数据集和优化过程）中双重下降的广泛而严格的证明。相关工作的扩展讨论见附录 C。

Model-wise double descent was first proposed as a general phenomenon by Belkin et al. (2018). Similar behavior had been observed in Opper (1995; 2001), Advani &amp; Saxe (2017), Spigler et al. (2018), and Geiger et al. (2019b). Subsequently, there has been a large body of work studying the double descent phenomenon. A growing list of papers that theoretically analyze it in the tractable setting of linear least squares regression includes Belkin et al. (2019); Hastie et al. (2019); Bartlett et al. (2019); Muthukumar et al. (2019); Bibas et al. (2019); Mitra (2019); Mei &amp; Montanari (2019). Moreover, Geiger et al. (2019a) provide preliminary results for model-wise double descent in convolutional networks trained on CIFAR-10. Our work differs from the above papers in two crucial aspects: First, we extend the idea of double-descent beyond the number of parameters to incorporate the training procedure under a unified notion of “Effective Model Complexity”, leading to novel insights like epoch-wise double descent and sample non-monotonicity. The notion that increasing train time corresponds to increasing complexity was also presented in Nakkiran et al. (2019). Second, we provide an extensive and rigorous demonstration of double-descent for modern practices spanning a variety of architectures, datasets optimization procedures. An extended discussion of the related work is provided in Appendix C.

5 模型维度的双重下降 5 Model-wise Double Descent

在本节中，我们研究在训练完成（固定大量优化步数）时，规模递增的模型的测试误差。我们展示了跨不同架构、数据集、优化器和训练过程的模型维度双重下降。临界区域在插值点附近表现出明显不同的测试行为，并且通常存在测试误差峰值，在带有标签噪声的设置中更为突出。

In this section, we study the test error of models of increasing size, when training to completion (for a fixed large number of optimization steps). We demonstrate model-wise double descent across different architectures, datasets, optimizers, and training procedures. The critical region exhibits distinctly different test behavior around the interpolation point and there is often a peak in test error that becomes more prominent in settings with label noise.

对于本节中的实验（图 4、5、7、7、8），请注意，所有增加插值阈值的修改（如添加标签噪声、使用数据增强和增加训练样本数量）也相应地将测试误差峰值向更大模型移动。显示这些模型早停行为的额外图，以及显示无标签噪声设置中双重下降的额外实验（例如图 19）见附录 E.2。我们还观察到对抗训练中的模型维度双重下降，即使在无标签噪声设置中也有显著的鲁棒测试误差峰值。见图 26（附录 E.2）。

For the experiments in this section (Figures 4, 5, 7, 7, 8), notice that all modifications which increase the interpolation threshold (such as adding label noise, using data augmentation, and increasing the number of train samples) also correspondingly shift the peak in test error towards larger models. Additional plots showing the early-stopping behavior of these models, and additional experiments showing double descent in settings with no label noise (e.g. Figure 19) are in Appendix E.2. We also observed model-wise double descent for adversarial training, with a prominent robust test error peak even in settings without label noise. See Figure 26 in Appendix E.2.

完全理解深度神经网络中模型维度双重下降的机制仍然是一个重要的开放问题。然而，即使在线性模型中也会出现模型维度双重下降的类似现象。最近一系列理论工作分析了这一设置（Bartlett 等人（2019）；Muthukumar 等人（2019）；Belkin 等人（2019）；Mei & Montanari（2019）；Hastie 等人（2019））。我们相信类似的机制可能在深度神经网络中起作用。

Fully understanding the mechanisms behind model-wise double descent in deep neural networks remains an important open question. However, an analog of model-wise double descent occurs even for linear models. A recent stream of theoretical works analyzes this setting (Bartlett et al. (2019); Muthukumar et al. (2019); Belkin et al. (2019); Mei &amp; Montanari (2019); Hastie et al. (2019)). We believe similar mechanisms may be at work in deep neural networks.

非正式地，我们的直觉是，对于处于插值阈值的模型大小，实际上只有一个模型能拟合训练数据，并且这个插值模型对训练集中的噪声和/或模型错误指定非常敏感。也就是说，由于模型刚好能拟合训练数据，强制其拟合甚至略微有噪声或错误指定的标签将破坏其全局结构，并导致高测试误差。（参见附录图 28，该实验通过展示集成在临界参数化区域显著有帮助，证明了这种噪声敏感性）。然而对于过参数化模型，存在许多拟合训练集的插值模型，SGD 能够找到一个“记忆”（或“吸收”）噪声同时仍在分布上表现良好的模型。

Informally, our intuition is that for model-sizes at the interpolation threshold, there is effectively only one model that fits the train data and this interpolating model is very sensitive to noise in the train set and/or model mis-specification. That is, since the model is just barely able to fit the train data, forcing it to fit even slightly-noisy or mis-specified labels will destroy its global structure, and result in high test error. (See Figure 28 in the Appendix for an experiment demonstrating this noise sensitivity, by showing that ensembling helps significantly in the critically-parameterized regime). However for over-parameterized models, there are many interpolating models that fit the train set, and SGD is able to find one that “memorizes” (or “absorbs”) the noise while still performing well on the distribution.

上述直觉在线性模型中有理论依据。一般来说，这种情况即使在线性模型中没有标签噪声也会出现（Mei & Montanari（2019）），并且每当真实分布的结构与模型族之间存在模型错误指定时就会发生。我们相信这种直觉也适用于深度学习，并且与我们的实验一致。

The above intuition is theoretically justified for linear models. In general, this situation manifests even without label noise for linear models (Mei &amp; Montanari (2019)), and occurs whenever there is model mis-specification between the structure of the true distribution and the model family. We believe this intuition extends to deep learning as well, and it is consistent with our experiments.

6 周期维度的双重下降 6 Epoch-wise Double Descent

在本节中，我们展示了一种关于训练周期的新型双重下降形式，这与我们统一的有效模型复杂度（EMC）视角和广义双重下降假设一致。增加训练时间会增加 EMC——因此一个足够大的模型在训练过程中从欠参数化过渡到过参数化。

In this section, we demonstrate a novel form of double-descent with respect to training epochs, which is consistent with our unified view of effective model complexity (EMC) and the generalized double descent hypothesis. Increasing the train time increases the EMC—and thus a sufficiently large model transitions from under- to over-parameterized over the course of training.

如图 9 所示，足够大的模型会表现出“双重下降”行为：测试误差先下降，在插值阈值附近上升，然后再次下降。相比之下，对于“中等大小”的模型，训练到完成时仅能勉强达到≈0 误差，测试误差随训练时间呈经典 U 形曲线，提前停止效果更好。过小无法达到近似阈值的模型将停留在“欠参数化”区域，增加训练时间会单调降低测试误差。我们的实验（图 10）表明，在存在标签噪声的情况下，许多数据集和架构设置都表现出逐轮双重下降。此外，这一现象在优化器变化和学习率调度中具有鲁棒性（见附录 E.1 的附加实验）。与模型级双重下降类似，测试误差峰值因标签噪声而加剧。

As illustrated in Figure 9, sufficiently large models can undergo a “double descent” behavior where test error first decreases then increases near the interpolation threshold, and then decreases again. In contrast, for “medium sized” models, for which training to completion will only barely reach ≈0absent0\approx 0 error, the test error as a function of training time will follow a classical U-like curve where it is better to stop early. Models that are too small to reach the approximation threshold will remain in the “under parameterized” regime where increasing train time monotonically decreases test error. Our experiments (Figure 10) show that many settings of dataset and architecture exhibit epoch-wise double descent, in the presence of label noise. Further, this phenomenon is robust across optimizer variations and learning rate schedules (see additional experiments in Appendix E.1). As in model-wise double descent, the test error peak is accentuated with label noise.

传统观点认为训练分为两个阶段：（1）第一阶段，网络学习一个泛化差距较小的函数；（2）第二阶段，网络开始过拟合数据，导致测试误差增加。我们的实验表明，这并非全貌——在某些情况下，测试误差会再次下降，并可能在训练结束时达到比第一个最小值更低的数值（见图 10 中 10%标签噪声的情况）。

Conventional wisdom suggests that training is split into two phases: (1) In the first phase, the network learns a function with a small generalization gap (2) In the second phase, the network starts to over-fit the data leading to an increase in test error. Our experiments suggest that this is not the complete picture—in some regimes, the test error decreases again and may achieve a lower value at the end of training as compared to the first minimum (see Fig 10 for 10% label noise).

In this section, we investigate the effect of varying the number of train samples, for a fixed model and training procedure. Previously, in model-wise and epoch-wise double descent, we explored behavior in the critical regime, where EMC𝒟,ϵ​(𝒯)≈nsubscriptEMC𝒟italic-ϵ𝒯𝑛\mathrm{EMC}_{\mathcal{D},{\epsilon}}(\mathcal{T})\approx n, by varying the EMC. By increasing n𝑛n, the same training procedure 𝒯𝒯\mathcal{T} can switch from being effectively over-parameterized to effectively under-parameterized.

我们表明，增加样本数量对测试误差与模型复杂度图有两种不同影响。一方面，（如预期）增加样本数量会缩小曲线下面积。另一方面，增加样本数量也会“将曲线向右移动”，并增加测试误差峰值处的模型复杂度。

On the one hand, (as expected) increasing the number of samples shrinks the area under the curve. On the other hand, increasing the number of samples also has the effect of “shifting the curve to the right” and increasing the model complexity at which test error peaks.

8 结论与讨论 8 Conclusion and Discussion

我们提出一个广义双重下降假设：当模型和训练过程的有效模型复杂度与训练样本数量相当时，它们会表现出非典型行为。我们在现代深度学习设置中为我们的假设提供了广泛证据，并表明它对数据集、架构和训练过程的选择具有鲁棒性。特别地，我们展示了现代深度网络的“模型级双重下降”，并刻画了更大模型可能表现更差的区域。我们还展示了“逐轮双重下降”，据我们所知，这此前未被提出。最后，我们表明双重下降现象可能导致一个区域，其中训练更多数据会导致更差的测试性能。初步结果表明，当固定模型改变正则化量时，双重下降也成立（见图 22）。

We introduce a generalized double descent hypothesis: models and training procedures exhibit atypical behavior when their Effective Model Complexity is comparable to the number of train samples. We provide extensive evidence for our hypothesis in modern deep learning settings, and show that it is robust to choices of dataset, architecture, and training procedures. In particular, we demonstrate “model-wise double descent” for modern deep networks and characterize the regime where bigger models can perform worse. We also demonstrate “epoch-wise double descent,” which, to the best of our knowledge, has not been previously proposed. Finally, we show that the double descent phenomenon can lead to a regime where training on more data leads to worse test performance. Preliminary results suggest that double descent also holds as we vary the amount of regularization for a fixed model (see Figure 22).

我们还相信，我们对临界状态的刻画为实践者提供了一种有用的思考方式——如果模型和训练过程刚好能拟合训练集，那么对模型或训练过程的微小改变可能导致意外行为（例如，使模型稍大或稍小、改变正则化等可能会损害测试性能）。

We also believe our characterization of the critical regime provides a useful way of thinking for practitioners—if a model and training procedure are just barely able to fit the train set, then small changes to the model or training procedure may yield unexpected behavior (e.g. making the model slightly larger or smaller, changing regularization, etc. may hurt test performance).

我们注意到，我们强调的许多现象在最优提前停止时通常不会发生。然而，这与我们的广义双重下降假设一致：如果提前停止阻止模型达到 0 训练误差，那么我们就不会看到双重下降，因为 EMC 未达到训练样本数量。此外，我们展示了至少一种设置，其中即使使用最优提前停止，模型级双重下降仍然会发生（CIFAR-100 上无标签噪声的 ResNet，见图 19）。我们尚未观察到使用最优提前停止时更多数据有害的设置。然而，我们并不清楚阻止这种情况发生的理由。将完全理解双重下降的最优提前停止行为留作未来工作的重要开放问题。

We note that many of the phenomena that we highlight often do not occur with optimal early-stopping. However, this is consistent with our generalized double descent hypothesis: if early stopping prevents models from reaching 00 train error then we would not expect to see double-descent, since the EMC does not reach the number of train samples. Further, we show at least one setting where model-wise double descent can still occur even with optimal early stopping (ResNets on CIFAR-100 with no label noise, see Figure 19). We have not observed settings where more data hurts when optimal early-stopping is used. However, we are not aware of reasons which preclude this from occurring. We leave fully understanding the optimal early stopping behavior of double descent as an important open question for future work.

在我们的实验中，我们在有标签噪声的设置中观察到最强的双重下降。然而，我们认为这一效应从根本上并非关于标签噪声，而是关于模型误设。例如，考虑一个设置，其中标签噪声并非真正随机，而是伪随机（相对于正在训练的分类器族）。在此设置中，贝叶斯最优分类器的性能不会改变（因为伪随机噪声是确定性的且可逆），但我们会观察到与真正随机标签噪声相同的双重下降。因此，我们将添加标签噪声视为使分布“更难”的代理——即增加模型误设的程度。

In our experiments, we observe double descent most strongly in settings with label noise. However, we believe this effect is not fundamentally about label noise, but rather about model mis-specification. For example, consider a setting where the label noise is not truly random, but rather pseudorandom (with respect to the family of classifiers being trained). In this setting, the performance of the Bayes optimal classifier would not change (since the pseudorandom noise is deterministic, and invertible), but we would observe an identical double descent as with truly random label noise. Thus, we view adding label noise as merely a proxy for making distributions “harder”— i.e. increasing the amount of model mis-specification.

我们的有效模型复杂度概念与经典复杂度概念（如 Rademacher 复杂度）相关，但在几个关键方面有所不同：（1）EMC 依赖于数据分布的真实标签，（2）EMC 依赖于训练过程，而不仅仅是模型架构。

Our notion of Effective Model Complexity is related to classical complexity notions such as Rademacher complexity, but differs in several crucial ways: (1) EMC depends on the true labels of the data distribution, and (2) EMC depends on the training procedure, not just the model architecture.

其他不包含特征（1）和（2）的模型复杂度概念不足以刻画双重下降峰值的位置。例如，Rademacher 复杂度由模型架构拟合随机标签训练集的能力决定。但 Rademacher 复杂度和 VC 维都不足以确定模型级双重下降峰值位置，因为它们不依赖于标签分布——我们的实验表明，添加标签噪声会移动峰值位置。

Other notions of model complexity which do not incorporate features (1) and (2) would not suffice to characterize the location of the double-descent peak. Rademacher complexity, for example, is determined by the ability of a model architecture to fit a randomly-labeled train set. But Rademacher complexity and VC dimension are both insufficient to determine the model-wise double descent peak location, since they do not depend on the distribution of labels— and our experiments show that adding label noise shifts the location of the peak.

此外，Rademacher 复杂度和 VC 维仅依赖于模型族和数据分布，而不依赖于用于寻找模型的训练过程。因此，它们无法捕捉训练时的双下降效应，例如“逐轮”双下降，以及数据增强对峰值位置的影响。

Moreover, both Rademacher complexity and VC dimension depend only on the model family and data distribution, and not on the training procedure used to find models. Thus, they are not capable of capturing train-time double-descent effects, such as “epoch-wise” double descent, and the effect of data-augmentation on the peak location.
