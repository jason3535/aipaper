没有黄仁勋的开源 AI

Open-source AI without Jensen

内森·兰伯特 Nathan Lambert · Interconnects · 2026-11-14 · Interconnects ↗

打开互动全文版（逐段中英对照 + 针对本篇提问）→

摘要 · Abstract

本文探讨英伟达 CEO 黄仁勋离任对美国开源 AI 生态的影响。文章认为，尽管英伟达仍有财务动机支持开放模型，但黄仁勋的离开使开源模型经济失去了一位关键的运营领导者和创始人式拥护者——正是他的资金与庇护，让初生的开放模型经济得以有时间建立应用场景和收入。核心论点提出一个利好：开放模型已正确抓住智能所有权和企业后训练；以及一个利空：鉴于算力稀缺，真正商品化的智能仍遥遥无期，这让规模经济更优的闭源实验室得以胜出。作者总结道，开放模型要成为有意义的经济引擎，仍需诸多条件同时成立，而该生态需要一位新的拥护者来缓解危机、抵制草率监管，并在美国孵化出真正处于前沿的开源模型玩家。

This article examines the implications of Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's resignation for the US open-source AI ecosystem. It argues that while Nvidia retains financial incentives to support open models, Huang's departure removes a key operational leader and founder-style champion whose funding and protection gave the nascent open model economy time to establish use-cases and revenue. The core argument presents one upside—open models have correctly latched onto ownership of intelligence and enterprise post-training—and one downside: true commoditized intelligence remains far off given compute scarcity, allowing closed labs with better economies of scale to win. The author concludes that a lot still needs to go right for open models to become a meaningful economic engine, and that the ecosystem needs a new champion to abate crises, counter sloppy regulation, and incubate a truly frontier open model player in the US.

核心贡献 · Key contributions

  • 黄仁勋的离任移除了创始人式的捍卫者，其资金与庇护曾为开放模型经济争取成熟时间。
    Huang's exit removes a founder-style champion whose funding and protection gave the open model economy time to mature.
  • 开放模型已正确锁定“智能所有权”与企业后训练作为关键赛道。
    Open models have correctly claimed ownership of intelligence and enterprise post-training as their key lane.
  • 算力稀缺使真正的商品化智能遥不可及，让具备规模优势的闭源实验室胜出。
    Compute scarcity keeps truly commoditized intelligence far off, letting closed labs with scale advantages win.
  • 英伟达仍有支持开放模型的财务动机，以避免客户集中与自研芯片。
    Nvidia retains financial incentives to back open models, to avoid customer concentration and self-built chips.
  • 生态需要新的捍卫者来化解危机、抵制草率监管并孵化前沿开放模型玩家。
    The ecosystem needs a new champion to abate crises, counter sloppy regulation, and incubate a frontier open player.
  • 约束在于人才而非资金；顶尖研究者集中在少数闭源实验室。
    Talent, not money, is the binding constraint; top researchers cluster at a few closed labs.

局限 · Limitations

  • 论证依赖单一领导者离任，黄仁勋离任的反事实影响具有推测性。
    Argument rests on one leader's departure; counterfactual impact of Huang's exit is speculative.
  • 开放模型经济难以度量，因最有价值的部分运行在私有基础设施上。
    Open model economy is hard to measure since valuable activity runs on private infrastructure.
  • 商品化论断依赖算力供给，供给变化可能削弱该结论。
    Commoditization thesis depends on compute supply, which may shift and weaken the conclusion.
  • 安全与监管风险多为断言，缺乏实证量化。
    Safety and regulation risks are asserted rather than empirically quantified.
  • 美国前沿开放玩家的可行性由人才分布推断，未经实证。
    US frontier open player feasibility is inferred from talent distribution, not demonstrated.

全文 · Full text（逐段中英对照）

斩首。是时候重建了。 A decapitation. It’s time to rebuild.

英伟达 CEO 黄仁勋的辞职是美国开放模型生态系统发展的重大挫折。英伟达在支持开放模型方面仍有财务利益，即避免权力集中在少数客户手中，这些客户会开发自己的芯片并减少英伟达的利润，但支持开放生态系统更像是一个棘手的谜题，而不是一个可以通过努力解决的工程问题。

The resignation of Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang is a major setback for the US development of an open model ecosystem. Nvidia still has the financial interests in supporting open models, which are to avoid concentration in power among a few customers who would develop their own chips and reduce Nvidia's profits, but supporting the open ecosystem is a tricky puzzle more than an engineering problem to grind out a solution on.

英伟达的业务运营众所周知极其依赖黄仁勋作为运营者，因此人们普遍认为未来面临挑战（甚至可能出现更多高调离职，通过芯片走私调查或老套的鲨鱼围绕更易挖角的人才）。英伟达的首要目标是稳定这艘船，在未来 24 个月内向客户交付，并防止内爆。开放模型一直是黄仁勋更为雄心勃勃的、创始人风格的赌注之一。他一直在向该领域大量注入资金，这种方式很可能将枯竭。

Nvidia's business operations are famously extremely dependent on Jensen as an operator, so it is a consensus opinion that there are challenges ahead (or even more high-profile departures through chip-smuggling investigations or plain-old sharks circling more poachable talent). Nvidia's number one goal is to stabilize the ship, deliver to customers in the next 24 months, and prevent implosion. Open models have been one of Jensen's more ambitious, founder-style bets. He has been flooding the zone with funding in a way that will very likely dry up.

在此期间，开放模型经济尚处于萌芽阶段。这是建立用例、收入流和继续训练下一代模型动机的关键年份。我在最近于 Fireworks Forge 的演讲中讨论了这些早期年份的敏感性（幻灯片、录音）。论点的核心是开放模型的一个上行空间、一个乐观的理由和一个下行风险。

In this period, the open model economy is in its nascency. These are crucial years for establishing use-cases, revenue flow, and motives to continue training the next generation models. I discussed the sensitivity of these early years in my recent talk at Fireworks Forge (slides, recording). The core of the argument is one upside for open models, a reason to be optimistic, and one downside.

积极的案例是，开放模型经济已正确地将“智能所有权”和企业后训练作为关键赛道。有待确定的是下游市场究竟有多大。

The positive case is that the open model economy has correctly latched onto “ownership of intelligence” and enterprise post-training as a crucial lane. What is to be determined is exactly how big the downstream market is.

不利的一面是，在算力如此短缺的情况下，开放模型作为真正商品化智能的**长期**真正益处还远未实现。短期内，推理初创公司也难以获得足够的芯片（因为 Anthropic 和 OpenAI 购买了太多产能），开放权重模型几乎没有利润竞争。如果开源模型也以巨大的推理利润提供服务，正如我们从 DeepSeek 的 DeepInfer 发布中学到的，它们远非商品。在这种范式下，具有更好规模经济以及从模型到数据中心和芯片整合的封闭实验室将获胜。我在最近关于开放模型经济的文章中详细阐述了这一挑战。

The downside is that the true _long-term_ benefit of open models, as truly commoditized intelligence, is very far from realization with compute in such short supply. In the near term, where inference startups are also struggling to get enough chips (because Anthropic and OpenAI are buying so much capacity), there’s little margin competition on open-weight models. If open-source models are also served at giant inference margins, as we learned with DeepSeek’s DeepInfer release, they’re far from a commodity. In this paradigm, the closed labs with better economies of scale and integration from model to datacenter and chips will win. I expanded on this challenge in my recent piece on the open model economy.

总之，开放模型要成为有意义的经济引擎，还有很多方面需要顺利发展。黄仁勋的支持和保护给了生态系统时间去解决这个问题。

Together, a lot still needs to go right for open models to be a meaningful economic engine. Jensen’s support, and protection, gave the ecosystem time to figure this out.

黄仁勋不仅仅是一个啦啦队长。一旦早期风向开始变化，他会阻止很多糟糕的事情发生。他愿意在开放模型上运用他的力量。

Jensen was more than a cheerleader. He would stop a lot of shit from going wrong once early winds were stirring. He was willing to use his power on open models.

民主党政府资金大量涌向想要监管 AI 行业、促进“安全”的候选人。其中许多人觉得需要做点什么，并普遍认为开放模型不安全，却没有受过关于其益处复杂性的教育。我近期最大的担忧是州级草率监管，其次是国家级监管。自今年夏天以来，我们一直处在一个强大的中国开放权重模型引发危机的边缘。这种情况仍然存在——尤其是当我们进入一个新的、动荡的网络安全平衡时。我们还没有看到由流行的中国开放权重模型驱动的大规模黑客攻击，但这是时间问题，而不是是否会发生的问题。当“开放权重鹰派”来猎杀时，我们需要有人鼓励谨慎和生态系统视角。

There’s a major swell in Democratic government funding towards candidates that want to regulate the AI industry, fostering “safety”. Many of these people feel a need to do something and have general opinions that open models are unsafe, while not being educated on the complexities of their benefits. My biggest near-term worry is sloppy regulation at the state level, followed by national regulation. We’ve been one, strong Chinese open-weight model away from a crisis since this summer. It still holds – especially as we enter a new, turbulent equilibrium of cybersecurity. We have not seen a major hack driven by a popular Chinese open-weight model, but it is a matter of when, not if. We need someone to encourage caution and an ecosystem perspective when the “open-weight hawks” come hunting.

今天推倒开源 AI 多米诺骨牌很容易——一旦这样做，要消除下游的权力集中、安全研究的减少以及对长期创新的扼杀就难得多了。在公开数据上，这看起来像是在平衡长期创新与短期风险，但这是因为衡量开放模型经济有多么困难（例如，其最有价值的方面运行在私有基础设施上）。这是生态系统和我的工作面临的反复出现的挑战。

Knocking over the open-source AI domino today is easy — if done, unwinding the downstream concentration of power, reduction in safety research, and stifling of long-term innovation is far harder. On public data, it looks like balancing long-term innovation versus short term risks, but this is due to how horribly hard it is to measure the open model economy (e.g. the most valuable aspects of it run on private infrastructure). It’s a recurring challenge facing the ecosystem and my work.

顺便说一句，如今媒体生态系统围绕时机而非信息的精确可获得性运转的程度，令人难以置信。所有这些 Jensen 事件都是已知的，但在正确的时机——中期政治摇摆之后——将其曝光，扳倒了这位王者。

As a brief aside, the extent to which the media ecosystem is revolving around timing today, rather than exact availability of information, is incredible. All of these Jensen affairs were known, but getting them out at the right time, right after the midterm political swing, took down the king.

今年夏天 Jacob Coxon 的骚动也是如此。围绕顶级实验室的所有那些安全文化都是众所周知的，但围绕数据中心和奥弗顿窗口的正确时机使其成为全国性问题。这很可能在某个时候发生在开放模型安全上，因为推动现代媒体算法的往往是伤害、风险或安全相关的叙事。我无法想象我在开放科学上的工作会驱动这样的媒体周期，因为它永远不会发生。

The same goes for the Jacob Coxon brouhaha this summer. All of that safety culture around top labs was well known, but the right timing around datacenters and the Overton window made it a national issue. This could very well happen at some point for open model safety, as it tends to be harm, risk, or safety-related narratives that drive the modern media algorithms. I cannot imagine my work on open-science driving such a media cycle, because it will never happen.

对此唯一持久的解决方案是在美国孵化一个真正的前沿开放模型参与者。例如，我完全同意 SemiAnalysis 首席执行官 Dylan Patel 关于美国开放模型生态系统核心问题的看法。摘自最近 ChinaTalk + SemiAnalysis 紧急播客：

The only lasting solution to this is to incubate a truly frontier open model player in the US. For example, I agree completely with Dylan Patel, CEO of SemiAnalysis, on a core problem in the US open model ecosystem. From a recent ChinaTalk + SemiAnalysis emergency podcast:

**Jordan Schneider：** 我确实想听听你们对这件事有多糟糕的看法，Jensen 现在已不在美国创新的未来考虑之中。我们应该谈谈开放模型。我的意思是，他曾是最大的 champion，对吧？Dylan，你之前说过——我仍然不太理解为什么有人不直接花几十亿美元，做出比 Kimi 或 DeepSeek 更好的东西并开源。

**Jordan Schneider:** I do want your guys’ take on just how bad this is, that Jensen’s now off the table for the future of American innovation. And we should talk about open models. I mean, he was the big champion, right? Dylan, you were talking earlier — I still don’t understand quite why someone didn’t just spend a few billion dollars and make something better than Kimi or DeepSeek and open source it.

**Dylan Patel：** 没那么容易。不是钱的问题，是人才，伙计。所有最有才华的人主要都在 Anthropic、OpenAI、DeepMind 和 Meta TBD 工作，对吧？然后那些不在的人分散在一百家不同的初创公司。我的意思是，是的，Thinking Machines 有一个很棒的团队，但 Thinking Machines 和 Moonshot 一样好吗？不。Moonshot 在技能上可能和美国实验室一样好。他们只是在算力和部署上有很大赤字。所以我认为如果我们没有规模优势，你就赢不了。是的，这些较小的实验室在模型上花了很多钱。但 DeepMind、Grok、Meta 经常落后于中国实验室的前沿，而且他们花得更多。所以这不仅仅是，哦，花几十亿美元就能做出更好的模型。

**Dylan Patel:** Not that easy. It’s not money, it’s talent, man. All of the most talented people primarily work at Anthropic, OpenAI, DeepMind, and Meta TBD, right? And then those who don’t are split across a hundred different startups. I mean, yes, Thinking Machines has a cracked team, but is Thinking Machines as good as Moonshot? No. Moonshot skill-wise is probably just as good as the US labs. They just have a big deficit in compute and deployment. So I think if we don’t have a scale advantage, then you don’t win. Yes, these smaller labs have spent a lot of money on models. But DeepMind, Grok, Meta have regularly fallen behind the frontier of Chinese labs, and they’re spending more. So it’s not just, oh, spend a few billion dollars and you can make a model that’s better.

吸引人才最合适的人选，是那些站在运动最前沿的人。Jensen 助推了这一进程，而且在许多指标上仍处于落后。Nemotron 拥有恰当的雄心水平和强大的人才，但尚未达到可以被称为“任务完成”的程度。在构建前沿模型方面的竞争，比大多数人愿意承认的更为激烈。拥有一个坐拥数十亿美元、人才扎实、氛围良好的组织，未必就能让你抵达目标。你通常需要一些特别的东西。

The best people for attracting talent are the ones at the front of movements. Jensen helped catalyze this, and on many metrics was still losing. Nemotron has had the right level of ambition and strong people, but it hasn't delivered to the point of being called "mission accomplished." The competition here in building leading-edge models is more fierce than most give credit to. Having an organization with billions of dollars, solid people, and good vibes may not get you there. You normally need something special.

有可能 Thinking Machines 在坚持开放模型方面已经走向成熟，其近期的 Inkling 2 Small 是相当长一段时间以来最好的西方开放权重模型。尽管如此，它仍远远落后于 Kimi K3.1 Pro，而人们对智谱下一次 GLM 更新的期望很高。我们至少在进行这场辩论，这是一个更好的迹象，但开放生态系统中充斥着诸如此类的问题与答案——_也许他们能成功？_ 在等式的另一边，像 Anthropic 这样的闭源实验室看起来是有史以来最伟大的企业之一，这让人对其路径的持久性抱有信心。

It could be the case that Thinking Machines has gotten to maturity in its commitment to open models, with its recent Inkling 2 Small being the best Western open-weight model in a while. Still, it's far behind Kimi K3.1 Pro and expectations are high for Zhipu's next GLM update. It's a better sign that we're at least having this debate, but the open ecosystem is riddled with questions and answers like this – _maybe they can pull it off?_ On the other side of the equation, closed labs like Anthropic look like some of the greatest businesses ever assembled, which inspires confidence in the longevity of their approach.

当 Mira 需要完成下一轮融资时，他们会打给谁？据说以 400 亿美元估值进行的下一轮已经板上钉钉，但这不足以长期跟上闭源前沿的步伐。

Who is Mira going to call when they need to close their next funding round? Supposedly the next round at a $40B valuation is a done deal, but that won't be enough to keep pace with the closed frontier for long.

根本问题在于，开放权重生态系统需要有人愿意接起电话来缓解危机。前方还有更多挑战。我们最可能求助的盟友来自超大规模云厂商，比如 Zuckerberg、Andy Jassy 或 Satya 这样的人。鉴于 AI 安全社区发起了一场协调一致的压力运动，将开放模型视为负面目标，我担心主动出击是保持领先的唯一方式。Jensen 已经扛起这面旗帜约两年，而在他于 2025 年明确挺身而出之前，时局颇为混乱。当风险与攻击注定会上升之际，我们这场运动的运营能力却在退步。我们这些开放模型信仰者理应进行一番自我反思和联盟建设，以构建一个新平台。有些鞋子已被腾空，且永远无法完全填补。

The fundamental problem is that the open-weight ecosystem needs someone who will pick up the phone to abate a crisis. There are more challenges ahead. The most likely allies we could turn to are from the hyperscalers, the likes of Zuckerberg, Andy Jassy, or Satya. With such a coordinated campaign of pressure from the AI safety community that has open models as a negative target, I worry that being proactive is the only way to stay ahead. Jensen has carried that mantle for about two years, and times were messy before he stepped up clearly in 2025. We're regressing in the operational capability of our movement, when the stakes and attacks are destined to rise. We open model believers are due some soul-searching and coalition building to construct a new platform. Shoes have been emptied that will never fully be refilled.
