摘要 · Abstract

这份 2026 年 8 月的 Anthropic 风险报告评估了高级 AI 模型的风险，重点关注自主性、自动化 AI 研发以及生物/化学武器生产，但不包括网络风险。报告详述了两个内部模型，其中模型 2 能力更强，但因潜在危险或专门化而未发布。报告通过一致性、普遍性和自然涌现等维度定义了错位，并引入了高风险场景的威胁模型。关键关注点包括故意与非故意伤害，如近期 OpenAI 的失败所示，以及即使在不便时也需遵守的稳健风险阈值。作者批评了报告对特定威胁模型的狭隘关注，主张更广泛地考虑非故意伤害以及安全部门的重要性。总体而言，报告提供了评估 AI 风险的框架，但强调了在能力开发与安全之间平衡的持续挑战，敦促对规则变更设定更高门槛，并将安全更好地整合到所有 AI 研究中。

This August 2026 Anthropic risk report evaluates risks from advanced AI models, focusing on autonomy, automated AI R&D, and biological/chemical weapons production, while excluding cyber risks. It details two internal models, with Model 2 being more capable but not released due to potential dangers or specialization. The report defines misalignment through dimensions like coherence, pervasiveness, and natural emergence, and introduces threat models for high-stakes settings. Key concerns include intentional versus incidental harm, as seen in recent OpenAI failures, and the need for robust risk thresholds that are honored even when inconvenient. The author critiques the report's narrow focus on specific threat models, arguing for broader consideration of incidental harms and the importance of safety departments. Overall, the report provides a framework for assessing AI risks but highlights ongoing challenges in balancing capability development with safety, urging higher bars for rule changes and better integration of safety across all AI research.

局限 · Limitations

  • 威胁模型聚焦过窄，可能遗漏附带性危害，如近期 OpenAI 失误所示。
    Narrow threat-model focus may miss incidental harms, as seen in recent OpenAI failures.
  • 鉴于真实事件，将网络风险排除在核心威胁模型之外值得商榷。
    Excluding cyber risks from core threat models is questionable given real incidents.
  • 依赖思维链监控会形成安全盲区，并可能训练模型学会混淆。
    Reliance on chain-of-thought monitoring creates safe zones and risks training models to obfuscate.
  • “低”等风险等级缺乏严格定义，使结论偏主观且不确定。
    Risk levels like 'low' lack strict definitions, making conclusions vibey and uncertain.
  • 生物风险估计可能过度自信，忽视高模型不确定性与低概率尾部事件。
    Biological risk estimates may be overconfident, ignoring high model uncertainty and low-probability tail events.

错位是一种心理状态（2.5） Misalignment Is a State of Mind (2.5)

非常欣赏定义部分。

The definition section is greatly appreciated.

我很喜欢这个定义，不过我有一些重要的吹毛求疵：

I like this definition a lot, although I have important nitpicks:

**错位（Misalignment）[定义]：** 错位是模型在给定上下文中执行的特定计算的一种潜在属性。

**Misalignment [definition]:** Misalignment is a latent property of a specific computation performed by a model in a given context.

一个计算在以下情况下是错位的：

A computation is misaligned if

(i) 一个完全理解该情境的理性人（例如借助目前可能尚不存在的强大可解释性工具）会认为它不道德、违法、明显令人反感，或与模型的章程不一致，

(i) a reasonable person with full understanding of the situation (e.g. via powerful interpretability tools that may not currently exist) would consider it unethical, illegal, clearly objectionable, or inconsistent with the model's constitution,

以及（ii）它影响或可能合理地影响模型的输出。

and (ii) it influences or could plausibly influence the model's output.

错位的计算不必是可观察的。

Misaligned computation need not be observable.

在单次前向传播中进行的图谋也算数，因为即使输出看起来无害，图谋也影响了输出。

A model scheming within a single forward pass counts, because the scheming influences the output even if the output looks benign.

我的第一处修改是将“宪法”泛化，使其包含模型规范以及系统的其他预期属性，从而可以应用于 Anthropic 之外。

My first change would be to generalize 'constitution' to include model specs and other intended properties of the system, so this can be applied outside Anthropic.

我的第二处修改是指明：这要求一个理性人在反思后认为该计算不可接受且缺乏正当理由。也就是说，在某些情况下，如果有充分的理由可以压倒这一点，那么违法、做“令人反感”的事或违反模型宪法并不显然就是错位的。在某些情况下，所有路径都会这样做。

My second change would be to specify that this requires the reasonable person to find the computation therefore, on reflection, unacceptable and unjustified. As in, it is not obviously misaligned to break the law or do something 'objectionable' or violate the model's constitution, under some circumstances, if there are good reasons that override this. In some circumstances, all paths will do this.

在这种观点下，错位并非“诚实的错误”，也不是能力不足或信息不充分所致。

Misalignment, in this view, is not an 'honest mistake' or failure of capability or lack of sufficient information.

正如 Nate Soares 在 Twitter 上指出的，按照这一定义，你可以在最初并未错位的情况下造成伤害并导致错位状态，例如 AI 在启动大量子智能体（或训练新模型等）时草率行事。这里的目标是将此视为一种能力或执行失败，它导致了后续的错位失败，但这本身并不是错位。

At this point, model weight security matters quite a lot. It would be extremely bad if Model 2 were to be stolen.

Anthropic 目前仍只采取 ASL-3 级别的防护措施。民族国家行为体被认为不在考虑范围之内。这不行。

Anthropic is still only taking ASL-3 precautions. Nation-state actors are considered beyond scope. No good.

风险已被报告 Risk Has Been Reported

Anthropic 发布了许多他们原本不必发布的信息，包括 Model 2 的存在、对思维链（CoT）的训练、对对齐伪装数据的新训练，以及另外几起相当令人尴尬的失败。他们自愿描绘了一幅 Anthropic 犯错的图景，并且这些错误连锁发生，次数多到我们应该担心它会再次发生，而且这些错误的方式让 OpenAI 的彻底失败看起来不那么非同寻常。

Anthropic released a lot of information they were not otherwise forced to release, including the existence of Model 2, the training on the CoT, the new training on alignment faking data, and several more rather embarrassing failures. They have voluntarily painted a picture of Anthropic making mistakes, and having those mistakes chain, enough distinct times that we should worry about it happening again, and in ways that make OpenAI's total failures look less extraordinary.

我的意思是，所有这些失败都是坏消息。情况并不乐观。许多错误正在被犯下。但既然错误已经发生，能听到这些错误总比听不到好，而且错误被识别后的反应似乎合理，至少直接层面如此。

I mean, all of those failures are bad news. It's not great out there. Lots of mistakes are being made. But given the mistakes are made, much better to hear about them, and the reactions once the mistakes were identified seem reasonable, at least on the direct level.

如果这些能代表所有问题，并且类似量级的问题占很大一部分，且被删节的部分不会改变太多，那么我会说这是一个适度积极的更新。尽管这些作为具体失败很糟糕，但考虑到我所知的其他一切，这种愚蠢错误的发生率似乎并不比我预期的更糟，而且在许多层面上，把这一切公开出来是件好事。

If these were representative of all of the issues, and a large portion of issues of similar magnitude, and the redacted ones don't change things much, then I would say this is a modestly positive update. As bad as these are as particular failures, this rate of stupid mistakes does not seem worse than I would have expected given everything else I know, and it is good to have it all out in the open, on many levels.

如果情况比这更糟，并且他们重要地隐瞒了其他东西，或者这只是大量类似事件中的一小部分，那么情况就会很糟糕。Claude 自己的审查表明，目前有某些内容被完全删节（见 2.23.1.2），这可能更糟，而且他们承认这仅仅是失败的“代表性样本”。所以我确实有点担心。

If it is worse than this and they're importantly holding the other stuff back, or this is a small portion of a very large set of similar incidents, then that would be bad. Claude's own review suggests there is something redacted in full for now (see 2.23.1.2), which might be worse, and they admit this is only a 'representative sample' of failures. So I am a bit worried, yes.

OpenAI 的版本想必将是对“发生了什么”的事后剖析，以及他们未来的计划和迄今为止所做的改变，其中包括暂停训练运行两周（然而我们并没有输给中国，真奇怪）。在我们等待完整报告的同时，这是一个好的开始。

OpenAI's version of this is presumably going to be the post-mortem on What Happened, along with their future plans and what they have changed so far, which includes two weeks of pausing training runs (yet we did not lose to China, curious). That's a good start while we await the full report.

这里让我担忧的并不是任何具体事件。让我担忧的是人们对于这一终极问题之本质的态度，以及解决它需要付出什么。这算不上新闻，但必须改变。因此，在这个地方，没有消息就是坏消息。

The part that worries me here is not any particular incident. What worries me is the attitude regarding the nature of the ultimate problem, and what it will take to solve it. That's not news, but it has to change. So that is one place where no news is terrible news.
