Lightning OPD 2.0: Mitigating Style Bias in Cross-Teacher On-Policy Distillation for Large Reasoning Models

摘要 · Abstract

在线策略蒸馏（OPD）从教师模型提供密集的令牌级监督，但其有效性可能依赖于教师一致性，即提供OPD监督的模型也应该生成用于训练监督微调（SFT）参考的示范。然而，当SFT数据来源混合或未知，或当SFT数据生成与后续蒸馏偏好不同模型时，这一条件在实践中经常被违反。在这种跨教师设置下，即使更强的OPD教师也只能比SFT参考带来微小的改进。我们发现，原始的教师-参考分歧既包含可能有用的上下文特定教师证据，也包含与措辞、格式和推理节奏差异相关的重复成分。我们引入了闪电OPD 2.0，采用交叉拟合风格残差化，利用轨迹级交叉拟合估计这一重复成分作为风格令牌偏差的操作性代理，并在构建令牌级OPD更新之前将其减去。在数学推理和代码生成基准上，闪电OPD 2.0在跨教师设置下始终优于闪电OPD。从Klear-Reasoner-8B-SFT开始，闪电OPD 2.0在AIME 2024上达到82.4%，在LiveCodeBench v5上达到63.0%。这些结果共同确立了闪电OPD 2.0作为跨教师OPD的实用方法，放宽了教师一致性作为前提条件，允许SFT数据生成器和蒸馏教师独立选择。代码即将发布。

On-policy distillation (OPD) provides dense token-level supervision from a teacher, but its effectiveness can depend on teacher consistency, meaning that the model providing OPD supervision should also have generated the demonstrations used to train the supervised fine-tuning (SFT) reference. However, this condition is frequently violated in practice when SFT data have mixed or unknown provenance or when different models are preferred for SFT data generation and subsequent distillation. In such cross-teacher settings, even a stronger OPD teacher can yield little improvement over the SFT reference. We find that raw teacher--reference disagreement contains potentially useful context-specific teacher evidence as well as a recurring component associated with differences in wording, formatting, and reasoning cadence. We introduce Lightning OPD 2.0 with cross-fitted style residualization, which uses rollout-level cross-fitting to estimate this recurring component as an operational proxy for style-token bias and subtracts it before constructing the token-level OPD update. Across mathematical reasoning and code generation benchmarks, Lightning OPD 2.0 consistently outperforms Lightning OPD in cross-teacher settings. Starting from Klear-Reasoner-8B-SFT, Lightning OPD 2.0 reaches 82.4% on AIME 2024 and 63.0% on LiveCodeBench v5. Together, these results establish Lightning OPD 2.0 as a practical approach to cross-teacher OPD, relaxing teacher consistency as a prerequisite and allowing the SFT data generator and distillation teacher to be selected independently. Code will be released soon.

核心贡献 · Key contributions

  • 识别出教师-参考不一致中反复出现的风格词元偏差，并将其与上下文特定证据区分开来。
    Identifies style-token bias as a recurrent component in teacher-reference disagreement, distinguishable from context-specific evidence.
  • 提出使用 rollout 级交叉拟合的风格残差化方法来估计该偏差。
    Proposes cross-fitted style residualization to estimate this bias via rollout-level cross-fitting.
  • 引入 Lightning OPD 2.0，在词元级 OPD 更新前减去估计的风格偏差。
    Introduces Lightning OPD 2.0, subtracting estimated style bias before token-level OPD updates.
  • 在数学和代码基准上证明跨教师场景中始终优于 Lightning OPD。
    Shows consistent improvements over Lightning OPD in cross-teacher settings across math and code benchmarks.
  • 放宽了教师一致性前提，允许独立选择 SFT 数据生成器和蒸馏教师。
    Relaxes teacher consistency prerequisite, allowing independent selection of SFT data generator and distillation teacher.
  • 从 Klear-Reasoner-8B-SFT 出发，在 AIME 2024 上达到 82.4%，在 LiveCodeBench v5 上达到 63.0%。
    Achieves 82.4% on AIME 2024 and 63.0% on LiveCodeBench v5 from Klear-Reasoner-8B-SFT.

局限 · Limitations

  • 交叉拟合在 rollout 处理中可能引入额外计算成本。
    Cross-fitting may introduce additional computational cost during rollout processing.
  • 风格残差化仅是操作层面的代理，并未完全移除所有风格偏差。
    Style residualization serves as an operational proxy, not a complete removal of all style biases.
  • 评估局限于 8B 规模模型及特定数学/代码基准，可扩展性尚不明确。
    Evaluation limited to 8B-scale models and specific mathematical/code benchmarks; scalability unclear.
  • 需要教师和参考两者的 rollout；在无 rollout 时无法应用。
    Requires rollouts from both teacher and reference; inapplicable when no rollouts are available.
  • 代码尚未发布，限制了可复现性。
    Code release pending, limiting reproducibility.
