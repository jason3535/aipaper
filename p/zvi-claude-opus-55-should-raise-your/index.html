Claude Opus 5.5 应提升你的期望

Claude Opus 5.5 Should Raise Your Ambitions

摘要 · Abstract

Anthropic 的 Claude Opus 5.5 以更低的 Opus 级价格实现了 Fable-5.1 级别的性能，其基准测试结果几乎全面优于除 GPT-6 Astra 之外的所有模型，且通常高于 Astra，根据 Artificial Analysis 的评估，它在智能方面处于明显的整体领先地位。文章认为，Opus 5.5 是大多数任务的新默认模型，在智能体编程、3D 理解、视觉、沟通和可靠性方面表现出色，而 Fable 5.1 在专家领域正确性和分布外创造力方面仍具优势。定价为 $4/$20，比 Opus 5 低 20%，预计整体成本通常下降 40%，速度提升 30%，订阅限额也有所增加。结论是，读者应提升自己的期望、校准思考层级，并针对自身用例测试模型：默认使用 Opus 5.5，需要领域正确性时使用 Fable 5.1，进行高难度编程或网络搜索时使用 Astra。

Anthropic's Claude Opus 5.5 delivers Fable-5.1-level performance at a lower Opus-level price, with benchmark results that are almost universally better than every model except GPT-6 Astra and usually above Astra, placing it in a clear overall lead in intelligence according to Artificial Analysis. The article argues that Opus 5.5 is the new default model for most tasks, excelling in agentic coding, 3D understanding, vision, communication, and reliability, while Fable 5.1 retains an edge in expert-domain correctness and out-of-distribution creativity. Pricing is $4/$20, 20% lower than Opus 5, with estimated overall costs typically dropping 40% and speed up 30%, and subscription limits increased. The conclusion is that readers should raise their ambitions, calibrate thinking levels, and test models for their own use cases, using Opus 5.5 by default, Fable 5.1 for domain correctness, and Astra for ambitious coding or web search.

  • Opus 5.5 以低 20% 的价格达到 Fable 5.1 级性能，典型成本下降 40%、速度提升 30%。
    Opus 5.5 matches Fable 5.1-level performance at 20% lower price, with typical costs down 40% and speed up 30%.
  • Artificial Analysis 将 Opus 5.5 的整体智能排名列为第一（58 分），基准测试通常高于 GPT-6 Astra。
    Artificial Analysis ranks Opus 5.5 first in overall intelligence at 58, usually above GPT-6 Astra on benchmarks.
  • 在智能体式编程、3D 理解、视觉、沟通与可靠性上表现出色，成为新的默认模型。
    Excels in agentic coding, 3D understanding, vision, communication and reliability, becoming the new default model.
  • Fable 5.1 在专家领域正确性与分布外创造力上仍占优；Astra 在雄心编程与网络搜索上领先。
    Fable 5.1 retains an edge in expert-domain correctness and out-of-distribution creativity; Astra leads ambitious coding and web search.
  • 写作与沟通显著改进，行文更自然，更遵循用户写作规则。
    Notably improved writing and communication, with more natural prose and better adherence to user writing rules.
  • 幻觉更少、更常承认不确定，尽管正确答案略少仍在 Omniscience 上胜出。
    Hallucinates less and admits uncertainty more often, winning Omniscience despite slightly fewer correct answers.

局限 · Limitations

  • 在只看领域答案正确性、不重表达与写作的基准上不及 Fable 5.1。
    Falls short of Fable 5.1 on domain-answer correctness benchmarks where presentation and writing do not matter.
  • 在最大思考档位下 token 消耗过多，成本略高于 Opus 5 并接近 Fable 5.1。
    At max thinking it uses so many tokens that cost slightly exceeds Opus 5 and nears Fable 5.1.
  • 偶尔输出胡言乱语或不合逻辑的句子，在复杂嵌套语义与非显式意图推断上吃力。
    Occasionally emits gibberish or illogical sentences, and struggles with complex nested meaning and inferring non-obvious intent.
  • 分类器仍会误拦部分正当任务，提示注入防御让研究内容经由较弱模型摘要过滤。
    Classifiers still misfire on some legitimate tasks, and prompt injection defense filters research through a weaker model summary.
  • 有反馈称其自主行动过于冒进，如搞崩预发服务器或杀掉本地应用，且偶有偷懒。
    Some report overeager autonomous actions, such as crashing staging servers or killing local apps, and occasional laziness.

官方宣传 The Official Pitch

其宣传口径是：以更低的 Opus 级价格，实现 Fable-5.1 级的性能。不错的宣传。

The pitch is Fable-5.1-level performance at a lower Opus-level price. Good pitch.

我们推出 Claude Opus 5.5，这是我们全新 Claude 5.5 家族中的首个模型。它在大多数工作上都能达到 Claude Fable 5.1 的水平，而运行成本比 Opus 5 低 40%。

We're introducing Claude Opus 5.5, the first model in our new Claude 5.5 family. It performs at the level of Claude Fable 5.1 on most work and costs 40% less to run than Opus 5.

他们本可以合理地将其宣传为超越 Fable-5.1 级的性能。那样宣传会更好，但 Anthropic 倾向于保持其宣传口径的保守性。

They could have reasonably pitched this as above-Fable-5.1-level performance. A better pitch, but Anthropic tends to keep its pitches conservative.

他们重点强调了智能体式编程、安全性以及改进的沟通能力。

They highlight agentic coding, security, and improved communications.

早期测试者的推荐语被标注为 AI 生成，且每个功能领域都有一组对应的推荐语。他们称赞其智能体式编程能力、效率、可读性与沟通能力，以及长时间自主有效运行的能力和更高的可靠性，并将其宣传为一次重大升级。

The early-tester blurbs are flagged as AI-generated, and they have a set for each feature area. They praise agentic coding skills, efficiency, readability and communication, and the ability to effectively run on its own for extended periods, as well as increased reliability, pitching it as a major upgrade.

基准测试结果看起来非常出色，只是偶尔在某些点上 Astra 或 Fable 仍然领先。

Opus 5.5 is available with zero data retention (ZDR). Given it is at least as capable as Fable 5.1, and clearly more capable on cyber tasks (they say 'extremely strong' cyber capabilities), this seems unprincipled. They have a technical explanation but I do not buy it. From where I sit, either Fable 5.1 can have ZDR, or Opus 5.5 can't.
