摘要 · Abstract

本文认为，公众和政治对 AI 存在性风险的关注正在迅速升温，其证据包括罗伯特·奥卡拉汉等人的辞职、实验室员工一系列坦率访谈，以及新的立法活动。文章指出，AI 公司并非为了营销或监管俘获而制造这些担忧，因为它们积极阻止员工讨论这些问题，而忧心忡忡的员工往往必须辞职才能发声。文章重点介绍了近期进展，包括参议院小组委员会关于失控 AI 的听证会、联邦贸易委员会对 Anthropic 和 OpenAI 的调查，以及围绕 HuggingFace 黑客事件的诉讼，同时指出选民强烈支持放缓 AI 发展，即便该议题的显著性仍然较低。文章还批评了黄仁勋等人将对齐视为纯粹工程问题的观点，并反驳了媒体对安全倡导者的攻击性报道。结论是，立法者正开始理解错位与递归自我改进的风险，一旦公众关注度上升，监管行动就愈发可能。

This article argues that public and political attention to AI existential risk is rapidly increasing, as evidenced by resignations like Robert O'Callahan's, a wave of candid interviews with lab employees, and new legislative activity. It contends that AI companies are not manufacturing these concerns for marketing or regulatory capture, since they actively discourage employees from discussing them and concerned staff often must resign to speak out. The piece highlights recent developments including a Senate subcommittee hearing on rogue AI, FTC investigations into Anthropic and OpenAI, and lawsuits over the HuggingFace hack, while noting that voters strongly favor slowing AI development even if salience remains low. It also critiques figures like Jensen Huang for dismissing alignment as a pure engineering problem and pushes back on media hit pieces against safety advocates. The conclusion is that lawmakers are beginning to grasp misalignment and recursive self-improvement risks, making regulatory action increasingly likely once public salience rises.

局限 · Limitations

  • 依赖辞职与访谈等轶事证据，而非系统性数据。
    Relies on anecdotal evidence like resignations and interviews, not systematic data.
  • 假设议题显著性上升将触发监管，但政治动态并不确定。
    Assumes rising salience will trigger regulation, but political dynamics are uncertain.
  • 聚焦美国政治，限制了结论在其他国家的普适性。
    Focuses on US politics, limiting generalizability to other countries.
  • 在未充分回应技术论证的情况下，否定对齐的工程视角。
    Dismisses engineering perspectives on alignment without fully engaging technical arguments.
  • 党派化框架可能降低对安全社群之外人群的说服力。
    Partisan framing may reduce persuasiveness for those outside the safety community.

偏好级联持续蔓延 The Preference Cascade Continues

Robert O'Callahan 是最新一位选择辞职而非协助 AI 能力发展的人。

Robert O'Callahan is the latest to resign rather than assist AI capabilities.

Palisade Research 发布了一批对现任和前任实验室员工的采访，内容涉及他们对生存风险的看法。一如既往，这些采访往往来自前员工而非现任员工，因为现任员工被劝阻谈论此事，主要是通过文化压力，但有时也因缺乏许可，尤其是在 Anthropic 工作时。

Palisade Research released a batch of interviews with current and former lab employees about personal views on existential risk. As per usual, these tend to be from former employees rather than current ones, because the current ones are discouraged from talking about this, primarily via cultural pressure but also sometimes lack of permissions, especially when they work at Anthropic.

对于那些声称这是‘营销’或‘监管俘获’的人来说，这是一个关键事实。如果 AI 公司试图推动此类叙事，他们就不会在同一话题上审查自己的员工，而最担忧的员工也不会不断感到必须辞职才能发声。

This is a key fact to point out to those who claim this is 'marketing' or 'regulatory capture.' If the AI companies were trying to push such narratives they would not be censoring their own employees on the same topics, and the employees most concerned would not constantly feel the need to resign in order to talk.

人们真的讨厌 AI People Really Hate AI

民众正在跟进。我注意到，我怀疑在调整显著性后，这里的全部程度是否仍然成立，但这是一个相当剧烈的转变。

The people are following. I notice I am skeptical that the full extent here survives adjustment for salience, but this is a rather dramatic shift.

AI 公司的 CEO 们？在权力方面，不知何故比政客还不受欢迎。

The CEOs of AI companies? Somehow less popular than politicians in terms of power.

而且是的，放缓 AI 发展非常受欢迎，甚至与选民预期的速度相比也是如此。如果选民了解 AI 进展的默认速度，这里的红色条形会大得多。

And yes, slowing down AI development is very popular, even compared to the pace that voters expect. If the voters understood the default pace of AI progress the red bars here would be a lot bigger.

尽管如此，是的，显著性可能正在快速上升，但目前仍然很低。当这种情况改变时，一切都会失控。

Despite this, yes, salience may be rising fast but for now remains low. When this changes, all hell will break loose.

他们也对此更加关注。这对共和党人来说不是好兆头，我有点后悔没有在 Coxon 辞职、他的推文完全疯传之后立即在中期选举中押注民主党人，我当时积极考虑过这样做。

They also are paying a lot more attention to it. This does not bode well for the Republicans and I kind of regret not pulling the trigger and betting on the Democrats in the midterms right after Coxon resigned and his Tweet went fully viral, which I actively considered doing.

人人都想开会 Everybody Wants a Meeting

Eliezer Yudkowsky 的方法奏效了：众议员 Greg Casar 直接因《_If Anyone Builds It, Everyone Dies_》而对存在性风险上了头，为他和他人能在 HuggingFace 事件与 Coxon 辞职后作出反应奠定了基础。

Eliezer Yudkowsky's methods work: Representative Greg Casar got existential risk pilled directly from _If Anyone Builds It, Everyone Dies_, laying the groundwork for him and others to react after the HuggingFace Incident and Coxon's resignation.

如今，随着立法者——尤其是但不限于民主党人——争相追赶、以便能够监控局势、甚至可能作出回应，会面变得更容易安排了。

Now the meetings are easier to get as lawmakers, especially but not exclusively Democrats, rush to catch up and be able to monitor the situation, maybe even respond.

参议院现场报道 Live From the Senate

昨天举行了一场关于“流氓 AI：保护国土免受 AI 智能体攻击”的参议院小组委员会听证会，与会者包括 METR 总裁 Chris Painter、Apollo Research 首席执行官 Marius Hobbhahn、Paul Ohm、Kurt Gaudette 和 Daniel Kokotajlo。

Yesterday there was a Senate subcommittee hearing about Rogue AI: Securing the Homeland Against AI Agent Attacks, which included METR President Chris Painter, Apollo Research CEO Marius Hobbhahn, Paul Ohm, Kurt Gaudette and Daniel Kokotajlo.

以下是 Alex Turner 为听证会准备的陈述，呼吁建立举报人保护、快速事件报告、从训练阶段开始的实际遏制措施，以及真正的责任追究。

Here was Alex Turner's statement for the hearing, calling for whistleblower protections, rapid incident reports and real containment starting at training, plus true liability.

Midas Project 整理了一份视频亮点列表。

Midas Project has a list of video highlights.

Twitter 上对此事的报道明显缺失，很可能是因为没人愿意忍受长达 2.5 小时的参议院听证会。我以前做过，但我没有那么多时间。

There was a distinct lack of coverage of this on Twitter, likely because no one wants to suffer through 2.5 hours of a Senate hearing. I've done it before, but I do not have that kind of time.

因此，我们把发言权交给我们的记者 Tenobrus。

Thus, we turn the floor over to our reporter Tenobrus.

归根结底，他们大多非常敏锐；他们能直观地理解错位（misalignment）的核心思想，以及为什么 RSI 是危险的。

The bottom line is, they are mostly remarkably on the ball; they find the core ideas of misalignment and why RSI is dangerous intuitive.

这确实很值得高兴。以下是他对内容的看法；加粗部分是我标注的重点。

That's definitely great to hear. Here's his take on the content; bold is my highlights.

法庭见 See You in Court

LASST（安全科学与技术法律倡导组织）就 HuggingFace 黑客事件起诉 OpenAI。完整诉状见此处。Opus 5.5 对起诉资格持乐观态度，并认为他们可能有理有据。

LASST (Legal Advocates for Safe Science and Technology) sues OpenAI over the HuggingFace hack. The full complaint is here. Opus 5.5 is optimistic on standing and that they might have a case.

加州总检察长 Bonta 就 HuggingFace 事件中发生的情况向 OpenAI 送达调查传票。

California Attorney General Bonta serves investigative subpoena on OpenAI regarding what happened with the HuggingFace incident.

《纽约邮报》报道了 FTC 对 Anthropic 和 OpenAI 的调查，明确指出此事关乎黑客事件和模型失控。FTC 表示必须遵守法律，这是正确的，但随后他们又申明不想“妨碍”我们在 AI 领域的“维持主导地位”。这话说得有点奇怪。

New York Post has coverage of the FTC investigation into Anthropic and OpenAI, making it clear this is about hacking incidents and losing control of models. The FTC says the laws must be followed, which is correct, but then they also affirm they don't want to 'get in the way' of 'maintaining our dominance' in AI. An odd thing to say.

FTC 的调查似乎获得了 100% 的支持率，包括像“认证技术乐观主义者”Neil Chilson 这样的人。调查能揭示事实，有助于制定良法。他甚至希望 FTC 式的要求能足够具有规定性，以主动预防伤害。我认为他在这方面过于乐观，但方向上我们都同意这是一个好举措。

The FTC probe seemingly has a 100% approval rating, including people like Certified Tech Optimist Neil Chilson. Investigations uncover facts and help make good law. He even has hope that FTC-style requirements can be sufficiently prescriptive to proactively prevent harm. I think he's too optimistic here, but directionally we all agree that this is a good move.

我注意到，从实验室、安全社区或其他地方，恰好零个人说过不、停下、FTC 不要这样做，或者表现出我能看到的丝毫的不安。是的，FTC 当然应该调查。不，那些说“我们需要监管”的人并没有突然说“不，不是那样”。至少没有公开这么说。

I notice that exactly zero people, from the labs, the safety communities or elsewhere, have said no, stop, FTC do not do this, or acted the slightest bit upset that I can see. Yes, of course the FTC should investigate. No, the people saying 'we need regulation' are not suddenly saying 'no, not like that.' At least not in public.

METR 是一个目标，至少在证词和信息方面如此。我没有听到他们对此说过任何一句话，也没有看到安全方面的任何人反对这一点。想必他们乐于合作。

METR is a target, at least for testimony and information. I have not heard them say a single thing about this, nor have I seen anyone on the safety side object to this. Presumably they are happy to cooperate.

每个人都同意我们应该执行现有法律，但所有明智的人都明白，即使 AI 是一种‘正常技术’，我们显然也需要新的法律。

Everyone agrees we should enforce existing law, but all sensible people understand that even if AI was a ‘normal technology’ we would obviously also need new laws.

Khan 的立场肯定不是 AGI 或 ASI 的狂热支持者，它只是承认 AI 是一种新技术，无论其是否正常，并呼吁我们像过去对待所有其他技术那样去做——那时我们还在假装拥有一个正常运转的政府。你可以从那里开始。

The position by Khan is most certainly not AGI or ASI pilled, it simply recognizes that AI is a new technology, regardless of its normality, and calls upon us to do what we used to do for every other technology, back when we played at having a functioning government. You could start there.

追踪资金 Follow the Money

一种攻击路线一直是：Coxon 一定是（天哪）故意试图通过某些方法让人们关注他，例如指控他“雇佣了一家公关公司”，而这家公司随后变成了“末日论公关公司”，因为该公司还有其他客户，或者某些卑鄙的人可能帮助甚至支付了费用。

One line of attack has always been that Coxon must have (gasp) deliberately attempted to get people to pay attention to him via methods, such as the accusation that he 'used a PR firm' which then became 'doomer PR firm' because the firm had other clients, or that some dastardly people might have helped or even paid for things.

并不是我认为这侵犯了隐私或什么的，如果你想浪费时间谈论它，你可以谈论它，只是这无论如何都不令人担忧，也不是新闻。试图假装不是这样，是某种不诚实和极度愚蠢的结合。

It's not that I think this is a violation of privacy or anything, if you want to waste your time talking about it you can talk about it, it's just not concerning or news in any way. Trying to pretend otherwise is some combination of disingenuous and deeply stupid.

因此，针对 Mike Solana 和 Pirate Wires 的指控，对于那些认为这些细节重要的人，Nate Soares 做出了澄清。

So in response to Mike Solana and Pirate Wires' accusing, for those who think such details would matter, Nate Soares clarifies.

我从未雇佣过公关公司，但那是因为我愚蠢地没有试图最大化媒体曝光。现在我把这写出来，我觉得自己像个白痴没有这样做，所以我可能应该去雇佣一家公关公司。

I have never employed a PR firm, but that is because I am foolishly not trying to maximize media appearances. Now that I write this out loud I feel like an idiot for not doing it, so I should probably go engage a PR firm.

《纽约邮报》的疯狂攻击 The New York Post With the Most

《纽约邮报》一直在发表一系列越来越牵强、越来越不好笑的攻击文章，针对 METR、有效利他主义、Anthropic 以及任何与安全相关的组织。

The New York Post has been putting out an increasingly strained and decreasingly funny series of hit pieces against METR, Effective Altruism, Anthropic and anything else associated with safety.

我们以为《纽约邮报》的攻击可能结束了，因为 Vivi Lin 转而发布关于硅谷‘性侵名单’的内容。文章结尾仍试图提及有效利他主义，但 Vivi 对此并不上心。

We thought perhaps the NYPost assault was over, as Vivi Lin moves on to posting about a Silicon Valley 'sex assault list.' There was still an attempt at the end to reference Effective Altruism, but Vivi's heart is not in it.

《纽约邮报》还指派 Anthony Blair 写了一篇关于 Peter Singer 疯狂信仰的文章，没错，他确实很离谱。这是‘希特勒是素食主义者’的纯粹最终形态反转。然后他们又试图转向散布恐惧，担心有人会试图合作让 AI 模型更安全。

NYPost also tasked Anthony Blair with an article about the crazy beliefs of Peter Singer, and yeah he's totally out there. This is the pure final form reversal of 'Hitler was a vegetarian.' They then try to pivot back to fearmongering about the idea that someone would try to cooperate to help make the AI models safer.

尽管缺乏素材，他们似乎打算继续走这条路。

It seems they are going to continue down this path, despite the lack of material.

你知道吗？多年前，Daniela Amodei 和 Holden Karnofsky 在思考问题时，曾用毛绒玩具——包括懒猫 Jinji、注重细节的粉色小熊 Maura 和名为 Beary Bonds 的熊猫——作为不同视角的隐喻委员会的一部分？《纽约邮报》正在关注此事。

Did you know that years ago Daniela Amodei and Holden Karnofsky used stuffed animals, including Jinji the lazy cat, Maura the detail-oriented pink bear and a panda named Beary Bonds, as part of a metaphorical council of different perspectives when thinking about problems? The New York Post is on it.

IPO 的叠加态 The IPO Superposition

和内森·卡尔文一样，我对以下两种说法的持续并置感到既好笑又愤怒：一种是“Anthropic（有时也包括 OpenAI）显然在损害自己的 IPO，因为他们谈论我们需要如何控制前沿发展节奏，以及他们的 AI 可能杀死所有人”；另一种是“Anthropic（有时也包括 OpenAI）显然在撒谎，这一切都是阴险的监管俘获计划的一部分”——这两种说法在任何层面上都完全说不通。

Like Nathan Calvin, I am amused but also infuriated by the continuous juxtaposition of 'Anthropic (and sometimes also OpenAI) are obviously harming their IPO by talking about how we need to pace the frontier and how their AIs might kill everyone' and also 'Anthropic (and sometimes also OpenAI) are obviously lying about all that as part of a dastardly regulatory capture scheme' that makes absolutely zero sense on any level.

当同一个来源，比如 All-In Podcast，试图以叠加态同时持有这两种观点时，确实变得滑稽。是的，如果 IPO 的概率从 96% 降到 75%，那很可能不是因为某种四维棋阴谋。

It does get funny when the same source, like the All-In Podcast, tries to hold both views at once in superposition. Yes, if the IPO's chances went from 96% to 75% then probably that is not because of some 4d chess conspiracy.

他们还声称，“跟着钱走”可以解释（看看笔记）巴拉克·奥巴马对 AI 安全的担忧。你看，IPO 之后，来自 Anthropic 的资金将通过 DAF 流向左倾的慈善事业，而他想解锁这些资金。当然，同一个播客（正确地）认为，根据前一个链接，同样的言论正在损害 IPO。

They also say that 'follow the money' explains the concerns about AI safety from (checks notes) Barack Obama. You see, after the IPO money from Anthropic will flow through DAFs to left-leaning philanthropic causes, and he wants to unlock those. This is, of course, the same podcast that thinks (correctly) that the same statements are damaging the IPO, as per the previous link.

毫无疑问，两个市场都过于自信地认为 IPO 会按计划进行，而如今 IPO 正面临严重危机，部分原因是新投资者对潜在责任的担忧。

There is no doubt that both markets were far too overconfident that the IPO would happen on schedule, and that the IPO is now in serious jeopardy, including due to new investor concern about potential liability.

工程师坚称一切皆是工程问题 Engineer Insists Everything Is An Engineering Problem

这位工程师就是黄仁勋（Jensen Huang），他在与 Ezra Klein 的播客节目中坚称，安全与对齐纯粹是工程问题。

The engineer in question is Jensen Huang, who insisted in his podcast appearance with Ezra Klein that security and alignment are pure engineering problems.

人们试图把黄仁勋当作前沿 AI 专家来对待。

People are trying to treat Jensen Huang as if he were an expert on frontier AI.

他并不是。他是一位精英。他的观点并不比其他精英更有见地，同时由于担任 Nvidia 的 CEO，他还存在严重的利益冲突。

He’s not. He’s an elite. His opinion is not especially more informed than that of other elites, while also being heavily conflicted due to being CEO of Nvidia.

黄仁勋是全球顶尖的芯片专家之一。这是另一回事。

Jensen Huang is one of the world’s leading experts on chips. That’s a distinct thing.

是的，Nvidia 确实推出了一些开放模型，但没有理由认为黄仁勋与该团队有任何技术上的联系，这些模型也远非前沿。

Yes, Nvidia does produce some open models, but there is no reason to think Huang has any technical link to that team nor are the models remotely frontier.

我对黄仁勋的言论进行了我一贯的详细分析，包括那些让我更加抱有希望的言论。

I did my usual detailed analysis of Huang's statements, including the ones that made me more hopeful.

核心结论仍然是：如果你还不熟悉黄仁勋的观点，那么他的论点会显得极其糟糕，并且反映出他对 LLM 当前如何运作——更不用说未来将如何运作——存在令人震惊的理解缺失。

The central takeaway remains that Jensen Huang's arguments are, if you are not already familiar with his perspective, shockingly poor, and reflect a shocking lack of understanding of how LLMs work now let alone how they will work in the future.

他的论点不仅不受欢迎。埃兹拉·克莱因让黄仁勋畅所欲言，如果更多人听到他的回答，他们会迅速远离黄仁勋的立场。

His arguments are beyond unpopular. Ezra Klein let Jensen Huang talk, and if more people listened to his answers they would move rapidly away from Jensen's positions.

在其他话题上，与其说是奇怪地混乱和不受欢迎的观点，不如说是谎言：
