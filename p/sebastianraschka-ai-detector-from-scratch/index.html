从零构建 AI 文本检测器

Building an AI Text Detector From Scratch

摘要 · Abstract

本文提供了一个从零构建简单 AI 文本检测器的实战教程，灵感来源于 Substack 的 AI 检测功能。作者的核心目标是通过开发一个返回 0-100 分（表示 AI 生成内容可能性）的分类器，来解释 AI 检测器的工作原理。项目涉及在人类与 AI 写作文本数据集上微调 DistilBERT 模型，并探索将检测器用作验证器，训练小型语言模型生成可规避检测的文本。文章涵盖了整个流程，包括数据准备、模型训练、评估以及通过 API 和用户友好界面进行本地部署。作者强调，AI 检测是一场猫鼠游戏，存在误报等局限性，需要持续更新。读者将获得构建基于验证器的 LLM 应用的实用见解，并理解 AI 文本检测中的权衡与挑战。

This article presents a hands-on tutorial for building a simple AI text detector from scratch, inspired by Substack's AI detection feature. The author's core goal is to explain how AI detectors work by developing a functional classifier that returns a 0-100 score indicating the likelihood of AI-generated content. The project involves fine-tuning a DistilBERT model on a dataset of human and AI-written texts, and it also explores using the detector as a verifier to train a small language model to produce text that avoids detection. The article covers the entire pipeline, including data preparation, model training, evaluation, and local deployment via an API and user-friendly UI. The author emphasizes that AI detection is a cat-and-mouse game, with limitations such as false positives and the need for continuous updates. Readers will gain practical insights into building a verifier-based LLM application and understand the trade-offs and challenges in AI text detection.

局限 · Limitations

  • AI 检测器容易产生误报，将人类撰写的文本标记为 AI 生成。
    AI detectors are prone to false positives, flagging human-written text as AI-generated.
  • 随着新的 LLM 避免已知模式，检测器可能过时，需要持续更新。
    Detectors may become obsolete as new LLMs avoid known patterns, requiring continuous updates.
  • 分类器的分数基于训练分布，并非 AI 作者身份的真实概率。
    The classifier's score is based on training distribution, not a true probability of AI authorship.
  • 教程中的检测器简单，可能无法泛化到多样化的写作风格或领域。
    The tutorial's detector is simple and may not generalize to diverse writing styles or domains.
  • 基于验证器的训练可能导致文本过度优化以逃避检测，可能降低质量。
    The verifier-based training may lead to text that is overly optimized to evade detection, potentially reducing quality.
