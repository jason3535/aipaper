在前沿工作塑造对未来的预期

Working at the frontier shapes expectations for the future

摘要 · Abstract

本文报告了两项研究，分别考察 AI 如何重塑 Anthropic 内部的工作方式，以及公众与 Anthropic 员工对 AI 经济影响的预期差异。在内部，Anthropic 已将招聘重心从初级和技术岗位转移，同时 AI 智能体的工作时长激增，到 2026 年 8 月智能体数量已是员工的六倍，编码、销售和合同处理的生产率随之上升。随着 AI 采用加速，人类的注意力成为瓶颈，员工不得不花更多时间监督智能体的工作。第二项研究将 Anthropic 员工与近 1.1 万名美国成年人的代表性调查进行比较，发现员工预期的 AI 进展远快于公众——91%对 39%的人认为到 2030 年会出现诺贝尔奖级别的发现，超过半数员工对 5%的美国人认为 AI 能承担其当前至少 80%的工作。然而，两组人群在将 AI 能力与失业预期相联系的经济模型上却相似。作者得出结论：驱动未来经济预测分歧的是个人与 AI 互动的体验差异，而非理论差异；Anthropic 或可作为更广泛劳动力市场变化的先行指标。

This article reports on two studies examining how AI is reshaping work inside Anthropic and how expectations about AI's economic impact differ between the public and Anthropic employees. Internally, Anthropic has shifted hiring away from junior and technical roles while AI agents' working hours have surged, with agents outnumbering employees six to one by August 2026 and productivity rising in coding, sales, and contract processing. As AI adoption accelerates, human attention has become the bottleneck, leading employees to spend more time monitoring agent work. The second study compares Anthropic staff with a representative survey of nearly 11,000 US adults, finding that staff expect far faster AI progress than the public—91% versus 39% expect Nobel-level discoveries by 2030, and over half of staff versus 5% of Americans expect AI to handle at least 80% of their current work. Yet both groups share similar economic models linking AI capability to unemployment expectations. The authors conclude that differing personal experiences with AI, rather than differing theories, drive divergent forecasts about the future economy, and that Anthropic may serve as a leading indicator of broader labor market shifts.

局限 · Limitations

  • 结论来自单一 AI 实验室，难以推广到传统企业与更广泛经济。
    Findings come from one AI lab, limiting generalization to traditional firms and the broader economy.
  • 人均代码量等生产力指标被承认并不完善。
    Code-per-person and similar productivity metrics are acknowledged as imperfect measures.
  • 员工与公众调查是自报预期，而非观测到的劳动力市场结果。
    Employee and public surveys are self-reported expectations, not observed labor market outcomes.
  • 智能体工时与人数对比可能不反映真实经济产出或价值。
    Agent working hours and headcount comparisons may not reflect real economic output or value.

概述 Overview

我们分享两份报告：第一份报告探讨 AI 如何改变 Anthropic 内部的就业情况，第二份报告则考察公众与 Anthropic 员工对 AI 将如何影响工作与经济的预期之间的差距。

We're sharing two reports: the first covers how AI is changing employment inside Anthropic, and the second looks at the gap between how the public and Anthropic employees expect AI to affect work and the economy.

Anthropic 员工与美国劳动者对 AI 如何影响就业的看法似乎并无不同。相反，他们对 AI 能力究竟是什么有着不同的体验。这些不同的体验似乎与他们对未来工作走向的认知密切相关。

Anthropic staff and US workers don't seem to have different theories about how AI affects jobs. Instead, they have different experiences of what AI capabilities are. Those different experiences seem closely related to perceptions about what will happen to work in the future.

Anthropic 是一家处于 AI 应用前沿的公司，而这种应用已经改变了我们的招聘方式。我们发现，在过去一年里，我们招聘初级岗位的比例有所下降，从事技术岗位的员工比例也有所下降。与此同时，AI 智能体工作小时数的增速急剧上升。到 2026 年 3 月，智能体工作的小时数已超过员工。到 8 月，Anthropic 的智能体数量已是员工数量的六倍，生产力也随之提升。

Anthropic is a company at the frontier of AI adoption, and that adoption has changed the way we hire. We find that over the past year, we're hiring for a smaller share of junior positions, and a smaller share of our employees work in technical roles. At the same time, the rate of hours worked by AI agents has shot up. By March 2026, agents worked more hours than employees. By August, there were six times more agents than employees at Anthropic, and productivity increased.

这使 Anthropic 的人均代码产出增加，但这一指标众所周知并不完美。我们在其他方面也看到了生产力提升的迹象：每位销售代表完成的销售额更多，处理的合同也更多。当我们的研究人员对同一名员工进行长期跟踪时，他们发现，随着该员工对 AI 工具的使用增加，其产出也随之上升。

That's led to Anthropic producing more code per person, but that's a notoriously imperfect measure. We see signs of increased productivity elsewhere: we've closed more sales per sales representative, and processed more contracts. When our researchers follow the same employee over time, they find the employee's output rises as their adoption of AI tools increases.

所有这些加速意味着，瓶颈正日益变成人类的注意力。因此，员工花更多时间构建各种方法，以更好地监督其智能体所做的工作。

All this acceleration means that, increasingly, the bottleneck is human attention. As a result, employees spend more time building ways to better monitor work their agents are doing.

Anthropic 可能是 AI 将如何改变整个经济中的工作组织、招聘和劳动力需求的一个领先指标。AI 实验室在将 AI 融入工作方面与更传统的企业不同，但关于我们自己工作场所的研究发现，对于更广泛的转型——包括未来 AI 原生公司的动态——可能仍具有启发意义。

Anthropic could be a leading indicator of how AI will shift work organization, hiring, and labor demand across the economy. An AI lab is different from more traditional businesses in how it adopts AI into its work, but research findings about our own workplace may still be instructive about broader transformations, including dynamics for future AI-native companies.

由于 Anthropic 员工对 AI 对工作的影响体验如此不同，因此可以推断，他们对 AI 对工作 _未来_ 的影响可能有不同的预测。这正是我们的第二份报告所调查的内容。我们调查了 Anthropic 员工，并将他们与 Morning Consult 在 8 月进行的一项调查进行了比较，该调查涵盖了近 11,000 名美国成年人，并进行了加权以代表成年人口。

Because Anthropic employees experience AI's effect on work so differently, it stands to reason that they might have different predictions about AI's effect on the _future_ of work. That's what our second report investigates. We surveyed Anthropic employees, and compared them to an August survey of almost 11,000 US adults, fielded by Morning Consult and weighted to be representative of the adult population.

公众预计 AI 能力在未来几年将迅速提升；Anthropic 员工则倾向于预期更快的进展。有许多问题在员工和公众之间存在巨大差距。例如，39% 的美国成年人和 91% 的员工预计，到 2030 年 AI 将能够做出诺贝尔奖级别的发现。超过一半的 Anthropic 员工预计，到 2030 年 AI 将能够完成他们当前至少 80% 的工作；只有 5% 的美国人预计 AI 会有这样的能力。

The public expects AI capabilities to advance rapidly in the coming years; Anthropic staff tend to expect even faster progress. There are a number of questions with big gaps between staff and the public. For example, 39% of US adults—and 91% of staff—expect AI will be able to make a Nobel-level discovery by 2030. More than half of Anthropic staff expect AI to be able to do at least 80% of their current work by 2030; only 5% of Americans expect AI to be that capable.

尽管如此，两个群体内部都存在相当大的差异：十分之二的 Anthropic 员工的期望与典型美国成年人相似。而且 Anthropic 员工和公众在某些关键问题上也意见一致。在每个分组中，那些预计 AI 能完成自己更多工作的人，也预期失业率会有类似的上升。换句话说，公众和 Anthropic 员工对 AI 如何影响劳动力市场有着相似的经济模型，但最终他们与 AI 的个人经历导致他们对未来经济有不同的期望。

That said, there's considerable variation in both groups: two out of 10 Anthropic staff have expectations similar to those of the typical US adult. And Anthropic staff and the public also agree on some key questions. In each bucket, people who expect AI to do comparably more of their own work anticipate similar increases in unemployment. In other words, both the public and Anthropic staff have similar economic models of how AI affects the labor market, but ultimately their personal experiences with AI lead them to different expectations for the economy of the future.
