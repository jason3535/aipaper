Gemini 2.5: Pushing the Frontier with Advanced Reasoning, Multimodality, Long Context, and Next Generation Agentic Capabilities

摘要 · Abstract

本报告介绍了 Gemini 2.X 模型系列：Gemini 2.5 Pro 和 Gemini 2.5 Flash，以及早前的 Gemini 2.0 Flash 和 Flash-Lite 模型。Gemini 2.5 Pro 是我们迄今最强大的模型，在前沿编码和推理基准上取得了最先进的性能。除了惊人的编码和推理能力外，Gemini 2.5 Pro 还是一个擅长多模态理解的思考模型，现可处理长达 3 小时的视频内容。其长上下文、多模态和推理能力的独特结合可解锁新的智能体工作流。Gemini 2.5 Flash 以极低的计算和延迟需求提供了出色的推理能力，而 Gemini 2.0 Flash 和 Flash-Lite 则以低延迟和低成本提供高性能。总体而言，Gemini 2.X 模型系列覆盖了模型能力与成本的完整帕累托前沿，让用户能够探索复杂智能体问题解决的边界。

In this report, we introduce the Gemini 2.X model family: Gemini 2.5 Pro and Gemini 2.5 Flash, as well as our earlier Gemini 2.0 Flash and Flash-Lite models. Gemini 2.5 Pro is our most capable model yet, achieving SoTA performance on frontier coding and reasoning benchmarks. In addition to its incredible coding and reasoning skills, Gemini 2.5 Pro is a thinking model that excels at multimodal understanding and it is now able to process up to 3 hours of video content. Its unique combination of long context, multimodal and reasoning capabilities can be combined to unlock new agentic workflows. Gemini 2.5 Flash provides excellent reasoning abilities at a fraction of the compute and latency requirements and Gemini 2.0 Flash and Flash-Lite provide high performance at low latency and cost. Taken together, the Gemini 2.X model generation spans the full Pareto frontier of model capability vs cost, allowing users to explore the boundaries of what is possible with complex agentic problem solving.

核心贡献 · Key contributions

  • 推出 Gemini 2.X 模型系列，覆盖从 2.5 Pro 到 Flash-Lite 的能力与成本完整帕累托前沿。
    Introduces the Gemini 2.X model family spanning the full Pareto frontier of capability versus cost, from 2.5 Pro to Flash-Lite.
  • Gemini 2.5 Pro 在前沿编码与推理基准上取得最先进结果，包括 Aider Polyglot、GPQA（diamond）和 Humanity's Last Exam。
    Gemini 2.5 Pro achieves state-of-the-art results on frontier coding and reasoning benchmarks including Aider Polyglot, GPQA diamond, and Humanity's Last Exam.
  • Gemini 2.5 Pro 是一款具备强大多模态理解能力的思考模型，可处理长达三小时的视频内容。
    Gemini 2.5 Pro is a thinking model with strong multimodal understanding, capable of processing up to three hours of video content.
  • 长上下文、多模态理解与推理能力的结合解锁了新的智能体式工作流，并已驱动众多 Google 产品。
    The combination of long context, multimodal understanding, and reasoning unlocks new agentic workflows and powers numerous Google products.
  • Gemini 2.5 Flash 以更低的算力与延迟实现强大推理能力，而 2.0 Flash 与 Flash-Lite 则提供高效低成本性能。
    Gemini 2.5 Flash delivers strong reasoning at reduced compute and latency, while 2.0 Flash and Flash-Lite offer efficient low-cost performance.
  • Gemini 2.5 在保持强安全标准的同时更加有用，更少拒绝用户查询或给出高高在上的说教式回答。
    Gemini 2.5 maintains strong safety standards while becoming more helpful and less likely to refuse user queries or respond sanctimoniously.

局限 · Limitations

  • 基准测试迅速饱和，且由于专家作者池受限，创建新基准愈发昂贵和缓慢。
    Benchmarks saturate quickly, and creating new ones grows more expensive and slower given a restricted pool of expert authors.
  • 评估开发难以跟上模型能力的快速提升，尤其是在强大的推理智能体出现之后。
    Evaluation development struggles to keep pace with rapid model improvements, especially with the advent of capable reasoning agents.
  • 智能体式系统需要复杂得多的基准，需覆盖能力广度、难度以及具有经济价值的任务。
    Agentic systems demand far more complex benchmarks that cover capability breadth, difficulty, and tasks with economic value.
  • Gemini 2.5 在网络安全和机器学习研发等关键能力上显著提升，但未跨越关键能力等级。
    Gemini 2.5 shows notable increases in cybersecurity and machine-learning R&D capabilities, though it has not crossed Critical Capability Levels.
  • 一年内，Gemini Pro 在 Aider Polyglot 上提升 5 倍、在 SWE-bench verified 上提升 2 倍，显示出进展超越评估。
    Within a year, Gemini Pro improved 5x on Aider Polyglot and 2x on SWE-bench verified, illustrating progress outpacing evaluation.
