摘要 · Abstract

本文细读 Anthropic 的 S-1 文件，回顾公司财务状况并分析文件中最具影响的披露。文章认为，Anthropic 淡化了生存风险以及自身承担真实安全成本的意愿；其核心商业计划是递归自我改进；其治理结构通过 F 类股让创始人无限期保持控制权，而长期利益信托发挥的是治理而非 AI 安全机构的作用。财务上，作者主张 Anthropic 是盈利的：其报告的 800 亿美元亏损主要反映可转换票据重估带来的会计费用，而公司已募资 1411 亿美元、仍持有约 1310 亿美元，仅消耗了 94 亿美元。第三季度增长环比放缓至 45%，但作者预计第四季度将因 Opus 5.5 而表现强劲。结论是 Anthropic 的前景非常看好，尽管其约 8000 亿美元的计算承诺意味着它还需要更多资本，且 S-1 的样板文字中仍有值得注意的段落。

This article provides a close reading of Anthropic's S-1 filing, recapping the company's financial state and analyzing the document's most consequential disclosures. It argues that Anthropic downplays existential risk and its willingness to bear real safety costs, that its core business plan is recursive self-improvement, and that its governance structure leaves founders in control indefinitely through the Class F share, with the Long-Term Benefit Trust functioning as a governance rather than an AI safety body. Financially, the author contends Anthropic is profitable: its reported $80 billion loss largely reflects accounting charges from convertible note revaluations, while it has raised $141.1 billion and still holds about $131 billion, having burned only $9.4 billion. Growth slowed in Q3 to 45% quarter-over-quarter, but the author expects a strong Q4 driven by Opus 5.5. The conclusion is that Anthropic's forward prospects look very good, though its roughly $800 billion in compute commitments mean it will need more capital, and the S-1's boilerplate still contains passages that matter.

Anthropic struggled in Q3 relative to OpenAI and others. Based on prediction markets Anthropic was down ‘only’ ~4% on the news, but that was because we already knew that Anthropic went through a rough patch in terms of relative practical model quality. Anthropic 拥有资金 Anthropic Has The Money

……我们正在迈向这样一个世界：Claude 可以被赋予持续数小时、数天或数周的无限制任务，然后以一位才华横溢的同事般的判断力和主动性自主执行这些任务。

… We are building towards a world where Claude can be given open-ended tasks lasting hours, days, or weeks, and then execute them autonomously with the judgment and initiative of a brilliant colleague.

……Claude 扩散的一个核心驱动力是其智能。

… A core driver of Claude’s diffusion is its intelligence.

……我们发布的每一个新原语和应用都扩展了它们可以在 Claude 上运行的工作流范围。

… Each new primitive and application we release expands the range of workflows they can run on Claude.

你确实需要做一些外推，否则你会错过即将发生的大部分事情。

You do have to extrapolate a bit, or you miss most of what is going to be happening.

否则，你能说多少次你会做各种好事？很多次。

Otherwise, how many times can you say you will do the various good things? A lot.

They spell out what all their current products are: Claude [Code / Cowork / .ai / Security / Tag / Design / Science]. You've got Claude for [Financial Services / Financial Advisors / Healthcare / Life Sciences / Legal / Education / Government]. It's all Claude.

One term that was new to me was Prosumers, which I think stands for 'professional consumers' and means individual Pro or Max subscribers. I'm a prosumer.

They show off and highlight successful partnerships with Accenture, AIG (oh no, is this alphabetical? 不同公司给出的数据和用例各不相同。对于大量编写代码的用户，收益看起来很大。代码产出可信地达到 +100% 或更高。对其他用户而言则较为有限，例如 PayPal 每位销售团队成员每周节省 45 分钟。话说回来，一个定制插件节省约 2% 的时间，算少还是算多？SAP 每位顾问每月节省 25 小时，约 14%，又该怎么看？我预计在未来一年内，这个答案会发生变化。

Different companies offer different stats and use cases. The gains look large for those who do a lot of coding. Coding outputs seem credibly to be +100% or more. For others it is modest, such as PayPal's 45 minutes saved per sales team member per week. Then again, is ~2% time saved from a custom-built plugin not much, or is it a lot? What about the 25 hours per month saved per consultant at SAP, or ~14%? Over the next year I expect that answer to change.

别对我的监管毫不在意 Don’t Give a Damn About My Regulation

Anthropic 重申其立场，并将自己描绘成支持监管的一方。

Anthropic reiterates its position, and paints itself as pro-regulation.

我们认为，负责任的 AI 发展需要深思熟虑的治理，我们积极支持政府和监管机构建立促进安全、有益 AI 的框架的努力。我们成立的前提是，AI 系统的开发必须以安全和社会效益为核心优先事项，我们相信精心设计的监管可以在整个行业强化这些价值观。

We believe that the responsible development of AI requires thoughtful governance, and we actively support efforts by governments and regulatory bodies to establish frameworks that promote safe, beneficial AI. We were founded on the premise that AI systems must be developed with safety and societal benefit as central priorities, and we believe that well-designed regulation can reinforce those values across the industry.

由于前沿 AI 开发是一个相对新生且快速发展的领域，当前的法律、法规和标准未必是针对这些技术设计的。我们主动与美国及国际的立法者、监管者和其他利益相关者接触，以推动制定我们认为对社会、客户和经济长期健康有益的监管框架，并打算继续这样做。

Because AI development at the frontier is a relatively nascent and rapidly evolving field, the laws, regulations, and standards in place today were not necessarily designed with these technologies in mind. We have proactively engaged with legislators, regulators, and other stakeholders in the United States and internationally to promote the development of regulatory frameworks that we believe are good for society, our customers, and the long-term health of the economy, and we intend to continue doing so.

例如，我们与 CAISSI 达成协议，承诺在模型公开发布前后提供模型访问以进行测试和评估，同意根据与 CAISSI 的协议向英国 AI 安全研究所提供模型访问以进行部署前测试，并公开支持加利福尼亚州的《前沿人工智能透明度法案》（SB 53）。

For example, we entered into an agreement with the CAISSI whereby we committed to provide access to our models for testing and evaluation before and after their public release, agreed to provide model access to the U.K. AI Security Institute for pre-deployment testing consistent with our agreement with the CAISSI, and publicly endorsed California’s Transparency in Frontier Artificial Intelligence Act (SB 53).

See **recent****other****posts** on Anthropic for my response to that. Anthropic is in some ways performing better than other labs, and they do prioritize the provisions they feel are most important, even when they are not the obvious ones. But I do not feel their proposals are meeting the moment, and this is not a new issue.

这是一份 S-1 文件，因此这里真正的问题在于对 Anthropic 利润的影响。除了列举一些基本的过往事件外，他们对此并没有太多说明。

This is an S-1, so the actual question here is the impact on Anthropic's bottom line. They don't have that much to say about that, other than laying out some basic past events.

底线 The Bottom Line

归根结底，底线就是底线。S-1 文件的核心在于投资者关心什么，以及什么能防止公司因证券欺诈被起诉，或避免让金融界人士对 Anthropic 大为光火。其他受众都是次要的。

Ultimately, the bottom line is the bottom line. The S-1 is all about what investors care about, and what will guard against being sued for securities fraud or otherwise getting the financial types Big Mad at Anthropic. Other audiences are secondary.

Anthropic 仍然是一家令人沮丧的公司。现在我们对它有了更多了解。

Anthropic remains a frustrating company. Now we know a lot more about it.
