前沿的节奏

The Pacing of the Frontier

摘要 · Abstract

本文讨论了迫切需要对前沿 AI 能力的发展进行节奏控制以减轻生存风险，认为当前的发展轨迹可能导致灾难性后果。文章对比了主张建立未来减速能力的《前沿节奏》公开信与呼吁立即暂停的观点，并探讨了协调困难及潜在益处等反对意见。作者强调，可扩展的对齐对于递归自我改进至关重要，并警告狭隘的解决方案是不够的。结论是，虽然全面暂停尚不必要，但准备优雅地引导 AI 发展并根据风险升级调整节奏至关重要，因为不受控制的进步可能摧毁人类价值观和自由秩序。

The article discusses the urgent need to pace the development of frontier AI capabilities to mitigate existential risks, arguing that current trajectories could lead to catastrophic outcomes. It contrasts the Pacing the Frontier letter, which advocates for building the ability to slow down later, with calls for an immediate pause, and explores objections such as coordination difficulties and potential benefits. The author emphasizes the importance of scalable alignment for recursive self-improvement, warning that narrow solutions are insufficient. The piece concludes that while a full pause is not yet warranted, preparing to steer AI development gracefully and adjusting pace as risks escalate is critical, as uncontrolled progress could destroy human values and liberal order.

核心贡献 · Key contributions

  • 主张必须为前沿 AI 发展定速，以避免生存性灾难，而非简单暂停或加速。
    Argues frontier AI development must be paced to avoid existential catastrophe, not merely paused or accelerated.
  • 区分“定速”（保留日后减速的能力）与立即全面暂停。
    Distinguishes 'pacing' (retaining ability to slow later) from an immediate full pause.
  • 警告狭隘的对齐方案不足，递归自我改进需要可扩展对齐。
    Warns narrow alignment fixes are insufficient; scalable alignment is needed for recursive self-improvement.
  • 将选择框定为实验室间的协调问题，而非简单的囚徒困境。
    Frames the choice as a coordination problem among labs, not a simple prisoner's dilemma.
  • 提出安全论证、对齐最低算力配额，以及带反垄断豁免的行业联盟。
    Proposes safety cases, minimum compute for alignment, and industry consortia with antitrust carveouts.
  • 结论是失控的进展可能摧毁人类价值与自由秩序。
    Concludes uncontrolled progress could destroy human values and the liberal order.

局限 · Limitations

  • 定速依赖跨实验室与跨国难以建立和验证的协调机制。
    Pacing requires coordination mechanisms that are hard to build and verify across labs and nations.
  • 未给出衡量或执行预期速度限制的具体指标。
    No concrete metric for measuring or enforcing the intended speed limit is offered.
  • 假设可扩展对齐能及时实现，但这尚未得到证明。
    Assumes scalable alignment can be achieved in time; this remains unproven.
  • 风险估计（如 40%灾难概率）被承认是粗略直觉，而非严谨结论。
    Risk estimates (e.g. 40% catastrophic odds) are acknowledged as approximate vibes, not rigorous.
  • 批评者认为或许已该全面暂停，渐进定速并不足够。
    Critics argue a full pause may already be warranted, making gradual pacing insufficient.

危险，威尔·罗宾逊 Danger, Will Robinson

对于某一给定能力水平会有多危险、或它将如何物理地影响世界，人们也存在分歧；对于我们有怎样的选项、各种协调机制或政府干预的性质，以及如何平衡不同的神圣价值，同样存在分歧。人们往往只正确地看到关键权衡的一半。

There are also disagreements about how dangerous a given level of capability would be, or how it would physically impact the world, and disagreements about what options we have, the nature of various coordination mechanisms or government interventions, and balancing different sacred values. Often people only properly see one half of a key trade-off.

人们常常真诚地只看到此类关键权衡的一半，主要原因是他们预期 AI 进展如此之小，以至于缓解存在性风险或担心人类失去控制都是不必要的。

Mostly the reason people often sincerely only see one half of such key trade-offs is that they anticipate so little AI progress that mitigating existential risks or worrying about humans losing control is unnecessary.

或者他们预期 AI 进展如此之大，以至于缓解存在性与灾难性风险、维持人类控制必须成为优先事项，而这必然意味着某一群人以某种方式集体选择并规划一条审慎的路径，穿越因果空间，走向让我们得以生存的结果。

Or they anticipate so much AI progress that mitigating existential and catastrophic risks and maintaining human control has to be the priority, and that necessarily is going to mean some group of people collectively choosing and charting, in some way, a deliberate path through causal space towards outcomes that allow us to survive.

是的，这必然意味着让某一群人拥有某种集体机制，来规划地球穿越因果空间的某些方面；是的，有理由对此感到担忧，但正因如此，我们才应该努力找出做到这一点的最佳方式。

Yes, that necessarily means enabling some group of people to have some collective mechanism to chart some aspects of Earth's path through causal space, and yes there are reasons to worry about that, but that is why we should work to figure out the best way to do that.

进步的快与慢 Progress Fast and Slow

反对《为前沿定速》公开信的人并不想“放慢”脚步，而是希望 AI 走得“快”；但他们所设想的快，尽管以历史标准衡量已属超快，其实并没有那么快。

Those opposing the Pacing the Frontier letter do not want to 'slow down' and instead want AI to go 'fast,' but their vision of fast is, while super fast by historic standards, not all that fast.

签署《为前沿定速》公开信的人大多希望 AI 至少以反对者所主张的速度发展。他们想要避免的是 AI 以超级、极其、超乎寻常、近乎疯狂的速度发展。

Those who signed the Pacing the Frontier letter mostly want AI to go at least as fast as the opposition. What they want to avoid is AI going super duper ultra hyper fast.

Daniel Eth（AI 安全）：以下是我对这些词的理解：

Daniel Eth (AI Safety): Here's how I'm interpreting the words:

暂停（Pause）：踩下刹车

Pause: step on the brakes

定速（Pace）：不要在车尾装上一台巨大的火箭发动机，把我们从此 65 英里/小时加速到 10,000 英里/小时——至少在我们无法将其关闭之前不要这么做。另外，不要禁用刹车。

Pace: don't attach a giant fucking rocket engine on the back of the car that will accelerate us from 65mph to 10,000 mph, at least not unless we can turn it off. Also, don't disable the brakes.

几乎所有签署这封信的人都希望有更好的聊天机器人、更好的医生、更好的科学以及其他形式的扩散。他们不希望在 2027 年出现超级智能和奇点。

Almost everyone signing the letter wants better chatbots and doctors and science and other forms of diffusion. They don’t want superintelligence and a singularity in 2027.

Nick：许多反对派想要更好的聊天机器人、医生之类的东西，许多支持派则预期短期内会出现疯狂得多的世界，并希望它们在我们更好地控制这些东西之后稍短一点的时间内到来。有很多重叠。

Nick: A lot of the anti crowd wants better chatbots and doctors and stuff, a lot of the pro crowd expects like way crazier worlds in the short term and wants them to come in the just slightly less short term when we’ve figured out how to control these things better. Plenty of overlap.

如何做到，不知道，如何衡量速度限制，也不知道，但我觉得这个框架避免了暂停的一些问题，而暂停也有大致相同的问题。

How to do it, no idea, and also how to measure the speed limit, no idea, but I feel like this framing avoids some of the issues with pause, which also has roughly the same questions.

Dean W. Ball：这就是我认识的大多数签署“节奏”信的人（包括我自己）的想法。我们设想的放缓是暂时的，并且是放缓到一个仍然比今天快得多的进步速度。

Dean W. Ball: This is what most people I know who signed the “pacing” letter (myself included) think. The slowdown we have in mind is temporary, and to a rate of progress that is still much faster than even today’s rate.

Augmented Fifth：现在是 12 月 19 日，学校放假了，圣诞礼物在树下。“我们等到二月吧”对孩子们来说行不通。

Augmented Fifth: It’s Dec 19, school is out, and the Christmas presents are under the tree. “Let’s wait until February” is not going to fly with the kids.

我引用最后那句话，是因为当前的情况是：反对《为前沿发展定速》公开信的人表示他们不会等到二月，要求必须在 12 月 25 日拿到礼物，而且最好能拿到那把 BB 枪。

I quote that last one because what is happening is that the people who are arguing against the Pacing the Frontier letter are saying they won't wait until February and demanding they get the presents on December 25 and they'd better get that BB gun.

而签署这封信的人则在说：也许我们至少应该等到明天再订购更多礼物，因为房子里已经没有空间了；此外，也许应该让玩具保持合理，只给你那把会打中你眼睛的 BB 枪，孩子，而不是一把 AK-47、战术核弹，或者那个性感的冯·诺依曼探测器。

Whereas those signing the letter are saying maybe we should wait until at least tomorrow before we order even more presents and there is no more room left in the house, plus maybe keep the toys reasonable, and only get you the BB gun that'll shoot your eye out kid and not an AK-47 or tactical nuke or that sexy Von Neumann probe.

对《为前沿技术设定节奏》的支持声明 Statements of Support For Pacing the Frontier

另一位签署《为前沿技术设定节奏》公开信的人士的强烈评论：

Another strong comment from one of those who signed the Pacing the Frontier letter:

Drake Thomas（Anthropic）：非常高兴签署了《为前沿技术设定节奏》公开信；它的存在让我对人类的生存充满希望！

Drake Thomas (Anthropic): Very happy to have signed the Pacing the Frontier letter; its existence gives me a lot of hope for humanity’s survival!

我认可这封信的原文（并且认为，在现有约束下，对于这种程度的共识来说，它可能已经接近最佳版本），但有些地方我与它的隐含语气不同：

I endorse the letter as written (and think, given the constraints, it’s probably close to the best it could be for this level of consensus), but some places where I differ from its connotational tone:

(1) AI 不仅“不保证”能创造一个显著更好的未来，失败的概率还高得可怕：我认为*大约有 40% 的概率我们会得到与人类灭绝相当或更糟的结果，另有 30% 的概率我们会得到一个未来，虽然包含一些好的事物，但远远达不到一个更明智的文明本可以获得的水平（比如，不到一个真正伟大未来价值的 5%）。

(1) Not only is AI “not guaranteed” to make a dramatically better future, the odds of failure are terrifyingly high: I think* there’s something like a 40% chance we get an outcome around as bad as human extinction or worse, and another 30% chance we get a future that, while containing some good things, falls radically short of what a wiser civilization could have obtained (say, <5% of the value of a truly great future).

(2) 我并不真正认同“实现 AI 潜力”这种氛围；我认为一个更为直接和紧迫的动机是“避免 AI 造成灾难性坏结果，导致大量人员死亡”。（当然，潜力也非常重要，但我认为，在风险如此之高且几乎没有什么能比暂时延迟这种潜力更有用的情况下，大多数道德理论都会将其视为次要的。）

(2) I don’t really endorse the vibes of “to realize AI’s potential”; I think a much more immediate and pressing motivation is “to avoid catastrophically bad outcomes from AI that will kill a lot of people”. (The potential is also very important, ofc, but I think most moral theories would view it as being of secondary importance when risks are this high and there’s little that would do more than temporarily delay that potential anyway.)

(3) “应对新兴风险、制定安全措施并加强监督”就其本身而言是好的，但有点模糊。在放缓 AI 发展的情况下，我希望做的具体事情包括：大幅提升可解释性；建立一个强大且资源充足的第三方生态系统，对 AI 公司进行独立审计，并为其监督工作积累大量经验；投入大量精力实现对齐研究的自动化；研究 ASI 的治理机制；发展一门关于 AI 行为的本质与发展的、远比现在优越的科学；构建更强大的控制机制；将大量强大的 AI 劳动力投入到雄心勃勃的可扩展对齐项目中（例如 ARC 的工作）；对个别 AI 行为事件进行深入调查（包括外部审计）等。此外，还要做好文明层面的生物安全防范准备。

(3) “Address emerging risks, develop security measures, and strengthen oversight” is fine so far as it goes but a little vague. Concrete things I’d like to do with slower AI development: way better interpretability, build up a robust and well-resourced third-party ecosystem for independent auditing of AI companies and get lots of reps in for their oversight, put tons of effort into the automation of alignment research, work on governance mechanisms for ASI, develop a vastly better science of the nature and development of AI behavior, build much more powerful control mechanisms, point lots of powerful AI labor at ambitious scalable alignment projects (e.g., work like ARC’s), deep dives (including external audits) of individual AI behavior incidents, etc. Also getting civilizational biosecurity preparedness in order.

*认知状态：非常粗略的感觉，我的数字会每天变化，并取决于具体的操作化方式。

*Epistemic status: very approximate vibes; my numbers will change day to day and depending on the exact operationalization.

塞缪尔·哈蒙德（Samuel Hammond）就我们可能即将着手、并需要准备应对的实际事情发表了一份非常好的声明。如果你在对数图上不断画直线，并遵循所有相关事物的逻辑，你就会得到这样的结果。你可以随意援引荒谬性启发法或“瓶颈”之类的说法，但这正是实验室实际预期的情况。哈蒙德提供的版本让事情加速了不少，但对于这个周期的最终目的，他甚至还没有完全接受 ASI（超级智能）的理念：

And a very good statement about the actual thing we may be on the verge of doing, and need to prepare to handle, from Samuel Hammond. You get results like this if you keep drawing straight lines on logarithmic graphs and follow the logic of everything involved. You can invoke the absurdity heuristic or ‘bottlenecks’ or what not all you want, but this is what the labs actually expect. The variation Hammond offers has things accelerating quite a bit but is not even fully ASI (superintelligence) pilled about the ultimate ends of this cycle:

塞缪尔·哈蒙德：相反，我认为自由主义起源于霍布斯时刻，当时我们共同服从一个更高的权力，以保护我们的能动性并避免互相残杀。

Samuel Hammond: On the contrary, I’d argue liberalism originated in the Hobbesian moment when we jointly deferred to a higher power to preserve our agency and avoid killing each other.

在屈服于陷入政治理论深渊、进行内省式凝视的诱惑之前，值得退后一步，澄清究竟正在发生什么以及正在被提议什么。

Before succumbing to the temptation to navel-gaze into the political theory abyss, it’s worth stepping back and clarifying what exactly is happening and being proposed.

几家美国公司正处于完全自动化 AI 研发闭环的边缘，包括预训练/后训练、环境创建、数据生成、评估、算法与内核设计、系统工程、架构搜索等——全栈。

Several US companies are on the precipice of fully automating the AI R&D loop, inclusive of pre/post training, env creation, data generation, evals, algorithm and kernel design, systems engineering, architecture search, etc. -- the full stack.

我们已经处于通过部分自动化 SWE 实现弱 RSI 的状态，但完全闭环代表了量变到质变。进步的步伐将是爆炸性的，并且可能无法控制。

We are already in a regime of weak RSI via partially automated SWEs, but closing the loop altogether represents a difference in degree becoming a difference in kind. The pace of progress will be explosive and potentially uncontrollable.

最接近这一门槛的美国公司警告说，他们对失控的智能爆炸毫无准备，却感觉彼此之间以及在一定程度上与中国陷入了囚徒困境。

The US companies closest to this threshold are warning that they are unprepared for a runaway intelligence explosion, and yet feel locked into a prisoners dilemma vis a vis each other and to a lesser extent vis a vis China.

我们已经看到，在可验证的强化学习领域中，进展是多么迅速且相对无界，导致数学和网络领域出现尖峰形式的超级智能，包括能够证明开放数学猜想、发现破解加密的巨大加速以及执行复杂多步漏洞利用的模型。最近，由于监控和沙盒实践相对于模型能力不足，我们也看到了几起严重的“失控”/不对齐事件。此外，这些新能力主要源于在传统集群上扩大长视野后训练，而大量新算力即将上线/正在建设中。

We’ve already seen how rapid and comparatively unbounded progress is in verifiable RL domains, leading to spikey forms of superintelligence in math and cyber, including models that can prove open math conjectures, discover massive speed-ups for breaking encryption, and execute sophisticated multi-step exploits. We’ve also recently seen several severe examples of “loss of control” / misalignment incidents given inadequate monitoring and sandboxing practices relative to model capability. Moreover, these new capabilities mostly stem from scaling-up long-horizon post-training on legacy clusters, with OOMs of new compute about come online / in construction.

在 RSI 之前的状态下，人为摩擦在新模型发布之间创造了自动缓冲，使研究人员和社会有时间探索涌现能力、设计更好的评估、开发新的对齐技术，并适应/加固其基础设施。随着进展加速，能力提升已经开始超越我们的适应能力，这体现在 METR 无法评估超过 13 小时的模型自主性，以及网络防御者为 Mythos 的开放权重版本做准备的窗口很窄。

In the pre-RSI regime, human frictions created automatic buffers between new model releases, giving researchers and society time to probe emergent capabilities, design better evals, develop novel alignment techniques, and adapt / harden their infrastructure. As progress has accelerated, capability improvements have already started outstripping our adaptive capacity, as manifest in METR’s inability to evaluate model autonomy beyond 13 hours, and narrow window for cyber defenders to prepare for open weight versions of Mythos.

RSI 将加剧上述所有问题，并制造出全新的问题。至少，我们应当预见到：

RSI will exacerbate all these issues and create entirely new ones. At minimum, we should anticipate:

- 能力上相当于从 GPT-5.2 → 5.6 的跃升至少每 24 小时发生一次（相比此前的 3–6 个月大幅缩短），

- the equivalent of a GPT-5.2 → 5.6 leap in capabilities at least every 24 hours (down from 3–6 months),

- 同步的算法改进使模型在任意给定能力水平上都被致密化到超高效规模，

- concurrent algorithmic improvements densifying models to ultra-efficient sizes at any given capability level,

- 在大多数可验证领域（包括化学、核与生物）出现 100 倍于 Mythos 级别的能力，

- 100x Mythos-like capabilities across most verifiable domains, including chemistry, nuclear, and biology,

- 新形式的多智能体失配风险

- new forms of multi-agent misalignment risk

- “盒中公司”智能体，经训练可组建整个组织/公司

- "company in a box" agents trained to stand up whole organizations / corporations

- 类似“网络核弹”的能力，仅需极低基础设施

- "cyber nuke"-like capabilities that require de minimis infrastructure

- 若干 Transformer 规模的突破，例如长期记忆/持续学习、开放式领域，和/或面向理想化“GPT-zero”式元学习器的全新训练技术

- several transformer-scale breakthroughs, such as for long-term memory / continual learning, open-ended domains, and/or all-new training techniques for idealized "GPT-zero"-esque metalearners

- 任何互补技术领域的同步加速，即研发和新型发现的爆炸式增长

- concurrent speedups in any complementary technical domain, i.e. explosive rates of R&D and novel discoveries

在我看来，拥有“调节”这些发展的社会技术，而不是在零行业/政府协调下任其狂飙，似乎失少得多、得多得多，尤其是技术上没有法律明确禁止公司让 RSI 循环无限运行并将另一端产生的任何东西释放到世界中。

It seems to me there is little to lose, and much to gain, from having the social technology to "pace" these developments rather than to let them rip with zero industry / gov't coordination, particularly as there is technically no law explicitly prohibiting a company from letting an RSI loop run indefinitely and unleashing whatever comes out the other end into the world.

一场缺乏协调的智能爆炸可能以无数种方式对自由主义事业造成彻底灾难，包括权力集中失控、社会迅速失稳、失控 AI/失去控制场景、大规模杀伤性武器扩散、脆弱世界技术等。

There are innumerable ways an uncoordinated intelligence explosion could become an unmitigated disaster for the cause of liberalism, including runaway power concentration, rapid societal destabilization, rogue AIs / loss of control scenarios, WMD mass proliferation, vulnerable world technologies, and beyond.

Political theory is great, and I would love to use our limited steering capacity to guide AI development toward a future that maximizes individual liberty, but as a discussion baseline, gesturing at philosophical abstractions is simply non-responsive to the crisis at hand. A red-herring at best, a suicidal circlejerk at worst.

如果你从事能力研究，也请转向可扩展对齐，这对递归自我改进（RSI）和 AI 研发自动化至关重要。

If you are in capabilities, also pivot to scalable alignment that matters for RSI (recursive self-improvement) and automation of AI R&D.

Mo Bavarian（OpenAI）：这是一个超现实的时刻。很少有人能预测到，在 GPT-2/3 之后仅仅几年，AI 就会进步到解决最高水平的数学问题。那时的模型还不能可靠地解决小学级别的数学问题。它们勉强能起草电子邮件。它们仍然非常像统计鹦鹉。

Mo Bavarian (OpenAI): This is a surreal moment. Few people could have predicted that the AI will advance to solving math problems at the highest level only a few years after GPT-2/3. The models then couldn’t reliably solve grade school math problems. They barely were good enough to draft emails. They still very much felt like statistical parrots.

所有看似根本性的限制，随着一些进展（例如大规模强化学习）在几年内慢慢消失，而这几年实际上非常短。我们应该既敬畏又难以置信地注视这一刻。再几年的进步会带来什么？它将如何影响世界和整个社会？我们是否准备好迎接指尖上的智能海啸？

All things that looked like fundamental limitations slowly faded with some advances (e.g. high scale RL) in a span of a few years, which is really a short time. We should behold this moment both in awe and disbelief. What will a few more years of progress bring? How is it going to impact the world and society at large? Are we ready for the tsunami of intelligence at our fingertip?

比任何其他时刻都更让我觉得这是奇点的前夜。Glasswing 和 huggingface 事件进一步增加了这种严重性。

More than any other moment, this feels to me like the eve of singularity. Glasswing & huggingface incident further increase the gravity.

几年前，我内心深处觉得从事对齐研究为时过早。如果你有热情，那当然很好，但在我看来，事物的形态还不够清晰，不足以使其成为关键。你最终从事的工作对对齐真正的 AGI 毫无用处的可能性很高。现在不同了。现在，这感觉像是我们面临的最关键的事情。

A few years ago, deep down I felt working on alignment is premature. It’s nice to do if it’s your passion, but the shapes of things weren’t clear enough for it to be critical in my opinion. The chances that you end up working on things that are useless for aligning the actual AGI was high. It’s different now. Now, it feels like it’s the most critical thing facing us.

我假设 Mo 指的是旨在作为长期解决方案的对齐工作。显然，“世俗对齐”工作一直是有价值的。我认为尽早思考这类问题一直很有价值，因为许多工作有很长的前置时间且无法并行进行，但我同意我们现在需要大力投入真正的 RSI 对齐。

I assume Mo means alignment work intended as a long-term solution. Obviously 'mundane alignment' work was always worthwhile. I think it was always valuable to get to thinking about such problems early, as much of this has long lead times and cannot be done in parallel, but I agree that we need to go heavily into real RSI-alignment now.

我强烈赞同 Yo Shavit 下面的陈述。你需要将你的 RSI 对齐努力——那些真正重要的——集中在完全具备 Scaling（规模扩张）意识的通用解决方案上。狭窄的解决方案和渐进的局部步骤是另一个部门的事，而且如果有风险的话，它们会掩盖问题。狭窄的解决方案只有在能让你获得可用于解决通用问题的模型时才有用。

I strongly endorse Yo Shavit's statement below. You need to focus your RSI-alignment efforts, the ones that count, on general solutions that are fully scaling pilled. Narrow solutions and incremental local steps are a different department, and if anything risk disguising the problem. The narrow solutions are useful insofar as they get you models you can use to solve the general problems.

Yo Shavit（OpenAI 基金会）：Mo 多年来一直是 OpenAI 的核心能力研究员。他的话很有分量。我希望 OAI 开始真正将其大量研究人员的日常工作转向这些关键的 RSI 对齐+控制项目，这已被长期讨论但仍未执行。（据我所知，目前仍然是 1000 多人中的约 20 人，而且通过招聘无法足够快地弥合这一差距，这意味着需要领导层将优先级从不太关乎使命的项目上转移过来。）是时候了，虽然至少还有几个月的时间，但根据我的经验，除非有足够数量的研究人员用他们的声音和行动来推动，否则这不会发生。

Yo Shavit (OpenAI Foundation): Mo has been a core capabilities researcher for many years at OpenAI. His words carry weight. I hope OAI starts actually pivoting the mass of its researchers' day-to-day work towards these critical RSI alignment+control projects, as has been long discussed but still not executed on. (AFAIK it is still ~20 people out of >1000, and there is no way to bridge that gap fast enough with hiring, meaning it requires leadership to shift priorities from less-Mission-critical projects.) It is time, while there are still at least months left, but in my experience it will not happen unless a needed mass of researchers push for it with their voices and feet.

JTBC，对齐和控制项目不仅仅意味着 ML 研究人员不擅长的深奥数学，它可以包括极其明显的项目，比如“构建更好的监控器并研究你可以引发共谋的条件”，“研究预训练人格对齐在 RL 运行深度上的泛化”，“对早期世代的 RL 环境进行历史分析，修复/修剪那些环境中每一个 hack 模式，使得 hacking 事实上不可能，然后训练一个不奖励 hack 的 RL 模型，看看你是否真的可以通过足够的努力消除奖励黑客行为”，甚至“研究评分器算力与智能体算力的缩放定律，以确定最小化奖励黑客行为的均衡点”。顶级实验室的任何优秀 RS 都可以做这些中的任何一个。如果你在想法上遇到瓶颈，私信我，我会给你几百个。

JTBC, alignment and control projects don't just mean esoteric math that ML-researchers aren't suited for, it can include extremely obvious projects like 'build better monitors and study the conditions under which you can elicit collusion', 'study generalization of pretrained persona alignment across RL run depth', 'do historical analyses of RL envs from earlier generations, fix/prune every hack pattern in those envs so hacking is de facto impossible, and then train an RL model that doesn't reward hack to see whether you can actually expunge reward-hacking with sufficient effort', or even 'study scaling laws of grader compute vs. agent compute to identify equilibria that minimize reward hacking'. Any good RS at a top lab can do any of these. If you're bottlenecked on ideas, dm me, I will get you hundreds.

我所说的 RSI 对齐*不是*指“想出一个数据集来修复最近一次运行中你迄今为止发现的最普遍的 hack 类型”。虽然这对下个月发布有用（并且对那些投入其中的人无可指责），但这种方法严重缺乏 Scaling（规模扩张）意识，并且意味着我们一直在让模型暂时大致可用，而未解决的问题却越来越大。

What I *don't* mean by RSI-alignment is 'come up with a dataset that fixes the most recent run's most prevalent type of hack you have so far caught in the latest run'. While useful for shipping in the next month (and no shame to the people who've put their back into it), it is a deeply un-scaling-pilled approach and has meant we keep making models temporarily roughly usable while the unaddressed problems get bigger and bigger.

你可以也应当从当前训练运行中剪除作弊模式（hack patterns），但你也需要一个即便存在潜在作弊模式仍能成立的解决方案，因为足够先进的 AI 会找到作弊模式。

You can and should prune the hack patterns out of the current training runs, but you also need a solution that survives there being potential hacking patterns, because a sufficiently advanced AI will find hacking patterns.

快速推进 Taking It Fast

大型实验室中的许多人认为，事态即将以更快的速度升级。但除此之外，几乎没有人预期在未来五年内会出现真正意义上的奇点——就其对短期经济的影响而言。

Many at the major labs think things are about to escalate even more quickly. Almost no one else expects a true singularity in the next five years, in terms of its short-term economic impacts.

Patrick Collison：就 AI 的经济学做了一次快速调查。

Patrick Collison: Made a quick survey about the economics of AI.

Tenobrus：该死。你们这些家伙还真没一个相信奇点啊。

Tenobrus: damn. none of you motherfuckers really believe in the singularity huh

人们对 AI 资本支出（Capex）也抱有很高期望。这里的长尾分布有点疯狂，但没错，我们应当预期数字继续上涨（Number Go Up）。

There are also high expectations for AI Capex. The long tail here is kind of crazy, but yes we should expect Number Go Up.

全速前进 Full Speed Ahead

那么，实验室里的人大体上是怎么想的？

Whereas what do those at the labs largely think?

这里的“真正好的模型”是暗语，指的是 AI 研发自动化的开端、递归式自我改进，基本上就是奇点。

'Really good models' here is code for the start of automation of AI R&D, recursive self-improvement and basically a singularity.

Tibo：我们开发出真正好的模型的那一天。会有迹象的。尽管负载不断上升，可靠性却在提高。效率突然提升。事情变得更快。重置。诸如此类的事情。

Tibo: The day we develop really good models. There will be signs. Reliability increasing despite load going up and up. Sudden efficiency gains. Things getting faster. Resets. These kinds of things.

Sam Altman（OpenAI CEO）：它本可以更快。

Sam Altman (CEO OpenAI): it could be faster.

Amanda Askell（Anthropic）：不要对那些说深度学习正撞上墙的人不友善。我们都需要生活中有一点希望。

Amanda Askell (Anthropic): Do not be unkind to those who say deep learning is hitting a wall. We all need a little hope in our lives.

我确实同意，我们应当预期的一个迹象是大量效率提升。你知道的，就像 OpenAI 在 Luna 和 Sol 上报告的那样，而且每隔几周就不断有模型发布。我确信这没什么大不了的。

I do agree that one sign we should expect is a bunch of efficiency gains. You know, like what OpenAI is reporting with Luna and Sol, and constantly getting model releases every few weeks. I'm sure it's nothing.

大约一天后，OpenAI 宣布它已解决了 10 个重大开放数学问题。

About a day later OpenAI announced it had solved 10 major open math problems.

自杀小队 Suicide Squad

Robin Hanson 是这样总结的：“人们正确地注意到，他们默认情况下都会死去，他们所珍视的一切都将被摧毁，而他们并不喜欢这一点”：

This is how Robin Hanson summarizes 'people correctly notice they by default are all going to die and everything they value will be destroyed, and they don't like that':

MTS：斯坦福经济学家 @pawtrammell 警告说，让机器拥有自身会导致一个马尔萨斯式的未来，在那里自我复制的机器人会烧尽宇宙中的所有能量：

MTS: Stanford economist @pawtrammell warns that letting machines own themselves leads to a Malthusian future where self-replicating bots burn all the energy in the universe:

“有一些所谓的‘继承主义者’，他们认为，如果 AI 在各方面都比我们更好，如果它们更聪明，如果它们有感知能力、能感受到比我们更多的幸福，那么我们就应该直接把世界交给它们。”

"There are so-called successionists who think that if the AIs are just sort of better than us in every way, if they're more intelligent, if they're sentient and able to feel more happiness than we can, then we should just turn the world over to them."

“显然，一群智能生命并不必然实现对其而言处于帕累托前沿的结果。它们可能陷入各种协调失败之中。”

"Obviously, groups of intelligent beings don't necessarily achieve outcomes that are on the Pareto frontier for them. They can get locked into all kinds of coordination failures."

“一群自我复制的机器人把自己带入马尔萨斯式的未来，并不是你想要的结局。世界和宇宙中充满了那些复制得最快的东西，它们为了扩散而烧尽那里所有的能量。没有人想要那样。”

"A swarm of self-replicating bots getting itself into a Malthusian future isn't where you want to end up. The world and the universe are filled with whatever's replicating most quickly and just burning all the energy up there in an attempt to spread. No one wants that."

“你真正想要的是某种能让所有人繁荣发展的未来方案。而就目前而言，我认为这意味着要拥有这样的机器人：它们听从指令，并在我们与它们之间建立起受到良好规范的互动模式。”

“What you want is some plan for the future in which we all flourish. And for the time being, I think that means having bots that do what they’re told and create well-regulated patterns of interaction between us and themselves.”

Robin Hanson：很多人确实不喜欢达尔文式选择的世界。

Robin Hanson: Many people really don’t like worlds of Darwinian selection.

准备调整你的节奏 Prepare To Adjust Your Pace

我就是这样的人之一。我不指望会喜欢一场不受控制的达尔文式选择向前推进所带来的结果。我也不指望会喜欢在当前条件下构建超级智能 AI 时可能发生的各种其他事情。

I am one of those people. I do not expect to like the results of an uncontrolled forward process of Darwinian selection. I also do not expect to like various other things that might happen if we build superintelligent AIs under current conditions.

我认为目前并不需要完全暂停，但情况可能很快改变。我确实认为这是一个重新评估许多事情的机会，而且我们发现迫切需要把我们的各项事务整理妥当，这很可能会延缓短期能力进展。我认为这些行动必须采取，OpenAI 原则上同意这一点。

I do not think a full pause is warranted at this time, but that could quickly change. I do think this is an opportunity to reassess quite a few things, and we have found an urgent need to get various parts of our house in order, in ways that will likely delay short-term capabilities progress. I think those actions need to be taken, and OpenAI agrees in principle.

因此，我们集体需要评估我们的处境，增强我们优雅地掌控方向的能力，并准备好调整我们的节奏。如果我们不准备优雅地做到这一点，那么要么我们会等待太久而完全做不到，要么更可能的是，我们会以极不优雅的方式去做，没有合适的工具，随手使用当时手边可用的东西，就像我们人类处理这类事情时经常做的那样。让我们不要让事情发展到那一步。

Thus, we collectively need to assess our situation, increase our ability to steer gracefully, and be ready to adjust our pace. If we do not prepare to do it gracefully, then either we will wait too long and fail to do it at all, or more likely we will attempt to do it highly ungracefully, without the right tools, using whatever is handy at the time, as we humans often deal with such matters. Let's not let it come to that.
